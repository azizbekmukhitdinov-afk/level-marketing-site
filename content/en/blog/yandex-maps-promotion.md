Someone in Tashkent searching for "dentist near me", "car service Chilanzar" or "flower delivery" often sees the results on a map — and picks one of the first 3–5 listings. That's why ranking high on the map means a free flow of customers for a local business. We covered adding a listing in [add your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/). This article is about the next step: strengthening your Yandex Maps listing and moving it up.

## What affects your position on the map

Yandex doesn't disclose its full algorithm, but in practice three groups of factors show up:

| Factor | What it is | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Relevance | How well the listing matches the search | The right categories, a list of services, a description |
| Distance | How close the person is to you | You can't change it — but make the address precise |
| Prominence and quality | Reviews, rating, activity | A review system, replies, updates |

The third group is where the most work — and the most results — lie.

## 1. Complete your listing 100%

- **Name** — your official name, without stuffed keywords.
- **Categories** — a main one plus extras: "dentistry" + "children's dentistry" + "implants".
- **Address and map pin** — with the exact entrance.
- **Phone, website, messengers** — all working.
- **Opening hours** — updated for holidays too.
- **Services and prices** — a list with "from" prices.
- **Description** — in Uzbek and Russian, naturally using the words people search for.

On language and script in search — [Uzbek search](/en/blog/uzbek-search-latin-cyrillic/).

## 2. Photos: real and recent

- the façade and entrance — so people can find the building;
- the interior, the team, the work process;
- products or results;
- new photos every month — the listing should look "alive".

Stock photos and images taken from elsewhere reduce trust.

## 3. A review system

Reviews are one of the strongest map factors and the main reason people choose you. The system:

1. **When to ask**: right after the service or within 1–3 days.
2. **An easy path**: a QR code at the till, a link in a Telegram message.
3. **Ask everyone**, not just happy customers.
4. **Reply to every review** — positive and negative.

No fake reviews: if exposed, they damage your reputation and bring platform penalties and misleading-advertising risk. More in [online reputation and reviews](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/). Drawing conclusions from reviews — [AI review analysis](/en/blog/ai-review-analysis/).

## 4. Activity: updates and offers

If the listing lets you post updates and offers, use it: a new service, a seasonal offer, changed hours. It shows both people and the system that the listing is active.

## 5. Paid visibility

Yandex offers paid visibility tools for businesses — for example, Maps placement campaigns through Yandex Direct. Yandex's help centre says that if an organisation has the extended plan in Yandex Business, a Maps placement campaign appears in its list of Direct campaigns ([Yandex Direct help](https://yandex.ru/support/direct/ru/company-ads/maps)). Plans and availability for Uzbekistan may change — check in your Yandex Business account.

Paid visibility boosts a good listing but won't rescue a poor one: with few reviews and old photos, people won't click. On Yandex advertising — [Yandex Direct in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/yandex-direct-in-uzbekistan/).

## Measuring results

Track the statistics in your Yandex Business account:

- listing views;
- actions: calls, route requests, website clicks;
- which searches people find you through.

And add a "maps" option to the "How did you hear about us?" question in your CRM. Measuring offline results — [measure offline advertising](/en/blog/measure-offline-advertising/).

## Google Maps too

Many people use Google Maps as well. Keep information identical in both listings — name, address, phone, hours. It matters for AI answers and search too — [GEO: getting into AI answers](/en/blog/geo-ai-search-visibility/).

## Common mistakes

- A listing created once and never updated for years.
- A wrong category, or only one.
- No replies to reviews, especially negative ones.
- Outdated hours — people arrive at a closed door.
- Stuffing keywords into the name: "Dentist Tashkent cheap best".

## Questions and answers

### How long does it take to climb on the map?

Once the listing is complete, changes may show within a few weeks. Reviews and activity are ongoing work.

### What if I have several branches?

A separate listing for each branch. More in [marketing a second branch](/en/blog/marketing-a-second-branch/).

### Can I give a gift for a review?

Rewarding only positive reviews is risky — for platform rules and trust alike. Ask for reviews, but don't buy their content.

### Can I delete a negative review?

You can report reviews that break the platform's rules. For an ordinary negative review, a calm, constructive reply is the best route.

## Conclusion

To rank high on Yandex Maps, you need three things: a 100% complete listing, real photos and an ongoing review system — with a reply to every review.
