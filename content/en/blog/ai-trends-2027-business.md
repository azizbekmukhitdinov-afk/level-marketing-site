In 2025–2026, AI went from an interesting toy to an everyday work tool: copy, images, translation, spreadsheet analysis. In 2027 the question won't be "do we use AI?" but "which process do we rebuild with AI?". Here are the AI directions that matter for businesses in Uzbekistan, with a practical step for each.

## 1. From chat to agent

An AI agent doesn't just answer — it carries out a task: reads an enquiry, logs it in the CRM, drafts a reply to the customer, reminds the salesperson. Simple agents can already be built without code — [AI agents for business](/en/blog/ai-agent-for-business/), [build an AI agent](/en/blog/build-an-ai-agent/).

## 2. AI search and AI ads

People put their questions to Google's AI answers and to ChatGPT. Ads in ChatGPT launched in 2026 and reached 89 countries ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html)). For businesses, it's a new space for visibility — [Google AI Mode](/en/blog/google-ai-mode-for-business/), [ChatGPT ads](/en/blog/chatgpt-ads/).

## 3. Video from photos and avatars

AI can turn a photo into a short video, text into a voice and create a "digital avatar". That lowers the cost of product videos and explainers. But for trust, real people and real products still matter — [photo-to-video AI](/en/blog/photo-to-video-ai/), [AI avatar videos](/en/blog/ai-avatar-videos/).

## 4. Managing sales with AI

Transcribing and analysing call recordings is the most useful and most underrated direction. Instead of listening to every call, AI gives a summary: objections, script compliance, the next step — [AI call analysis](/en/blog/ai-call-analysis/).

## 5. Vibe coding: internal tools without a developer

With AI, you can build a page, a calculator or an internal tool by describing it in plain words. For small businesses, that's automation that used to be expensive — [vibe coding for business](/en/blog/vibe-coding-for-business/).

## 6. Google's tools and AI Studio

Gemini is moving deeper into Google's work tools, and Google AI Studio is a free place to test models and get an API key — [how to use Gemini for business](/en/blog/how-to-use-gemini-for-business/), [Google AI Studio](/en/blog/google-ai-studio-for-business/).

## 7. The Uzbek language

Gemini officially supports Uzbek ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/11/29/google-gemini-introduces-uzbek-language-support-on-its-ai-platform)), and other models are improving too. But quality is still uneven — a native speaker should check Uzbek texts — [AI translation for business](/en/blog/ai-translation-for-business/).

## 8. Law and accountability

In January 2026, Uzbekistan introduced a fine of 50–100 BCA for unlawfully processing personal data with AI ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8008401)). Set rules before anyone puts customer data into AI — [AI law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).

## A plan for the team: 3 steps

1. **Inventory**: list the 10 tasks your team repeats every week.
2. **Choose**: try 2–3 of them with AI — copy, analysis, translation.
3. **Rules**: what data must never be entered, who checks, where it's stored — [train your team on AI](/en/blog/train-team-on-ai/).

## What not to hand to AI

Final decisions on prices and contracts, sensitive customer disputes, unchecked facts and your brand's core voice — [what not to delegate to AI](/en/blog/what-not-to-delegate-to-ai/).

## Questions and answers

### Which AI tool should I choose?

Start from the task, not the tool. Test 2–3 tools on one task — [ChatGPT or Gemini for business](/en/blog/chatgpt-or-gemini-for-business/).

### Will AI replace employees?

In many small businesses, no — it frees employees from routine work. Checking and decisions stay with people.

### How much does AI cost?

Many tools have a free version; paid team plans can start at a few tens of dollars a month — check prices on the official pages.

## Conclusion

AI in 2027 means agents, AI search and ads, video, sales oversight and tools built without developers. The advantage lies not in the tool but in processes and rules.
