In many businesses, Instagram ads run from the media buyer's personal account: when they leave, the ad account, statistics and audiences leave too. To prevent that, you need a **Meta business portfolio** (formerly Business Manager): all the company's assets — Pages, Instagram, ad accounts, pixels — in one place and under the owner's control. Here's how to create and set one up.

## Why you need it

- **Ownership**: the assets belong to the company, not an employee.
- **Access rights**: staff and agencies get access at the right level, without passwords.
- **Security**: two-step verification and control over admins.
- **Advertising**: several ad accounts, pixels and audiences in one place.

## Step 1: create the portfolio

1. Go to business.facebook.com with the business owner's Facebook account.
2. Create a business portfolio: company name, your name, a work email.
3. Fill in the company details.

## Step 2: add assets

| Asset | What to do |
|---|---|
| Facebook Page | Add an existing one or create a new one |
| Instagram account | Connect it — [create an Instagram business account](/en/blog/create-instagram-business-account/) |
| Ad account | Create a new one or add an existing one; choose the currency and time zone carefully |
| Pixel / data source | For your website — [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/) |

**Important:** if an ad account or Page sits in someone else's portfolio, you add it by requesting access, not by claiming ownership — find out exactly who owns it.

## Step 3: people and roles

- **Admins** have full control: at least two people (for example, the owner and a trusted manager).
- **Employees** get access only to the assets they need, at the level they need.
- Review every role regularly.

## Step 4: working with an agency

Don't give an agency your password — add it as a **partner** and grant access to the relevant assets. When the work ends, you remove the access and keep the assets — [give an agency access to your Meta ad account](/en/blog/give-agency-access-meta-ad-account/).

## Step 5: payments

- Add the company card to the ad account.
- Restrict who can change payment methods.
- Download invoices regularly.

## Step 6: security

- Require two-step verification for all admins in the portfolio settings.
- Don't respond to suspicious "Meta Support" messages — [business account security](/en/blog/business-account-security/).

## Common mistakes

- The ad account sits in the media buyer's personal portfolio.
- A single admin — if they're blocked, everything stops.
- The wrong currency or time zone — these can't be changed later, so you'd need a new ad account.
- Full admin rights for every employee.

What to do if your ad account is blocked — [ad account blocked](/en/blog/ad-account-blocked/).

## Questions and answers

### Are Business Manager and business portfolio the same thing?

Yes — Meta renamed Business Manager to business portfolio.

### Can one portfolio hold several Instagram accounts?

Yes, for several brands or branches.

### Is creating a portfolio paid?

No, it's free. You pay only for advertising.

## Conclusion

A Meta business portfolio keeps the company's assets in the company's hands: Page, Instagram, ad account and pixel in one place, two admins, password-free access rights and two-step verification. In a free audit, Level Marketing will check your portfolio and access rights.
