"Uzum seller" is one of the most searched phrases among business owners. The marketplace gives access to millions of shoppers, but success there comes not from "listing the product" but from doing the maths, listing quality and ratings. Here are the first steps to start selling on Uzum Market. On building a brand on the marketplace — [building a brand on Uzum Market](/en/blog/building-a-brand-on-uzum-market/).

![Step 1: registration](/img/blog/illus/uzum-market-sotuvchi-1.jpg 1200x675)

## Step 1: registration

- Apply via the seller page (seller.uzum.uz).
- You usually need to be registered as a legal entity or sole trader — check the exact requirements and documents on the seller page.
- Then the contract, bank details and access to the seller account.

## Step 2: the maths — before listing

On a marketplace, revenue and profit are very different things. For each product, calculate:

| Cost | Note |
|---|---|
| Cost of goods | Product + packaging |
| Commission | Varies by category — check the terms in your account |
| Logistics and storage | Depend on the fulfilment model and volume |
| Advertising | On-platform and external |
| Returns | Especially for clothing and footwear |
| Taxes | Depending on your tax regime |

If no margin remains after all costs, change the price or the range — [unit economics](/en/blog/unit-economics-for-small-business/).

## Step 3: the listing is your salesperson

- **Photos**: white background, several angles, size and use — [AI product photos](/en/blog/ai-product-photos/).
- **Title**: product type + key feature + brand, using the words shoppers search for.
- **Description and specifications**: complete and precise — answer questions in advance.
- **Video**: short, showing the product in use.

Speeding up listings with AI — [AI product listings on Uzum](/en/blog/ai-product-listing-uzum/).

![Step 4: pricing](/img/blog/illus/uzum-market-sotuvchi-2.jpg 1200x675)

## Step 4: pricing

- Watch competitors, but don't compete on price alone.
- Work out your margin before joining promotions — [the discount trap](/en/blog/discount-trap/).

## Step 5: warehousing and delivery

Uzum offers different fulfilment models — handing goods to its warehouse or shipping from your own. Check in your seller account which models are available and on what terms. The key rule: don't run out of stock — when stock runs out, your listing drops.

## Step 6: ratings and reviews

- Good packaging and accurate descriptions prevent negative reviews.
- Answer questions and reviews quickly.
- Respond to negative reviews calmly, with a solution — [online reputation and reviews](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/).

![A plan for the first 30 days](/img/blog/illus/uzum-market-sotuvchi-3.jpg 1200x675)

## A plan for the first 30 days

| Week | What to do |
|---|---|
| 1 | 5–10 best products, with complete listings |
| 2 | Tune prices and stock levels, collect first reviews |
| 3 | Test on-platform advertising |
| 4 | Review: what sells, and what's the margin |

## Questions and answers

### I have my own shop — do I need a marketplace?

Usually yes, as an extra channel. But the customer base stays with the marketplace, so keep building your own channels too.

### Can I send Instagram ad traffic to my Uzum listing?

Yes — external traffic can help lift a listing. Measure the results.

### What's the most common mistake?

Setting prices without accounting for commission and logistics.

## Conclusion

Selling on Uzum Market means doing the maths first, then building quality listings, pricing correctly, keeping stock and growing your rating. Start with 5–10 products and review results after 30 days. In a free audit, Level Marketing will check your online sales channels.
