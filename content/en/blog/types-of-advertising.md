"Which advertising works best?" is the question business owners ask most. The honest answer: none is "best" — each type of advertising has its own job. A billboard builds awareness, search ads capture existing demand, targeted ads create new demand. Here are the main types of advertising, what each does and how to choose the right ones for your business.

## The main types: an overview

| Type | Job | Best for | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Targeted ads (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok) | New demand and leads | Many businesses, especially visual products | Precise: leads, sales |
| Search ads (Google, Yandex) | Capturing existing demand | Services people actively search for | Precise |
| Influencers | Trust and fast awareness | New brands, mass-market products | Moderate: promo codes, links |
| Telegram channel ads | Reaching a specific audience | Education, services, B2B | Moderate |
| Marketplace ads | Visibility inside a marketplace | Sellers on Uzum and other platforms | Precise |
| Outdoor (billboards, signage) | Awareness, local footfall | Stores, restaurants, clinics | Hard |
| TV and radio | Broad reach | Brands with large budgets | Hard |
| SMS advertising | Reminding existing customers | Businesses with a customer base | Precise, but needs consent |

## Online advertising

### Targeted ads

Ads on social networks shown to a chosen audience. Often the main channel for small and mid-sized businesses in Uzbekistan. More in [what is targeted advertising](/en/blog/what-is-targeted-advertising/).

### Search ads

Shown at the moment of searching — demand already exists. More in [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).

### Influencers and Telegram channels

Advertising through someone else's audience and trust. The ad must be labelled. More in [influencer advertising](/en/blog/influencer-advertising-uzbekistan/) and [Telegram advertising](/en/blog/telegram-advertising/).

## Offline advertising

### Outdoor advertising

Billboards, signs, transport ads — a source of awareness for local businesses. Outdoor advertising has its own rules and permits — [storefront signs and outdoor advertising](/en/blog/storefront-sign-outdoor-advertising-uzbekistan/).

### SMS advertising

Messages to your customer base. Promotional SMS requires the subscriber's consent — [SMS advertising rules](/en/blog/sms-advertising-rules-uzbekistan/).

### TV, radio, print

Broad reach, but expensive and hard to measure. Usually not the first choice for a small business.

How to measure offline advertising — [measure offline advertising](/en/blog/measure-offline-advertising/).

## Choosing by funnel stage

It helps to map ad types to stages of the customer journey:

| Stage | Customer's state | Suitable advertising |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Doesn't know you yet | Targeted ads, influencers, outdoor |
| Searching | Has a need, looking for options | Search ads, maps |
| Comparing | Weighing several options | Retargeting, reviews |
| Returning customer | Has bought before | SMS, Telegram — with consent |

On retargeting — [how retargeting works](/en/blog/how-retargeting-works/).

## How to choose: 4 questions

1. **How do customers find me?** Do they search, or come across me by chance?
2. **Is my product visual?** Yes — targeted ads and video. No — search ads and articles.
3. **What's the budget?** Small — 1–2 online channels. Large — a channel mix.
4. **Can I measure the result?** A big budget on unmeasurable advertising is risky.

Budget planning — [planning a marketing budget](/en/blog/planning-a-marketing-budget/). Comparing platforms — [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

## The law applies to all types

Whatever type you use, the Law "On Advertising" applies: misleading ads are prohibited, prices must be in soum and advertising must be recognisable as such ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6052631)). More in [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## Common mistakes

- "A competitor put up a billboard, so will we" — without thinking about the job.
- Launching 5–6 channels at once on a small budget.
- Putting the biggest budget into unmeasurable advertising.
- One channel fails, so "advertising doesn't work".
- Sending SMS without consent.

## Questions and answers

### Which type is best for a small business?

Usually targeted ads plus a maps listing — easy to control the budget and measure results.

### Should several types be combined?

Yes, but in stages: get one channel working first, then add more.

### Does outdoor advertising still work?

For local businesses, yes — especially stores and restaurants. But measure it with a QR code or promo code.

### What's the cheapest advertising?

The one with the lowest cost per customer — not the lowest cost per impression.

## Conclusion

Ad types don't compete with each other — each works at a different stage of the customer journey. Choose one or two channels based on how customers find you, measure results by sales, and only then expand.
