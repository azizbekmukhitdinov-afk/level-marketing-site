Most of the time spent working with AI goes into thinking up the request (the prompt). This article gives you 50 ready-made marketing prompts — for content, ads, customer replies, analysis and strategy. They work in ChatGPT, Gemini and other chat assistants. But before using them, take one step: **fill in the square brackets with your own business details**. That's what turns a generic answer into one that fits your business.

## Before any prompt: a context block

Send this block once at the start of a conversation — every prompt after that builds on it:

> You're the marketer for a [type of business] in [city]. We sell [what], priced [range]. Our customers are [who], and they worry most about [problem]. What sets us apart: [1–3 real advantages]. Write in [language], simply and naturally. Don't use "best", "No. 1" or unprovable numbers.

More on the parts of a good prompt — [how to use ChatGPT for business](/en/blog/how-to-use-chatgpt-for-business/).

## Rules for working with prompts

- **Don't enter personal data**: customers' names, phones and chats only in anonymised form. More in [AI and the law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).
- **Don't publish the first answer**: refine it 2–3 times and edit.
- **Check the facts**: remove or verify numbers and claims the AI adds.
- **Keep your voice**: give your best posts as examples.
- **Measure results**: compare AI-written and human-written posts.

## Questions and answers

### Do these prompts work in Uzbek and Russian?

Yes. Specify the language in the context block. A separate adaptation for each language works best.

### Which AI should I use?

They work in most chat assistants. A comparison — [ChatGPT or Gemini](/en/blog/chatgpt-or-gemini-for-business/).

### How long should a prompt be?

The more context, the more precise the answer. But keep one task per prompt.

### Can I post AI copy straight away?

No. Always check the language, facts and brand voice.

## Conclusion

Fifty prompts are a starting point, not a finished result. Fill in the context block, edit every answer, check the facts and keep customer data out. Then AI will speed up your content and reply work several times over. The Level Marketing team uses prompts like these daily — but every text is checked by a specialist before it goes out.
