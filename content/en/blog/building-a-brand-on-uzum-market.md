When dozens of sellers offer the same product on a marketplace, competition comes down to price and profit disappears. The way out is building your own brand. Here is how to move from reselling to a brand on marketplaces like Uzum Market.

## Why a brand protects you from price wars

- **Your own listing.** Other sellers can't sell your branded product under the same name.
- **Reviews accumulate with you.** Every rating works for your product.
- **Repeat purchases.** Customers remember the brand and search for it again.
- **You set the price.** People pay more for a brand they know.

## Steps

1. **Your own product.** Your own production, or ordering from a manufacturer under your name (private label).
2. **Name and packaging.** A memorable name and recognisable packaging — rules in [how to name a business](/en/blog/how-to-name-a-business/).
3. **Register a trademark.** In Uzbekistan this goes through the Intellectual Property Agency — it protects your name from being copied.
4. **A strong listing.** A keyword-rich title, quality photos and infographics, video, a clear description and specs.
5. **Work with reviews.** Answer every question and review — more in our [online reputation](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/) article.
6. **External traffic.** Bring people straight to your listing from Instagram, Telegram and ads.
7. **Analytics.** Track views, add-to-carts, sales and returns.

## Listing checklist

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Main photo | White or clean background, product clearly visible |
| Extra photos | Size, material, use, packaging |
| Infographics | 3–5 key benefits |
| Video | The product in action |
| Title | Type + brand + key feature |
| Description | Who it's for, why, how to care for it |

## Why external traffic matters

Relying only on internal search is hard when competition inside the marketplace is fierce. Buyers coming from Instagram and Telegram often improve your sales history, which may also help the listing's visibility on the platform. For setting up Instagram ads, see our [separate guide](/en/blog/how-to-set-up-instagram-ads/).

## Common mistakes

- Using competitors' photos.
- Ignoring negative reviews.
- Not tracking stock — when a product runs out, the listing loses its position.
- Selling only through discounts.

## Conclusion

A brand on a marketplace is the way out of price wars and toward stable profit. Your own product, a registered name, a strong listing and external traffic are the four main steps. To find what sets you apart, read our [USP](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/) article.
