Furniture is an expensive, infrequent purchase. Customers compare for a long time, want to see and touch the product, and fear getting the size, quality or colour wrong. That's why marketing a furniture store has to be built on trust and on showing rather than telling. Here is how to use Instagram, maps and paid ads.

## What makes furniture sales different

- Decisions take weeks, often with the whole family involved.
- A high average order — instalments play a big role.
- The product is visual: good photos and video are half the sale.
- Delivery and assembly are part of the offer.

## Instagram profile and content

- Photos **in real interiors**, not just catalogue shots.
- Videos showing dimensions, materials and mechanisms.
- The production process — "how it's made".
- Furniture in customers' homes — their photos and videos ([UGC](/en/blog/ugc-content/)).
- Highlights: catalogue, prices and instalments, delivery, reviews, address. More in [an Instagram profile that sells](/en/blog/instagram-profile-that-sells/).

## Creatives that work

| Format | Why it works |
|---|---|
| Room walk-through | Customers picture the furniture in their own home |
| Materials close-up | Answers fears about quality |
| Before/after | Shows how the room changes |
| Customer testimonial | Builds trust |
| Offer: instalments, free delivery | Removes the price barrier |

For more on choosing a format, see [types of video ads](/en/blog/types-of-video-ads/).

## Instalments: a strong offer

A price of "from X som a month" is easier to accept for an expensive item. Our cases show how strong a financial offer is for big-ticket purchases: for the [Global Avenue](/en/blog/case-global-avenue/) residential complex, a 3-year interest-free instalment offer helped sell 100 apartments in 6 months, and for the [iComfort](/en/blog/case-icomfort/) store we rebuilt the Instagram page — expert videos, product reviews, clear content about prices and instalments — and monthly sales grew from 100 to 700+.

## Maps

Furniture is often bought in person, so your Google and Yandex Maps listing matters: photos, opening hours, phone number and reviews. See [how to add your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/).

## Paid ads

- Separate campaigns for the area around the showroom and for the whole city.
- Audiences interested in property, renovation and interior design.
- A [lookalike audience](/en/blog/lookalike-audiences/) built from your buyer list.
- [Retargeting](/en/blog/how-retargeting-works/) people who watched your catalogue videos.

## From lead to sale

1. A fast reply with photos, dimensions and price — [response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/) is decisive.
2. An invitation to the showroom or a video-call tour.
3. Measurements and instalment terms.
4. A follow-up a few days later — a [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/) helps.
5. After delivery, ask for a review with photos.

## Conclusion

The best advertising for a furniture store is a real interior, a clear price and easy payment. Build content on trust, targeting on the local audience and sales on fast replies. Level Marketing can help you prepare a strategy and ad campaign for your store.
