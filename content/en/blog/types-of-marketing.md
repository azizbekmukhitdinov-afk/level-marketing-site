"Performance marketing", "brand marketing", "content marketing", "digital" — there are so many marketing terms that owners lose track of which one they need. In reality, they don't contradict each other: they're parts of one system. Here are the main types of marketing in plain language, and where a small business should start.

## The main types of marketing

| Type | Key question | Result | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance marketing | How many leads and sales today? | Measurable leads | Fast |
| Brand marketing | Do people remember and trust us? | Awareness, trust, higher prices | Slow |
| Content marketing | What value do we give customers? | Trust, search traffic | Slow |
| SMM | How do we look on social media? | Trust and conversation | Medium |
| Local marketing | Do people nearby find us? | Visits, calls | Medium |
| Partnership and influencer marketing | Through whose audience? | A new audience | Medium |
| Referral marketing | Do customers bring others? | Cheap customers | Slow |
| CRM and retention marketing | Do customers come back? | Repeat sales | Medium |

## Performance marketing

Marketing where every soum is measured against results: targeted ads, search ads, leads, cost per sale. Its strength is speed and precision. Its weakness: stop the ads and the flow stops. The basics — [what is targeted advertising](/en/blog/what-is-targeted-advertising/) and [what is a lead](/en/blog/what-is-a-lead/).

## Brand marketing

Work that makes people remember you, trust you and tell you apart: name, visual style, tone of voice, values. Results are slow, but brand makes performance cheaper — more people respond to ads from a familiar brand. More in [what is a brand](/en/blog/what-is-a-brand/) and [what is a brand book](/en/blog/what-is-a-brand-book/).

## Content marketing and SMM

Trust through useful content: videos, articles, advice. SMM is the part of that content on social networks, plus conversations. More in [what is content marketing](/en/blog/what-is-content-marketing/) and [what is SMM](/en/blog/what-is-smm/).

## Local marketing

For stores, restaurants, clinics and salons: maps listings, reviews, signage, ads within a radius of the location. Often the fastest-acting type — [ranking higher on Yandex Maps](/en/blog/yandex-maps-promotion/).

## Partnership and influencer marketing

Reaching people through other businesses' or influencers' audiences: joint promotions, influencer ads, brand ambassadors. More in [influencer advertising](/en/blog/influencer-advertising-uzbekistan/).

## Referral and retention marketing

The cheapest customer is an existing one, plus the friend they bring. Reviews, a referral programme, attention to regulars — [types of customers](/en/blog/types-of-customers/) and [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

## B2B and B2C

This isn't a "type" of marketing but who you sell to: companies (B2B) or individuals (B2C). The approach differs a lot — [B2B vs B2C](/en/blog/b2b-vs-b2c/).

## Where a small business should start

You don't need to do everything at once. A logical order:

1. **The offer and brand basics**: for whom, what, what's different — [unique selling proposition](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/).
2. **Local marketing**: a maps listing and reviews — cheap and fast.
3. **Performance**: ads in one channel, judged by sales.
4. **Content and SMM**: for trust and better ad performance.
5. **Retention and referrals**: once a customer base builds up.

How it fits into a system — [marketing system for growth](/en/blog/marketing-system-for-growth/).

## Common mistakes

- Performance only — without brand and trust, leads keep getting pricier.
- Brand only — pretty, but unmeasured and not selling.
- Every type at once on a small budget.
- Forgetting existing customers.
- Deciding "we need digital" based on buzzwords rather than results.

## Questions and answers

### Performance or brand — which matters more?

Both. Performance delivers today's sales; brand delivers tomorrow's sales and cheaper advertising.

### What is digital marketing?

All marketing through online channels: ads, SMM, SEO, content, messengers.

### How many types does a small business need?

Two or three to start: local, one performance channel and basic content.

### Who manages the different types?

In a small business, the owner or a single marketer. As you grow, a team or an agency — [agency, freelancer or in-house](/en/blog/agency-freelancer-or-in-house/).

## Conclusion

The types of marketing are parts of one system: performance brings today's leads, brand and content build trust, local marketing brings nearby customers and retention marketing earns more from existing ones.
