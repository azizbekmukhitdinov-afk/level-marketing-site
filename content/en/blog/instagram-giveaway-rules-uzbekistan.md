A giveaway brings followers fast. But many businesses end up with the same picture: the prize is gone, and a month later so are the followers. This guide covers Instagram giveaway rules in Uzbekistan: what the law and the platform require, which mechanics sit closer to sales, how to pick a winner fairly, and where people usually go wrong.

![Instagram giveaway rules: the law and the platform](/img/blog/illus/instagram-giveaway-qoidalari-1.jpg 1200x675)

## Instagram giveaway rules: the law and the platform

The requirements come from two places. The first is Article 21 of the Law on Advertising, No. ZRU-776 ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/6052633)). In legal terms, a giveaway is an incentive promotion. The article covers promotions with valuable prizes and also promotions where a person has to do something to enter. Following, commenting and tagging a friend all count as doing something. So even with a small prize, an ordinary giveaway falls under this article.

The second is Instagram's promotion guidelines ([Instagram Help](https://www.facebook.com/help/instagram/179379842258600)). Instagram does not help run promotions and states that all responsibility lies with the organizer.

| Requirement | Source | What to do in practice |
|---|---|---|
| Terms are published openly before the promotion starts | Law, Art. 21 | Terms first, giveaway post second |
| Dates, prize name, quantity, place and procedure for collecting it are stated | Law, Art. 21 | All on one page |
| Name, address and contacts of the advertiser and organizer are stated | Law, Art. 21 | Company details, phone, email |
| Online terms stay open for at least 6 months after the end | Law, Art. 21 | Do not delete the page or post |
| The prize fund actually exists | Law, Art. 21 | Prize ready before launch |
| The chance of winning is not exaggerated | Law, Art. 21 | No "you're sure to win" |
| Each entrant releases Instagram from liability | Instagram | A separate clause in the terms |
| The promotion states it is not associated with Instagram | Instagram | One sentence |
| People are not tagged inaccurately | Instagram | Don't ask people to tag themselves in photos they are not in |

The same article settles the lottery question. A prize draw is not a lottery if the prize fund and costs do not come from direct cash contributions by participants or from selling tickets unrelated to the advertised product, and if participation is purely promotional. A "send 20,000 soums to enter" format crosses that line. For the other requirements and the fines, see [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## The terms: a ready template

The full terms do not need to fit in the post. The safest place is a separate page on your website or an open Telegram post: Instagram often requires an account to view, while a website is open to everyone. Keep the post short and send people to the full text through the link in your profile.

Here is a template for a furniture store (illustrative example):

- **Name and dates:** "A New Desk for the New Year", December 1–15, until 20:00.
- **Prize:** one writing desk, model name, price in soums; number of prizes: 1.
- **Who can enter:** people aged 18 or over living in Uzbekistan. Store employees cannot enter.
- **How to enter:** follow the page and answer in the comments: "Which room do you want to refresh?"
- **How the winner is chosen:** December 16, random selection, recorded on video.
- **How to collect the prize:** at the store's address, within 10 days, with an ID document.
- **If the winner doesn't respond:** a backup winner is chosen after 72 hours.
- **Organizer:** legal name, address, phone, email.
- **About Instagram:** this promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram; each entrant fully releases Instagram from liability.

State the prize price only in soums: the advertising law prohibits showing prices in foreign currency.

## Which mechanics sit closer to sales

The main question for a giveaway is who shows up. The simple "follow and comment" format brings everyone, including accounts that only hunt for prizes. The more closely the mechanic is tied to your product, the fewer entrants you get, but the better they are.

| Mechanic | What it gives you | Risk | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Follow + comment | Fast activity, wide reach | Prize hunters, unfollows afterwards | New accounts, introduction stage |
| Tag friends | New people see the post | Feels like spam, those tagged may object | Use with care |
| Answer a question | You learn what customers need | Answers take time to read | Furniture, renovation, education |
| Photo or video with the product | Ready-made content | Few entrants, consent needed | Clothing, cafes, cosmetics |
| Among buyers | Direct sales | Receipts and a list to keep | Stores, restaurants |
| Sign up through a bot | You keep the contact | Data consent is required | Services, training centers |

If you ask for photos with the product, get separate permission before using them in ads. Our article on [UGC content](/en/blog/ugc-content/) explains how.

Reach is another factor. Meta calls posts that explicitly ask for votes, shares, comments or tags engagement bait, and says the distribution of such content may be reduced ([Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/)). That page is mainly about Facebook, and we did not find a separate document like it for Instagram. The practical takeaway is the same: don't run giveaways every week, and don't let your page depend on them. For how reach is built, see [how the Instagram algorithm works](/en/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works/).

**Tie the prize to the product.** Everyone wants a phone, so it attracts people who will never be your customers. Let a furniture store give away an armchair, a training center a month of a course, a cafe dinner for two. Fewer people will care, but those who do are your audience.

## How to pick a winner fairly: 6 steps

1. **Collect the list right after the deadline.** Export comments by hand or through a service. Never give a service your account password.
2. **Remove those who didn't meet the terms.** Only by criteria stated in advance: duplicate comments, not following, store employees.
3. **Choose at random and record it.** A screen recording or a live stream: the list is visible and the moment of selection is not cut.
4. **Announce the result.** The winner's account name is enough. Don't publish a phone number or full name.
5. **Contact the winner.** Only from the official account. No response means the backup procedure from the terms applies.
6. **Keep the records.** The list, the video, a note that the prize was handed over. Advertising materials must be kept for three years from the last publication (Article 15 of the law).

Ask only for the data you need to hand over the prize. For how to handle a winner's data, see [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/).

## 7 common mistakes

1. **Terms change during the promotion.** Or get written after it starts. Publish them before launch and keep them unchanged until the end.
2. **The post is deleted after the giveaway.** Online terms must stay open for at least six months. If you want to tidy your feed, the terms page still stays.
3. **The prize is stated in dollars.** Not "a $100 gift" but a price in soums.
4. **The prize has nothing to do with the product.** More followers, the same sales.
5. **No one watches for fake accounts.** During a giveaway, scammers may set up a lookalike account and write "you won, send your card number." Say in the post up front: "We never ask for card details and only contact the winner from this account."
6. **It's unclear who the organizer is.** If several brands and a blogger run it together, the terms must state who the advertiser is, who the organizer is and who hands over the prize. The advertising must also be labeled.
7. **There is no plan for afterwards.** On the last day, new followers are seeing you for the first time. Prepare two weeks of content: the product, customer feedback, a useful post, a soft offer. Answer questions fast; this is where [handling comments and DMs](/en/blog/handling-comments-and-dms/) turns into sales.

## How to measure the result

In the end, count what remains after 30 days, not follower growth. Here is an illustrative example for a furniture store:

| Metric | Illustrative example |
|---|---|
| Prize value | 3,000,000 soums |
| New followers during the promotion | 1,200 |
| Remaining after 30 days | 780 |
| Product questions in DMs | 34 |
| Sales | 5 |
| Cost per retained follower | ≈ 3,850 soums |
| Cost per inquiry | ≈ 88,000 soums |
| Cost per sale | 600,000 soums |

Compare the last three rows with what paid ads achieve on the same budget. If the giveaway isn't cheaper, there is no reason to repeat it. To attribute a sale to the giveaway, ask customers how they heard about you.

## Questions and answers

**Do I have to publish terms if the prize is small?**
If entering requires a follow, comment or other action, yes. Article 21 covers not only valuable prizes but also promotions that require an action.

**Is a giveaway a lottery?**
No, if the prize fund does not come from participants' direct cash contributions and the promotion is purely for advertising. You still have to publish the terms in advance.

**Is the prize taxable?**
We did not find a clear answer in open sources on the tax on a prize or who pays it. For an expensive prize, talk to your accountant before launch and put the answer in the terms.

**Do the same rules apply on Telegram?**
Article 21 of the advertising law also covers online promotions, so the requirement for terms is the same on Telegram. The clauses about Instagram apply only to Instagram.

## Conclusion

A giveaway is not a strategy on its own; it is one way to introduce a new audience to your brand. Publish the terms in advance, keep them open for six months, tie the prize to the product and prepare content for the next two weeks. Level Marketing plans promotions like these together with the content plan and advertising, and measures results in inquiries and sales rather than followers.
