Gemini is Google's AI assistant. If your business runs on Gmail, Google Docs, Sheets or Drive, Gemini is especially convenient: it can help inside the tools you already use. In November 2025 it was announced that Gemini officially supports the Uzbek language ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/11/29/google-gemini-introduces-uzbek-language-support-on-its-ai-platform)). Here's how to use Gemini in a business, with 10 practical tasks.

## Getting started

1. Go to gemini.google.com or open the Gemini app with a Google account.
2. Start with the free version — it's enough for many tasks.
3. Use a separate Google account for work, so personal and business data don't mix.

Features and limits depend on the plan and change often — check Google's own pages for the latest details.

## Where Gemini is strong

- **Working with Google tools**: on Workspace plans, Gemini helps inside Gmail, Docs and Sheets — email drafts, document summaries, questions about a spreadsheet.
- **Files and images**: you can upload a PDF, image or screenshot and ask questions about it.
- **Long texts**: turning a contract, report or presentation into a short summary.
- **Multilingual work**: across Uzbek, Russian and English.

How it compares with ChatGPT — [ChatGPT or Gemini for business](/en/blog/chatgpt-or-gemini-for-business/).

## 10 business tasks

**1. Drafting posts and ad copy.** "Write an Instagram post for a furniture store in Tashkent: kitchens, ready in 14 days, free measurement. In English, 80 words, ending with a call to action" (an illustrative example).

**2. A content plan.** A month's list of topics — [AI content plan](/en/blog/ai-content-plan/).

**3. Replying to customer emails.** A draft in Gmail that you check and send.

**4. Summarising documents.** Upload a long contract or report: "List the key terms and risks in 5 points." For important documents, have a lawyer check the summary.

**5. Analysing reviews.** Paste in customer reviews: "The 5 most frequent complaints and 5 most frequent compliments" — [AI review analysis](/en/blog/ai-review-analysis/).

**6. Working with spreadsheets.** Ask about your sales sheet: "Which month had the highest average order value?" — then verify in the original sheet.

**7. Translation.** A product description from Uzbek to Russian — [AI translation for business](/en/blog/ai-translation-for-business/).

**8. Sales scripts and objections.** Five ways to answer "it's too expensive" — [AI sales scripts](/en/blog/ai-sales-script/).

**9. Researching competitors.** A list of questions and an analysis structure based on public information — [AI competitor analysis](/en/blog/ai-competitor-analysis/).

**10. Images and ideas.** A visual idea or image for a post — [AI images for business](/en/blog/ai-images-for-business/).

## A good prompt structure

Like ChatGPT, Gemini needs clear instructions:

- **Role**: "You are a marketer for small businesses in Uzbekistan."
- **Context**: the business, audience and offer.
- **Task**: exactly what you need.
- **Format**: length, language, a list or a table.
- **Constraints**: "Don't invent numbers", "Give prices in soum".

Ready-made prompts — [marketing prompts](/en/blog/marketing-prompts/).

## Safety and limitations

- **Confidential data**: don't enter customer lists, passwords or bank details. Fines apply for unlawfully processing personal data with AI — [AI law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).
- **Check the facts**: AI can produce convincing but wrong information — especially about laws, prices and statistics.
- **Brand voice**: AI text can sound generic, so edit it.
- **What not to hand over to AI** — [what not to delegate to AI](/en/blog/what-not-to-delegate-to-ai/).

## Questions and answers

### Is Gemini free?

There's a free version. Advanced features and Workspace integration come with paid plans, and the terms change from time to time.

### How good is it in Uzbek?

Official support exists, but quality depends on the task. Have someone fluent in Uzbek check important texts.

### Gemini or ChatGPT — which should I choose?

Try both for an hour on your own tasks — [ChatGPT or Gemini for business](/en/blog/chatgpt-or-gemini-for-business/).

## Conclusion

Gemini is a convenient AI assistant, especially for businesses that work in Google's tools: copy, document summaries, review analysis, translation and ideas.
