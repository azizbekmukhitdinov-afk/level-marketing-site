"How to make a website" is one of the most common searches among business owners. Today there are three routes: a site builder, AI tools or a developer. Each has a different cost, timeline and set of capabilities. The wrong choice means either wasted money or rebuilding the site later. Here's when each route fits and what a site needs to actually bring in leads.

## First question: why do you need a website?

Before building anything, define the goal:

- **Leads from ads** → a landing page — [a landing page that converts](/en/blog/landing-page-that-converts/);
- **Customers from Google** → several pages and a blog for SEO — [what is SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo/);
- **Selling online** → an online store;
- **Trust and introduction** → a company website (B2B, big-ticket purchases).

Do you need a website at all — [does a business need a website](/en/blog/does-a-business-need-a-website/).

## Three routes compared

| | Site builder | AI tools | Developer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timeline | A few days | Hours to days | Weeks to months |
| Cost | Low (subscription) | Low | High |
| Flexibility | Within the template | Limited, needs fixing | Full |
| Best for | Landing pages, small sites | Quick prototypes, simple pages | Complex systems, integrations |
| Risk | Platform lock-in | Quality and security control | Dependence on the developer |

### Site builders

Services like Tilda and Wix: ready-made blocks, drag and drop, hosting included. The fastest and cheapest route for a landing page or small site. The downsides: leaving the platform is hard, and you pay monthly.

### AI tools

Today, AI can build a page from a text prompt, write code and suggest designs. That's fast for a prototype or a simple page. But you have to check the result: copy errors, the mobile layout, whether the form works, security. AI is a fast assistant, but you remain responsible — [AI in marketing for small business](/en/blog/ai-in-marketing-for-small-business/).

### A developer or web studio

For complex systems: customer accounts, CRM, payments, 1C integration, a large catalogue. More expensive and slower, but fully flexible. Write into the contract that the rights to the code and the domain pass to you.

## Domain and hosting

- **The domain** is your site's address: .uz or .com. A .uz domain is registered in Uzbekistan. Register the domain **in your own name** — not the developer's or the agency's.
- **Hosting** is the server where your site's files live. Site builders include it.
- **SSL (https)** is a secure connection; without it, browsers show a warning.

## The structure of a site that brings leads

- A hero section: what, for whom, the next step.
- Services or products — a separate page for each (good for SEO too).
- A price or a "from" price.
- Trust: case studies, reviews, photos.
- Contacts: phone, Telegram, map, opening hours.
- A short form and a privacy policy — [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/).

## The technical minimum

- **Mobile version** — most visits come from phones.
- **Speed** — a slow site loses leads.
- **Analytics** — Google Analytics or Yandex Metrica.
- **An ad pixel** — [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/).
- **Google Search Console** — to see how Google sees your site.
- **Leads flowing into a CRM** — with no manual copying — [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/).

## After launch

A website isn't a one-off job. Check monthly: how many visits, how many leads, which pages work, where people leave — [what is conversion rate](/en/blog/what-is-conversion-rate/).

## Questions and answers

### I have Instagram — do I need a website?

Many B2C businesses don't need one at the start. But for search ads, SEO and B2B, you will.

### Can I build the site myself?

A landing page on a site builder — yes. The hardest part isn't design but a clear offer and copy.

### How soon will it appear on Google?

Indexing takes days to weeks, but high rankings take months of quality content.

## Conclusion

Before building a website, define its goal: a landing page, SEO, an online store or trust. A site builder is fast and cheap, AI is good for quick prototypes, a developer is for complex systems.
