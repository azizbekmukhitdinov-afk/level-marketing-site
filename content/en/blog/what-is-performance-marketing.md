**Performance marketing** is marketing that works towards a measurable result: a lead, a call, a purchase. The key question isn't "is the ad beautiful?" but "what did every soum bring in?". Because digital channels can track every click and lead, this approach has become standard for small and medium businesses too. Here's what performance marketing is and how it works.

## Brand marketing vs performance marketing

| | Brand marketing | Performance marketing |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Awareness, trust, emotion | Leads, sales, a specific action |
| Metrics | Reach, recall | CPL, CAC, ROAS, ROMI |
| Time to results | Long term | Fast — days and weeks |
| Example | A brand film, a billboard | Paid social with lead forms, search ads |

They complement each other: a strong brand makes performance advertising cheaper — [what is a brand](/en/blog/what-is-a-brand/).

## The key metrics

- **CPL** — cost per lead — [cost per lead](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/).
- **CAC** — customer acquisition cost — [CAC](/en/blog/customer-acquisition-cost-cac/).
- **ROAS** — revenue from ads — [what is ROAS](/en/blog/what-is-roas/).
- **ROMI** — profit from marketing — [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).
- Supporting metrics: CTR, CPM, CPC, conversion rate.

## Measurement is the foundation

Performance marketing doesn't work without measurement:

1. A pixel and Conversions API on your website — [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/).
2. UTM tags on every link — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).
3. Leads in a CRM with source and status — [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/).
4. Sales linked to leads.
5. Regular reports: spend → leads → sales → profit — [what an ad report should include](/en/blog/what-an-ad-report-should-include/).

## Who it suits

- Businesses with a clear offer and price.
- Businesses with a sales team that handles leads fast.
- Owners who want to see results in numbers.

## The risks

- **Chasing only cheap leads** — quality drops — [the cheap leads trap](/en/blog/cheap-leads-trap/).
- **Forgetting the brand** — over time, ads get more expensive.
- **Trusting ads-manager numbers blindly** — always compare with the CRM.

## Questions and answers

### Is performance marketing the same as paid social?

No. Paid social is one channel; performance marketing is the whole approach focused on measurable results.

### Does it suit small businesses?

Especially so: with a small budget, measuring every soum matters.

### When will I see results?

The first leads come fast, but a stable system takes 2–3 months to form — [what to expect from marketing in 90 days](/en/blog/what-to-expect-from-marketing-in-90-days/).
