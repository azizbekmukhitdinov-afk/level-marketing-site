**Client:** AutoRobot — premium car detailing with many clients and a strong reputation.

**Goal:** show the quality of the work.

**Partnership:** 8 months.

## What we did

- Built a **content matrix** strategy: a plan that regularly rotates process, results, expertise and client stories.
- Focused on before/after and behind-the-scenes videos.

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Followers | **11,000 from zero** |
| Paid ads | not used |

## Takeaway

When quality can be shown, systematic content becomes the advertising. A content matrix moves a page from random posts to steady growth.
