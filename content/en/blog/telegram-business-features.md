In Uzbekistan, many customers tap "Message us on Telegram", and the sale happens in a personal chat. But a personal account isn't built for business: opening hours aren't visible, a customer who writes at night gets no reply, and a salesperson types the same answer 50 times. **Telegram Business** is a set of features included in Telegram Premium that turns a regular account into a business account ([Telegram blog](https://telegram.org/blog/telegram-business)). Here's how to set it up for sales.

## Key features

| Feature | What it gives you |
|---|---|
| Opening hours | Your profile shows whether you're open or closed |
| Location | A map pin in your profile |
| Greeting message | An automatic first reply to new customers |
| Away message | An auto-reply outside working hours or on holiday |
| Quick replies | Ready templates via "/" |
| Chat links | A link to your chat with pre-filled text, for ads and your website |
| Chatbots for business | A bot replies in personal chats on your behalf |
| Start page | A short company intro in new chats |

Telegram Business requires a Telegram Premium subscription.

## Setting it up for sales in 6 steps

### 1. The profile

Opening hours, location and a start page: "Custom kitchens. For a price, send us your room dimensions" (an illustrative example).

### 2. The greeting message

A first reply reduces waiting and asks for what you need: "Hello! A salesperson will reply within 15 minutes. Meanwhile, tell us: which product and which district?"

### 3. The away message

Outside working hours: when you'll reply, plus a catalogue link. Customers who write at night aren't lost — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).

### 4. Quick replies

Price, delivery, payment, warranty, address — 10–15 templates. The salesperson types "/price" and the ready answer appears — [replying to "how much?"](/en/blog/replying-to-how-much/).

### 5. Chat links

Separate links for your Instagram bio, website and ads, with pre-filled text such as "Hi, I'm writing from Instagram". That tells you where each customer came from.

### 6. Connecting a bot

A business chatbot answers simple questions or logs enquiries in the CRM — [a Telegram AI assistant](/en/blog/telegram-ai-assistant/), [a Telegram bot for leads](/en/blog/telegram-bot-for-leads/).

## If you have several salespeople

Telegram Business works per account. With several salespeople:

- give each a separate work account and a clear district or product;
- or distribute enquiries via a bot plus CRM;
- keep all conversations on company accounts, so customers stay when a salesperson leaves.

Account security — [business account security](/en/blog/business-account-security/).

## What to measure

- Average time to first reply.
- The number of enquiries in the evenings and at weekends.
- Leads and sales from Telegram (by chat link).

## Questions and answers

### Does Premium pay for itself?

If even a few customers a month write at night or at weekends, it often does: one saved sale covers the subscription.

### What's the difference between a channel and a business account?

A channel is for content and audience; a business account is for one-to-one conversations and sales. They work together — [grow a Telegram channel](/en/blog/grow-a-telegram-channel/).

### Don't auto-replies feel robotic?

Not if they're short, warm and include a concrete next step.

Telegram Business turns a personal account into a sales channel: opening hours, greeting and away messages, quick replies, chat links and a bot.
