Most people don't buy on first contact: they visit your profile, check the price and leave "to think about it". **Retargeting** means showing ads again to exactly these people to bring them back to a decision. Here is who to retarget, which creatives work and how to keep it from becoming annoying.

![What retargeting is](/img/blog/illus/retargeting-qanday-ishlaydi-1.jpg 1200x675)

## What retargeting is

Retargeting (or remarketing) is advertising to people who have already interacted with your business. They know you, so these ads usually bring leads more cheaply than a cold audience. But the audience is small, so retargeting doesn't replace a flow of new customers — it complements it.

## Who to show ads to again

| Audience | Source | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Website visitors | Meta Pixel | 7–30 days |
| People who viewed a product page | Pixel: ViewContent | 7–14 days |
| People engaged with your Instagram profile | Instagram account | 30–90 days |
| People who watched 50–75% of a video | Video views | 30 days |
| People who messaged in Direct but didn't buy | Messages | 14–30 days |
| People who opened a lead form but didn't submit | Instant form | 7–30 days |

For website events, you need [Meta Pixel and the Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/) set up.

## Which creatives work

Your retargeting audience already knows you, so repeating the first ad is pointless. What works:

- **Reviews and results** — "others bought and are happy".
- **Answers to objections** — price, delivery, warranty, instalments.
- **A time-limited offer** — "free measurement until the end of the week".
- **A reminder** — showing the product or service they looked at.

## Keep frequency under control

Give a small audience a big budget and one person sees your ad several times a day and gets irritated. Watch frequency: if it climbs fast while CTR drops, cut the budget or refresh the creative. Retargeting usually needs only a small share of the total budget, for example 10–20%.

![Who to exclude](/img/blog/illus/retargeting-qanday-ishlaydi-2.jpg 1200x675)

## Who to exclude

- People who already bought (unless your goal is repeat sales).
- People who wrote about jobs or partnerships.
- Very old visitors — interest usually cools after 90 days.

## Setting up a retargeting campaign: 5 steps

1. **Check the Pixel and events** — make sure site visits and actions are recorded correctly.
2. **Create custom audiences** in Ads Manager: site visitors, video viewers, people who engaged with your profile.
3. **Exclude buyers** — they need a different message.
4. **Prepare 2–3 creatives per audience** — a testimonial, an answer to an objection and an offer.
5. **Check after a week:** frequency, CTR and cost per lead.

## A retargeting sequence (example)

| Days | What to show |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | A customer testimonial or result |
| 4–7 | An answer to a common objection: price, warranty, delivery |
| 8–14 | A time-limited offer |

A sequence like this lands more gently than the same ad over and over: each time the person learns something new.

![A budget for retargeting](/img/blog/illus/retargeting-qanday-ishlaydi-3.jpg 1200x675)

## A budget for retargeting

A retargeting audience is much smaller than a new one, so it usually needs a smaller share of the total budget. Manage the budget through frequency: if a person sees the ad too many times a week, cut the budget or broaden the audience. Track the results separately: retargeting leads are often cheaper, but only because both campaigns work together. And remember that retargeting doesn't replace advertising to new people. If no new visitors reach your site and profile, the retargeting audience slowly shrinks.

## Questions and answers

### How big should a retargeting audience be?

In a very small audience the ads wear out quickly, and the ads manager warns you about it. In that case, extend the window (say, 90 days instead of 30) or combine several sources.

### Does retargeting only work on Instagram?

No: Google Ads and Yandex Direct can also show ads again to your website visitors, as long as their tags are installed on the site — more in [Yandex Direct in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/yandex-direct-in-uzbekistan/).

### When should you stop retargeting?

If frequency rises and cost per lead keeps growing for several weeks, first refresh the creative or broaden the audience. If that doesn't help, move the budget to a new audience.

### What's the difference between retargeting and lookalike audiences?

Retargeting shows ads to people who already know you, while a lookalike audience finds new people similar to them. They complement each other: lookalikes bring new people in, and retargeting nudges them to leave a request. More in [Lookalike audiences](/en/blog/lookalike-audiences/).

## Conclusion

Retargeting brings back people who were interested but didn't decide. The right audience, creatives that answer objections and controlled frequency are the three conditions for success. To find new people, use [lookalike audiences](/en/blog/lookalike-audiences/).
