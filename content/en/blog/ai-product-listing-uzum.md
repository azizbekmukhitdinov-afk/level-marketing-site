For marketplace sellers, the most time-consuming job is product listings: title, description, specifications, photos and answers to questions. With 50 products, that's weeks of work. No wonder searches for "AI for product listings" are growing fast. AI speeds this up a lot — but used wrongly, it increases returns and negative reviews. Here's how to prepare listings for Uzum Market and other marketplaces with AI.

## What each part of a listing affects

| Part | What it affects | AI help |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Showing up in search, first impression | High — variations |
| Main photo | Clicks | Medium — background and clean-up |
| Extra photos | Trust, understanding | Medium — infographic copy |
| Description | Decision, fewer questions | High — drafts |
| Specifications | Filters, returns | Low — real data only |
| Q&A | Trust, conversion | High — reply templates |
| Review replies | Reputation | High — drafts |

## Title: variations with AI

Give AI the product data and ask for several titles:

> Product: [name], [material], [size], [colour], [for whom]. Write 10 title options for Uzum Market: product type first, then the key feature and purpose. No promotional words ("best", "super"), separately in Uzbek and Russian.

Then check how people actually search: the marketplace's search suggestions show the real words. On Latin, Cyrillic and Russian differences — [Uzbek search](/en/blog/uzbek-search-latin-cyrillic/).

## Description: answer questions in advance

A good description isn't pretty words — it answers buyers' questions. A prompt for AI:

> List 10 questions a buyer would ask about this product. Then, based on the answers, write a 600–900 character description: what it is, who it's for, how to use it, care, what's included. Use only the information I've given: [specifications].

That last sentence matters a lot: AI tends to "fill gaps" itself — it may invent a feature, a wrong size or a warranty you don't offer.

## Photos: where AI helps

AI is useful with photos for:

- **removing and cleaning backgrounds** — white or uniform;
- **showing products in a setting** — a lamp in a room, for example;
- **infographic copy** — short benefits, dimensions.

But **the product itself** — colour, shape, material, size — must be real. If AI changes the product (a darker colour, thicker fabric, different details), the buyer receives something else. Result: returns, negative reviews, a falling listing rating. More on product photos — [AI product photos](/en/blog/ai-product-photos/).

And remember the law: misleading advertising is prohibited ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6052631), Article 5). A marketplace listing is also a way of offering a product.

## Marketplace rules

Each marketplace has its own requirements for photos, titles and descriptions: dimensions, backgrounds, banned words, logo and text placement. They're updated from time to time. Before uploading AI-assisted content, check the current requirements in your seller account — a non-compliant listing may fail moderation.

## Q&A and review replies

This is where AI saves the most time:

- templates for frequent questions;
- a draft of a calm reply to a negative review — acknowledge, fix, contact;

## The workflow for 50 listings

1. **A table**: for every product — name, specs, size, material, colour, price (real data only).
2. **AI**: draft titles and descriptions from the table.
3. **Human**: check every listing against the table — do the specs match?
4. **Photos**: real photography; AI for backgrounds and infographics.
5. **Upload and monitor**: 2–4 weeks — views, clicks, add-to-carts, returns.
6. **Improve**: rework the weakest listings.

## Common mistakes

- AI-invented features left in the description.
- Photos where AI changed the product's colour or shape.
- Every listing in the same description style — nothing stands out.
- Russian and Uzbek descriptions contradicting each other.
- Not tracking reasons for returns.

## Questions and answers

### How much time does AI save on listings?

On the copy, a lot: a draft in minutes. But budget time for checking — it's mandatory.

### Are AI-generated photos allowed on the marketplace?

That depends on the marketplace's rules. The core principle: the product must look as it really is. Check the requirements in your seller account.

### Which language should the description be in?

Both Uzbek and Russian. Adapt each separately rather than translating word for word.

### What if the listing doesn't increase sales?

Check price, reviews and delivery terms too — the listing is only one part. Returns are often caused by a mismatch between the description, photos and the product.

## Conclusion

AI speeds up listings: title options, descriptions, Q&A and photo backgrounds. But product specs and appearance must be real — otherwise the time saved is lost to returns.
