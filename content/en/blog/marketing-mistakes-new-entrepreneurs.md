At the start of a business, marketing mistakes are especially costly: money is short and time is shorter. Here are the 7 most common mistakes new entrepreneurs make, and how to fix them.

## 1. A product "for everyone"

"Everyone needs our product" means your ads speak to no one in particular. Choose one core segment: who they are, where they are and what problem they have. See our guide to [target audiences](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/).

## 2. Ads without a strategy

Randomly boosting posts is the fastest way to waste a budget. Before launching, define the goal, the offer and the customer journey: ad → profile or page → lead → sale. More on the [sales funnel](/en/blog/what-is-a-sales-funnel/).

## 3. Decisions without numbers

If you don't know how many leads came in, how many became sales and what each customer cost, you can't tell which ads work. Start with a simple spreadsheet or a [CRM](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/), and mark sources with [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).

## 4. Blindly copying competitors

The same offer, the same photos, the same copy — price becomes the only difference, and a price war begins. Find what makes you different: your [unique selling proposition](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/).

## 5. Expecting instant results

Switching ads off after 2–3 days because "it doesn't work" is a common mistake. Every campaign needs a test period: try several creatives and audiences, then keep the best. Here's how: [A/B testing ad creatives](/en/blog/ab-testing-ad-creatives/).

## 6. Weak lead handling

The ad brought a lead, but the reply comes hours later or the manager doesn't know what to say. The money spent on ads then walks away with the lost customers. The fix: a [response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/) standard and a [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/).

## 7. No budget plan

The money runs out in a month — or a channel that works never gets scaled. Make a monthly plan: how much for tests, how much for proven channels, how much for content — see [planning a marketing budget](/en/blog/planning-a-marketing-budget/).

## Summary table

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Product for everyone | One core segment and a customer persona |
| Ads without strategy | Goal, offer and funnel |
| Decisions without numbers | Spreadsheet or CRM, UTM tags |
| Copying | Your own USP |
| Expecting instant results | A test period and A/B tests |
| Slow replies | A response standard and a script |
| Unplanned budget | A monthly budget table |
