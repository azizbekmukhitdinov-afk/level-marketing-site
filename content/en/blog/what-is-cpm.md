**CPM** (cost per mille) is the cost of 1,000 ad impressions. On Meta, TikTok and other platforms, ads are sold mainly through an impression auction, so CPM is the first metric for understanding whether advertising is "expensive" or "cheap". Here's what CPM is, what drives it and how to judge it.

## The formula

**CPM = spend ÷ impressions × 1,000**

An illustrative example: you spend $50 and the ad is shown 25,000 times. CPM = 50 ÷ 25,000 × 1,000 = **$2**.

## What affects CPM

- **Audience**: narrow, competitive audiences (such as business owners in Tashkent) cost more.
- **Season**: before Black Friday, in December and around holidays, competition rises and so does CPM — [December ad budget](/en/blog/december-ad-budget/).
- **Creative quality**: creatives people like get shown more readily and can cost less.
- **Placement**: Reels, feed and Stories are priced differently.
- **Objective**: "leads" or "sales" objectives can produce a higher CPM than "reach", because the system picks pricier but better-targeted people.

## CPM, CPC and CPL

| Metric | What you pay for | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | 1,000 impressions | The cost of reaching an audience |
| CPC | 1 click | The cost of interest — [what is CPC](/en/blog/what-is-cpc/) |
| CPL | 1 lead | The cost of a result — [cost per lead](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/) |

They're linked: **CPC = CPM ÷ (CTR × 10)**. So even if CPM stays the same, a higher CTR makes each click cheaper — [what is CTR](/en/blog/what-is-ctr/).

## When CPM matters

- In **awareness and reach** campaigns, it's the main metric.
- For **diagnosis**: if your cost per lead rises, is it because CPM went up (the market got pricier) or because CTR or conversion fell (a creative or landing page problem)?

## Common mistakes

- Chasing "cheap CPM" alone — cheap impressions often go to the wrong audience.
- Comparing CPM directly across seasons and industries.
- Blaming the creative for rising CPM — sometimes it's just the season.

## Questions and answers

### What's a normal CPM?

There's no universal figure — it depends on region, industry and season. Compare against your own history.

### How can I lower CPM?

Better creative, a broader audience and the right placements. But the real goal isn't CPM — it's cost per lead and per sale.

### Is there CPM in Telegram channels?

Yes — divide the post price by the number of views — [advertising in Telegram channels](/en/blog/buying-ads-in-telegram-channels/).
