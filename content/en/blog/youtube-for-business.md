YouTube isn't just an entertainment platform — it's a huge search engine where people look up "how to choose", "what's the difference" and "review". Here is which businesses YouTube suits, the roles of Shorts and long videos and how to start a channel.

## Who it suits best

- **Complex or expensive products:** real estate, cars, furniture, electronics — buyers research a lot.
- **Expert services:** lawyers, doctors, marketers, teachers.
- **Education:** courses, language centres, workshops.
- **B2B:** equipment, manufacturing, corporate services.

## Shorts vs long videos

| | Shorts | Long videos |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Reach and introductions | Trust and search traffic |
| Length | Up to 60 seconds | 5–20 minutes or more |
| Lifespan | Short | Can be watched for years |
| Content | A hook, one idea | A guide, review, comparison |

The best strategy uses both: Shorts bring new people, long videos turn them into customers. The same Reel can be posted as a Short.

## Topics that bring views from search

- "How to choose…", "… what's the difference", "… how much does it cost".
- Product reviews and comparisons.
- Answers to customers' frequent questions.
- Process: "how … is made".

## Title, thumbnail and description

- **Title** — with the words people search for: "How to choose a sofa: 7 tips".
- **Thumbnail** — a large face, 3–4 big words, contrasting colours.
- **Description** — a short summary in the first lines, a website or Telegram link, timestamps.

## A starting plan

1. Set up the channel: name, avatar, banner, links.
2. List 10 topics from customers' questions.
3. One long video every 2 weeks and 3 Shorts a week.
4. Film one [expert interview](/en/blog/expert-interview-videos/) and cut several Shorts from it.
5. Review the stats every month.

## What to measure

Not views, but: watch time, thumbnail click-through rate (CTR), subscribers, clicks on description links and leads from YouTube.

## Conclusion

YouTube is a slow but long-term channel: a video posted today can bring customers for years. Start with short videos, give detailed answers to customers' questions and measure results in leads. For editing tools, see our [video editing apps](/en/blog/video-editing-apps/) article.
