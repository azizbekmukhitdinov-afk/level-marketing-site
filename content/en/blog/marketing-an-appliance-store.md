Appliance shoppers compare more than anyone: the same model is sold in several shops, on marketplaces and in Telegram channels. They check prices in several places and pick the cheapest. If your marketing is all about "the lowest prices", your margin disappears. Here's how an appliance store can escape the price war.

## What shoppers compare

Price comes first, but it isn't the only thing:

- **warranty** — official or the shop's own, and for how long;
- **delivery and installation** — decisive for air conditioners and washing machines;
- **payment** — instalments and their terms;
- **service** — where to go if it breaks.

Get shoppers comparing the **full package**, not just the price.

## The "full package" offer

An illustrative example: "Air conditioner + installation + a year's free servicing + delivery" — one price. That's hard to compare with another shop's price tag. More in [the discount trap](/en/blog/discount-trap/).

## Instalments

Much appliance buying happens on instalments. How you show them in ads depends on the legal requirements: information on the mark-up and the final price — [installment advertising rules](/en/blog/installment-advertising-rules/), [credit ad warnings](/en/blog/loan-advertising-risk-warning-uzbekistan/).

## Content: video reviews work

- **A model review** in 60 seconds: main features, who it suits.
- **Comparisons**: "Which washing machine: 5 kg or 8 kg?" — help with choosing.
- **The installation process** — it shows your service quality.
- **Mistakes and tips**: "3 mistakes when choosing an air conditioner".

Product video — [product video mistakes](/en/blog/product-video-mistakes/).

## Ad channels

- **Search ads** on queries with model names and "price" — high intent — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).
- **Instagram/Facebook** — seasonal offers and video reviews, plus catalogue ads and retargeting — [how retargeting works](/en/blog/how-retargeting-works/).
- **Maps** — your shop address and "collect today".

## Seasons

- Late spring — air conditioners;
- autumn — heaters;
- Black Friday and New Year — appliances as gifts — [Black Friday for business](/en/blog/black-friday-for-business/);
- wedding season — a "new home bundle".

## After the sale

Appliances are long-term purchases, but customers buy other appliances and recommend you to friends. A follow-up call, service reminders and a referral bonus — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

## Questions and answers

### Should prices appear in ads?

For popular models, yes, in soum. Showing the package price is even better.

### How do we compete with marketplaces?

Fast delivery, installation, service and advice — things a marketplace can't provide.

### How should we present warranties?

Specifically: who provides it, for how long and where to go.
