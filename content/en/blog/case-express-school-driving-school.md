**Client:** Express School — driving school in Tashkent.

## Point A

- Monthly sales: **40**
- Marketing budget: $2,000

## What we did

- Reels with real students: graduates holding their new licences are the strongest proof.
- Videos answering the top questions: "how many days does it take to get a licence", "how much does a licence cost in Tashkent".
- Paid ads to put the Reels in front of the right audience.

## Point B

| Metric | Point A | Point B |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly sales | 40 | **300+** |
| Reach | — | **5 million+** |
| Business | — | new office in Nest One |

## How to apply this approach

1. Film short videos with graduates regularly — at the moment the result is in their hands.
2. List the questions customers ask most and make a separate video for each.
3. Use paid ads to take your best organic Reels to the right audience.
4. Reply to leads quickly and offer a clear next step.

## Who this suits

Education services: driving schools, language centres, courses — and any service with visible results. More in [Marketing an education centre](/en/blog/marketing-an-education-centre/) and [Types of video ads](/en/blog/types-of-video-ads/).

## Questions and answers

### How do you get graduates to appear on video?

Ask on the day they get their result, in that happy moment, and say the video will be short. Always get their consent — taking part should be voluntary.

## Takeaway

In education, the best ad is the graduate. A video showing a real result works better than any promise.
