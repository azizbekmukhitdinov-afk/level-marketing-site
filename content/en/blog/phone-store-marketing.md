A phone store sells the same models as dozens of other stores, often on the same street. The customer can compare prices in a minute, so the store that simply posts «iPhone in stock, best price» competes only on price and loses margin. The stores that grow win on trust, on timing around new models, on trade-in and installments, and on what they sell together with the phone. Here is how to build that system.

## Where the money really is

The phone itself is usually the lowest-margin item in the receipt. Profit sits around it. The table below is an illustrative example of how the structure often looks; check your own numbers from the till.

| Item | Role | Margin (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | Brings the customer, sets the check | Low |
| Case and protective glass | Bought almost always, if offered | High |
| Charger, cable, earbuds, power bank | Bought if explained | Medium to high |
| Setup, data transfer, glass fitting | Service the customer values | High |
| Trade-in phone resale | Second sale from one customer | Depends on grading |

The marketing conclusion: an ad brings a person for a phone, but the economics are decided at the counter. That is why the metrics below include accessories and trade-in, not only phone sales.

## Trust comes first: original, registered, guaranteed

The main fear of a phone buyer is a fake, a refurbished device sold as new, or a phone that stops working with a local SIM. Answer these fears in every channel.

- **IMEI registration.** In Uzbekistan mobile devices must have their IMEI registered in the UzIMEI system; an individual who does not register within 30 days after connecting a local SIM loses access to local operators' services ([UzIMEI](https://uzimei.uz/en/about)). According to [Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2021/01/04/cell-phone-salesmen-now-responsible-for-imei-code-registration), retail sellers are responsible for registering the devices they sell. Show this in content: «We register the IMEI at purchase, check it yourself by dialing *#06#».
- **Sealed box opened in front of the customer**, serial number checked together.
- **Warranty in writing:** who gives it, for how long, where repairs are done.
- **Prices in soums.** Uzbekistan's advertising law bans prices in foreign currency in advertising; see [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

One short video «How we check a phone before handing it over» often does more for sales than ten price posts.

## New model launches: plan by days

New flagship models create a wave of searches and questions. The store that prepares in advance collects demand before the phones arrive.

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Before the announcement | Post «What we know and what we don't», no rumours presented as facts |
| Announcement day | Short Reels with the key changes, a «notify me when in stock» lead form |
| Before arrival | Pre-order list in the CRM, a clear deposit and refund rule in writing |
| Arrival day | Unboxing, first impressions, calls to everyone on the list by order of sign-up |
| First 30 days | Comparisons with the previous model, trade-in offer for owners of older models |

The «notify me» form is the key asset. People on that list are the warmest audience you will ever have, and they should be called first, quickly. Speed matters here more than anywhere: see [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).

## Trade-in: a reason to upgrade now

Trade-in removes the main barrier for upgrading: «my old phone is still fine and I don't want to sell it on a marketplace». But it works only if the customer understands the price.

- **Online estimate form:** model, memory, battery health, condition, a photo. Answer with a price range within the hour.
- **Transparent grading:** what counts as excellent, good or fair condition, shown with photos.
- **Data wipe in front of the customer.** This removes a real fear.
- **One offer, one number:** «Your phone for X, the new one for Y, you pay the difference».

### Illustrative example: trade-in plus accessories

The numbers below are an illustrative example.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| New phone price | 14,000,000 soums |
| Trade-in value of the old phone | 5,000,000 soums |
| Customer pays | 9,000,000 soums |
| Case, glass, charger as a bundle | 600,000 soums |
| Margin on the bundle (illustrative 50%) | 300,000 soums |
| Margin on resale of the old phone after check (illustrative) | 700,000 soums |

One customer brings three sources of profit instead of one. If 100 trade-in deals happen in a month, the store earns 100,000,000 soums of extra margin in this example, without spending more on ads.

## Installments: honest numbers sell better

Installments are often the main argument for a phone purchase, and the rules have tightened. Presidential Resolution PQ-265 requires every advertisement of credit, installments and deferred-payment sales to individuals to state the overpayment above the current price and the total cost after all payments ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/8329406)). The details of the new requirements and a checklist are in [installment advertising](/en/blog/installment-advertising-rules/).

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| «Phone from 990,000 soums a month!» | «12,000,000 soums in cash or 12 months × 1,100,000 soums» |
| Overpayment not shown | «Overpayment 1,200,000 soums» |
| Total not shown | «Total in installments 13,200,000 soums» |

All numbers in this table are an illustrative example. If your installment has no markup, say so: «overpayment 0 soums» is a strong argument in itself.

## Accessories: the upsell that customers thank you for

Most customers want a case and glass but forget about them until the phone is in their hands. A good upsell is not pressure; it is a checklist.

- **Starter kit at a bundle price:** case, glass, fitting. Offer it every time.
- **Explain, don't push:** «This model comes without a charger in the box; here is the one that supports fast charging».
- **Show the result:** fitting the glass at the counter takes minutes and the customer leaves ready.

Measure the share of phone receipts that include at least one accessory and the number of items per receipt; see [units per transaction](/en/blog/units-per-transaction-upt/). More techniques in [upselling and cross-selling](/en/blog/upsell-and-cross-sell/).

## Reels: unboxing and honest comparisons

Short video is the natural format for gadgets. What works for a store:

- **Unboxing in 20 to 30 seconds:** box, design, first switch-on, price in soums at the end.
- **«Three differences»:** new model against the previous one, without technical jargon.
- **Camera test** in real Tashkent conditions: evening street, indoor light.
- **«Which phone for X soums»:** three options at one budget. This content gets saved and shared.
- **Trade-in story:** what the old phone was worth and what the customer paid in the end, with the customer's consent.

The first seconds decide whether people keep watching; see [10 Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/). End each video with one action: the lead form, the bot, or the store address.

## Ads and maps

- **Lead forms** for «notify me», trade-in estimates and installment applications.
- **Retargeting** of people who watched unboxing videos or opened the price list.
- **Maps profile** with photos of the shop, hours, phone number and a note about IMEI registration and warranty.
- **Separate campaigns** for new models, trade-in and accessories; do not mix them in one ad.

## Common mistakes

- Competing only on price with stores that have deeper pockets.
- Installment ads that show only the monthly payment.
- No record of who left a «notify me» request.
- Sellers who hand over the phone without offering the starter kit.
- Content that copies product photos from the brand's site instead of showing your store.

## Questions and answers

### Should a phone store show prices in ads?

Yes, in soums, and with installment terms stated fully. Hiding prices in a market where everyone compares only makes people go to the store that shows them.

### Is trade-in worth it for a small store?

If you can check and resell used phones honestly, yes. Start with a limited list of models you understand well and a clear grading table.

### How fast should we react to a new model launch?

Publish the first content on announcement day and open the «notify me» form at once. The list you collect before arrival matters more than being first to post an unboxing.

### Which metrics should a phone store watch?

Phone sales, share of receipts with accessories, trade-in deals, installment approvals and the cost per sale from ads. Revenue alone hides whether you are earning.

## Conclusion

A phone store grows when it sells trust and a complete purchase, not just a device: a registered phone, an honest installment, a fair trade-in price, a starter kit and video that shows the real product.
