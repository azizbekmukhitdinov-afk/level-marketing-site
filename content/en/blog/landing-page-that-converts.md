The ads work, people click — then land on the page and leave without submitting anything. Often the problem isn't the ad but the page: it's confusing, slow, awkward on a phone or the offer is unclear. A landing page is a page built for one goal: turning a visitor into a lead. Here's the structure of a landing page that converts, block by block.

## Landing page vs website

| | A regular website | A landing page |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Many: introduce, catalogue, contacts | One: a lead or a call |
| Menu | Many sections | Minimal or none |
| For whom | All visitors | People arriving from a specific ad or offer |
| Measurement | Complex | Simple: visit → lead |

Do you need a website at all — [does a business need a website](/en/blog/does-a-business-need-a-website/). Ways to build one — [how to build a business website](/en/blog/how-to-build-a-business-website/).

## The structure: 8 blocks

### 1. The hero section

In 3–5 seconds, visitors must understand three things: **what**, **for whom**, **the next step**. The headline states a result or offer, with one sentence below and a prominent button.

Bad: "Quality furniture is our goal." Good: "Custom kitchens in Tashkent — in 14 days, with a free measurement" (illustrative example).

### 2. Problem and solution

The customer's problem in their own words, and how you solve it.

### 3. What you get

Three to five concrete benefits, not vague words: "5-year warranty", "installation included".

### 4. Trust

Reviews, customer videos, photos of work, numbers (real ones only) — [social proof](/en/blog/social-proof-in-ads/).

### 5. Process

In 3–5 steps: request → call → measurement → contract → installation. It reduces fear of the unknown.

### 6. Price

An exact price, a "from" price or a range — [should you show the price in your ads](/en/blog/should-you-show-price-in-ads/).

### 7. Objections (FAQ)

The 5–7 most frequent questions with short answers — [content from customer objections](/en/blog/content-from-customer-objections/).

### 8. Form and contacts

A short form (name, phone, 1–2 questions), plus call and Telegram buttons. Form questions — [lead form questions](/en/blog/lead-form-questions/).

## Matching your ads

The most common mistake: the ad promises one thing and the page shows another. The rule: the ad's offer should appear in the hero section **in the same words**. Give each major offer its own landing page — e.g. separate pages for "kitchens" and "bedrooms".

## Mobile is the main version

Most visitors from ads arrive on a phone. Check:

- does the page load fast (on mobile internet);
- can text be read without zooming;
- is the button easy to tap with a finger;
- is the form easy to fill in with a phone keyboard?

## Tracking and measurement

- **Analytics**: Google Analytics or Yandex Metrica;
- **an ad pixel**: Meta Pixel, the Google tag — [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/);
- **UTM tags**: the source of each lead — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/);
- **a CRM**: leads arrive automatically.

The key metric is visit-to-lead conversion — [what is conversion rate](/en/blog/what-is-conversion-rate/).

## Law and privacy

If the form collects personal data, you need a privacy policy and consent — [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/). Prices in soum, no misleading claims — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## Common mistakes

- A logo and "welcome" in the hero — but no offer.
- A long form: 8 fields.
- Slow loading on phones.
- Menus and links that lead visitors away.
- The ad's offer doesn't match the page.
- No tracking — you can't tell which ad works.

## Questions and answers

### How long should a landing page be?

It depends on the offer: short for a cheap, simple product; longer, with more trust blocks, for an expensive service.

### Is an Instagram profile enough instead of a landing page?

In many B2C niches, yes. But for search ads and B2B, a landing page often performs better.

### How long does a landing page take?

A simple one on a site builder takes a few days. Most of the time goes on copy and photos.

### Should I test several versions?

Yes: an A/B test of the headline, hero section or form can change conversion noticeably.

## Conclusion

A landing page that converts has one goal, a clear hero section, trust, a process, a price, answers and a short form. It must load fast on phones, match the ad's offer and show the source of every lead.
