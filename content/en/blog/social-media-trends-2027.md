Social networks change every year, but the shifts of 2025–2026 led businesses to one big conclusion: follower count no longer guarantees reach. The recommendation feed judges each video on its own. That's good news for small businesses — and bad news for a "post and wait" strategy. Here's what to focus on in social media in 2027.

## 1. Recommendations, not followers

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube increasingly show content to people who don't follow you. So every Reel is a first introduction to someone new. The hook, a clear idea and a reason to visit your profile all matter — [how the Instagram algorithm works](/en/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works/).

## 2. Testing is a normal part of SMM

Instagram's trial Reels feature lets you show a video to non-followers first. That makes social media management test-driven rather than gut-driven: three hooks — which one works? — [Instagram Trial Reels](/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels/).

## 3. Series and micro-dramas

Instead of one-off posts, recurring segments and short series: "part 1, part 2". People wait for the next episode and come back — [micro-drama Reels](/en/blog/micro-drama-reels/).

## 4. People and expertise — or faceless content

Two opposite paths both work: a founder or expert on camera, or faceless content (hands, process, screen, voice-over). What matters is a consistent format — [faceless content](/en/blog/faceless-content/), [Reels for experts](/en/blog/reels-for-experts/).

## 5. Longer short videos

Reels and Shorts can now run up to 3 minutes. That doesn't mean you need long videos — for most, short is still better. But explanations, case studies and before/after stories now have room for a longer format — [video ad length](/en/blog/video-ad-length/).

## 6. Mobile editing apps

Instagram's Edits app and other tools have made professional editing easy on a phone. A social media manager — or even the founder — can now produce content quickly — [Instagram Edits app](/en/blog/instagram-edits-app/).

## 7. New platforms: Threads

Meta's Threads is growing and also serves as an ad placement. It's not for every business, but experts and B2B companies can test its text-first format — [Threads for business](/en/blog/threads-for-business/).

## 8. Live streams and selling

A live stream lets you show the product, answer questions and take orders on the spot. It suits clothing, cosmetics and homeware especially well — [live selling](/en/blog/live-selling/).

## 9. DMs and Telegram: where sales happen

Content sparks interest, but the sale happens in conversation. Response speed and automation are half of social media results — [handling comments and DMs](/en/blog/handling-comments-and-dms/), [Telegram Business](/en/blog/telegram-business-features/).

## 10. Security

Business account hacks have become more common. Two-step verification and access rights are a mandatory part of SMM — [business account security](/en/blog/business-account-security/).

## How to measure social media

In 2027, not likes and followers, but:

- profile visits and link clicks;
- DM and Telegram enquiries;
- shares and saves;
- sales from social media (with the source recorded in the CRM).

## Questions and answers

### Do followers no longer matter at all?

They matter, but for a different reason: they're a source of repeat purchases and referrals. For reach, each piece of content has to work on its own.

### How many posts a week do I need?

There's no universal number. Consistency and quality matter more than volume: three good Reels beat seven average ones.

### Can I run social media myself?

To start, yes, with mobile tools. To grow, you'll need strategy, video and analysis — [SMM service pricing](/en/blog/smm-service-pricing/).

## Conclusion

Social media in 2027 means content for recommendations, a testing culture, series, people or a consistent faceless format, live streams, fast replies and security — measured through to sales.
