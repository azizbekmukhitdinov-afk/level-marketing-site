Every year-end brings lists of "next year's trends", and most are written for foreign markets. For a business in Uzbekistan the question is different: which changes will affect *our* customers and *our* channels? Here are 10 directions for 2027 — with a practical step for each.

## 1. Search is moving into AI

AI answers (AI Overviews and AI Mode) take up more and more space in Google Search, and many people ask ChatGPT or Gemini directly. That means your website and content need to make sense not only to Google but to AI answers too.

**Step**: useful Q&A-style content, precise facts and a complete maps profile — [GEO: visibility in AI search](/en/blog/geo-ai-search-visibility/) and [Google AI Mode](/en/blog/google-ai-mode-for-business/).

## 2. Ads inside AI chats

Ads arrived in ChatGPT in 2026, and in October they expanded to 89 countries ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html)). It's a new ad channel: being visible at the moment someone asks a question. **Step**: read how it works in [ChatGPT ads](/en/blog/chatgpt-ads/) and consider a small test.

## 3. Creative, not targeting

Meta's ad system increasingly finds audiences on its own; the main lever is creative diversity. You don't need 10 near-identical versions of one ad — you need creatives built on different ideas. **Step**: [Meta Andromeda](/en/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-diversity/) and [Advantage+ audience](/en/blog/advantage-plus-audience/).

## 4. Short video is the main language

Reels, Shorts and TikTok remain the main format for attention. What's new: platforms keep adding tools — trial Reels, mobile editing apps. **Step**: [Instagram Trial Reels](/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels/), [YouTube Shorts for business](/en/blog/youtube-shorts-for-business/).

## 5. Telegram is a sales channel

In Uzbekistan, Telegram isn't just channels; it's the hub for CRM, bots and customer conversations. **Step**: [Telegram Business features](/en/blog/telegram-business-features/) and leads via bots.

## 6. Your own data is your most valuable asset

Platforms change and ads get more expensive, but your customer base stays yours. A CRM, contacts collected with consent and purchase history are the foundation of repeat sales. **Step**: [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/) and [loyalty programmes](/en/blog/loyalty-program/).

## 7. Founders and staff are the face of the brand

People trust people more than logos. A founder on camera, staff stories and expert content build trust quickly. **Step**: [founder on camera](/en/blog/founder-on-camera/), [video podcasts](/en/blog/video-podcast-for-business/).

## 8. AI is the team's assistant

AI makes copy, images, translation, call analysis and simple agents cheaper. The advantage isn't the tool but the process: who hands which task to AI, with what checks. **Step**: [AI trends 2027](/en/blog/ai-trends-2027-business/), [build an AI agent](/en/blog/build-an-ai-agent/).

## 9. Results are measured through to sales

Reports on "reach" and "likes" are giving way to "lead → sale → profit". Ads manager and CRM numbers need to match. **Step**: [ad leads vs CRM mismatch](/en/blog/ad-leads-vs-crm-mismatch/), [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).

## 10. Content in Uzbek

There's still little high-quality content in Uzbek — and that's an opportunity. In search and in AI answers alike, precise answers in Uzbek give you an edge. **Step**: [ad language: Uzbek or Russian](/en/blog/ad-language-uzbek-or-russian/).

## What not to treat as a trend

- Jumping onto every new app — first check your audience is there.
- "AI will do everything" — unchecked AI content damages a brand.
- Trends for their own sake — if they don't lead to sales, they're just costs.

## Questions and answers

### Should a small business follow every trend?

No. Pick one or two — the closest to your customers and the fastest to affect sales.

### Which trend pays off fastest?

Often creative diversity and fast lead response. Neither requires a new channel.
