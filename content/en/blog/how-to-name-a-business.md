A name is the first word customers hear about you. A good name is memorable, easy to find and easy to recommend; a bad one needs explaining in every ad. Here are 8 rules for naming a business and the process to follow.

## 8 rules

1. **Short and easy to say.** Uzbek, Russian and English speakers should all pronounce it the same way.
2. **A positive meaning in all three languages.** Check it doesn't sound funny or negative in another language.
3. **Unique.** Not similar to competitors' names — otherwise customers get confused.
4. **Not too narrow.** "Chilanzar Sofas" gets in the way if you expand.
5. **The domain and social handles are free.** Check the .uz domain, Instagram and Telegram.
6. **A trademark check.** Search the Intellectual Property Agency's register for similar marks and register your own.
7. **Easy to spell.** Avoid Latin/Cyrillic confusion: o‘, g‘, sh and ch may be typed differently in searches.
8. **Test it on customers.** Say it to 10 target customers and ask what they imagine.

## Types of names

| Type | Example | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | "Tashkent Furniture" | Instantly clear | Doesn't stand out, hard to register |
| Invented | "Zentro" | Unique | Meaning needs explaining |
| Founder's name | "Karimov Clinic" | Trust, personality | Harder to sell or expand |
| Metaphor | "Level" | Memorable, meaningful | Hard to come up with |

## The process

1. 50 options — a free brainstorm.
2. Narrow to 5 using the rules.
3. Check the domain, social handles and trademark.
4. Test with customers.
5. Choose and register it straight away.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing a name just because you like it.
- Making it similar to a competitor's name.
- Not registering a trademark — and risking losing the name.
- Foreign words that are hard to pronounce.

## Conclusion

A good name is simple, unique, clear in three languages and legally protected. Check first, fall in love second. A name is only one part of a brand — for the rest, see [what a brand is](/en/blog/what-is-a-brand/) and [visual identity](/en/blog/visual-identity-for-small-business/).
