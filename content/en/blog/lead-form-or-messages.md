Instagram advertising has two main ways to collect leads: an **instant lead form** and **messages** in Direct. Both bring leads, but they suit different businesses. Here is how they differ, when each works better and how to improve lead quality.

## The two approaches in brief

**Instant form** — the user leaves a name and phone number without leaving Instagram. Your manager then calls them.

**Messages** — the ad button opens a chat in Direct. The customer asks a question and your manager answers in the chat and leads them to a sale.

| Criterion | Instant form | Messages (Direct) |
|---|---|---|
| Convenience for the customer | Very easy — two taps | They have to type |
| Cost per lead | Often cheaper | Usually higher |
| Lead quality | Variable | Often higher |
| Manager's job | Call back | Sell in the chat |
| Best for | Services sold by phone | Products sold through questions |

## When the instant form wins

- The service is sold by phone: clinics, education centres, real estate, renovation.
- You have someone who calls leads back quickly.
- You need volume and have a process for qualifying leads.

## When messages win

- Customers ask first: price, size, colour, delivery.
- Visual products: clothing, furniture, cosmetics.
- A small team is used to selling in chat.

## How to improve form lead quality

1. Try the **"higher intent"** form type instead of "more volume" — it adds a step where people confirm their details.
2. Add one qualifying question: "Which district are you in?" or "When do you plan to start?".
3. State the offer and the next step at the top of the form: "A manager will call you within 15 minutes".
4. Call back as fast as possible — leads cool off quickly.

## How to get more sales from messages

- Set up an automatic greeting and buttons for frequent questions.
- Response standard: a few minutes during working hours.
- Prepare a chat script — greeting, discovery, offer, close.

## How to choose

The most reliable way is to test both in parallel and compare **cost per sale, not cost per lead**. If cheap form leads don't convert, pricier message leads may turn out more profitable. ## Conclusion

Forms bring fast, cheap leads; messages bring warmer conversations. The right choice depends on your sales process. At Level Marketing we test both in every project and decide by sales figures.
