Millions of people now ask ChatGPT, not Google, about products and services: "Which course should I choose in Tashkent?", "Which hob is best for my kitchen?". In 2026, OpenAI launched ads in ChatGPT — first as a test in the US, then in Europe, Asia and other regions. On 21 October, ads expanded to 26 more countries, reaching 89 markets in total ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/openai-chatgpt-ads-26-countries.html)). Here's how this new channel works and whether your business needs it.

## Key facts

- **Who sees them**: only users on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro and Enterprise have no ads.
- **Where**: separate from the answer, in a block labelled as an ad, matched to the topic of the conversation.
- **Effect on answers**: OpenAI says ads don't influence ChatGPT's answers.
- **Privacy**: OpenAI says it keeps conversations private from advertisers.
- **Limits**: under OpenAI's policy, ads aren't shown to minors or around sensitive topics such as health, mental health and politics.

## How to buy

- **Self-serve through Ads Manager**, or via technology partners.
- **Pricing model**: cost per thousand impressions (CPM) at launch, with cost-per-click (CPC) bidding added later ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/advertisers-in-26-more-countries-gain-chatgpt-ads-bringing-the-total-to-89/)).
- **Targeting**: not keyword lists but "context hints" about the conversation topic.

Check in ChatGPT Ads Manager whether your country is supported and what the minimum budget is — the list and terms are changing fast.

## How these ads differ

| | Google Search | Meta (Instagram) | ChatGPT |
|---|---|---|---|
| What the person is doing | Searching | Scrolling a feed | Asking for advice |
| Intent | High | Low to medium | High, but still choosing |
| Format | Text ad | Video, image | A block next to the answer |
| Targeting | Keywords | Audience and creative | Conversation context |

**The insight:** in ChatGPT, people are usually at the "how do I choose?" stage. What works there isn't hard-sell advertising but a trustworthy offer with a clear benefit.

## Who it suits

- Services involving a complex choice: education, healthcare (within the restrictions), real estate, B2B services.
- Online stores, for product-choice questions.
- Businesses that want to show up early, before competitors arrive.

Who doesn't need it yet: small local businesses whose customers come from maps and Instagram.

## What a first test should look like

1. Check availability and the minimum budget in Ads Manager.
2. Use one clear offer and a landing page — [a landing page that converts](/en/blog/landing-page-that-converts/).
3. Tag every lead — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).
4. Run it for 2–4 weeks with a budget set in advance.
5. Compare cost per lead and per sale with Google and Meta.

## Questions and answers

### Do ChatGPT ads "buy" the answer?

According to OpenAI, no: the ad sits in a separate block and the answer is independent.

### Should a small business start now?

Your core channels — Google, Meta and maps — should be working first. ChatGPT is an additional channel to experiment with.

### Can an agency run this channel?

Yes, if you give it access to the ads account — just as with Google and Meta.

## Conclusion

ChatGPT ads are a new way to be visible to people at the choosing stage: a separate block, context-based targeting, CPM and CPC models.
