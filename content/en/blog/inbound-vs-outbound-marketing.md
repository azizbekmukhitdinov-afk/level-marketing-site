There are two ways to find customers: either they find you (you create useful content and visibility), or you find them (you reach out by phone, message or advertising). The first is **inbound** marketing, the second **outbound**. Knowing the difference helps you allocate budget and people properly.

## The difference

| | Inbound | Outbound |
|---|---|---|
| Who starts | The customer | The business |
| Channels | SEO, blog, useful content, maps, search ads | Cold calls, messages, outdoor ads, trade shows |
| Speed of results | Slower, but compounds | Faster, but stops when you stop |
| Customer readiness | High — they searched | Lower — they may not yet see the need |
| Costs | Time and content | People and every contact |

## Inbound channels

- **SEO and blogging** — people search for answers to their questions — [what is SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo/).
- **Content marketing** — Reels, Telegram, useful posts — [what is content marketing](/en/blog/what-is-content-marketing/).
- **Maps and reviews** — [Google Business Profile](/en/blog/google-business-profile-guide/).
- **Search ads** — paid, but the person is searching — [what is search advertising](/en/blog/what-is-search-advertising/).
- **Lead magnets** — [lead magnet](/en/blog/lead-magnet/).

## Outbound channels

- **Cold calls** (B2B) — [cold calling](/en/blog/cold-calling/).
- **Direct messages** on LinkedIn and Telegram — personal, not spam.
- **Trade shows** — [exhibiting at a trade show](/en/blog/exhibiting-at-a-trade-show/).
- **Outdoor and broad-reach advertising**.

Paid social sits in between: you reach out, but based on interests.

## Which suits whom

- **B2C, small orders**: mainly inbound plus paid social — people search and scroll.
- **B2B, large deals**: outbound (calls, meetings) plus inbound (expert content, case studies) together — [B2B marketing](/en/blog/b2b-marketing-uzbekistan/).
- **A new category**: outbound and paid social — people aren't searching yet.

## Combining both

The strongest results come from combining them: after an outbound call, the prospect googles you and finds useful articles, reviews and case studies. Inbound builds trust; outbound brings speed.

## The law

Be careful with outbound: advertising calls and SMS are subject to time limits and consent requirements — [SMS advertising rules](/en/blog/sms-advertising-rules-uzbekistan/).

## Questions and answers

### Is inbound free?

It costs less money but plenty of time and expertise. Results build up over months.

### Is outbound outdated?

No, especially in B2B. It just needs to be personal and well prepared.

### Where should I start?

If you need leads fast, start with outbound and paid social, and build inbound foundations in parallel.

## Conclusion

With inbound, customers find you; with outbound, you find them. For most businesses the best answer is a balance: outbound and ads for speed, inbound for long-term trust. In a free audit, Level Marketing will show you which channels suit you.
