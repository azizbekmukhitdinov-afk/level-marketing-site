A business whose customer is "everyone" usually can't speak clearly to anyone. **Segmentation** means dividing the market into groups with similar needs, behaviour or characteristics. Choosing a message, offer and channel for each group makes advertising cheaper and selling easier. Here's how to segment. Building a single customer persona — [target audience and customer persona](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/).

## The 4 main criteria

| Criterion | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Geographic** | City, district, Tashkent or the regions |
| **Demographic** | Age, gender, family status, income, occupation |
| **Psychographic** | Values, lifestyle, interests |
| **Behavioural** | Purchase occasion, frequency, loyalty, price sensitivity |

For B2B: industry, company size, decision-maker, purchase volume — [B2B marketing](/en/blog/b2b-marketing-uzbekistan/).

## Example: an education centre (illustrative)

| Segment | Need | Message | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| School pupils and parents | University entry | "Results and discipline" | Telegram, Instagram |
| University students | Studying abroad | "IELTS 7+ fast" | Instagram, TikTok |
| Working adults | Career | "Evening groups that fit around work" | Search ads, Telegram |

One product, three different ads. That often brings cheaper leads than one general ad.

## How to choose a segment

A good segment is:

- **big enough** to be profitable;
- **measurable**, so you can find it in advertising;
- **distinct** in its needs from other segments;
- **reachable**, so you can serve it.

Small businesses should start with one or two core segments.

## Putting segmentation into practice

1. **Data**: customers in your CRM — who, where from, what they bought — [marketing research](/en/blog/marketing-research/).
2. **Grouping**: 3–5 segments.
3. **For each**: need, message, offer, channel.
4. **Advertising**: separate creatives or ad sets.
5. **Measurement**: which segment brings cheaper leads and higher order values.

## Common mistakes

- Only age and gender, ignoring needs.
- Too many tiny segments, spreading the budget thin.
- The same message for every segment.
- Segmenting on guesswork rather than data.

## Questions and answers

### How does segmentation differ from a target audience?

Segmentation divides the market into groups; the target audience is the group (or groups) you choose.

### Is segmentation still needed now that Meta ads favour broad audiences?

Yes — segmentation now shows up in the creative and message, with a creative for each segment — [Meta Andromeda](/en/blog/meta-andromeda-creative-diversity/).

### When should I revisit segments?

Once a year, or when your product or market changes.
