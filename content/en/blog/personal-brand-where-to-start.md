People trust a person faster than a company. That's why, for a doctor, lawyer, teacher, marketer or business owner, a personal brand is one of the cheapest ways to attract clients. Here is where to start, how to choose your positioning and how to plan the first 90 days.

![What a personal brand gives you](/img/blog/illus/shaxsiy-brend-qayerdan-boshlash-1.jpg 1200x675)

## What a personal brand gives you

- **Trust.** Clients already "know" you from your videos.
- **Cheaper leads.** Referrals and organic reach cut ad spend.
- **Pricing power.** People pay more for a well-known expert.
- **Opportunities.** Partnerships, speaking, interviews.

## Positioning in one sentence

Formula: "I help [who] achieve [result] through [method]."

- "I help Tashkent entrepreneurs get a steady flow of leads through paid ads."
- "I help parents get their children's teeth treated without pain."

The more specific, the more memorable. "Everything for everyone" isn't positioning.

## Choosing a platform

| Platform | For whom |
|---|---|
| Instagram | The main platform for almost every expert |
| Telegram channel | Deeper content and a loyal audience |
| YouTube | Complex topics and detailed answers |
| LinkedIn | B2B and corporate clients |

One main and one secondary platform are enough to start.

## Content pillars

1. **Expertise** — tips, mistakes, how-tos.
2. **Cases** — results and client stories.
3. **Personal** — values, your journey, moments from your workday.
4. **Opinion** — your stance on industry issues.
5. **Offer** — a consultation, service or product.

![A plan for the first 90 days](/img/blog/illus/shaxsiy-brend-qayerdan-boshlash-2.jpg 1200x675)

## A plan for the first 90 days

| Period | What to do |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Positioning, profile, bio, pinned posts |
| Weeks 3–8 | 3–4 posts or Reels a week, Stories every day |
| Weeks 9–12 | A first offer: a consultation or service; collaborations, interviews |

For setting up your profile, see [an Instagram profile that sells](/en/blog/instagram-profile-that-sells/); for the video format, [expert interview videos](/en/blog/expert-interview-videos/).

## Common mistakes

- Copying others — you'll be compared with the original.
- Only selling — people leave a page that gives nothing.
- Inconsistency — a month of activity, then silence.
- Hiding your face — a personal brand needs a person.

## Where to find content ideas

The best ideas come from the work itself:

- **Customer questions** — write down every question for a week; each one becomes a separate post or video.
- **Objections** — "too expensive", "later", "I'll think about it" — an open answer to each.
- **Customer mistakes** — the ones you often see in your work.
- **Cases** — the task, the process and the result (with the client's permission).
- **Industry news** — not just the news, but your opinion on it.
- **Moments from your working day** — the process, the team, the preparation.

## Finding the time: a weekly rhythm

You don't need to spend hours a day on a personal brand. A workable rhythm:

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Once a week, 2 hours | Shoot 3–4 videos or posts |
| Every day, 20–30 minutes | Stories, replies to comments and messages |
| Once a month | Review the results and plan next month's topics |

Editing and design can go to an assistant or an SMM manager, but the ideas and the voice must stay yours.

![What to check after 90 days](/img/blog/illus/shaxsiy-brend-qayerdan-boshlash-3.jpg 1200x675)

## What to check after 90 days

Follower count isn't the main metric. More important: how many enquiries came through your content, whether clients arrive already knowing you from your videos, and whether interview and partnership invitations have started. Ask in every enquiry, "Where did you hear about me?" — it's the simplest measure.

## Questions and answers

### What if you're afraid of talking to the camera?

Start with Stories, text posts and voice messages. Write a short script and record several takes — the first ten videos are the hardest. For shooting tips, see [Shooting Reels on a phone](/en/blog/shooting-reels-on-a-phone/).

### How much of your private life should you show?

Set your boundaries in advance. Values, your working day and interests are usually enough; talking about family and health is entirely your choice. Share what supports your positioning.

### Do you need a personal brand if the company has an account?

Yes, they complement each other: the company page shows the product, while the personal page builds trust. Link from your personal profile to the company page and back.

### Which language should your personal brand use?

The one your clients read and search in. If your audience is bilingual, pick one main language and support key posts with subtitles or a short note in the second language. Only run both languages equally if you have the resources — otherwise consistency suffers.

## Conclusion

A personal brand is one-sentence positioning, consistent useful content and honesty. Start small, stay consistent for 90 days and turn results into cases. The personal brands we've worked with are on our [personal brand cases](/en/blog/case-personal-brand/) page.
