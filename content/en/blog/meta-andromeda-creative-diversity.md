Since 2025, one of the most discussed words among media buyers has been **Andromeda**. It's the retrieval stage of Meta's ad system: out of millions of ads, it builds a short list of those that could be shown to a particular person ([Jon Loomer](https://www.jonloomer.com/meta-andromeda/)). In connection with this, Meta stresses creative diversification as the main lever for finding audiences. Here's what that means for your business's ads.

## What Andromeda does, in plain words

Meta's ad delivery works in stages:

1. **Retrieval** — Andromeda: from all ads, it picks a few thousand that might suit a specific person.
2. **Ranking** — other models decide which of those are best.
3. **Auction** — determines which ad is shown.

The key change: the system "understands" the ad itself more deeply — image, video, text, theme — and finds matching people on its own. In other words, the creative now does part of the targeting.

## What changes

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Choosing audiences by narrow interests | A broad audience; the creative finds its own people |
| 10 near-identical versions of one ad | Creatives built on different ideas |
| Many small ad sets | Fewer, richer campaigns |
| A/B tests of colours or buttons | Tests of ideas, formats and messages |

## What a "diverse" creative is

The system may treat near-identical ads as one. Real diversity means:

- **Different motivations**: price, quality, speed, status, safety.
- **Different customer personas**: a young family, an entrepreneur, a retiree — [target audience and customer persona](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/).
- **Different formats**: the founder on camera, a customer review, a product close-up, a static image, a carousel.
- **Different hooks**: a question, a result, a mistake, a story — [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).

An illustrative example: 6 creatives for a furniture store — (1) the founder on the guarantee, (2) a customer's home before/after, (3) "3 mistakes when choosing a kitchen", (4) price and instalments, (5) the production process, (6) a promotion as a static image.

## Campaign structure: practical tips

- **Less fragmentation**: instead of many small ad sets with the same goal, fewer sets with enough budget each — [Meta "learning limited"](/en/blog/meta-learning-limited/).
- **Broad audiences**: broad or Advantage+ audiences instead of narrow interests — [Advantage+ audience](/en/blog/advantage-plus-audience/).
- **Several different creatives in one ad set**: give the system a choice.
- **Regular refreshes**: watch for creative fatigue — [creative fatigue](/en/blog/creative-fatigue/).

These are general practitioner recommendations; test the results in every account.

## The takeaway for small businesses

You don't need a big budget, but you do need a **creative production system**: several new ideas every month. Getting 10 creatives in different formats out of one shoot day is the most cost-effective route — [one shoot, 10 ad creatives](/en/blog/one-shoot-10-ad-creatives/).

## Common mistakes

- Treating one video with 10 different captions as "diversity".
- A separate ad set and a tiny budget for every creative.
- Only one format (for example, only static images).
- Judging results after 1–2 days.

## Questions and answers

### Do I no longer need a media buyer?

You do, but the job has changed: less "audience setup", more creative strategy, testing and analysis — [how to choose a paid ads specialist](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-paid-ads-specialist/).

### How many creatives do I need?

There's no universal number. On a small budget, 3–6 genuinely different creatives are a good start.

### Does Andromeda affect ads in Uzbekistan?

Yes. It's Meta's global ad system and doesn't depend on the country.

## Conclusion

Meta Andromeda has shifted advertising from an "audience setup" game to a "creative ideas" game. Creatives with different motivations, formats and hooks, broad audiences and less fragmented campaigns are the foundation of Meta advertising in 2027.
