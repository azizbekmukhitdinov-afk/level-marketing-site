During Ramadan, people's daily routines, shopping habits and social media activity all change. For restaurants, groceries, clothing, gifts, delivery and charity-related businesses, it's one of the most important seasons of the year. In 2027, Ramadan is expected to begin around **8 February** ([timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/uzbekistan/ramadan-begins)) — the official date will be announced closer to the time by the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan. Eid al-Fitr (Ramazon hayiti) will fall around 9–10 March. Preparation should start in January.

## What changes during Ramadan

- **Routine**: suhoor early in the morning, iftar in the evening. Activity peaks shift to the hours after iftar and late evening.
- **Shopping**: groceries, the iftar table, hosting guests, gifts, clothing for Eid.
- **Content**: family, spiritual and charity themes draw more interest.
- **Restaurants**: iftar bookings and corporate iftars.

## A preparation plan (illustrative example)

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| Mid-January | The offer: iftar menu, bundles, Eid collection |
| End of January | Content plan, shoots, creatives |
| A week before Ramadan | Take corporate iftar and large orders |
| During Ramadan | Align ad timing with active hours |
| 10 days before Eid | Eid gifts and clothing campaign |
| After Eid | Thank customers, review results |

## Advertising: timing and audience

- **Scheduling**: in the ads manager you can schedule delivery for active hours — pay attention to the post-iftar hours and the evening. Check against your own statistics from last year.
- **Delivery**: "delivered before iftar" must be a specific promise you can keep.
- **Corporate iftars**: a B2B offer to companies from early February — [B2B marketing](/en/blog/b2b-marketing-uzbekistan/).
- **Retargeting** people who viewed your menu or catalogue — [how retargeting works](/en/blog/how-retargeting-works/).

## Content ideas

1. Recipes or bundles for the iftar table.
2. How your team breaks the fast together — a sincere video — [team content](/en/blog/team-content-employees/).
3. A charity project: part of every order goes to charity — only if it really happens, with a report.
4. Suhoor and iftar times — useful, but name your source.
5. Eid gift ideas.

## Etiquette rules

Ramadan is a spiritual month, and audiences are sensitive. The rules:

- Don't turn religion into a sales slogan: a "Ramadan offer" is different from "the cheapest prices in the holy month".
- Don't set those who fast against those who don't.
- Keep charity promises and show it openly.
- Too many ads in the evening are irritating. Watch your frequency.
- General advertising rules — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## The sales team

Working hours may change during Ramadan. Announce your hours to customers in advance and keep someone on duty to answer enquiries after iftar too — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).

## Questions and answers

### Should I pause advertising during Ramadan?

For many businesses, no — it's peak season. But adapt your message, timing and tone.

### Which businesses see a low season in Ramadan?

Daytime eateries and some entertainment services may see lower daytime demand. They shift to iftar and evening formats — [advertising in low season](/en/blog/advertising-in-low-season/).

### Where can I find the official date?

From announcements by the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan and official media.
