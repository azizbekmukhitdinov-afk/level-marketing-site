Cakes and sweets are visual products: people "eat with their eyes" in photos first, then order. So for a cake shop or bakery, Instagram is the shop window and Telegram is the order desk. Yet many bakers lose sales despite beautiful photos because they tire of answering "how much?". Here's how to market systematically.

## Two businesses, two kinds of marketing

| | Custom cakes | Daily sales (bread, pastries) |
|---|---|---|
| Customer | Celebrations, weddings, birthdays | Lives nearby |
| Decision | A few days ahead | Right here, right now |
| Main channel | Instagram, Telegram | Maps, shop window, signage |
| What matters | Design, taste, deadline | Freshness, price, convenience |

## Instagram is your shop window

- **A catalogue in highlights**: cake types, price ranges, how to order — [an Instagram profile that sells](/en/blog/instagram-profile-that-sells/).
- **Process videos**: piping cream, decorating, cutting — the most-watched format — [faceless content](/en/blog/faceless-content/).
- **Customer reactions**: the smile when they collect the cake (with consent).
- **Honest photos**: the finished cake must look like the photo — otherwise expect a bad review.

## The "how much?" problem

Every cake is unique, so bakers avoid naming prices — and customers go elsewhere. The fix:

- **a pricing formula**: "from X soum per kg, depending on decoration" (prices in soum);
- **ready-made options**: "Kids' cake, 2 kg, in 3 designs";
- **an order form** or Telegram bot: weight, date, design, address — [a Telegram bot for leads](/en/blog/telegram-bot-for-leads/).

Reply templates — [replying to "how much?"](/en/blog/replying-to-how-much/).

## Seasons

New Year, 8 March, Navruz, Ramadan, graduations and wedding season all send orders soaring. In advance:

- a seasonal collection and pre-orders;
- a production capacity plan — overbooking also damages your reputation;
- corporate orders — [corporate gifts](/en/blog/corporate-gifts-sales/).

The annual plan — [marketing calendar 2027](/en/blog/marketing-calendar-2027-uzbekistan/).

## For daily sales

- A complete maps profile with precise hours, such as "open from 7 am".
- The window display and the smell — offline marketing.
- "−20% after 8 pm" to clear leftovers (with the margin worked out).

## Delivery and safety

Cakes are fragile. Make delivery terms, boxes and times clear. Follow sanitary and labelling requirements.

## Regular customers

Birthdays come round every year. With the customer's consent, save the date and send a reminder a week before — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

## Questions and answers

### Can a home baker advertise?

Yes, but register the business properly and follow sanitary requirements.

### Which ads work?

Instagram ads targeted by city or district, using process videos. The goal: a Telegram message or a form.

### How far in advance should orders be taken?

For complex cakes, 2–3 days. Say so in your profile and auto-reply.
