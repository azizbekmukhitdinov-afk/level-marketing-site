**Client:** TEH — power tools store.

**Partnership:** over 1 year.

## What we did

- Rebuilt the Instagram page in one consistent brand style: red-and-black design and product spec cards.
- Presented each tool's technical specs in a clear visual format.
- Ran paid campaigns optimised for leads and messages.

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Ad spend | $7,000 |
| Online sales | **over $40,000** |

## Takeaway

With technical products, buyers compare specs. Content that shows them clearly and attractively turns online into a reliable sales channel.
