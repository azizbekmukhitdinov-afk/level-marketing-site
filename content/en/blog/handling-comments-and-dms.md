Ads and content bring people into your comments and DMs — that's where the sale starts. An unanswered question or a curt "price in DM" loses a customer who was ready to buy. Here are standards for handling comments and messages, ready templates and how to respond to criticism.

## Why it matters

- **Everyone sees comments.** One reply informs dozens of readers.
- **Speed decides the sale.** Customers message several pages at once. More in our article on lead response time.
- **Engagement affects reach.** A post with a lively discussion gets shown to more people.

## Response standards

| Situation | Standard |
|---|---|
| Message during working hours | Within a few minutes |
| Outside working hours | Auto-reply + first thing in the morning |
| Question in the comments | A public answer + an invitation to DM if needed |
| Complaint | Calm, with a concrete fix |

## Ready templates

**Price:** "Hello! It starts from 1,200,000 som; the exact amount depends on the size. Send me the size and I'll calculate it now."

**Availability:** "Yes, this model is in stock. Order today and we'll deliver tomorrow. Which colour would you like?"

**Delivery:** "Delivery within Tashkent is free; regions take 2–3 days. Which city are you in?"

A template is only a starting point: always adapt it to the customer's name and question. If every reply ends with a question, the conversation keeps going.

## Negative comments

1. Reply quickly and calmly — don't delete (except spam and abuse).
2. Acknowledge the problem and offer a solution.
3. Sort out the details in DM or by phone.
4. Once resolved, post a short update in the comments.

A well-handled complaint often impresses new customers more than praise. For working with reviews, see our [online reputation](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/) article.

## Spam and automation

- Turn on the hidden words filter in Instagram settings.
- On a professional account, set up saved replies and FAQ buttons.
- With high message volume, a chatbot can answer first questions and hand over to a manager.

## What to measure

Weekly: number of messages, leads from them, sales from leads and average response time. For turning a chat into a sale, see our sales script article.

## Conclusion

Comments and DMs are your page's checkout. Fast, specific and friendly replies, ready templates and calm handling of criticism bring more sales from the same ad budget.
