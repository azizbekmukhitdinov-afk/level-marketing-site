Before buying, people read reviews: ratings on Google and Yandex Maps, comments on Instagram, scores on Uzum Market. A handful of good reviews can work harder than advertising, while one unanswered complaint can scare customers away. Here is how to collect reviews and how to respond to negative ones.

## Where reviews live

| Platform | Who reads them |
|---|---|
| Google and Yandex Maps | People looking for a service nearby |
| Uzum Market and other marketplaces | Product buyers |
| Instagram comments and DMs | Followers and people coming from ads |
| Telegram chats | People asking for recommendations |

## How to collect reviews

1. **Ask at the right moment** — when the customer is happy with the result: after delivery, at the end of treatment, when a course finishes.
2. **Make it easy** — give a direct link to the review page or a QR code.
3. **Remind them** — once, politely.
4. **Reply to every review** — positive ones too.

Never buy reviews or write fake ones: it breaks platform rules, and when it's discovered you lose your rating and reputation.

## Replying to a negative review: 5 steps

1. **Thank them** — "Thank you for your feedback".
2. **Acknowledge the problem** — don't argue.
3. **Explain or offer a solution** — specific and brief.
4. **Move to a private channel** — phone or DM.
5. **Post the outcome** — once resolved, add a short update to your reply.

> "Sardor, thank you for your feedback. We apologise for the late delivery — there was an error at our warehouse. Our manager will contact you today to offer compensation."

A well-handled complaint often impresses new customers more than praise does.

## Unfair or fake reviews

If a review breaks the rules (abuse, advertising, unrelated to you), report it using the platform's tools. Still reply openly and calmly — other readers see your answer too.

## Using reviews in content

- Put your best reviews (with permission) in an Instagram "Reviews" highlight.
- Use video reviews in ads — more in our [UGC content](/en/blog/ugc-content/) article.
- Words that recur in reviews are ready-made ideas for ad copy.

## Conclusion

Online reputation is capital you build every day. Ask happy customers for reviews regularly, reply to every one and handle criticism calmly with a solution.
