Before buying, people read reviews: ratings on Google and Yandex Maps, comments on Instagram, scores on Uzum Market. A handful of good reviews can work harder than advertising, while one unanswered complaint can scare customers away. Here is how to collect reviews and how to respond to negative ones.

![Where reviews live](/img/blog/illus/onlayn-obro-sharhlar-1.jpg 1200x675)

## Where reviews live

| Platform | Who reads them |
|---|---|
| Google and Yandex Maps | People looking for a service nearby |
| Uzum Market and other marketplaces | Product buyers |
| Instagram comments and DMs | Followers and people coming from ads |
| Telegram chats | People asking for recommendations |

## How to collect reviews

1. **Ask at the right moment** — when the customer is happy with the result: after delivery, at the end of treatment, when a course finishes.
2. **Make it easy** — give a direct link to the review page or a QR code.
3. **Remind them** — once, politely.
4. **Reply to every review** — positive ones too.

Never buy reviews or write fake ones: it breaks platform rules, and when it's discovered you lose your rating and reputation.

## Replying to a negative review: 5 steps

1. **Thank them** — "Thank you for your feedback".
2. **Acknowledge the problem** — don't argue.
3. **Explain or offer a solution** — specific and brief.
4. **Move to a private channel** — phone or DM.
5. **Post the outcome** — once resolved, add a short update to your reply.

> "Sardor, thank you for your feedback. We apologise for the late delivery — there was an error at our warehouse. Our manager will contact you today to offer compensation."

A well-handled complaint often impresses new customers more than praise does.

## Unfair or fake reviews

If a review breaks the rules (abuse, advertising, unrelated to you), report it using the platform's tools. Still reply openly and calmly — other readers see your answer too.

![Using reviews in content](/img/blog/illus/onlayn-obro-sharhlar-2.jpg 1200x675)

## Using reviews in content

- Put your best reviews (with permission) in an Instagram "Reviews" highlight.
- Use video reviews in ads — more in our [UGC content](/en/blog/ugc-content/) article.
- Words that recur in reviews are ready-made ideas for ad copy.

## How to reply to a positive review

Don't stop at "Thank you!" — a reply is content too, and other customers read it:

> "Nodira, thank you for the kind words! We'll be sure to pass them on to our craftsman Jasur. May the sofa serve you for many years — if you have any questions about caring for it, just message us."

A good reply includes the customer's name, a specific detail from the review and a next step: a return visit, a care tip or a reminder about a new service.

## Monitoring your reputation: a weekly routine

| Where | What to check |
|---|---|
| Google and Yandex Maps | New reviews, questions, rating |
| Marketplaces | Ratings and product questions |
| Instagram | Comments, tags and DMs |
| Telegram | A search for your brand name: mentions in chats and channels |

Assign a responsible person and ask for a short weekly report: how many new reviews, the average rating and which complaints keep coming up.

## Common mistakes

- The same copy-pasted reply to every review.
- Arguing with the customer in public or blaming them.
- Revealing the customer's personal details in a reply — especially in clinics.
- Answering only new reviews and ignoring older ones.

![When many negative reviews arrive at once](/img/blog/illus/onlayn-obro-sharhlar-3.jpg 1200x675)

## When many negative reviews arrive at once

For example, a delivery failure produces dozens of complaints in a single day. In that situation:

1. **Don't try to delete the reviews**, and don't rush out the same reply to each one.
2. **Find the common cause** — what happened and how many customers were affected.
3. **Make an open statement** on Instagram or Telegram: what happened, what's being done and when it will be resolved.
4. **Reply to each review individually** and offer compensation or a solution.
5. **Fix the cause** and report back on the result in a week or two.

A mistake admitted openly and fixed quickly usually does your reputation less harm than silence.

## Questions and answers

### How quickly should you reply to reviews?

Within one or two working days, and to negative ones the same day if possible. A quick reply shows the customer they've been heard and stops the problem from growing.

### Can employees write reviews?

No. Reviews from employees, their relatives or on behalf of the company are a conflict of interest and break the platforms' rules. Such reviews may be removed, and your listing can suffer.

### Can you ask a customer to update a review once the problem is solved?

Yes — politely, without pressure and without any reward: "If you'd like, you can update your review." The decision stays with the customer.

### Do you need a perfect 5.0 rating?

Not necessarily. A few critical reviews with good replies often look more convincing than a perfect rating. How to collect customer opinions systematically is covered in [Collecting customer feedback](/en/blog/collecting-customer-feedback/).

## Conclusion

Online reputation is capital you build every day. Ask happy customers for reviews regularly, reply to every one and handle criticism calmly with a solution.
