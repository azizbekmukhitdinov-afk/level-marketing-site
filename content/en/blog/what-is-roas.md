**ROAS** (return on ad spend) shows how much revenue each soum spent on advertising brought in. For online stores and e-commerce it's the headline metric: "ROAS of 4" means every soum of ad spend generated 4 soum of revenue. But revenue isn't profit. Here's what ROAS is, how to calculate it and how it differs from ROMI.

## The formula

**ROAS = revenue from ads ÷ ad spend**

An illustrative example: you spend 10 million soum on ads, and sales from those ads bring in 40 million. ROAS = 40 ÷ 10 = **4** (or 400%).

## ROAS vs ROMI

| | ROAS | ROMI |
|---|---|---|
| What it calculates | Revenue ÷ ad spend | (Profit − marketing costs) ÷ marketing costs |
| Accounts for cost of goods | No | Yes |
| Used for | Comparing campaigns quickly | Knowing whether marketing truly makes a profit |

More on ROMI — [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).

## Break-even ROAS

Every business should know its minimum ROAS. The formula: **break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ margin share**.

An illustrative example: a 25% margin (0.25) gives a break-even ROAS of 1 ÷ 0.25 = **4**. So with a ROAS below 4, the ads lose money — even though "four times the revenue" sounds impressive. Calculating margin — [unit economics](/en/blog/unit-economics-for-small-business/).

## Measuring ROAS properly

- **Link sales to ads**: for websites, use the Pixel and Conversions API — [Meta Pixel and Conversions API](/en/blog/meta-pixel-and-conversions-api/); for Telegram and phone sales, record the source in the CRM.
- **Attribution window**: know the period over which the platform credits a sale to an ad — [Meta attribution window](/en/blog/meta-attribution-window/).
- **Compare the ads account with the CRM**: platform ROAS is often optimistic — [ad leads vs CRM mismatch](/en/blog/ad-leads-vs-crm-mismatch/).

## The high-ROAS trap

Advertising only to retargeting audiences and existing customers produces a high ROAS — those people were close to buying anyway. But the flow of new customers dries up. The balance: new audiences and retargeting together — [how retargeting works](/en/blog/how-retargeting-works/).

## For service businesses

If the sale is long and happens in an office or by phone, calculate ROAS monthly: the month's ad spend against sales from leads those ads generated (via the CRM).

## Questions and answers

### What's a good ROAS?

It depends on your margin: anything above your break-even ROAS is profit. There's no universal figure.

### How do ROAS and LTV relate?

If customers buy again, even a low ROAS on the first purchase can be profitable — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

### Can I trust the ROAS in the ads account?

For direction, yes; for final decisions, check it against CRM and accounting figures.
