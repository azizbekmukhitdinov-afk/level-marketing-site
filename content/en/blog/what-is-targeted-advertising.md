"We need to run targeted ads", "we found a media buyer", "the targeting didn't work" — in Uzbekistan these are some of the most common phrases among business owners (locally, targeted social ads are simply called "target"). But not everyone is clear on what targeted advertising really is, how it differs from ordinary advertising and when it works. Here's a plain-language explanation.

## A simple definition

Targeted advertising means showing ads **not to everyone, but to specifically chosen people**. Social networks hold data about users: age, city, interests, behaviour. The advertiser chooses who should see the ad, and the platform delivers it to those people.

Compare it with a billboard: everyone who passes sees a billboard — students and pensioners alike. A targeted ad, by contrast, is seen by, say, people in Tashkent who recently bought a flat or are interested in renovation.

## How it works: 4 parts

| Part | What it is | Example (illustrative: furniture store) |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | What you want from the ad | A lead, a DM, a website visit |
| Audience | Who sees it | Tashkent, 25–45, a broad audience |
| Creative | What they see | A Reel showing a kitchen being assembled |
| Budget | How much you spend | A set daily amount |

Platform algorithms are powerful today: rather than narrowing the audience too much, it often works better to provide a **strong creative** — the algorithm finds the right people itself. On producing creatives — [how ad creatives are produced](/en/blog/how-ad-creatives-are-produced/).

## Targeted vs search advertising

| | Targeted | Search (contextual) |
|---|---|---|
| When it appears | While someone scrolls a feed | When someone types a search query |
| Demand | May not exist yet | Already there |
| Best for | Visual products, impulse buys, new offers | Urgent needs: a repair, a service, delivery |

For many businesses, both together work best. A comparison — [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

## What drives the cost

Prices in targeted advertising are usually set by an auction: you compete with other advertisers for the same audience. Cost depends on:

- competition in your niche (real estate and education usually cost more);
- the season (ads get pricier before holidays);
- creative quality (good creatives deliver results more cheaply);
- your process from lead to sale.

More in [paid ads cost in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/paid-ads-cost-in-uzbekistan/) and [cost per lead](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/).

## Who is a media buyer?

A media buyer (locally, a "targetologist") is the specialist who sets up, tests and optimises targeted ads: choosing the audience and objective, testing creatives, managing the budget and measuring results. A good one doesn't stop at cost per lead — they track results through to sales. How to choose one — [how to choose a paid ads specialist](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-paid-ads-specialist/).

## When targeted ads don't work

- the offer is unclear or uninteresting;
- the profile or website doesn't inspire trust;
- leads get slow replies;
- the creative is weak and doesn't hold attention in the first seconds;
- results are judged by cost per lead, not by sales.

More in [why ads don't bring sales](/en/blog/why-ads-dont-bring-sales/).

## When to start

Before launching, have the minimum in place:

1. A clear offer and a price guide.
2. A trustworthy profile: highlights, reviews, real photos.
3. Someone who replies to leads quickly.
4. Two or three creatives (video is better).
5. A test budget for at least a month.

## Questions and answers

### Are targeted ads and SMM the same?

No. SMM is running the page and its content; targeted ads are paid advertising. They work together. More in [why social media doesn't sell](/en/blog/why-social-media-doesnt-sell/).

### Can I set up targeted ads myself?

A simple campaign, yes. But testing, optimisation and analysis take experience.

### When will I see results?

First leads usually come within days; stable results and an optimal cost take several weeks of testing. More in [what to expect from marketing in 90 days](/en/blog/what-to-expect-from-marketing-in-90-days/).

### How much money do targeted ads need?

It depends on the niche and goal. You can start small, but the budget must be enough for a test.

## Conclusion

Targeted advertising means showing ads to specifically chosen people: objective, audience, creative and budget. It works with a strong creative, a clear offer, a trustworthy profile and fast replies — and it's measured by sales.
