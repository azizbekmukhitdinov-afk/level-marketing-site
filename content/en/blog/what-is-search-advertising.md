**Contextual advertising** is advertising shown to match a person's search query or the content (context) of the page they're reading. The most familiar example: you type "custom kitchen" into Google and see listings marked "Ad" above the results. These ads appear at the moment of need — which is why they often bring the warmest leads. Here's what it is and how it works. A practical guide for Tashkent — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).

## Two types

| | Search ads | Display ads |
|---|---|---|
| Where | Google and Yandex search results | Websites, apps, YouTube and more |
| When shown | When someone types a query | When someone views a related page, or based on interests |
| Intent | High — actively searching | Low to medium |
| Format | Text ads | Banners, images, video |

For most businesses, the starting point is **search ads**.

## How it works: the auction

Every search triggers an auction for the ad slots. Who appears higher depends not only on the bid but on **ad quality**: relevance to the query, expected click-through rate and landing page quality. So a good ad can rank higher even with a lower bid.

## What you pay for

With search ads you usually pay **per click** — if the ad is shown but not clicked, you aren't charged. Cost per click — [what is CPC](/en/blog/what-is-cpc/).

## Who it suits

- Services and products people actively search for: renovation, tradespeople, clinics, legal services, education, auto parts.
- Urgent needs: "call a plumber", "tow truck".
- B2B: "wholesale", "supplier".

Less suitable: new products people don't know about yet — paid social works better for those — [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

## Pros and cons

**Pros**: high intent, precise measurement, a fast start and a small minimum budget.

**Cons**: demand is limited by search volume; clicks on competitive keywords are expensive; you need a website or landing page — [a landing page that converts](/en/blog/landing-page-that-converts/).

## Questions and answers

### How does search advertising differ from SEO?

Search ads are paid and fast; SEO earns free rankings but takes long-term work — [what is SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo/).

### Are there searches in Uzbek?

Yes, and they're growing; account for Latin and Cyrillic spelling — [Uzbek search: Latin and Cyrillic](/en/blog/uzbek-search-latin-cyrillic/).

### Does it work without a website?

Partly, with call ads. But a website or landing page makes it far more effective.

Contextual advertising reaches people at the moment of need: search and display formats, an auction of quality and bids, and payment per click.
