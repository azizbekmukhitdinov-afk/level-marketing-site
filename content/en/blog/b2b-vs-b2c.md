"B2B" and "B2C" show up constantly in business plans, job ads and marketing articles. They're more than jargon: who you sell to completely changes your marketing, your sales process and even your ad creative. Many businesses lose money by applying B2C methods to B2B — and vice versa. Here's the difference in plain language, with examples.

## A simple definition

- **B2C (business to consumer)** — selling to ordinary people: a clothing store, a restaurant, a salon, an online store.
- **B2B (business to business)** — selling to other businesses: wholesale, manufacturing, logistics, corporate services, software.

Some businesses do both: a furniture maker might sell to consumers and to hotels and offices. Then you need two different kinds of marketing.

## The main differences

| Aspect | B2C | B2B |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides | One person or a family | Several people: director, procurement, accountant |
| Decision time | Minutes to days | Weeks to months |
| Ticket size | Small or medium | Often large |
| Motivation | Emotion, convenience, status | Profit, lower risk, reliability |
| Relationship | Often one-off | A long-term contract |
| Main channels | Instagram, TikTok, targeted ads, maps | Search, LinkedIn, Telegram, trade shows, personal meetings |
| Content | Short, visual, emotional | Detailed: case studies, calculations, proposals |

## B2C marketing: speed and emotion

- **short videos and visual content** — [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/);
- **targeted ads** to a broad audience;
- **social proof**: reviews, customer videos;
- **fast replies** and a simple path to purchase;
- **repeat sales**: promotions, loyalty.

In family purchases, B2C starts to resemble B2B: several decision-makers — [family purchase decisions](/en/blog/family-purchase-decision/).

## B2B marketing: trust and numbers

- **visibility in search**: buyers search and compare — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/);

- **case studies and numbers**: a "how much you'll save" calculation;

## The difference in ad creative: an illustrative example

The same product — an office chair:

| | B2C ad | B2B ad |
|---|---|---|
| For whom | Someone working from home | An office manager, procurement |
| Message | "Your back won't tire — 8 hours of comfort" | "For 50 workstations: warranty, delivery and assembly in one day" |
| Call to action | "Send us a DM" | "Get a commercial proposal" |
| Proof | A customer video | A list of corporate clients (with permission), warranty terms |

## The difference in sales

- **B2C**: fast replies, a simple script, a sale in one or two touches — [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/).
- **B2B**: several meetings, a presentation, a proposal, a trial batch, a contract. Separate proof for each decision-maker — [presentations with AI](/en/blog/ai-presentations/).

## Common mistakes

- Trying to sell a B2B product with Reels and "20% off".
- Long, "formal" content in B2C.
- Judging B2B by cost per lead — one big client outweighs hundreds of cheap leads.
- Working with only one decision-maker.
- The same ad for both segments.

## Questions and answers

### What if a business is in both segments?

You need a separate offer, a separate page and separate ad campaigns. A separate salesperson for each helps too.

### Does Instagram work in B2B?

For trust and brand, yes. But most leads usually come from search, referrals and personal contacts.

### B2B sales cycles are long — how do I judge ads?

By intermediate stages: meetings, proposals sent, trial orders. And over 3–6 months.

### What is B2G?

Business to government: public procurement. It's a field with its own rules.

## Conclusion

B2C means fast, emotional, visual marketing and a simple path to purchase. B2B means several decision-makers, a long cycle, calculations, case studies and personal relationships.
