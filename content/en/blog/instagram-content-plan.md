If "what are we posting today?" comes up every day, the page becomes chaotic and doesn't sell. A content plan solves this: you know a month ahead what you'll post, when and why. Here is how to build a content plan, plus 30 ideas for any business.

## How much to post

A practical minimum for a small business: **3–4 posts or Reels a week** and a few **Stories** every day. Consistency beats volume: three posts every week is better than 30 posts in a month followed by a month of silence.

## Balancing content types

| Type | Share | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Useful | ~40% | Trust and saves |
| Proof | ~25% | Reviews, results, cases |
| Process and team | ~20% | Closeness to the brand |
| Offer | ~15% | Sales |

It isn't a strict rule, but a page made only of offers turns into a notice board.

## 30 ideas

**Useful**
1. How to choose the product — 5 tips.
2. Answers to 3 frequent customer questions.
3. Mistakes people make using the product.
4. "Cheap vs expensive option: what's the difference".
5. A seasonal tip.
6. A mini glossary: your industry's terms in plain words.
7. A "what to check before buying" checklist.
8. Myth vs fact.

**Proof**
9. A customer review (video or screenshot).
10. Before / after.
11. A project case with numbers.
12. A customer's question and your solution.
13. Awards, certificates, partners.
14. A year in numbers: customers, orders, projects.

**Process and team**
15. How the working day starts.
16. How an order is prepared.
17. Meet a team member.
18. Inside the office or production.
19. How we fixed a mistake — an honest story.
20. The founder's story: why they started the business.
21. How a new product is born.
22. The delivery process.

**Offer**
23. Launching a new product.
24. A time-limited promotion.
25. Instalments or special terms.
26. A bundle offer.
27. A "followers only" bonus.
28. A free consultation or measurement.
29. Gift ideas.
30. The month's results and next month's plans.

## How to lay out the plan

A simple table is enough (Google Sheets or Notion): date, content type, format (post, Reel, carousel), topic, call to action, status. Batch your shooting into one day — filming 8–10 videos in a day is far easier than one a day. For writing Reels scripts, see [Reels script templates](/en/blog/reels-script-templates/); for the first seconds, our [hooks article](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).

## Conclusion

A content plan turns a page of random posts into a systematic sales channel. Rotate content types, post consistently and review each month which posts brought leads. And don't forget your profile — see [an Instagram profile that sells](/en/blog/instagram-profile-that-sells/).
