A promotion started the day before a holiday often falls flat: the creative is rushed, the ads don't have time to learn, and competitors have already claimed the audience. A yearly marketing calendar solves this: the key dates are known in advance, so content, ads and the sales team can be ready 3–4 weeks ahead. This article gives a 2027 calendar for businesses in Uzbekistan and how to prepare for each season.

![How to use the calendar](/img/blog/illus/marketing-kalendari-2027-1.jpg 1200x675)

## How to use the calendar

- **Pick the dates that fit your industry.** Not every holiday matters to every business.
- **Start preparing early:** content and creatives 3–4 weeks ahead, ads 1–2 weeks ahead.
- **Prepare the sales team:** rotas, a script, promotion terms — [sales team during holidays](/en/blog/sales-team-during-holidays/).
- **Religious holiday dates shift every year** — keep an eye on the official dates.

| Month | Key dates | Who it matters to most |
|---|---|---|
| January | 1 January — New Year; 14 January — Defenders of the Motherland Day | Restaurants, travel, education (new-year goals), fitness |
| February | 14 February; start of Ramadan (expected in the first half of February) | Flowers, gifts, restaurants, groceries |
| March | 8 March — International Women's Day; Ramazan Hayit (expected early March); 21 March — Navruz | Beauty, flowers, gifts, clothing, restaurants, groceries |

| August | Back-to-school preparation | Clothing, stationery, kindergartens and schools, education centres |

Ramadan and the Hayit holidays follow the lunar calendar and are officially announced in advance — fine-tune campaigns once the official date is out.

## What's special about 2027: March

March 2027 is expected to be especially crowded: the end of Ramadan, Ramazan Hayit, 8 March and Navruz fall within a few weeks of each other. That means:

- competition in the ad auction will be high — prepare budget and creatives in February;
- one campaign can't cover every holiday — each needs its own creative;
- the load on sales and delivery will rise.

## Ideas by season

### New Year and December

The biggest sales season. Gift sets, corporate orders, "year in review" content. More — [New Year content plan](/en/blog/new-year-content-plan/) and [December ad budget](/en/blog/december-ad-budget/).

### 8 March and Navruz

Gifts, beauty services, flowers, festive food. Navruz is a national family holiday: content about family, tradition and spring lands well.

### Ramadan and the Hayit holidays

Iftar offers, charity and family-values themes, holiday gifts. The tone needs to be respectful and restrained.

### Summer

Travel, children's courses and camps, summer products. Travel ads should start before summer, in spring.

### August–September

School and the academic year. For education centres and schools, the main lead season of the year.

### November: 11.11 and Black Friday

The big discount season for online retail. Important: discounts must be genuine, and prices in ads must be in so‘m — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## Beyond holidays: your own dates

The strongest calendar dates are often not public holidays but your own:

- the company's birthday;
- a branch opening or product launch;
- an annual event for customers;
- a seasonal collection or menu.

Competition is low on these dates and the attention is all yours.

![Common mistakes](/img/blog/illus/marketing-kalendari-2027-3.jpg 1200x675)

## Common mistakes

- Running a promotion for every holiday — the audience gets tired.
- Starting 2–3 days before the holiday.
- Stopping at a single generic "happy holidays" post.
- Not briefing the sales team — a customer asks about the promotion and the manager doesn't know it.
- Not analysing results after the holiday.

## Questions and answers

### Should I advertise for every holiday?

No. Pick the 4–6 dates that matter most to your industry and prepare properly for them. For the rest, simple greeting content is enough.

### How can I know the exact Hayit dates?

Ramadan and the Hayit holidays depend on the lunar calendar and are officially announced each year. Plan the campaign around the expected date and confirm once it's announced.

### What budget does a holiday promotion need?

It depends on your usual budget and goal. Ad costs often rise during holidays, so it pays to plan the budget in advance and start the campaign earlier.

### How do I turn the calendar into a content plan?

Put the dates into your monthly content plan and, for each, list preparation tasks 2–4 weeks ahead — [Instagram content plan](/en/blog/instagram-content-plan/).
