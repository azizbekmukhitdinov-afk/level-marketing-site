"We got 300 leads this month", "the leads are poor quality", "lead generation" — "lead" is one of the most-used words in marketing and sales. For business owners, though, it often stays vague: is it a customer, an enquiry, or just someone curious? Here's a plain explanation of what a lead is, the different types and why quality matters more than quantity.

## A simple definition

A lead is someone who has shown interest in your business and left contact details: a name, a phone number or a DM conversation. They aren't a customer yet — they're a **potential customer**.

In Uzbekistan, a lead is often called an "ariza" (application or request): a lead form, a website form, a message to a Telegram bot, a call or a DM.

## Where leads come from

| Source | Example |
|---|---|
| Ad lead forms | An instant form on Instagram or Facebook |
| Messages | DMs, Telegram, WhatsApp |
| Website and landing page | A form, a call button — [a landing page that converts](/en/blog/landing-page-that-converts/) |
| Search and maps | A call or request from Google/Yandex |
| Telegram bot | A request completed in a bot — [Telegram bot for leads](/en/blog/telegram-bot-for-leads/) |
| Offline | Exhibitions, QR codes, referrals |

## Types of leads

| Type | Who | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hot | Wants to buy now, has a specific question | Reply immediately, propose the next step |
| Warm | Interested, comparing | Information, trust, follow-up |
| Cold | Just curious or clicked by accident | A gentle touch, or filter out |
| Qualified | Fits your customer profile | Handle first |
| Unqualified | Wrong number, not a fit, "don't know" | Analyse why |

## What lead generation is

Lead generation is the systematic process of getting leads: ads, content, lead magnets, websites, bots. The goal is a steady, predictable flow of enquiries.

But here's the key point: **the goal of lead generation isn't leads — it's sales**. Three hundred cheap leads that don't buy are worse than fifty pricier ones that do. More in [the cheap leads trap](/en/blog/cheap-leads-trap/).

## How to judge lead quality

Every lead in your CRM should pass through these stages:

1. **Was contact made?** — they answered the call or replied.
2. **Are they a fit?** — your area, budget, need.
3. **Was there a next step?** — a meeting, a measurement, an order.
4. **Did they buy?**

Compare sources across these four stages: which ad, which form, which channel produces sales. Form questions improve lead quality — [lead form questions](/en/blog/lead-form-questions/).

## Cost per lead

Cost per lead (CPL) = ad spend ÷ number of leads. Illustrative example: 5 million soum spent, 100 leads — one lead costs 50,000 soum. But CPL alone says little: look at it together with lead-to-sale conversion. More in [cost per lead](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/).

## From lead to customer

A lead is just the start. What turns it into a customer:

- **speed**: the first reply — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/);
- **a script**: clear questions and a next step — [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/);
- **follow-up** with people who say "I'll think about it" — ["I'll think about it" follow-up](/en/blog/i-will-think-about-it-follow-up/);
- **a CRM**: so no lead gets lost — [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/).

## Common mistakes

- Judging results by number of leads.
- Not recording each lead's source.
- Replying to leads late.
- Saying "the ads are bad" without analysing the poor leads.
- Not checking whether lead counts in the ad account and CRM match — [ad leads vs CRM mismatch](/en/blog/ad-leads-vs-crm-mismatch/).

## Questions and answers

### What's the difference between a lead and a customer?

A lead is a potential customer who left contact details. A customer is someone who bought.

### Are a lead and an enquiry the same?

In practice, often yes. Some companies call only a checked, qualified enquiry a "lead".

### How much should a lead cost?

It depends on the niche and price. What matters is that cost per sale is profitable for your business.

### Can I buy leads?

Buying databases collected elsewhere is risky in terms of consent and quality. More in [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/).
