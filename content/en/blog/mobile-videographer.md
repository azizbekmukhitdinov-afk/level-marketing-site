"Mobilograf wanted" is a common job ad among businesses in Uzbekistan. A mobile videographer shoots and edits video on a phone for Reels, Stories, TikTok and Shorts. Now that short video is the dominant format, they've become the most needed content person for many businesses. Here's what a mobile videographer does and how to choose one.

## What a mobile videographer does

- **Shooting** on a phone, with good light, sound and composition — [shooting Reels on a phone](/en/blog/shooting-reels-on-a-phone/).
- **Editing**: pacing, subtitles, music, effects in mobile apps — [video editing apps](/en/blog/video-editing-apps/).
- **Knowing trends**: which formats work right now — [using trends for business](/en/blog/using-trends-for-business/).
- **Working with people on camera**: helping staff or the founder feel at ease.

Usually a marketer or social media manager writes the script and the videographer turns it into video.

## Mobile videographer vs camera operator vs social media manager

| | Mobile videographer | Camera operator | Social media manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kit | Phone, gimbal, microphone | Professional camera | — |
| Format | Short vertical video | Ads, brand films, events | Content plan, the page |
| Speed | Fast, lots of videos | Slower, more in-depth | — |
| For | Social content and ad creatives | Large projects | Strategy and engagement |

## When you need one

- You need 3+ Reels a week, but no one on the team makes video.
- Your ads constantly need new creatives — [creative fatigue](/en/blog/creative-fatigue/).
- The founder or staff are ready to be on camera, but nobody shoots or edits.

## How to choose

1. **Portfolio**: not just pretty shots — views and results.
2. **A test task**: one short video about your business, in real conditions.
3. **Speed**: how many days until a finished video?
4. **Sound**: do they have a microphone, and is the audio clean? — [sound in video ads](/en/blog/sound-in-video-ads/).
5. **Communication**: can they direct people on camera?

## How pricing works

Prices usually depend on the number of videos, shoot days and editing complexity: per video, as a monthly package or per shoot day. Shooting several videos in one day is the most cost-effective approach — [one shoot, 10 ad creatives](/en/blog/one-shoot-10-ad-creatives/). On video costs — [video ad production cost](/en/blog/video-ad-production-cost/).

## How to work together

- The monthly content plan and scripts are ready in advance.
- 5–10 videos per shoot day.
- One round of revisions after editing, with clear rules.
- Music cleared for commercial use — [music for video ads](/en/blog/music-for-video-ads/).
- Consent from people on camera — [photo and video consent for advertising](/en/blog/photo-video-consent-for-advertising/).

## Questions and answers

### Should I hire one in-house or use a freelancer?

If you need lots of videos every week, hire in-house or use a regular partner; if only occasionally, a freelancer or agency.

### Is a phone enough, or do I need a camera?

For social media, a modern phone is enough. Sound and lighting matter more.

### Should the videographer write scripts?

It's better when scripts come from marketing; the videographer can suggest ideas.
