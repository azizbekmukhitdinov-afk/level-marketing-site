Leads arrive from Instagram, Telegram, the phone and your website, managers keep them in notebooks and chats — and some get lost. A **CRM** (customer relationship management system) collects every lead in one place and tracks it through to the sale. Here is when you need a CRM, how to choose one and how to roll it out.

## Signs you need a CRM

- Leads get lost or go unanswered.
- You don't know which ads bring sales.
- When a manager leaves, the customer base leaves too.
- Follow-ups get forgotten.
- Comparing the sales plan with actual results is hard.

## Start with a spreadsheet

While lead volume is low, Google Sheets is enough: date, name, phone, source, stage, amount, next step, manager. If there's no discipline with a spreadsheet, a CRM won't help either.

## How to choose a CRM

| Criterion | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Integrations | Instagram, Telegram, website forms, telephony — so leads arrive automatically |
| Stages (pipeline) | Configurable to your sales process |
| Tasks and reminders | So follow-ups aren't forgotten |
| Reports | Sources, conversion, managers' results |
| Mobile app | Managers work from their phones |
| Language and support | A clear interface and help |
| Price | Usually a monthly fee per user |

In Uzbekistan, small and medium businesses often use systems like amoCRM (Kommo) and Bitrix24; try several during free trials before choosing.

## Rolling it out: 5 steps

1. **Define pipeline stages:** new lead → contact made → offer sent → payment → closed.
2. **Mandatory fields:** source, amount, reason (if lost).
3. **Connect integrations** to cut manual entry.
4. **Train managers** and set a rule: "If a lead isn't in the CRM, it doesn't exist."
5. **A weekly report** based on your [sales funnel](/en/blog/what-is-a-sales-funnel/).

## Common mistakes

- Over-complicated setup — managers stop using it.
- Buying a CRM without a defined process.
- Not recording the source — you can't evaluate your ads; for website leads, [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/) help.

## Conclusion

A CRM is the tool for not losing leads and managing sales by the numbers. Start with a spreadsheet, define the process, then move it into a CRM. Combined with fast replies, it grows sales quickly — read our [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/) article.
