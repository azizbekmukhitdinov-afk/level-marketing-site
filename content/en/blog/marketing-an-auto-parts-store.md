People don't buy auto parts because they saw an ad. The car broke down, a part is needed, and it's needed today. They call or message several shops and buy from whoever gives a precise answer first. So in auto parts marketing, the key isn't attractive posts — it's a system for answering enquiries quickly and accurately. Here's how to build it.

## How customers search

- "Cobalt front brake pads price"
- "Gentra radiator Tashkent"
- In Telegram groups: "Who has a headlight for a Malibu 2?"
- Asking a mechanic: "Where can I get it?"

These are **specific-need** queries. The customer already knows what they need — or their mechanic does.

## What matters most: speed and accuracy

In auto parts, the first precise answer often wins the sale. The system:

- **One entry point**: Telegram or phone, so all enquiries land in one place.
- **Standard questions**: model, year, engine, VIN — the salesperson asks in the first message.
- **A reply template**: "In stock / not in stock, original or aftermarket, price, delivery time".
- **A stock database**, so the salesperson can answer without running to the warehouse.

More on response speed — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).

## Original vs aftermarket: a question of trust

The customer's biggest fear is buying a fake. Build trust:

- clearly separate originals, certified aftermarket parts and budget alternatives;
- give warranty terms for each;
- show photos of packaging, part codes and brands;
- publish useful content such as "How to tell an original from a fake".

## Partnering with car services and mechanics

Many parts are sold on a mechanic's recommendation. For car services:

- special pricing or a bonus programme (with written terms);
- fast delivery to the workshop;
- a separate Telegram chat or bot for orders.

On marketing car services — [marketing a car service and detailing](/en/blog/marketing-a-car-service-and-detailing/).

## Advertising: which channel

- **Search ads** on model and part queries: a specific need and high intent — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).
- **Maps** — "auto parts near me": address, opening hours, list of brands — [add your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/).
- **A Telegram channel** — new arrivals, kits for popular models, seasonal offers.
- **Instagram/Facebook** — less effective for a broad audience, but they work for seasonal offers (winter tyres, batteries) and nearby audiences.

## Content ideas

- "For Cobalt owners: what to replace every 10,000 km" — service schedules by model.
- "Original vs fake filter: 4 differences" — a video.
- Seasonal reminders: winter preparation, air conditioning.
- A mechanic's advice: which aftermarket brands are reliable.

## Turning buyers into regulars

Every car needs servicing every year. Keep each customer's model and purchase history (with consent) in a CRM and send timely reminders: "Time for an oil change" — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/). Choosing a CRM — [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/).

## Questions and answers

### Do I need an online catalogue?

For a large range, yes — with easy search. A small shop can manage with enquiries via Telegram.

### Should I show prices in ads?

For popular parts, yes — it speeds up enquiries. Prices must be in soum — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

### Can I advertise in Telegram groups?

Yes, if you follow the group rules and give genuinely useful answers. Spam damages your reputation.

## Conclusion

Auto parts marketing is a system of fast, precise answers to enquiries, trust around original vs aftermarket parts, partnerships with mechanics, ads on specific-need queries and turning buyers into regulars.
