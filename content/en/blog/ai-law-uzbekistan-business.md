Business owners using artificial intelligence face new questions: can an AI-generated image be used in an ad? Can customer reviews be uploaded to ChatGPT? Must AI content be labelled? In January 2026, Uzbekistan adopted a law regulating artificial intelligence. Here's a plain-language look at the parts that affect business, plus the other legal requirements that apply when you work with AI.

> This is not legal advice. We explain the text of the law in plain language. In complex cases, consult a lawyer and check the current version on lex.uz.

## ZRU-1115: the key law of 2026

On 21 January 2026, Law No. ZRU-1115 "On amendments and additions to certain legislative acts of the Republic of Uzbekistan in connection with regulating relations arising from the use of artificial intelligence" was signed ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8008401)). It amended the Law "On Informatisation" and the Code of Administrative Liability.

The key points for business:

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| Definition of AI | A set of technological solutions that imitate human knowledge and skills, including independent learning and searching for solutions |
| Do no harm | AI-generated information resources and AI systems must not harm a person's life, health, freedom, honour or dignity |
| Don't rely on AI alone | Legally significant decisions affecting people's rights and freedoms can't be based solely on AI outputs |
| Personal data | Unlawfully processing personal data with AI technologies and distributing it via the media, telecom networks or the internet is prohibited |

## The fine: 50 to 100 BRV

Under an addition to Article 46² of the Code of Administrative Liability, unlawfully processing and distributing personal data using AI carries a fine of 50 to 100 times the base calculation value (BRV), with confiscation of the means used (ZRU-1115). Given that 1 BRV has been 440,000 soum since September 2026, that's 22 to 44 million soum.

In practice: uploading customer databases, chats or call recordings to AI services without a lawful basis is now a specific risk.

## Personal data: the core law

The Law "On Personal Data" (ZRU-547) also applies when working with AI ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/4396419)): consent, purpose limitation, retention periods and protection. More in [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/).

Practical rules for AI:

- anonymise data for analysis — no names, phone numbers or addresses;
- if an AI bot collects leads, request and store consent;
- keep work data out of employees' personal accounts.

## Must AI content be labelled?

Mandatory labelling of AI-generated resources was proposed during the debate, but the final text of the law contains no such requirement ([Gazeta.uz](https://www.gazeta.uz/ru/2026/01/22/artificial-intelligence/)). However:

- ad platforms and marketplaces may have their own rules;
- advertising must be truthful regardless (see below).

## AI in advertising: the Law "On Advertising"

When AI content is used in ads, the usual advertising rules apply ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6052631)):

| Situation | Which rule | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| AI makes the product look better than it is | Article 5: unfair (misleading) advertising | Misleading customers |
| An AI image resembling a real person, without consent | Article 16: a person's name and image without consent | Prohibited |
| Reproducing another ad's music, voice or copy | Article 16 | Prohibited |
| AI copy claiming "best" or "No. 1" without proof | Article 16: comparative claims need evidence | Proof required |
| A dollar price in AI-written copy | Article 16: prices in foreign currency | Prohibited |

General rules — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/). Working with AI video — [making video ads with AI](/en/blog/ai-video-ads/).

## Copyright

According to a legal analysis on Anhor.uz, under Uzbekistan's legislation, material created solely by AI without an individual's creative activity is not an object of copyright ([Anhor.uz](https://anhor.uz/ru/pravovoy-razbor/ai-rights)). Practical takeaways:

- a logo or image created purely by AI may be harder to protect — [AI logo](/en/blog/ai-logo/);
- read the AI tool's commercial-use terms;
- consult a specialist about key brand elements.

## A checklist for business

- [ ] The team has written AI rules: what goes in, what doesn't.
- [ ] Customer data is given to AI only in anonymised form.
- [ ] If an AI bot collects leads, consent is requested and stored.
- [ ] Important decisions about customers and staff aren't based on AI output alone.
- [ ] AI content in ads shows the product as it really is.
- [ ] People's images and voices are used only with written consent.
- [ ] AI tools' commercial-use terms have been checked.
- [ ] Platforms' rules on AI content have been checked.

## Questions and answers

### Is it legal to use ChatGPT?

Yes, using AI tools isn't prohibited. What matters is not processing personal data unlawfully and following advertising rules.

### When did the law take effect?

The law was signed on 21 January 2026. Check the effective date and current version on lex.uz.

### If I use an AI image in an ad, must I label it "AI"?

The final text of the law contains no mandatory labelling. Check the platforms' rules.

### Who is liable if an employee uploads the customer base to AI?

That situation needs a lawyer's assessment. To prevent it, you need written internal rules and access restrictions.

## Conclusion

Since 2026, Uzbekistan has specific rules for AI: do no harm, don't leave important decisions to AI alone, and a fine of 50–100 BRV for unlawfully processing personal data with AI. In advertising, the usual rules apply: truthfulness, consent and proof.
