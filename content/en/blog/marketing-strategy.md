People often picture a "marketing strategy" as a thick document, or something "for big companies". In reality, a strategy answers one question: **who we sell to, what we sell, how, and with what budget**. Marketing without a strategy means a new "let's try this" every month: an influencer today, a discount tomorrow, a new page after that. Here's what a marketing strategy is and how to build one. A ready template — [one-page marketing strategy](/en/blog/one-page-marketing-strategy/).

## Strategy vs plan vs tactics

| | Strategy | Plan | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question | Where are we going, and why? | Who does what, and when? | How exactly do we do it? |
| Horizon | 6–12 months | A month, a quarter | A week, a day |
| Example | "Fast custom kitchens for young families" | "March–May: paid social + maps + reviews" | "This week: 5 Reels and 3 ad tests" |

## The 7 core parts

1. **Goal** — in numbers: "X sales a month", "revenue of Y". KPIs — [what is KPI](/en/blog/what-is-kpi/).
2. **Audience** — who buys and why — [target audience and customer persona](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/).
3. **Positioning** — how you're remembered — [brand positioning](/en/blog/brand-positioning/).
4. **Offer** — what you sell, at what price and how you differ — [unique selling proposition](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/).
5. **Channels** — where your customers are: paid social, search, maps, Telegram, influencers.
6. **Budget** — by channel and by month — [planning a marketing budget](/en/blog/planning-a-marketing-budget/).
7. **Measurement** — which metrics, when, and who reports — [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).

## How to build it in 5 steps

1. **Analyse the situation**: numbers, competitors, customers — [SWOT analysis](/en/blog/swot-analysis/), [competitor analysis](/en/blog/competitor-analysis/).
2. **Set the goal**: realistic and measurable.
3. **Make choices**: 2–3 core channels and one main message — not everything at once.
4. **Turn it into a plan**: monthly actions and owners.
5. **Review monthly**: compare plan and actuals, and adjust if needed.

## For small businesses

You don't need a thick document. One page is enough if it's specific: who, what, where, how much and how we'll measure. What matters is coming back to it every month.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a list of channels ("Instagram, Telegram, TikTok") as a strategy.
- A goal without numbers: "more customers".
- One message for every audience.
- A document nobody revisits all year.
- A strategy built without the sales team — [marketing and sales alignment](/en/blog/marketing-and-sales-alignment/).

## Questions and answers

### Can I build the strategy myself?

Yes, with a one-page template. But an outside view often spots mistakes you can't see.

### When should the strategy change?

When the market, the product or the results shift sharply; otherwise, do a big review once a year.

### How does a strategy differ from a marketing system?

The strategy is the direction; the system is the processes that carry it out every day — [marketing system for growth](/en/blog/marketing-system-for-growth/).

## Conclusion

A marketing strategy is a concrete answer to who you sell to, what, how and with what budget: goal, audience, positioning, offer, channels, budget and measurement.
