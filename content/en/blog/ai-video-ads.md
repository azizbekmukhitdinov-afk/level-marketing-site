"Making video with AI" is one of the fastest-growing searches of recent months. Type a few words and the video is ready — very tempting for business owners: ads with no shoot, no actors, no studio. In reality, AI speeds up video production but doesn't replace everything. Here's where AI helps in video ads, where it's risky and how to build the process.

## What AI video tools can do

| Task | AI capability | Suitable for ads |
|---|---|---|
| Script and hook | Options, structure | High — with human editing |
| Subtitles | Automatic text | High — manual checks essential in Uzbek |
| Editing assistance | Cutting, formatting, converting to 9:16 | High |
| Voice and dubbing | Voice-over, translation | Medium — often sounds artificial in Uzbek |
| Backgrounds, decor, effects | Replacing backgrounds, adding scenes | Medium |
| Fully generated video | Video from text | Low to medium — risky for products and people |

The takeaway: AI is good at the **surrounding work** — script, editing, subtitles, formats. The key shots of the product and people are best kept real.

## Why the product must be real

Three reasons:

1. **The law.** The Law "On Advertising" prohibits misleading (unfair) advertising (Article 5, [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/6052631)). If an AI-generated sofa looks better than your real one, that misleads customers.
2. **Returns and reviews.** Customers expect what they saw in the video. A mismatch means negative reviews and returns.
3. **Trust.** People are getting better at spotting AI footage. "Artificial" ads reduce trust.

Social proof comes from real people — more in [social proof in ads](/en/blog/social-proof-in-ads/).

## A 6-stage AI video ad workflow

### 1. Idea and script — AI + human

Ask AI for 10 hooks and 3 script options, then pick the best and adapt it to the customer's language. Ready-made prompts — [marketing prompts](/en/blog/marketing-prompts/). Script structure — [Reels script templates](/en/blog/reels-script-templates/).

### 2. Shot list — AI

From the script, AI builds a shot list: which scene, which angle, how many seconds. That speeds up the shoot day.

### 3. Filming — human

The product, the process, staff and customers — real footage. A phone works too — [shooting Reels on a phone](/en/blog/shooting-reels-on-a-phone/).

### 4. Editing — human + AI

AI assistants speed up cutting, converting to 9:16, and evening out colour and sound. Rhythm and story are human decisions.

### 5. Subtitles and voice — AI + checking

Auto-subtitles make many mistakes in Uzbek — check each one by hand. On voice — [AI voice-over in ads](/en/blog/ai-voiceover-ads/).

### 6. Variations — AI

Several versions from one video: a different hook, length or format. You need these for ad testing — [one shoot, 10 ad creatives](/en/blog/one-shoot-10-ad-creatives/).

## When fully AI-generated video fits

Fully generated video can work in some cases:

- **abstract ideas** — showing a service symbolically (saving time, order);
- **backgrounds and mood** — intro shots without the product;
- **internal use** — a storyboard sketch to show an idea to the team.

Wherever the product, people or results appear — use real footage.

## Ad platform rules

Ad platforms have their own rules on AI content, and they change often. Check the platform's current policy before launching. AI-generated likenesses and voices of well-known people are especially risky: they can break both platform rules and the law. Using a person's image in advertising without their consent is prohibited by law (Article 16).

## Measuring results

Compare the AI-assisted video with a regular one:

- production time and cost;
- retention in the first 3 seconds;
- cost per lead and conversion to sales.

The best result is often real footage plus AI-accelerated editing and variations. Testing — [A/B testing ad creatives](/en/blog/ab-testing-ad-creatives/).

## Common mistakes

- Making the product look "better" with AI.
- Publishing Uzbek auto-subtitles unchecked.
- Using AI likenesses or voices of famous people.
- Not reading the AI tool's licence — is commercial use allowed?
- Not filming at all because "we have AI".

## Questions and answers

### Are AI video ads cheaper?

For scripts, editing and variations, yes — they save time. But fully replacing real filming often hurts results.

### Can I use an AI voice in Uzbek?

You can, but test it against a human voice. AI voices often sound unnatural in Uzbek.

### Do I have to label AI video as "made with AI"?

The final text of Law ZRU-1115 contains no mandatory labelling requirement for AI content. But platforms may have their own rules — check them. More in [AI and the law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).

### Which AI video tool is best?

Tools change monthly. When choosing, look at commercial-use terms, Uzbek language support and export quality.

## Conclusion

AI speeds up video ads: scripts, shot lists, editing, subtitles and variations. But the product, people and results must be real — that matters for the law, reviews and trust.
