"We need an SMM person," many businesses say, without always knowing what to expect. One ends up only posting, while another is expected to handle ads, video and sales too. Here is what an SMM manager does, how they differ from an ads specialist and how to choose one.

## An SMM manager's responsibilities

- **Strategy and content plan** — content types, formats, posting rhythm.
- **Copy** — posts, Stories, Reels scripts.
- **Coordinating shooting and design** — working with a videographer and designer, or doing it themselves.
- **Publishing and Stories** — every day.
- **Community management** — replying to comments and, if agreed, messages.
- **Analytics** — a monthly report: reach, engagement, profile visits, leads.

## SMM manager vs ads specialist

| | SMM manager | Ads specialist |
|---|---|---|
| Main work | Content and the page | Paid advertising |
| Result | Organic reach, trust, leads from the page | Leads from ads |
| Key metrics | Reach, ER, saves | CPL, number of leads and sales |

They complement each other: a good page makes ads cheaper, and ads bring new people to the page. For choosing an ads specialist, see our [separate article](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-paid-ads-specialist/).

## Who to hire: in-house, freelancer or agency

| Option | Upside | Downside |
|---|---|---|
| In-house | Knows the business deeply, always available | One person can't do everything |
| Freelancer | Cheaper, flexible | Juggles projects, less stable |
| Agency | A team: SMM, video, design, ads | Pricier, needs a clear brief |

## What to ask in the interview

1. Work samples with numbers: reach and leads before and after.
2. A sample content plan.
3. How they measure results and report.
4. Who shoots the videos, and how many per month.
5. How quickly they reply to comments and messages.

## Which KPIs to set

Not just follower count:

- reach among your target audience;
- saves and shares;
- profile visits and link clicks;
- the number of organic leads;
- sticking to the posting plan.

## Red flags

- Offers to buy followers.
- Promises like "100,000 followers in a month".
- Working without a plan or reports.

## A brief for your SMM manager

Good results start with a clear brief. It should include:

- the business goal: leads, awareness or launching a new product;
- the [target audience](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/) and their questions;
- products, prices and your [unique selling proposition](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/);
- tone of voice: formal or casual, whether humour is allowed;
- competitors and examples of pages you like;
- what's off-limits: topics, words, promises;
- who approves content and how quickly.

## What to expect in the first month

| Period | Result |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | An audit of the page and competitors |
| Week 2 | Strategy, content series and a content plan |
| Weeks 3–4 | Shoots, posting and the first Stories series |
| End of month | The first report and next month's plan |

The first month is for setting up. Judge organic results after 2–3 months.

## What a monthly report should include

- How many posts, Reels and Stories went out against the plan.
- Trends in reach, saves, shares and profile visits.
- The best and worst posts — and why.
- The number of enquiries that came from the page.
- Conclusions and changes for next month.

## Questions and answers

### Should an SMM manager handle sales too?

Usually not. By agreement, they may give the first reply to messages, but a sales manager handles the sale. Write down where their responsibility ends: when and how an enquiry is handed to the sales team.

### How long should the trial period be?

One or two months, with specific tasks. Judge by plan delivery, content quality and communication, not by follower count.

### How do you link SMM results to sales?

Put UTM tags on the links from your page, log DM enquiries in the CRM with the source "Instagram organic", and count sales from that source every month. More on UTM in [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).

### How many projects can one SMM manager handle at once?

It depends on the content volume. Ask directly in the interview: with too many projects, response speed and quality suffer.

### Who gives the SMM manager material for content?

The business itself knows its product and processes best, so assign a responsible employee. Once a week, they pass on news, customer questions and cases, and make time for shoots. Simple phone photos and voice messages are good raw material too. If materials arrive late, the plan suffers — note this separately in the report so the reason for delays is visible.

## Conclusion

An SMM manager is your page's editor and voice. Expect a plan, consistency and reports in numbers, and think about a separate or team solution for ads and sales. For examples of SMM results, see our [iTOPS](/en/blog/case-itops/) and [AutoRobot](/en/blog/case-autorobot/) cases.
