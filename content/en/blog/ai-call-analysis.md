Many businesses record their calls, but nobody listens to them: a manager can't get through 100 calls a day. So nobody knows where leads from advertising are being lost. AI solves this: it transcribes recordings, scores them against criteria and produces conclusions. Here's how to set up AI call analysis.

## Why this is a marketing issue

Ads bring in the lead, but the sale happens on the call. If a salesperson quotes the price in the first minute and never agrees a next step, even the best ad wastes money. Call analysis settles the "the ads are bad" versus "the leads are poor" argument with facts — [why ads don't bring sales](/en/blog/why-ads-dont-bring-sales/).

## The process in 4 steps

1. **Recording**: via IP telephony, a mobile app or the CRM — every call is linked to a lead.
2. **Transcription**: AI turns speech into text. Check the quality for calls that mix Uzbek and Russian.
3. **Analysis**: AI scores the transcript against the criteria.
4. **Conclusions and action**: feedback for the salesperson, a report for the manager, the next step in the CRM.

## Scoring criteria (example)

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Greeting | Did the salesperson introduce themselves and the company? |
| Needs discovery | Did they ask at least 2–3 questions? |
| Offer | Did they offer something matched to the customer's need? |
| Price | Did they present the price together with the value? |
| Objections | Did they handle "too expensive" and "I'll think about it"? |
| Next step | Was a specific date and action agreed? |
| Tone | Were they polite and confident? |

Take the criteria from your own script — [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/).

## What AI tells you

- **For each call**: a short summary, what the customer wants, the next step, a score.
- **An objection map**: the week's most frequent objections — material for content and ads too — [content from customer objections](/en/blog/content-from-customer-objections/).
- **By salesperson**: who loses on price, who never sets a next step — [sales rep conversion](/en/blog/sales-rep-conversion/).
- **Ad quality**: which offer customers are calling about, and which leads are off-target.

## An example prompt

"You are a sales quality controller. Score the call transcript below on 7 criteria from 0 to 2 points. For each criterion, quote the transcript. At the end, list the customer's need, the objections, the next step and 2 tips for the salesperson. Don't guess — don't write anything that isn't in the transcript."

## The law and privacy

- A call recording is personal data. Collect and store it in line with the law ([Personal Data Law](https://lex.uz/docs/4396419)).
- Telling customers at the start of the call that it's being recorded is good practice.
- Before sending recordings to an AI tool, check its data-retention terms; fines apply for unlawful processing with AI — [AI law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).
- Restrict access to recordings.

## How to start

1. Collect a week of calls (20–50).
2. Draw up the list of criteria.
3. Analyse them with AI and check the results yourself on 5–10 calls.
4. Hold a weekly 30-minute review with the sales team.
5. Measure the outcome: lead-to-meeting and lead-to-sale conversion.

## Questions and answers

### Can AI understand calls in Uzbek?

Many tools recognise Uzbek, but quality varies. Mixed languages and background noise increase errors — test on several calls.

### Won't salespeople resist?

If the analysis is used for learning and bonuses rather than punishment, it's usually accepted. Highlight the best calls too.

### Should every call be analysed?

Automatically, yes; in-depth human review selectively — the lowest-scoring and the best calls.

## Conclusion

AI call analysis means managing your sales team on facts: recording, transcript, criteria, conclusions and weekly coaching. It reveals the gap between advertising and sales.
