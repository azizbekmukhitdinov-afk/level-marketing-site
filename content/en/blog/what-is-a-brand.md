Many people picture a logo when they hear "brand". But a logo is only a small sign of the brand. A brand is what customers think and feel about your company. Here is what a brand is made of, why a small business needs one and where to start.

## Brand vs logo

| | Logo | Brand |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A mark, an image | The picture in a customer's mind |
| Where | Signage, posts, packaging | Every touchpoint: ads, service, reviews |
| How it's made | A designer draws it | Through everyday actions |

A beautiful logo won't save bad service, while a strong brand works even with a simple logo.

## What a brand is made of

1. **Positioning** — who you're for, what you offer and how you differ.
2. **Promise** — what customers can always expect from you: speed, quality, low prices, care.
3. **Personality and voice** — how you talk: serious, friendly, expert, playful.
4. **Customer experience** — from ordering to after-sales contact.
5. **Visual identity** — logo, colours, fonts, photo style.

## Why a small business needs a brand

- **Protection from price wars.** People pay more for a recognisable, trusted brand.
- **Cheaper ads.** People click on ads from a brand they know.
- **Word of mouth.** A memorable brand gets recommended to friends.
- **Repeat sales.** Customers remember where they bought.

## Where to start: a one-page brand platform

| Question | Example (furniture store) |
|---|---|
| Who is our customer? | Families in Tashkent who bought a new flat |
| What's their problem? | Finding quality furniture quickly at a fair price |
| What's our promise? | Furniture made to your measurements in 10 days |
| Why believe us? | Our own workshop, a warranty, instalments |
| How do we talk? | Warm, specific, no pomp |

Fill in this table — it becomes the basis for all your advertising, content and service. To find what sets you apart, see our [USP](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/) article; for the visual side, visual identity.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a logo without thinking through positioning.
- Copying competitors — you become their shadow.
- A different voice and style on each channel: one on Instagram, another on Telegram.
- Breaking your promise — the fastest way to destroy a brand.

## Conclusion

A brand isn't a logo — it's a promise kept every day. Start with a one-page platform, apply it consistently across channels and watch the customer experience.
