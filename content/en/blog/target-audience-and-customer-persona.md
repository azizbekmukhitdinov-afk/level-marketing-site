"Our audience is women aged 18–45" isn't an audience description. A good customer persona tells you what to say in an ad, who to show it to and where. Here is how to segment your market, build a customer persona and use it in your ads.

## Step 1: segmentation

Split the market into groups with similar needs:

- **Demographics:** age, gender, income, city.
- **Situation:** when the product is needed — for furniture, say: moving into a new flat, renovating, fitting out an office.
- **Behaviour:** first or repeat purchase, price-focused or quality-focused.
- **Channel:** where people spend their time — Instagram, Telegram, search.

Then pick 1–2 priority segments: the biggest, the most profitable or the easiest to reach.

## Step 2: the customer persona

Describe one "typical" customer for each segment. **An example (illustrative):** an online children's clothing shop.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Who | Malika, 29, Tashkent, two children aged 3 and 6 |
| Situation | Buys clothes for the kids every season |
| Pains | Kids grow fast, no time to go to the bazaar, hard to balance price and quality |
| Desires | Natural fabrics, delivery, easy exchanges |
| Objections | "Will the size fit?", "Is it really cotton?" |
| Where she spends time | Instagram, parenting Telegram channels |
| What drives the decision | Reviews from other mums, real photos, delivery terms |

## Where to learn pains and desires

- Interviews with 5–10 customers — see [collecting customer feedback](/en/blog/collecting-customer-feedback/) for how to run them.
- Sales managers: what customers ask most often.
- Conversations in [comments and DMs](/en/blog/handling-comments-and-dms/).
- Reviews of competitors — see [competitor analysis](/en/blog/competitor-analysis/).
- Follower insights in the Instagram professional dashboard: age, gender, cities.
- CRM data: who buys more and comes back.

## Using the persona in ads

- **Creative:** show the customer's situation and pain — our article on [types of video ads](/en/blog/types-of-video-ads/) helps you pick a format.
- **Copy:** use the customer's own words and answer objections up front.
- **Settings:** location, age and interests; build a [lookalike audience](/en/blog/lookalike-audiences/) from your buyer list.
- **One ad per segment:** the same ad won't work for people moving house and for offices.

A precise creative also helps the algorithm find the right people: the system shows the ad to people similar to those who responded. For setup basics, see [how to set up Instagram ads](/en/blog/how-to-set-up-instagram-ads/).

## Conclusion

A persona isn't a one-off document: update it every six months with new data. A product "for everyone" is the most common mistake new entrepreneurs make; the rest are in our article on [7 marketing mistakes](/en/blog/marketing-mistakes-new-entrepreneurs/).
