The same product can be advertised with different video formats — and results can differ several times over. It's hard to guess in advance which format will work, so you need to prepare several types and test them. Here are the 7 video ad types that sell best and when each works.

## The 7 types

| Type | When it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Review | The product sells by being shown | "5 advantages of this sofa" |
| Before / after | The result is visible | Renovation, beauty, cleaning |
| Customer testimonial | Trust decides | Clinics, education, expensive services |
| Process | Quality and craft matter | Furniture making, kitchens |
| Expert | A complex choice | Doctor, lawyer, technical advice |
| Problem → solution | The customer has a specific pain | "Back pain? Here's why" |
| Offer | A warm audience, a promotion | "This week only: free measurement" |

## Tips for each type

- **Review:** one video, one product, its 3–5 key features.
- **Before / after:** shoot from the same angle and light so the difference is obvious.
- **Testimonial:** a real customer in their own words — don't make them read a script. For collecting customer videos, see our [UGC content](/en/blog/ugc-content/) article.
- **Process:** fast editing, 10–20 seconds, rhythmic music.
- **Expert:** open with a question and explain in plain words.
- **Problem → solution:** the problem in the first second, a specific action at the end.
- **Offer:** terms, deadline and how to order — clear at a glance.

## Which suits you

Ask three questions:

1. Can you show the product's result? If yes: before/after and review.
2. Do customers think long before deciding? If yes: testimonial and expert.
3. Does the audience already know you? If yes: offer.

## How to test

Prepare 3–4 videos of different types at once and compare them on the same audience and budget, following the rules of [A/B testing](/en/blog/ab-testing-ad-creatives/). Next month, make several variations of the winning type.

## Conclusion

Rotating video types is the simplest way to stop creatives wearing out and keep cost per lead in check. Every video needs a strong opening — see our [hooks article](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).
