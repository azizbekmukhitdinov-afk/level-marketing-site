Ask Google a question and the first thing you often see isn't a list of websites but a short AI-generated answer — that's **AI Overviews**. **AI Mode** goes further: conversational search where people ask follow-up questions and the AI assembles an answer from several sources. In October 2025, Google announced that AI Mode is available in more than 200 countries and territories ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-expands-languages-locations/)). Here's what that means for a business website.

## What's changing

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| People see 10 links and click | People read the AI answer, sometimes without clicking |
| One short query | Long, conversational questions |
| The keyword is what matters | A precise answer and a trusted source are what matter |

The upshot: traffic from simple informational queries ("what is X") may fall, but being cited as a source in an AI answer builds trust and brand visibility.

## Which queries are most affected

- **Informational**: "what is SMM", "how to set prices" — the AI answer is often enough.
- **Comparisons**: "X or Y" — AI combines several sources.
- **Local and transactional**: "dentist near me", "custom kitchen" — maps, reviews and your website still decide here.

## 7 steps to appear in AI answers

1. **Lead with the answer**: on every page, a direct answer in one or two sentences, then the details.
2. **Q&A sections** built on your customers' real questions.
3. **Facts and figures**: sourced, precise and up to date.
4. **Author and experience**: who wrote it and what experience they have — Google's helpful-content guidance stresses this.
5. **A complete maps profile**: category, hours, photos, reviews — [add your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/).
6. **Technical basics**: a fast site, a mobile version, indexing in Search Console — [what is SEO](/en/blog/what-is-seo/).
7. **Mentions on other sites**: reviews, media, partners — AI draws on multiple sources.

## Are there special requirements for Google's AI features?

Google's documentation on AI features says no separate special optimisation is required to appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode — normal SEO fundamentals and helpful content apply ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)).

## How to measure traffic

- In Search Console, track impressions and clicks by page.
- If impressions rise while clicks fall, AI answers may be the reason.
- The key metric is leads: enquiries from your site and brand searches, recorded with their source in the CRM — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).

## What about ads?

Google has also started showing ads alongside AI answers, but availability depends on country and language. In Uzbekistan, search ads currently run in the classic results — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).

## Questions and answers

### Does AI Mode work in Uzbekistan?

Availability depends on country, language and account, and it keeps expanding. Check yourself whether Google shows an "AI Mode" tab.

### Is SEO dead?

No, it's changing. AI answers also rely on information from websites, so a quality site becomes a source for AI.

### Is a blog still worth it?

Yes — but not generic "what is X" texts; write content with experience, numbers and local context.

## Conclusion

Google AI Mode and AI Overviews may reduce traffic from simple informational queries, but with precise answers, facts, experience and a strong maps profile, a business can become a source in AI answers.
