"Uzum Market promo code" and "delivery promo code" are among the most searched queries in Uzbekistan. Shoppers hunt for promo codes, while businesses often see them only as a discount. In fact, a promo code's most valuable job is different: it shows which influencer, channel or ad brought the sale. Here's how to use promo codes properly in marketing.

## A promo code's 3 jobs

1. **Measurement**: a separate code for each channel or influencer, so you know where each sale came from.
2. **Incentive**: a reason to make a first purchase or come back.
3. **Segmentation**: different offers for different audiences.

## Measuring with promo codes: the main benefit

An influencer post or a Telegram channel post doesn't always show clicks clearly: people see the post, then visit the shop or call. A personal promo code closes that gap:

| Channel | Code (illustrative example) | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Influencer A | BLOGER10 | Sales from the influencer |
| Telegram channel B | KANAL10 | The effect of the channel post |
| Offline flyer | FLAYER10 | Results from print advertising — [measuring offline advertising](/en/blog/measure-offline-advertising/) |

That way, numbers rather than gut feeling decide whether to pay an influencer again — [influencer advertising](/en/blog/influencer-advertising-uzbekistan/).

## Types of promo codes

- **Percentage discount**: "−10%" — simple, but it eats margin.
- **Fixed amount**: "50,000 soum off orders over 500,000" — raises the average order value.
- **A gift**: "free delivery with this code", or a free product.
- **First purchase**: for new customers only.
- **A comeback code**: given after a purchase, for the next order — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

## Protecting your margin

- Set a minimum order value.
- Set a deadline of 7–14 days — an open-ended code becomes a permanent discount.
- Don't let codes stack with other promotions.
- Cap the number of uses per code.

Work out first whether a sale with the code is still profitable — [the discount trap](/en/blog/discount-trap/).

## Preventing abuse

- Public codes leak onto promo code websites — use personal, single-use codes for important channels.
- One customer, one use (by phone number).
- Keep the list of codes and results in one spreadsheet or the CRM.

## Calculating the results

For each code, track:

- the number of uses;
- revenue and average order value;
- the total discount given;
- the share of new customers and their repeat purchases;
- channel costs (influencer fee, ads).

Formula: (revenue − cost of goods − discount − channel costs) = channel profit. More in [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).

## The law

A promo code is a promotion, and its terms must be open: the deadline, minimum order and restrictions. Prices in soum, and no misleading "today only" — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## Questions and answers

### Must a promo code always be a discount?

No. A gift or free delivery often hurts margin less.

### How long should an influencer's code run?

Usually 7–14 days after the post goes live — most sales happen in that window.

### Can I measure without promo codes?

For website traffic, UTM tags are enough — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/). For offline and phone sales, promo codes are more convenient.

## Conclusion

A promo code is more than a discount: it's a tool for measuring influencers, channels and offline ads.
