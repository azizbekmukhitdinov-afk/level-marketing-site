Edits is Instagram's standalone video editing app, released free on iOS and Android in April 2025 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-is-rolling-out-edits-its-capcut-competitor-163045930.html)). Its main difference: exported videos carry no watermark, and the tools built for Reels are all in one place. For businesses, it's a way to produce Reels quickly and well on a phone. Here's how to use Edits.

## What Edits offers

- **Free** — all features were free at launch.
- **Watermark-free export** — so videos can go to other platforms too.
- **High-quality recording** inside the app.
- **Automatic captions** for people watching on mute.
- **Green screen, cutting, effects and AI tools** — background replacement, object cut-outs.
- **Music and sound** from Instagram's library.
- **Ideas and drafts** for planning.

Features keep changing — check the latest list in the app.

## A business workflow

1. **Script**: 3–5 sentences, with a hook in the first second — [Reels script templates](/en/blog/reels-script-templates/).
2. **Shooting**: on a phone, in good light — [shooting Reels on a phone](/en/blog/shooting-reels-on-a-phone/).
3. **Editing in Edits**: trim, set the pace, add captions.
4. **Sound and music**: music cleared for business accounts — [music for video ads](/en/blog/music-for-video-ads/).
5. **Export and post**: straight to Instagram, then the watermark-free file to YouTube Shorts and TikTok — [YouTube Shorts for business](/en/blog/youtube-shorts-for-business/).
6. **Test**: post important videos as trial Reels — [Instagram Trial Reels](/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels/).

## Tips for businesses

- **A brand template**: the same font, colours and caption style keep your page tidy — [visual identity for small business](/en/blog/visual-identity-for-small-business/).
- **A bank of drafts**: 5–10 videos from one shoot, saved as drafts.
- **Several people**: the social media manager edits, the founder just approves.
- **Careful with AI effects**: don't alter how the product looks — customers must see the truth.

## Questions and answers

### Does Edits work without Instagram?

The app works with an Instagram account, but you can post the exported file anywhere.

### Does it replace desktop editing?

For most Reels, yes. For complex ads and long videos, desktop software is more convenient.

### What improves quality more: the app or the shoot?

The shoot: light, sound and a steady camera. Editing can't rescue poor footage.

Instagram Edits lets you produce Reels on a phone quickly, for free and without watermarks.
