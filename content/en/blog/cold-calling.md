A cold call is a call to someone who doesn't know you yet. Most people dislike them: lots of rejection, and salespeople burn out fast. But in B2B sales — supply, services, wholesale — cold calling is still a working channel for landing a first meeting. The key is that the goal isn't to sell but to secure the **next step**. Here's how to cold call properly.

## The law first: time restrictions

In Uzbekistan, from 1 November 2026, advertising calls and messages over telecom networks are banned **from 18:00 to 09:00** — [SMS advertising rules](/en/blog/sms-advertising-rules-uzbekistan/). Schedule cold calls between 09:00 and 18:00 on working days. Follow the law on personal data — [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/).

## Preparing the list

A poor list is the biggest time-waster:

- **an ideal customer profile**: industry, size, region — [target audience and customer persona](/en/blog/target-audience-and-customer-persona/);
- **the decision-maker**: who to call — the director, procurement, the marketing lead;
- **research first**: the company website, Instagram, news — note one line.

## The first 30 seconds

Within 30 seconds, people decide whether to listen or say "not interested".

1. **Who** — your name and company, in one sentence.
2. **Why them** — "I saw your company opened 3 branches in Tashkent" (an illustrative example).
3. **Value** — one specific benefit.
4. **A question** — "Is this relevant for you?"

## Script structure

1. The opening (30 seconds).
2. 2–3 questions to uncover needs.
3. A short offer matched to the need.
4. The next step: a 15-minute meeting, a sample or a proposal — [commercial proposal template](/en/blog/commercial-proposal-template/).

A script is a route map, not word-for-word text — [sales script](/en/blog/sales-script/).

## Common objections

| Objection | How to respond |
|---|---|
| "I don't have time" | "I understand. Would 2 minutes tomorrow work, or shall I send the offer on Telegram?" |
| "We already have that" | "Great. What would you improve about your current solution?" |
| "It's expensive" | "Before I give a price — may I ask which result matters most to you?" |
| "Send me a proposal" | "Of course. May I ask 2 questions so it fits you exactly?" |

## Metrics

- Calls → conversations with decision-makers → meetings → deals.
- Conversion at each stage.
- Call recording reviews — [AI call analysis](/en/blog/ai-call-analysis/).

## Common mistakes

- Delivering the whole pitch in the first minute.
- Opening with "Is this a good time?" — "no" is an easy answer.
- Calling without researching the list.
- Not logging outcomes in the CRM.
- Stopping after one rejection — many deals happen on the 2nd or 3rd contact.

## Questions and answers

### Does cold calling work in B2C?

Less well, and with more legal risk. For B2C, inbound leads and a consent-based database work better.

### How many calls a day is normal?

It depends on list quality and call length. Track meetings booked, not calls made.

### What can replace cold calling?

Messages on LinkedIn and Telegram, events, B2B content and advertising — [B2B marketing](/en/blog/b2b-marketing-uzbekistan/).
