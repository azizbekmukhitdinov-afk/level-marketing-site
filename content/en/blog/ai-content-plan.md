The content plan is the hardest job for many SMM managers and owners: coming up with 20–30 ideas every month, spreading them across weeks, writing the copy. As a result, the plan often survives until the 10th, and then "what do we post today?" mode kicks in. AI speeds this up considerably — if you give it the right material. Here's how to build a month's content plan with AI in about an hour, step by step.

## Preparation: what to give the AI (15 minutes)

AI doesn't know your business. For a good plan it needs four things:

1. **Business and customer**: what you sell, the price range, who your customers are, what worries them.
2. **Questions and objections**: 10–20 real questions from sales or DMs — [content from customer objections](/en/blog/content-from-customer-objections/).
3. **The month's events**: holidays, promotions, new products — [marketing calendar 2027](/en/blog/marketing-calendar-2027-uzbekistan/).
4. **Capacity**: how many Reels you can film a month, who appears on camera, what material you have.

Write it as one text — it barely changes month to month; only the events get updated.

## The 60-minute process

### Step 1: ideas (10 minutes)

Prompt:

> Based on the information above, write 40 post ideas for one month. Assign each a role: attract, convince, objection, offer. One line per idea.

Ask for 40 — you need spares to choose from.

### Step 2: selection (10 minutes)

A human job: pick 20–24 of the 40. Criteria:

- can you actually film it;
- does it answer your customers' questions;
- role balance: roughly 30% attract, 30% convince, 20% objections, 20% offer.

More on roles — [why social media doesn't sell](/en/blog/why-social-media-doesnt-sell/).

### Step 3: the table (10 minutes)

Prompt:

> Spread the 22 chosen ideas over 4 weeks, 5–6 posts a week. Table columns: date, format (Reels / carousel / post / Stories), role, headline, main idea, call to action, material needed.

### Step 4: draft copy (20 minutes)

For each post, a short draft: the opening line (hook), the body, the call to action. For Reels, a 15–30 second script. Ready-made prompts — [marketing prompts](/en/blog/marketing-prompts/).

### Step 5: checking (10 minutes)

- Facts: any numbers, promises or features the AI made up?
- Language: does the Uzbek read naturally?
- Law: no "best", dollar prices or unprovable claims — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).
- Repetition: does the same idea appear several times?

## The AI plan and real content

AI speeds up the plan and the copy, but the content itself is real: the product, the team, the customers. The "material needed" column helps organise a shoot day: footage for the whole month in a single day. ## Next month, based on results

At the end of the month, give the AI the results (anonymised):

> Here are this month's posts and metrics: reach, saves, DM enquiries. Which roles and topics worked best? 5 takeaways for next month.

Then build next month's plan with those takeaways. That way the plan gets smarter every month.

## Common mistakes

- Asking for "30 post ideas" with no context — generic ideas that fit everyone.
- Including ideas you can't actually film.
- Publishing all the copy unchecked.
- Only attracting content — followers, but no sales.
- Starting from scratch every month without analysing results.

## Questions and answers

### Will an AI plan replace the SMM manager?

No. AI provides ideas and drafts; the SMM manager selects, organises filming, edits and analyses results.

### Does it work for a Telegram channel?

Yes. Change the format in the prompt: longer texts, fewer videos. Channel differences — [Telegram or Instagram](/en/blog/telegram-or-instagram-for-business/).

### How many posts a month?

It depends on your capacity. Consistency beats volume: 4–5 good posts a week beat 10 random ones.

### Which AI tool is best?

Many chat assistants can do this. A comparison — [ChatGPT or Gemini](/en/blog/chatgpt-or-gemini-for-business/).

## Conclusion

A content plan with AI is an hour's work: 15 minutes of preparation, then ideas, selection, a table, drafts and checking. The key is to give the AI real customer questions and your actual capacity, and to leave selection and checking to a human. The Level Marketing team builds content plans from the client's sales questions, and a Reel is ready within 48 hours.
