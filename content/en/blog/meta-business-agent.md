A customer asks "how much is it?" at 11 pm, the manager replies in the morning — and by then the customer has bought elsewhere. **Meta Business Agent** is built for exactly this problem: it's Meta's AI assistant that answers customers in chats on a business's behalf, recommends products and collects enquiries. Here's what it can do, what to prepare before switching it on and where you still need a person.

## What Meta Business Agent is

Meta announced the tool on 3 June 2026 at its Conversations 2026 event in London ([US News / Reuters](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-06-03/meta-launches-enterprise-focused-ai-business-agent-to-automate-daily-operations)). The announced capabilities:

- answering customer questions;
- recommending products from the catalogue;
- booking appointments;
- qualifying leads — working out who is actually ready to buy;
- completing sales.

The key point: **the business decides when a human takes over.** Meta said more than a million businesses had already used business AIs on WhatsApp and Messenger, and that the tool is now expanding globally to businesses of all sizes. Larger companies also get a separate Business Agent Platform for building custom agents.

Availability and pricing vary by country. Whether the tool is in your account and what it costs — check in Meta Business Suite or the WhatsApp Business app. Look there for Instagram Direct as well.

## How it differs from a plain auto-reply

Many businesses already have an auto-reply: "Hi, thanks for your message, we'll get back to you soon." That's a template. An AI agent understands the question and gives a real answer.

| | Auto-reply | AI agent |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | One pre-written message | Understands the question and builds an answer from your knowledge base |
| "How much is it?" | "A manager will reply" | Gives the catalogue price and asks a clarifying question |
| Booking | No | Offers free slots |
| Risk of error | Low | Real — it can give wrong information |
| Preparation | 10 minutes | Knowledge base, catalogue, rules |

How to set up auto-replies is covered in [Automated responses in Meta Business Suite](/en/blog/meta-business-suite-automated-responses/). The equivalent for Telegram is [a Telegram AI assistant](/en/blog/telegram-ai-assistant/).

## Who benefits, and who doesn't need it yet

An AI agent pays off where dozens of identical questions arrive every day. For example: a furniture store in Tashkent (sizes, colours, delivery times), a dental clinic (prices, appointment times), a training centre (course schedule, fees), a hotel (free rooms, check-in time).

You may not need it yet if:

- you get a few messages a day and the manager answers quickly;
- every order is custom and the price is only given after a measurement;
- your customers mostly write on Telegram — then think about a Telegram solution before Meta's channels.

To see which channel matters, count for one week how many enquiries came through Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, Telegram and the phone.

## Before launch: 5 preparation steps

An AI agent only works with the information you give it. With an empty knowledge base it either won't answer or will make something up. So preparation is most of the job.

### 1. A Q&A knowledge base

Open your managers' chats from the last one or two months and list the 20–30 most frequent questions. Write a short, precise answer to each. Usually these are price, delivery, payment methods, instalments, warranty, address and opening hours. How to build a good answer to "How much?" is covered in [a separate article](/en/blog/replying-to-how-much/).

### 2. The catalogue

For the agent to recommend products, the catalogue must be clean: every item has a name, price, photo, key features and stock status. If an old price or a sold-out product stays in the catalogue, the agent will happily offer it.

### 3. A handover rule

Write down in advance when a chat moves to a manager (there's a list in the next section). And set how fast the manager must reply to a handed-over chat — otherwise the AI answers instantly and then the customer waits anyway.

### 4. Tone and language

What language and tone does the agent use? Polite address, short sentences, no emoji overload. Check the quality of answers in Uzbek and Russian separately: in Uzbek, AI sometimes produces odd phrasing.

### 5. Where the lead goes

The name, phone number and request the agent collects must land in a CRM, or at least a spreadsheet. Otherwise the enquiry stays in the chat and gets lost. On choosing a CRM — [What a CRM is and which one suits a small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/).

## When a human should step in

Meta leaves the decision to the business, so you write the rule. Hand the chat to a manager when:

- the customer explicitly asks to talk to a person;
- there's a complaint, a return or a warranty issue;
- the request involves a custom price, a discount or instalment terms;
- it's a large order or a corporate customer;
- the agent has failed to find an answer twice;
- the customer is irritated or keeps repeating the same question;
- medical, legal or financial advice is needed.

> AI answers fast, but decisions and promises come from a person.

## Risks and how to reduce them

| Risk | What it looks like | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong price or information | The agent quotes an old price | Update the catalogue weekly |
| Over-promising | "We'll deliver tomorrow" — actually 5 days | Write exact lead times, forbid promises |
| Making things up | "Answers" a question that isn't in the base | Rule: "if I don't know, I hand over" |
| Language quality | Uzbek answers sound like a translation | Read 10–20 chats a day in the first week |
| Personal data | The customer types a passport or card number | Never ask for such data in chat |

Which tasks shouldn't go to AI at all is covered in [What not to delegate to AI](/en/blog/what-not-to-delegate-to-ai/).

## The first 30 days: a rollout plan

Switching the agent on in a day and forgetting about it is the riskiest route. Bring it in gradually, like a new hire.

**Week 1: preparation.** Write the knowledge base, clean up the catalogue, set the handover rule and the managers' response time. Appoint one owner — the agent's "supervisor".

**Week 2: limited test.** Turn the agent on in one channel and only outside working hours. During the day, managers reply as usual. Each morning the owner reads the overnight chats and logs mistakes.

**Week 3: filling the gaps.** Collect the questions the agent couldn't answer and handed over. Write an answer for each and add it to the base. Find why wrong answers happened: an outdated base, a catalogue error or an unclear rule.

**Week 4: expand or stop.** Compare the metrics with the period before the test. If results are good, extend to daytime hours and other channels. If complaints went up, stop and rework the base.

This plan also reassures managers: they see the AI isn't replacing them but taking over the night-time and repetitive questions.

## How to measure the result

In the first month after launch, track these metrics:

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Time to first reply | How long the customer waits |
| Share of chats handed to a human | How complete the knowledge base is |
| Manager reply time after handover | Whether the team stays fast after the AI |
| Share of chats that became an enquiry (phone, order) | Whether the agent helps sales |
| Number of complaints | Whether the agent annoys customers |

> **Illustrative example.** A training centre gets 40 messages a day. In the first week the agent closes half of them itself; the rest go to a manager. After reviewing the handed-over questions and adding them to the base, the share of chats going to a human falls. The numbers are for illustration only.

## Questions and answers

### Does Meta Business Agent work in Uzbekistan?

Meta has said it is expanding globally, but availability depends on the country. Check Meta Business Suite and the WhatsApp Business app: if the section appears, you can test it.

### How much does it cost?

Pricing may depend on the country and the volume of use. Check the exact terms in Meta's information inside your account, and don't rely on rough figures from other people's posts.

### Will AI fully replace a manager?

No. It handles repetitive questions and collects enquiries. Complex questions, complaints, custom pricing and the final agreement are the manager's job. You set the handover rule.

### Can I do the same on Telegram?

Meta Business Agent works in Meta's channels. For Telegram there are other tools — a bot or an AI assistant connected through Telegram Business.

## Conclusion

Meta Business Agent is an AI that answers customers in chats, recommends products, books appointments and qualifies leads. It's useful for businesses with a steady flow of identical questions, but it only works with the knowledge base you give it. Before launch, prepare the Q&A base, a clean catalogue, a handover rule and a CRM connection — and in the first month, read the chats every day.
