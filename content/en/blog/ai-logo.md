"AI logo generator" is a popular search among business owners. Type a few words and dozens of options appear — no need, it seems, to pay a designer and wait weeks. Sometimes that really is enough. In other cases, an AI logo costs you later: similar marks, registration problems, details that vanish at small sizes. Here's when an AI logo is the right choice.

## What a logo is for

A logo isn't the brand — it's one of the brand's marks. It:

- sets you apart from others;
- looks the same everywhere: signage, packaging, profile picture, ads;
- becomes familiar over time.

Brand vs logo — [what is a brand](/en/blog/what-is-a-brand/). Other parts of visual identity — [visual identity for small business](/en/blog/visual-identity-for-small-business/).

## What AI does well for logos

- **Ideas**: 30–50 directions — symbols, font styles, colours. Great material for a designer brief.
- **Quick prototypes**: testing a business idea, a test page or a temporary mark for the first months.
- **Showing options**: asking a partner or team which direction they prefer.

## 5 risks of an AI logo

### 1. Similarity

AI was trained on millions of existing images. The result may resemble a well-known brand's mark — and you may not notice. That can lead to a legal dispute or a forced rebrand.

### 2. Copyright

According to a legal analysis on Anhor.uz, under Uzbekistan's legislation, material created by AI without an individual's creative activity is not an object of copyright ([Anhor.uz](https://anhor.uz/ru/pravovoy-razbor/ai-rights)). In practice, that means a mark created purely by AI may be harder to protect. If you plan to register the logo as a trademark, consult a specialist first.

### 3. The tool's terms

Every AI tool has its own terms for commercial use. Read them before using the logo.

### 4. Technical quality

AI usually produces a raster image. A logo needs to be vector so it can scale up for signage and down for a favicon. Many AI logos have tiny details, random lines and "almost-letters" that disappear at small sizes.

### 5. Text and letters

AI often draws letters wrong: an extra stroke, a malformed shape. It struggles especially with the Uzbek letters o‘ and g‘.

## Testing a logo: 7 checks

| Test | How to check |
|---|---|
| Small size | Is it legible at 32×32 px (favicon)? |
| One colour | Does it work in black only? |
| Dark background | Does it disappear on black? |
| Signage | Is it recognisable from 20 metres away? |
| Profile picture | Does it get cropped in a circle? |
| Similarity | Does a reverse image search find similar marks? |
| With the name | Does it suit the name and read easily? |

Reverse image search (Google Lens, Yandex Images) is a simple but incomplete similarity check. Trademark registration needs a professional search.

## When AI is enough and when you need a designer

| Situation | AI logo | Designer |
|---|---|---|
| Testing an idea, a test page | Enough | Not essential |
| First 1–3 months, small budget | As a temporary option | Later |
| Signage, packaging, a long-term brand | Only as an idea | Needed |
| Planning trademark registration | Risky | Needed |
| Premium segment | Risky | Needed — [premium segment marketing](/en/blog/premium-segment-marketing/) |

The best combination: collect 30–50 ideas with AI and give your favourite 3–5 to a designer as a brief. That saves the designer's time and your money.

## Common mistakes

- Putting the first AI option straight onto your signage.
- Not checking for similarity.
- Using a raster file as the logo — it blurs when enlarged.
- Too many details and gradients.
- Changing the logo every month — recognition is lost.

## Questions and answers

### Can I use an AI logo for free?

It depends on the tool's terms. Check that commercial use is allowed.

### How do I turn an AI logo into a vector?

There are auto-vectorising tools, but results often need manual clean-up. That's a designer's job.

### How many options do I need?

Three to five directions are enough for a brief. Too many options make the decision harder.

### Must I register my logo?

It's not mandatory, but it protects a long-term brand. Ask an intellectual property specialist about the procedure and terms.

## Conclusion

AI gives you logo ideas and quick prototypes — that's useful. But for a long-term brand, similarity, rights and technical quality call for a designer and a professional check.
