Since 15 October 2026, the sign above the door of a shop, café or salon in Uzbekistan is no longer treated as outdoor advertising, and it does not need an advertising site passport. A promo banner on the wall, a lightbox by the road or a large rooftop structure, however, are still outdoor advertising. This article explains what the new resolution actually says, where the line is, and what a business owner should check in a single day.

> This is not legal advice. We explain the resolution in plain language. Check the specific rules in your city with the khokimiyat or a lawyer.

## What changed: Resolution 563 in brief

On 13 October 2026, the Cabinet of Ministers adopted Resolution No. 563. It took effect on 15 October and amended Resolution No. 104 of 2020 on regulating the outdoor advertising market ([lex.uz](https://lex.uz/docs/8537985)). The key changes:

| What | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Name and type of business on the building the business occupies (the sign) | Many cities required a passport and fees, as for advertising | Not an outdoor advertising object, no passport needed — size and number do not matter |
| Brand and services on a company's own vehicles | An advertising site passport was required | A notice via my.gov.uz; the passport is generated automatically and free of charge |
| Old passport issued for a sign | Valid, fees continue | Can be voluntarily cancelled via the portal; the data goes to the tax authority automatically |
| Temporary ads on a construction site | — | Until construction is completed |
| Holiday and event ads on structures | — | Up to 30 calendar days |
| Removal of an illegal structure | Often without warning | Posted online at least 2 days ahead, written notice to the owner, weekdays 09:00–18:00, official report |

UzDaily and Anhor.uz covered the changes in detail ([UzDaily](https://www.uzdaily.uz/uz/ozbekiston-tashqi-reklamani-joylashtirish-qoidalarini-soddalashtirdi/), [Anhor.uz](https://anhor.uz/news/uzbekistan-672/)). The review followed a campaign of mass sign removals in Tashkent, and the new procedure is meant to reduce that risk.

## When a sign is not advertising — and when it is

The resolution's test is simple: if a business **tells people about itself on the building it occupies**, that is a sign. Advertising is information that draws attention to and promotes a product or service on the market. In practice, the line looks like this:

| Situation | Sign (no passport) | Outdoor ad (passport needed) |
|---|---|---|
| Above the entrance: "Furniture World — furniture store" | Yes | — |
| Opening hours, trade name, trademark by the door | Yes | — |
| On the same façade: "30% off sofas — November only" banner | — | Yes: it is a promotion, so an ad |
| On a neighbouring building or by the road: "Furniture World in 200 m →" | — | Yes: a place you do not occupy |
| Rooftop structure, pole lightbox, pylon | — | Yes |
| Logo and phone number on a company car | — | Simplified procedure: a notice |

The store name is an illustrative example. One question decides it: **does the text help people find you, or push them to buy?** "Pharmacy", "Barbershop", "Furniture store" help people find you. "Lowest prices", "0% instalments", "Sale" push them to buy — so that is advertising.

A subtle trap is combining the sign and a promotion on one panel. Add "discounts" under your name and the whole structure may be treated as advertising. Keep the sign clean and put promotions in the shop window or inside.

## Passport for outdoor advertising: when and how

If your structure falls in the "advertising" column, the permit regime still applies. An advertising site passport on a private building is processed via my.gov.uz. In May 2026, Kursiv Uzbekistan described the Tashkent procedure as follows: a one-off review fee of 90% of the base calculation amount, a 15-working-day review, and — if the building belongs to someone else — the owner's consent and a lease agreement ([Kursiv](http://uz.kursiv.media/uz/2026-05-04/tadbirkorlar-uchun-tashqi-reklama-pasportini-olish-tartibi-belgilandi/)).

Steps:

1. **Define the location.** Your own building, a lease, the ground floor of an apartment block. If you rent, get the owner's written consent.
2. **Prepare a sketch of the structure:** size, position, a photo of the façade. Work with a designer so it fits the neighbours on the same façade — Tashkent has a design code.
3. **Apply on my.gov.uz** and pay the fee. If issues are found, you can resubmit.
4. **Passport first, installation second.** Installing first and filing later is the shortest route to removal.
5. **Put the monthly fees in your calendar.** Arrears can be grounds for cancelling the passport.

The Tashkent design code is a separate matter: the khokimiyat must discuss it with businesses and the media by 10 December 2026, and changes take effect three months after official publication. If you are ordering a large structure now, follow the outcome of that discussion.

## Vehicle advertising: a notice is now enough

A legal entity that wants to put its trademark, type of business, products and services on its own vehicles (delivery van, service car, driving-school car) now only needs to send an electronic notice via my.gov.uz. The passport is generated in the system automatically and free of charge.

Keep in mind:

- This applies to **your own** vehicles. Ads on taxis or another company's cars are a different case.
- If false data is submitted or someone else's trademark is used unlawfully, the passport is cancelled.
- The text on a vehicle is still subject to advertising rules: avoid unproven "the best" claims and prices in dollars. More in [Uzbekistan's Advertising Law](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

A delivery van is a free billboard that drives around the city all day. Now that the paperwork is simpler, there is little reason not to use it.

## If they plan to remove your structure: your rights

Under the new procedure, before a structure found to be illegal is removed:

- the khokimiyat or authorised body posts the date and time on its website **at least two days in advance**;
- the owner receives **written notice**;
- the work is done only **on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00** by a contractor chosen through an open tender;
- an **official report** is drawn up with the structure's condition, the reason, photos and the storage address, and entered into the "Outdoor Advertising" electronic monitoring system. If the owner is unknown or refuses to sign, the mahalla chairperson may sign it.

Practical tip: if you receive a notice, check straight away whether you have a sign or an ad. If it only shows your name and type of business, it has not needed a passport since 15 October — reply in writing, citing the resolution's number and date.

## Sign text: language and content

Even without a passport, some requirements remain:

- **Language.** Signs, notices and price tags are issued in the state language and may be duplicated in other languages (Law on the State Language, Article 20). A Russian or English name goes next to the Uzbek version.
- **No advertising.** Signs and other information inscriptions may not carry ads or commercial offers — otherwise they become advertising.
- **Name.** Your sign is the first touchpoint with your brand. How to choose a name: [How to Name a Business](/en/blog/how-to-name-a-business/).
- **Visual identity.** When the sign repeats the logo, colours and font of your Instagram, people recognise you both on the street and in their feed. The minimum kit: [Visual Identity for a Small Business](/en/blog/visual-identity-for-small-business/).

The sign ties into online marketing too: when the name and façade photo on your Google and Yandex Maps listing match the sign on the street, customers find the entrance easily. How to set that up: [Add Your Business to Maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/).

## Questions and answers

### I got a passport for my sign. What now?

If it only shows your name and type of business, you can voluntarily cancel the passport via my.gov.uz. Once the electronic application is filed, it is cancelled automatically and the data goes to the tax authority. Check with your accountant first that there are no outstanding fees.

### Is there a limit on sign size or number?

Under the resolution, the size and number of trade names and trademarks do not turn a sign into an ad. A city design code may still set requirements for appearance — the Tashkent code is under discussion now.

### Is a product photo in the shop window advertising?

During the discussion of the draft resolution, it was stated that product information in the window of the same building is not advertising. Check the exact wording of the final text on lex.uz; the safer route is to treat prices and promotions in the window as advertising (state language, prices in soums).

### Do I need the owner's consent for a sign on a rented unit?

The resolution removed the passport, not your relationship with the owner. If the lease does not mention use of the façade, get written consent.

### How long can holiday banners stay up?

Holiday and event ads on structures may stay up for up to 30 calendar days. If you hang a New Year banner in early December, plan to take it down in early January.

## Conclusion

Since 15 October 2026, the line between a sign and an ad is clear: your name and type of business on your own building need no passport; promotions and structures elsewhere do. Set aside one day: list everything on your façade, strip ads from your sign, cancel an unnecessary passport and send notices for company vehicles.
