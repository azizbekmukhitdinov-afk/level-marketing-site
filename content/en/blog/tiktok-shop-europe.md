**TikTok Shop in Europe** reached four more countries in summer 2026, and selling directly inside videos now works in around ten European markets. A manufacturer from Uzbekistan can't open a shop there on its own, but there is a route through partners. This article covers where things stand, three export models, and the lessons local businesses can take for selling at home.

## TikTok Shop in Europe: where it runs

TikTok Shop is a store inside TikTok: a person watches a video or a live stream, taps the product and buys without leaving the app. In Europe it opened in stages:

| Period | Countries |
|---|---|
| First | United Kingdom |
| Then | Spain, Ireland |
| From 31.03.2025 | Germany, France, Italy |
| From 15.06.2026 | Austria, Belgium, Netherlands, Poland |

In June 2026 TikTok also launched a tool called "Sell Across Europe": it makes it easier for a seller already active in one EU market to expand to others ([eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/tiktok-shop-europe-expansion-amid-us-uncertainty), [Novadata](https://novadata.io/resources/news/tiktok-shop-europe-4-countries-sell-across-europe-june-2026)). Including the UK, that makes around ten European markets. Further countries have not been officially confirmed.

## Can you sell directly from Uzbekistan?

Short answer: no. TikTok Shop is not available to sellers based in Uzbekistan. In European markets, sellers are generally required to have a company registered in that country or in the EU, a local warehouse and a returns address. Exact requirements vary by country and category — check them in TikTok Shop Seller Center; the conditions in this article are general guidance only.

Europe also has its own product requirements: safety, labelling, CE marking for some categories, and separate rules for food and cosmetics. Clarify these in advance with your partner or a customs broker.

So the real question is not "how do I register on TikTok Shop" but "who in Europe will sell my product through TikTok Shop".

## Three export models

| Model | What you do | What the partner does | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Importer or distributor | Product, packaging, content materials | Company, warehouse, TikTok Shop store, sales | Price and brand controlled by the partner |
| 2. Your own EU company + 3PL warehouse | Everything: company, tax, store, marketing | Warehousing and delivery (3PL) | High cost and responsibility |
| 3. Partnering with an existing online seller | Supply wholesale | Sells in its own store | You're only a supplier |

For most manufacturers the first model is the most realistic start: you can test the market with a small batch. The second is the next step once sales are proven and volumes are large.

Where do you find a partner? International trade shows, industry associations, the Uzbek diaspora in Europe and LinkedIn. What to prepare for a trade show and how to follow up with contacts is covered in [our article on exhibiting at a trade show](/en/blog/exhibiting-at-a-trade-show/).

## Which products fit

General signs of a product that sells well on TikTok Shop:

- **easy to show on video** — the result, texture or use is visible;
- **quick decision** — mid-range price, no long deliberation;
- **light and unbreakable** — cheaper logistics, fewer returns;
- **has a story** — handmade, local raw materials, traditional techniques.

Illustrative examples for Uzbek manufacturers: home textiles (towels, bed linen, silk), ceramics and tableware, accessories with national patterns, dried fruit and nuts. The last one is food, so the rules are stricter — sort out certification first.

Weaker fits: heavy and bulky goods (furniture, building materials), clothing where exact sizing matters (many returns), medical products and anything that needs special permits.

## What to agree with a partner: checklist

Before signing, get clear answers to these:

- who is the seller of record on TikTok Shop — in whose name the sales happen;
- who sets the retail price, and is there a minimum price;
- what happens to returns and who covers the cost;
- how much stock sits in the warehouse and when it's replenished;
- who owns the rights to videos and photos, and how the brand name and logo are used;
- who pays commission to affiliate creators;
- what report you receive each month: sales, returns, reviews;
- how the contract ends and what happens to remaining stock.

This list is part of a manufacturer's overall marketing system. How to work with the dealer, the shelf and the end buyer is explained in [our article on marketing for manufacturers](/en/blog/marketing-for-manufacturers/) — a European partner is, in effect, a new type of dealer.

## Content: TikTok Shop sells through video

On TikTok Shop a store listing is not enough — sales come from videos and live streams. This is where a manufacturer can help the partner a lot.

**Content from production.** Videos from the workshop: how silk is woven, how ceramics are fired, how goods are packed. For a European buyer this builds trust and tells a story.

**Creators in the local language.** In Germany you need a creator who speaks German, in Poland one who speaks Polish. On TikTok Shop, creators often earn a commission on sales. The seller in Europe usually organises these partnerships.

**Live streams.** Showing the product live and answering questions is one of TikTok Shop's strengths. How to run live selling is covered in [our article on live selling](/en/blog/live-selling/).

> **Illustrative example.** A silk manufacturer from Margilan agreed with a distributor in Germany: every month it sends 10–15 raw videos from the workshop, and the distributor edits them in German and passes them to creators. The figures are illustrative.

## Lessons for local social commerce

TikTok Shop isn't available in Uzbekistan, but its logic works here too. Four lessons for businesses selling through Instagram, Telegram and marketplaces:

1. **Video beats a listing.** A short video showing the product in use brings more questions and orders than a plain photo.
2. **Keep the path to purchase short.** The more steps between video and order, the more people drop off. A button, link or lead form — one tap.
3. **Pay creators for results.** Instead of one-off ads, use a promo code and a share of sales — more transparent for both sides.
4. **Live streams are a sales channel.** Not entertainment, but a stream where the product is shown and questions are answered.

## The first 90 days: a plan

| Period | What you do |
|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Pick 2–3 products, check European requirements and certification, build a partner list |
| Days 31–60 | Negotiate with 3–5 partners, send samples, agree price and terms |
| Days 61–90 | Small test batch, a content pack from the workshop, first report and conclusions |

The timeline is illustrative — certification and logistics sometimes take longer. The key is to treat the first batch as a test: the goal isn't big sales but finding out whether European buyers like the product.

## Common mistakes

- **Starting with a large batch.** The market is untested, and goods sit in the warehouse.
- **Postponing compliance.** A shipment gets stuck at the border over labelling or certification.
- **Working without a contract.** Verbal agreements on price, returns and brand rights turn into disputes later.
- **Leaving content to the partner.** Without workshop videos, the partner can't set your product apart.
- **Competing on price alone.** In European markets, story and quality stand out, not cheapness.

## Questions and answers

### Can an Uzbek company open its own TikTok Shop store?

No, TikTok Shop is not available to sellers in Uzbekistan. Selling in Europe generally requires a local or EU-registered company, a warehouse and a returns address. Check the exact rules in TikTok Shop Seller Center.

### Which country should we start with?

It depends on the product and the partner. Big markets like Germany, France and Italy have many buyers but strong competition. It makes sense to start where your partner is based and in the language they can produce content in. The "Sell Across Europe" tool makes expanding to other countries easier later.

### Which countries will TikTok Shop launch in next?

No new countries have been officially announced. Follow TikTok's news and Seller Center, and don't build plans on guesses.

### If we're not going to Europe, does this matter to us?

Not directly, but the lessons are useful: showing products on video, a short path to purchase, paying creators for results and sales-focused live streams all work on Instagram, Telegram and local marketplaces too.

TikTok Shop in Europe runs in around ten markets, with Austria, Belgium, the Netherlands and Poland added in June 2026. A manufacturer from Uzbekistan can't open a store there directly, but can sell through an importer, distributor or online seller in Europe. Choose a product that works on video, clarify requirements early, agree terms with your partner in writing and prepare content from your workshop.
