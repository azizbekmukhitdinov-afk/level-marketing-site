**Client:** iTOPS — Apple gadget store, one of the wholesale leaders in its industry.

**Partnership:** 3 months.

## What we did

- Built a content strategy and Reels formats for the page.
- Bet on lively, face-to-camera videos — engaging, easy-to-follow content about gadgets.

## Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Followers | **1,000 from zero** |
| Organic views | **6M and 4M** (top Reels) |

## How to apply this approach

1. Choose a format with a face: a regular presenter becomes familiar to the audience.
2. Explain the product in an interesting, clear way: comparisons, Q&A, tests.
3. Analyse your most-viewed Reels and repeat their format.
4. Later, use the organic reach as an audience for retargeting and paid ads.

## Who this suits

Gadgets, electronics and any product that can be explained in an engaging way. More in [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/) and [How the Instagram algorithm works](/en/blog/how-the-instagram-algorithm-works/).

## Questions and answers

### Does organic Reels reach turn into sales?

Not by itself. Reach builds brand awareness and gathers an audience, but sales need a profile, a clear offer and proper lead handling.

## Takeaway

Reels in the right format reach millions of people even without an ad budget. That is a strong foundation for brand awareness and cheaper paid ads later.
