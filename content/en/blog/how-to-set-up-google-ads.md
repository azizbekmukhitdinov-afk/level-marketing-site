Google search ads appear exactly when someone needs something: "custom kitchen", "dentist Chilanzar". That's why search ads often deliver the warmest leads. But a poorly set-up campaign spends its budget on irrelevant searches. Here's how to set up a Google Ads search campaign step by step. On choosing a channel — [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

## Step 1: the account

- Sign up at ads.google.com with the company's Google account.
- Choose **Expert mode** rather than the simplified mode — it gives you full control.
- Check billing details and currency.

## Step 2: conversion tracking first

Without conversions, Google doesn't know what to optimise for:

- install the Google tag on your website;
- define conversion actions: form submissions, call buttons, clicks to Telegram;
- store leads in the CRM with their source — [ad leads vs CRM mismatch](/en/blog/ad-leads-vs-crm-mismatch/).

## Step 3: the campaign

- **Type**: Search.
- **Location**: a city or districts, with the "people in or regularly in your locations" setting.
- **Language**: account for Uzbek and Russian searches — [Uzbek search: Latin and Cyrillic](/en/blog/uzbek-search-latin-cyrillic/).
- **Budget**: daily; start small and grow with results.
- **Bidding**: start with maximise clicks or manual bidding, then switch to maximise conversions once conversions build up.

## Step 4: keywords

| Match type | Written as | What it shows for |
|---|---|---|
| Broad | kitchen furniture | Various searches with related meaning |
| Phrase | "kitchen furniture" | Searches that include this meaning |
| Exact | [kitchen furniture] | This meaning or very close to it |

To start: phrase and exact match with clear-intent words ("order", "price", "Tashkent", district names). One topic per ad group.

## Step 5: negative keywords

Block irrelevant searches upfront: "free", "job", "vacancy", "download", "DIY", "course" (if you don't sell courses). Review the search terms report weekly and add new negatives.

## Step 6: ads

A responsive search ad can have up to 15 headlines (up to 30 characters each) and up to 4 descriptions (up to 90 characters):

- headlines: the keyword, the offer, your difference, a call to action;
- any price in soum — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/);
- assets: sitelinks, calls, callouts.

The ad and the landing page must promise the same thing — [a landing page that converts](/en/blog/landing-page-that-converts/).

## Step 7: the first week

- Once a day: spend, clicks, conversions.
- Search terms report → negative keywords.
- Pause keywords that don't perform.
- First conclusions after 2–4 weeks.

## Questions and answers

### What budget do I need?

It depends on your industry and competition. Start with a small test budget and watch cost per lead — [search ads in Tashkent](/en/blog/search-ads-tashkent/).

### Does Google Ads work without a website?

Partly, with call ads — but a website or landing page works far better.

### Do I need Yandex too?

For Russian-language searches, Yandex adds extra reach — [Yandex Direct in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/yandex-direct-in-uzbekistan/).
