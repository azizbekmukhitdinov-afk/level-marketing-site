Pet owners are among the most loyal customers: once they trust a vet, they come back for years and recommend them to friends. But the first choice usually happens in an emergency: "My cat is ill — where do I go right now?". Marketing for a vet clinic or pet shop should be built around those two situations: emergencies and ongoing care.

![Emergencies: being found](/img/blog/illus/veterinariya-marketing-1.jpg 1200x675)

## Emergencies: being found

When a pet falls ill, the owner searches the map:

- a complete profile on Google Maps, Yandex Maps and 2GIS — [add your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/);
- **hours and on-call times** — if you're open 24 hours or in the evenings, say so clearly;
- someone always answers the phone — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/);
- a list of services: vaccinations, surgery, diagnostics, home visits.

## Trust: what owners look at

- **Who the vet is**: experience, specialism, photos and video — [Reels for experts](/en/blog/reels-for-experts/).
- **Inside the clinic**: cleanliness, equipment, the waiting area.
- **Clear prices**: "from" prices for core services — [should you show the price in your ads](/en/blog/should-you-show-price-in-ads/).
- **Reviews**: owners' reviews and your replies — [online reputation and reviews](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/).

## Content: a niche people love

Pet content naturally gets lots of views and shares:

- "A day at the clinic" — animals and the vet (with owners' consent);
- useful tips: vaccination schedules, winter care, nutrition;
- "When to see a vet urgently: 5 signs" — content people save;
- before/after stories of treated pets.

![Prevention: steady income](/img/blog/illus/veterinariya-marketing-2.jpg 1200x675)

## Prevention: steady income

Vaccinations, parasite treatments and check-ups are recurring services. The system:

- a record for every pet in the CRM: species, age, vaccination dates — [CRM for small business](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/);
- a reminder (with consent): "Bobik's vaccination is due in a week";
- an annual prevention package.

## For pet shops

- Food and litter are regular purchases: offer delivery and subscriptions.
- Link to vet advice: "The food your vet recommended is in our shop."
- Fast ordering via Telegram — [Telegram Business features](/en/blog/telegram-business-features/).

![Advertising](/img/blog/illus/veterinariya-marketing-3.jpg 1200x675)

## Advertising

- Instagram ads targeted within 3–5 km of the clinic.
- Search ads: "vet Yunusabad", "cat vaccination price".
- Retargeting people who visited your site or profile.

## Questions and answers

### Are there limits on advertising pet treatment?

Be careful with medical promises: claims like "100% cure" may be misleading.

### Should we offer a free check-up?

A short free first check-up or consultation attracts new clients — but work out the cost.

### How should we ask for reviews?

After a successful treatment, when the owner is happy.
