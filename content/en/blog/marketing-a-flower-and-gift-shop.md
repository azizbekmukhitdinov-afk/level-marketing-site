A flower and gift shop is one of the most "urgent" businesses there is. Customers usually search when they need something "tonight" or "in an hour": a birthday, a date, a visit, a holiday. So flower shop marketing depends on three things: being found at the right moment, trust in the bouquet and fast delivery. This article sets out a system for steady orders all year round for a flower or gift shop in Tashkent.

![What makes the flower business different](/img/blog/illus/gul-sovga-dokoni-marketing-1.jpg 1200x675)

## What makes the flower business different

| Feature | What it means for marketing |
|---|---|
| Urgent purchases | Maps and fast replies matter most |
| Holiday peaks | Plan ads and stock in advance |
| The product is an emotion | Photos and videos of real bouquets |
| Someone else receives the gift | Delivery quality and punctuality |
| Recurring dates | Repeat orders through reminders |

## Step 1. Maps and search

"Flower shop near me" and "flower delivery Chilanzar" are mostly map searches. Your listing needs:

- an exact category: florist, flower delivery, gifts;
- opening hours — including extended holiday hours;
- photos of real bouquets with prices;
- phone, Telegram and delivery area;
- a reply to every review.

More in [adding your business to maps](/en/blog/add-your-business-to-maps/) and [online reputation and reviews](/en/blog/online-reputation-and-reviews/).

## Step 2. Instagram — catalogue and mood

Flower buyers choose with their eyes. On the profile:

- **Real bouquets** — each with price and size. Stock photos or other shops' pictures destroy trust.
- **Price highlights** — "under 200k", "200–500k", "premium".
- **The making** — a florist assembling a bouquet in a 15-second Reel.
- **Delivery** — the moment a bouquet is handed over (with consent).
- **Seasonal flowers** — what has just arrived.

Reels ideas — [Reels script templates](/en/blog/reels-script-templates/); shooting on a phone — [shooting Reels on a phone](/en/blog/shooting-reels-on-a-phone/).

## Step 3. Ordering in one minute

An urgent customer won't wait through a long chat. Keep ordering simple:

1. **One main channel** — a Telegram bot or the phone; the same one on the profile, maps and ads.
2. **Ready options** — 5–10 bouquets by price; the customer picks a number.
3. **The key questions** — address, time, recipient's phone, card message.
4. **Payment** — online or on delivery.
5. **Order confirmation** — with an estimated delivery time.

How a Telegram bot helps — [Telegram bot for leads](/en/blog/telegram-bot-for-leads/); reply speed — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).

## Step 4. A photo of the bouquet before dispatch

The biggest fear with online flower orders is "the bouquet won't look like the photo". A simple fix: once the order is assembled, send the customer a photo of the actual bouquet before it leaves. It sharply raises trust and often gets mentioned in reviews. After delivery — a "delivered" message.

## Step 5. The holiday calendar

A flower shop's year is a series of peaks: 8 March, Navruz, 1 September and graduations, Teachers' Day (1 October), New Year, plus wedding season. Prepare for each peak 3–4 weeks ahead:

| When | What to do |
|---|---|
| 4 weeks before | Range and prices, agreements with suppliers |
| 3 weeks before | Open pre-orders, start ads |
| 1–2 weeks before | Corporate orders, reminder messages |
| The day itself | Extended hours, extra couriers |
| Afterwards | Reviews and photos, a database for next year |

Pre-orders are the most profitable tool: you can plan stock and ease the pressure on the day. Holiday budgets — [December ad budget](/en/blog/december-ad-budget/); sales team during holidays — [sales team during holidays](/en/blog/sales-team-during-holidays/).

## Step 6. Paid ads

- **Radius:** your delivery area;
- **Timing:** afternoons and evenings, when people think about gifts; all day before a holiday;
- **Creative:** a real bouquet, the price and delivery time: "Bouquet + delivery — illustrative example: from 250,000 UZS, within 90 minutes";
- **Objective:** messages or a click to the Telegram bot;
- **Retargeting:** a pre-holiday reminder for profile visitors.

Show prices in UZS in ads. Setup — [Instagram ads](/en/blog/how-to-set-up-instagram-ads/).

## Step 7. Corporate clients

Companies regularly congratulate staff and partners: birthdays, holidays, office decoration. Give them a separate offer: a monthly contract, invoices, a calendar of dates, guaranteed delivery. One corporate client places dozens of orders a year. On the B2B approach — [B2B marketing](/en/blog/b2b-marketing-uzbekistan/).

## Step 8. Date reminders — repeat orders

A customer once ordered flowers for their mother's birthday — they'll need them again next year. With consent, record the date and remind them 3 days before: "Last year around now you sent your mother a bouquet — shall we prepare one again?". It's the cheapest and most genuine sale there is. Handling customer data — [customer database and personal data](/en/blog/customer-database-personal-data-uzbekistan/); repeat sales — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

## What to measure

- number of orders and average order value;
- share of pre-orders during holiday periods;
- delivery times and late deliveries;
- share of repeat customers;
- cost of one order from ads.

![Common mistakes](/img/blog/illus/gul-sovga-dokoni-marketing-3.jpg 1200x675)

## Common mistakes

- Other shops' photos or stock images.
- Posts without prices and long chats.
- Preparing for holidays at the last minute — not enough stock or couriers.
- Not showing the customer the bouquet before dispatch.
- Not recording customers' dates.

## Questions and answers

### Which channels matter for a flower shop?

Maps and Instagram are the main ones; a Telegram bot handles orders. Paid ads boost the flow before holidays and within the delivery area.

### Should prices be shown?

Yes. Flowers are an urgent purchase and customers don't want to wait for a price. Price categories are convenient.

### How do you keep quality up when holiday orders surge?

Pre-orders, a limited range (holiday sets), extra florists and couriers. Don't take more orders than you can fulfil — a late bouquet means a bad review.

### How can a small shop stand out from big delivery services?

With a personal touch: a photo before dispatch, a handwritten card, date reminders and direct contact with the florist.
