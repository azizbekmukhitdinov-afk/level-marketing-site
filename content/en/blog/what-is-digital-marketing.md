Digital marketing means finding customers and selling through the internet and digital channels: social networks, search engines, messengers, websites and marketplaces. In Uzbekistan, many purchases start on a phone: people see something on Instagram, message on Telegram and find the address on a map. Here's what digital marketing is and where a small business should start. Marketing in general — [what is marketing](/en/blog/what-is-marketing/).

## Digital vs offline marketing

| | Offline (traditional) | Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Examples | Billboards, TV, flyers | Instagram, Google, Telegram |
| Targeting | By area, broad | By interest, location, behaviour |
| Measurement | Difficult | Every click and lead |
| Budget | Often a high minimum | From small amounts |
| Speed | Slow to change | Changes in a day |

They can work together — and offline results can be measured too — [measuring offline advertising](/en/blog/measure-offline-advertising/).

## Digital marketing's main advantage: measurement

You can see the result of every soum:

- ad impressions and clicks;
- leads and cost per lead — [cost per lead](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/);
- sales and return on marketing — [how to calculate ROMI](/en/blog/how-to-calculate-romi/).

But that needs a system: UTM tags, a pixel, a CRM — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).

## A starter plan for small businesses

1. **Foundations**: a maps profile, an Instagram business account and Telegram contact — free or nearly free.
2. **An offer** that's clear and easy to understand — [unique selling proposition](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/).
3. **One paid channel** where your customers are — usually Instagram ads or search ads.
4. **Lead handling**: fast replies and a CRM — [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/).
5. **Measure and expand**: scale the channel that works, then add a second.

## Common mistakes

- Spreading a little across every channel at once.
- Measuring results by likes and followers.
- Replying to leads late, wasting ad money.
- A website or page that doesn't match the ad's promise.
