"Which is better — ChatGPT or Gemini?" is one of the first questions business owners ask when they start with AI. There are hundreds of comparisons online, but they're mostly in English, focused on programmers' tasks, and outdated within months: the tools update every month. So we won't declare a "winner". Instead, here are the selection criteria and a way to test both on your own business tasks in an hour.

## Why there's no universal answer

- **The tools change fast.** Where one is stronger today, the other may lead tomorrow.
- **Tasks differ.** An Uzbek post, a Russian proposal, a table analysis, an image — results may vary for each.
- **Work environments differ.** Which email, documents and spreadsheets you use affects the choice.

So the most reliable answer comes from testing on your own tasks.

## Selection criteria

| Criterion | What to check |
|---|---|
| Uzbek | Does it write natural Latin-script Uzbek, with correct o‘ and g‘? |
| Russian | Quality of business documents and customer replies |
| Your tasks | Posts, replies, analysis, tables, images — which matter most |
| Fit with work tools | Does it work well with your email, documents, spreadsheets? |
| File handling | Uploading and analysing PDFs, tables, images |
| For teams | Multiple users, access management, plans |
| Data policy | How entered data is used — read the terms |
| Price | What's free and what a paid plan adds |

One fact about Uzbek: in November 2025, it was announced that Google Gemini officially supports the Uzbek language ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/11/29/google-gemini-introduces-uzbek-language-support-on-its-ai-platform)). ChatGPT works in Uzbek too. Which is better on your texts is something a test will show.

## A 1-hour test: 5 tasks

Give both tools **identical** prompts and record the results in a table:

1. **An Uzbek post**: "A 120-word Instagram post about [your product], in Uzbek, Latin script."
2. **A customer reply**: "A customer wrote 'too expensive' — a polite reply."
3. **A Russian document**: "A one-page commercial proposal to a partner."
4. **Analysis**: give 30 anonymised reviews — "group them by theme".
5. **Ideas**: "10 promotion ideas for the low season, no discounts."

Score each result 1–5 for natural language, accuracy, usefulness and editing time. Having 2–3 employees score makes it more objective.

## One tool or both?

For many small businesses, one main tool is enough — the team learns it well and there's one set of rules. A second tool is useful for checking: run an important text through another AI with "find the mistakes".

More important than the number of tools are team rules: what goes in, what doesn't, who checks. More in [train your team on AI](/en/blog/train-team-on-ai/).

## Data safety

Whichever tool you choose:

- don't enter customer data — anonymised only;
- use a work account for business, not a personal one;
- close access when an employee leaves;
- read the data-use terms in your plan and settings.

Uzbekistan has a specific fine for unlawfully processing personal data using AI (ZRU-1115, [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8008401)). More in [AI and the law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/ai-law-uzbekistan-business/).

## Common mistakes

- Choosing based on an online comparison without testing on your tasks.
- A different tool every day — the team never masters any.
- Ignoring the free version's limits.
- Work data in employees' personal accounts.
- Confusing tool choice with AI use itself — results depend on prompts and checking.

## Questions and answers

### Which is better in Uzbek?

It depends on the version and task, and it changes quickly. Check for yourself with the 5-task test above.

### Do I need a paid plan?

Try the free version first. If you use it daily and hit limits, a paid plan usually pays for itself.

### Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, many tools have mobile apps. But a computer is more convenient for long texts and analysis.

### Should I switch tools every year?

Repeat a short test every 3–6 months. If the difference is big, switch; if it's small, stay with the tool you know.

## Conclusion

There's no universal winner between ChatGPT and Gemini — the tools change fast and results depend on the task. Choose based on Uzbek language quality, your tasks, your work tools and data safety, and run a one-hour test.
