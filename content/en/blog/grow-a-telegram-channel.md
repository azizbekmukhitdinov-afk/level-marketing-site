A Telegram channel is the cheapest "own audience" a business can have: no algorithm cuts your reach, and every post goes straight to subscribers. But starting from zero and getting the first subscribers is hard. Here is how to build a channel, where to find the first 1,000 subscribers and how to turn them into customers.

## The channel's foundation

Before any advertising, the channel needs to be ready:

- **Clear positioning.** Who it's for and what value it gives: "Renovation tips and prices in Tashkent", "IELTS prep: one lesson a day".
- **Name and avatar.** A clear name people can find in search.
- **Description.** One sentence on why to subscribe, plus one contact.
- **A pinned post.** Who you are, what you offer and how to order.
- **At least 10–15 useful posts** — so newcomers don't leave an empty channel.

## Content: what to post

| Format | Why |
|---|---|
| Useful post (text + image) | Saves and trust |
| Video message (circle) | A face and live contact |
| Poll | Engagement and audience insight |
| Process and results | Proof |
| Offer | Sales |

Consistency matters: for example, one post a day or 4–5 a week — but always at the same rhythm.

## Where to get the first 1,000 subscribers

1. **Your own base.** Customers, Instagram followers, links in Stories and your profile bio.
2. **Ads in channels.** Paid posts in topic channels — how to choose and measure them in our [Telegram advertising](/en/blog/telegram-advertising/) article.
3. **Cross-promotion.** Swaps with channels that share your audience but aren't competitors.
4. **A lead magnet.** "Subscribe and get a free checklist" — a useful file or guide.
5. **Offline.** A QR code in your office, on packaging, on receipts.
6. **Your website.** A button that leads to Telegram.

## Turning subscribers into customers

- A clear offer and ordering instructions in the pinned post.
- A special discount or early access for subscribers.
- A bot or manager contact for orders and consultations.
- Regular proof: reviews, results, cases.

## Which metrics to track

- Post views relative to subscribers — how alive the audience is.
- Joins and leaves — after which posts people leave.
- Link clicks and leads from the channel.

## Common mistakes

- Buying subscribers — views don't grow, and both advertisers and algorithms can see it.
- Nothing but promotional posts.
- Posting without rhythm: every day for a week, then two weeks of silence.

## Conclusion

A Telegram channel is your own media outlet. Build the foundation, post useful content consistently, attract subscribers from several sources and turn them into customers with a clear offer.
