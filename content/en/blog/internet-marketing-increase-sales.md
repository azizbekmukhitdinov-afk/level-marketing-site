In Uzbekistan today, buyers look up almost any product on their phone first: they browse Instagram, ask for prices on Telegram and find the address on Google or Yandex Maps. Internet marketing means being found at every step of that path. Here is what each channel is for and where a small business should start.

![What internet marketing is made of](/img/blog/illus/internet-marketing-sotuvni-oshirish-1.jpg 1200x675)

## What internet marketing is made of

Internet marketing is not a single "run an ad" but several connected channels:

| Channel | Job | Especially good for |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram and SMM | Show the product, earn trust | Visual products, services |
| Paid ads | Attract new customers fast | Almost everyone |
| Telegram | Talk to customers, repeat sales | Education, retail, services |
| Google Ads and SEO | Capture people searching now | Urgent services, B2B |
| Google and Yandex Maps | Nearby customers | Offices, shops, clinics, cafés |
| Uzum Market | Online sales | Product sellers |

You don't need to launch everything at once. What matters is understanding where your customer makes the decision.

## Key terms

- **Lead** — a potential customer who left a phone number or messaged you.
- **CPL** — cost per lead. More in our [CPL guide](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/).
- **Conversion** — the share of people who took the next step (clicked, messaged, bought).
- **Funnel** — the customer's path: saw → got interested → messaged → bought.
- **Retargeting** — showing ads again to people who visited your site or profile but didn't buy.
- **LTV** — how much money a customer brings over the whole relationship.

## Where to start: 4 steps

### 1. Build a "home"

Ads have to lead somewhere: an Instagram profile, a Telegram channel or a website. It should show prices, examples of work, reviews and clear contacts. Advertising a page that doesn't inspire trust is wasted money.

### 2. Get on the maps

If you have an office or shop, create a business listing on Google and Yandex Maps: address, opening hours, phone, photos. It's free and brings customers from "… near me" searches.

### 3. Pick one main ad channel

Don't split a small budget across platforms. If your product sells by being shown — Meta (Instagram); if people search for you — Google. How to choose: [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

### 4. Turn leads into sales

Reply fast, write a call script and track customers in a spreadsheet or CRM. Many businesses lose money not in advertising but at exactly this stage.

## Content: what to post

Four types of content sell on social media:

1. **Useful** — tips, answers, "how to choose".
2. **Proof** — reviews, before/after, numbers.
3. **Process** — how the work is done, the team, production.
4. **Offer** — price, promotion, discount, a clear call to action.

A page of nothing but offers looks like a notice board, and purely useful content doesn't sell. You need balance. For the first seconds of a Reel, see [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).

## How to measure results

Every month, record at least:

- ad spend;
- number of leads and cost per lead (CPL);
- number of sales and lead-to-sale conversion;
- cost per customer and average order value.

Followers and likes are not sales on their own. Base decisions on numbers connected to sales.

## Common mistakes

- "Being everywhere" without a strategy and spreading the budget thin.
- Replying to leads hours later.
- Running the same creative for months.
- Judging results by reach alone.
- Blindly copying competitors instead of finding your own offer.

## How marketing and sales connect

Marketing brings the lead; the process decides the sale. The lead goes into the CRM, a manager replies within minutes, the conversation follows a script, and if there's no decision, there's a follow-up on an agreed day. Once a week, marketing and sales review the results together: which leads were good, which aren't turning into sales and why.

- When you no longer have time for marketing.

## Questions and answers

### How soon does internet marketing produce results?

The first leads from ads arrive within days. Organic channels — content, SEO, a Telegram channel — build up over months and deliver stable results later.

### Is internet marketing possible without a website?

Yes. Many businesses sell without one, through Instagram, Telegram and maps. A website becomes useful later for Google ads, search and trust.

### Which channel is cheapest?

In money terms, organic content and maps — but they take time. The right comparison is cost per sale, with your time included.

### How do you combine internet marketing with offline advertising?

Put QR codes and promo codes on banners, flyers and packaging, and ask customers "Where did you hear about us?". That way offline advertising shows up in the numbers too.

### How much time should an owner spend on marketing?

A few hours a week: decisions, the offer, content ideas and reviewing results. The technical work can be delegated, but the owner sets the direction.

### Which report should show the results?

Spend, leads, cost per lead, sales and cost per sale — by channel. The report structure is covered in [What an ad report should include](/en/blog/what-an-ad-report-should-include/).

### Which numbers should you check every day?

Daily: spend, number of leads and whether the leads were answered. Weekly: cost per lead and sales. Monthly: ROMI and the share of returning customers. That rhythm reveals problems before it's too late.

### Can one person run all the channels?

One or two channels at the start, yes. As channels multiply, you'll need a team or an agency for content, ads and lead handling: one person can't do everything well.
