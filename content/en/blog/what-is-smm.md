"What is SMM", "what does SMM do", "SMM vs targeted ads" — some of the most common marketing questions in Uzbekistan. Many people think SMM means "posting on Instagram". In reality it's much broader: understanding the audience, content, conversations, linking up with ads and measuring results. Here's what SMM is, in plain words.

## A simple definition

SMM (social media marketing) is marketing through social networks: presenting your business on Instagram, Telegram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube, talking with customers and leading them to buy.

The goal of SMM isn't a pretty page but **trust and sales**: someone looks at your page, thinks "I can trust them" and gets in touch.

## What SMM consists of

| Part | What's done |
|---|---|
| Strategy | For whom, about what, on which network, with what goal |
| Content | Posts, Reels, Stories, carousels |
| Profile | Bio, highlights, first posts — the page's "shop window" |
| Conversations | Replies to comments and DMs |
| Partnerships | Influencers, other businesses |
| Analytics | Reach, saves, enquiries, impact on sales |

On a profile that sells — [an Instagram profile that sells](/en/blog/instagram-profile-that-sells/). Content planning — [Instagram content plan](/en/blog/instagram-content-plan/).

## SMM vs targeted ads

The two are often confused:

| | SMM | Targeted ads |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Page, content, conversations | Paid advertising |
| Who it reaches | Mostly followers and recommendations | A chosen new audience |
| Speed of results | Slower, cumulative | Faster |
| Job | Trust, brand, repeat contact | A flow of new leads |

They complement each other: ads bring in new people, SMM convinces them. On targeted ads — [what is targeted advertising](/en/blog/what-is-targeted-advertising/).

## Who is an SMM manager?

An SMM manager runs the page: plans, produces or commissions content, replies to comments and analyses results. Their tasks and how to choose one — [what an SMM manager does](/en/blog/what-an-smm-manager-does/).

## What SMM gives a business

- **trust**: real photos, the team, reviews — [social proof](/en/blog/social-proof-in-ads/);
- **discoverability**: new people through Reels and recommendations;
- **repeat contact**: followers see your news;
- **better ad performance**: a good page turns people arriving from ads into leads.

If the page looks great but doesn't sell, the cause is usually structure, not content: [why social media doesn't sell](/en/blog/why-social-media-doesnt-sell/).

## How to measure SMM

| Metric | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Reach | How many people saw the content |
| Saves and shares | Whether the content is useful |
| DM enquiries | Whether interest turns into action |
| "How did you hear about us" answers | Impact on sales |
| Follower growth | A secondary metric |

Follower count isn't the main metric. A thousand real customers are worth more than ten thousand "empty" followers. Growing followers the right way — [how to grow Instagram followers](/en/blog/grow-instagram-followers/).

## Where to start

1. Write down the goal: sales, leads, brand or trust.
2. Choose one main network — [Telegram or Instagram](/en/blog/telegram-or-instagram-for-business/).
3. Tidy up the profile: bio, highlights, first 9 posts.
4. Plan 3–5 posts a week.
5. Set up fast DM replies.
6. Review results once a month.

## Common mistakes

- Treating SMM as design only.
- Buying followers — real reach drops.
- Trying to be on every network at once.
- Replying to DMs late.
- Judging results by likes alone.

## Questions and answers

### Can you make money with SMM?

That question usually means becoming an SMM specialist. For a business, SMM is a tool for sales and trust.

### How much does SMM cost?

It depends on volume, content type and who does it. How pricing works — [SMM service pricing](/en/blog/smm-service-pricing/).

### Can I do SMM myself?

At the start, yes. As the business grows, time runs out and you need a specialist.

### When will I see results?

Organic SMM is slow — usually several months. With ads, faster.
