When people think of search advertising, most only think of Google Ads. But in Uzbekistan, especially among Russian-speaking users, Yandex search and Yandex Maps are also widely used. **Yandex Direct** is Yandex's advertising system. Here is when it helps, how it differs from Google Ads and how to launch your first campaign.

## What Yandex Direct is

Yandex Direct shows ads in several places:

- **Search** — in Yandex results when someone types a query.
- **Advertising network (YAN)** — banners on Yandex partner websites and apps.
- **Yandex Maps** — a highlighted pin among businesses.

## Who it suits best

- Businesses whose customers search in Russian.
- B2B: equipment, wholesale, corporate services.
- Local services: repairs, car services, clinics — especially where visibility on Yandex Maps matters.

## How it differs from Google Ads

| Criterion | Yandex Direct | Google Ads |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | More Russian-speaking users | A broader audience |
| Maps | Yandex Maps | Google Maps |
| Analytics | Yandex Metrica | Google Analytics |
| Automation | Automated campaigns available | Performance Max and others |

The best results usually come from testing both systems and comparing cost per lead. For choosing between Google and Meta, see [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

## Your first campaign: 6 steps

1. Create a Yandex Direct account and check which payment methods are available for Uzbekistan — sometimes paying through an agency is easier.
2. Install **Yandex Metrica** on your site and set up goals: form submissions, call button clicks, Telegram clicks.
3. Collect keywords: product + city, service + price, problem + solution.
4. Add **negative keywords**: "free", "vacancy", "download" and other irrelevant queries.
5. Write ads: the query in the headline, your offer and advantage in the text, plus sitelinks.
6. Send each ad to a relevant page, not the home page.

## Common mistakes

- Broad keywords without negatives — budget goes on irrelevant searches.
- Running without Metrica and goals — you can't tell which keywords bring sales.
- Mixing search and network placements in one campaign.
- Judging results by cost per click alone.

## Conclusion

Yandex Direct doesn't fully replace Google Ads, but it can be an extra source of "hot" leads from Russian-speaking and B2B audiences. Always compare results by cost per lead and per sale, using the method from our [CPL guide](/en/blog/cost-per-lead-cpl-how-to-lower/).
