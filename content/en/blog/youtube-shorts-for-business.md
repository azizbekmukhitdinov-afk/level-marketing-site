Many businesses post vertical videos only to Instagram Reels. But the same video can work on YouTube Shorts too — at no extra cost. Since October 2024, Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/tall-updates-coming-to-shorts/)). YouTube has another advantage: it's part of Google's ecosystem, and videos stay discoverable for a long time. Here's how to use YouTube Shorts in a business.

## Shorts vs Reels vs TikTok

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
|---|---|---|---|
| Audience | Broad, very active in Uzbekistan | Younger | Broad, YouTube users |
| A video's "lifespan" | Mostly the first few days | Mostly the first few days | Longer: search and recommendations |
| Next step | Profile, DMs | Profile | A long video, the channel, a link |
| Search | Inside Instagram | Inside TikTok | YouTube and Google |

**The insight:** useful Shorts such as "how to choose" or "what's the difference" can keep getting views from search for months.

## Who it suits best

- **Experts and education**: Q&As, tips, explanations.
- **Complex products**: tech, cars, construction — "how it works".
- **Businesses with long-form video**: Shorts are a door to the channel — [YouTube for business](/en/blog/youtube-for-business/).
- **Multilingual audiences**: separate videos in Uzbek and Russian.

## Adapting Reels for Shorts

1. **Upload the original file** — without another platform's watermark.
2. **Title and description**: include a search keyword for YouTube — "Choosing kitchen furniture: 3 mistakes" (an illustrative example).
3. **The first seconds**: the hook decides here too — [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).
4. **Related video**: link the Short to a long video, if your channel has one.
5. **Channel profile**: description, links, contacts.

## A content plan: 3 segments

- **Q&A**: customers' real questions — twice a week.
- **Process**: how it's made, how it's installed — once a week.
- **Results**: before/after, a case — once a week.

Several videos from one shoot day — [one shoot, 10 ad creatives](/en/blog/one-shoot-10-ad-creatives/).

## Longer Shorts: when they make sense

The option to go up to 3 minutes doesn't mean everything should be long. A longer format suits explanations, case studies and before/after stories. Short videos remain the core — [video ad length](/en/blog/video-ad-length/).

## Measuring results

- YouTube Studio: views, average view duration, subscribers, traffic sources (search or the Shorts feed).
- The business metric: clicks from the channel to your site, and leads — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).

## Common mistakes

- Uploading with a TikTok watermark.
- No title, or just "#shorts".
- No contacts or link on the channel.
- Expecting results in 2–3 weeks — YouTube is slower but lasts longer.

## Questions and answers

### Can I post the same video everywhere?

Yes, but adapt the title and description for each platform.

### Can I advertise on Shorts?

Yes — video campaigns in Google Ads can also run on Shorts — [Google Ads vs Meta Ads](/en/blog/google-ads-vs-meta-ads/).

### What do I need to open a channel?

A Google account and a few minutes to create a YouTube channel.
