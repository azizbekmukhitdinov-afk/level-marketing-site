Two managers sell leads from the same ad very differently: one closes many, the other few. The difference is usually not talent but structure. A **sales script** is a step-by-step plan for the conversation: it helps new managers learn faster and reduces lost sales. Here is the structure of a script and a ready example.

## A script isn't robot-speak

A script isn't text to read word for word — it's a map: what to ask at each stage and where to steer. A live conversation adapted to the customer's name and question gets the best results.

## 6 stages

1. **Greeting and introduction.** Name, company and reason for the call: "Hello, Dilnoza! I'm Sardor from the Home Furniture store — you left a request about a sofa."
2. **Discovery.** Open questions: "Which room is the sofa for? What size? What matters more — comfort or durability?"
3. **Presentation.** 2–3 key advantages and your [USP](/en/blog/unique-selling-proposition/), tied to what the customer said.
4. **Handling objections.** See the table below.
5. **Closing.** A specific next step: an order, a measurement, a showroom visit, a payment.
6. **Follow-up.** If there's no decision, schedule the next contact with a specific date and time.

## Objections and answers

| Objection | How to respond |
|---|---|
| "It's expensive" | Explain what the price includes, offer instalments, show a cheaper option |
| "I'll think about it" | "What would you like to think over — the price or the model?" — find the real doubt |
| "It's cheaper elsewhere" | Compare what's included: warranty, delivery, materials |
| "Later" | Agree on a specific date; mention a promotion or price deadline (only if it's real) |

## Specifics for Telegram and Direct

- Short messages — one idea per message.
- Photos, video or voice notes are more convincing than text.
- End every message with a question so the conversation doesn't stall.
- Reply fast — see our [lead response time](/en/blog/lead-response-time/) article.

## How to improve the script

- Record calls and review 3–5 of them together every week.
- Track in your CRM at which stage customers drop off.
- When new objections appear, add the answers to the script.

## Conclusion

A sales script is the system that turns ad leads into sales. Write it, train your managers and update it every week. To avoid losing leads, use a [CRM](/en/blog/crm-for-small-business/); for chat templates, see [handling comments and DMs](/en/blog/handling-comments-and-dms/).
