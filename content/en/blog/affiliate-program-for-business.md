The most trusted advertising is a recommendation from someone you know. An **affiliate programme** turns that into a system: customers, tradespeople, influencers or other businesses bring you customers and earn a reward on each sale. You pay only for results — which makes it a safe channel for small businesses. Here's how to build one.

## Who can be a partner

| Partner | Example |
|---|---|
| Happy customers | Bring a friend and you both get a bonus |
| Industry specialists | A builder for a building materials store, a designer for a furniture store |
| Related businesses | A wedding venue for a photographer and florist; an accountant for a lawyer |
| Influencers and channels | A commission on every sale via a personal promo code |

## Types of reward

- **A commission on sales** — the fairest: payment matches results.
- **A fixed amount** for each new customer.
- **A bonus or service** instead of cash: a discount, a free service.
- **Two-sided**: for both the referrer and the new customer — works best for customer referrals.

## The economics

The reward must cost less than your other acquisition channels. An illustrative example: if a customer from ads costs 300,000 soum, a 150,000 soum partner reward is a good deal. The calculation — [customer acquisition cost](/en/blog/customer-acquisition-cost-cac/).

## Tracking who brought whom

- **A personal promo code** for offline and phone sales — [promo codes in marketing](/en/blog/promo-codes-marketing/).
- **A personal link** with a parameter for your website or bot — [UTM tags](/en/blog/utm-tags/).
- **A source field in the CRM**: "Referral: Ali (builder)".

## Write the terms down

One page of rules:

- who can become a partner;
- when rewards are paid (for example, once the customer has paid in full);
- what happens with returns;
- which promotion methods are banned (spam, fake reviews, advertising in the brand's name);
- payment timing and method.

Agree reward and tax questions with your accountant.

## Fraud protection

- Rewards only for **real sales**, not leads.
- Check for self-referrals and duplicate phone numbers.
- Partners' advertising must comply with the law — [advertising law in Uzbekistan](/en/blog/advertising-law-uzbekistan/).

## Launching the programme

1. Find your happiest customers — those who gave an NPS of 9–10 — [what is NPS](/en/blog/what-is-nps/).
2. Give them a personal offer and promo code.
3. Meet 5–10 potential partners in your industry.
4. Each month, review: who brought how many customers, and the rewards paid.
5. Reward your best partners.

## Questions and answers

### Does a small business need an affiliate programme?

Yes — it's a channel where you pay only for results. You can start with a simple referral bonus.

### How big should the reward be?

It depends on your margin and customer value. It should be noticeable for the partner and profitable for you — [repeat sales and LTV](/en/blog/repeat-sales-and-ltv/).

### With influencers: commission or upfront payment?

A commission model lowers your risk, but big influencers often ask for a fixed fee — [influencer advertising](/en/blog/influencer-advertising-uzbekistan/).
