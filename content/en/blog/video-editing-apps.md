A well-shot video either comes alive in editing or dies there. But you don't need complex professional software — for many businesses, a phone app is enough. Here are the main apps for phone and computer, how to handle captions and tricks to speed up editing.

## The main apps

| App | Platform | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| CapCut | Phone, computer | Free + paid features | Reels, templates, auto captions |
| Edits (Instagram) | Phone | Free | Quick editing for Instagram |
| InShot | Phone | Free + paid | Simple trimming and text |
| VN | Phone, computer | Free | Working with multiple tracks |
| DaVinci Resolve | Computer | Powerful free version | Colour, sound, complex edits |
| Premiere Pro | Computer | Subscription | Professional teams |
| Final Cut Pro | Mac | One-off purchase | Professional editing on a Mac |

Apps and their terms change over time, so check availability in your country and pricing before installing.

## Captions

Many people watch without sound, so captions are a must. Many apps generate captions automatically, but Uzbek captions may contain mistakes — always check them by hand. Use a large font, a contrasting colour and the middle of the screen so text doesn't hide under the phone's interface.

## Music: organic vs ads

- For organic Reels you can use music from Instagram's library.
- In ads that music is often not allowed — choose royalty-free tracks or ones licensed for advertising.

## How to edit faster

1. **Build a template:** font, colours, logo, caption style — don't start from scratch each time.
2. **Cut pauses automatically** — many apps have this feature.
3. **Organise files:** "raw", "editing", "done" folders.
4. **Edit several videos in one session** — working in one style is faster.
5. **Learn keyboard shortcuts** (on a computer).

## Common editing mistakes

- A logo and long intro in the first seconds.
- Music louder than the voice.
- Captions with typos or too small.
- Videos longer than needed — 15–45 seconds is often enough.
