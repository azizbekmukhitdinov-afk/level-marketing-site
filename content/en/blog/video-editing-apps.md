A well-shot video either comes alive in editing or dies there. But you don't need complex professional software — for many businesses, a phone app is enough. Here are the main apps for phone and computer, how to handle captions and tricks to speed up editing.

## The main apps

| App | Platform | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| CapCut | Phone, computer | Free + paid features | Reels, templates, auto captions |
| Edits (Instagram) | Phone | Free | Quick editing for Instagram |
| InShot | Phone | Free + paid | Simple trimming and text |
| VN | Phone, computer | Free | Working with multiple tracks |
| DaVinci Resolve | Computer | Powerful free version | Colour, sound, complex edits |
| Premiere Pro | Computer | Subscription | Professional teams |
| Final Cut Pro | Mac | One-off purchase | Professional editing on a Mac |

Apps and their terms change over time, so check availability in your country and pricing before installing.

## Captions

Many people watch without sound, so captions are a must. Many apps generate captions automatically, but Uzbek captions may contain mistakes — always check them by hand. Use a large font, a contrasting colour and the middle of the screen so text doesn't hide under the phone's interface.

## Music: organic vs ads

- For organic Reels you can use music from Instagram's library.
- In ads that music is often not allowed — choose royalty-free tracks or ones licensed for advertising.

## How to edit faster

1. **Build a template:** font, colours, logo, caption style — don't start from scratch each time.
2. **Cut pauses automatically** — many apps have this feature.
3. **Organise files:** "raw", "editing", "done" folders.
4. **Edit several videos in one session** — working in one style is faster.
5. **Learn keyboard shortcuts** (on a computer).

## Common editing mistakes

- A logo and long intro in the first seconds.
- Music louder than the voice.
- Captions with typos or too small.
- Videos longer than needed — 15–45 seconds is often enough.

## Editing one Reel: step by step

1. **Pick your shots** — keep the best take and delete the rest.
2. **Put the strongest shot first** — a result or a question in the first 2–3 seconds; ideas in [Reels hooks](/en/blog/reels-hooks/).
3. **Cut pauses and filler words** — the pace picks up and the video gets shorter.
4. **Add captions and proofread them** — especially names, prices and terms.
5. **Turn the music down** — speech must be clearly audible.
6. **Choose a cover** — a frame that looks good in the profile grid, plus a short title.
7. **Watch it on a phone with the sound off** — does it still make sense from the captions?

## Export settings

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Format | Vertical 9:16 |
| Resolution | 1080×1920 |
| Frame rate | Same as recorded, usually 30 fps |
| File | MP4 |
| Text placement | Away from the edges, so interface buttons don't cover it |

If you send a video through a messenger as a video, it may be compressed and lose quality. Send it as a file or through cloud storage.

## Hire an editor or edit yourself

With one or two videos a week, you or your SMM manager can usually handle editing. Once the volume grows and videos go into ads, a dedicated editor saves time. When briefing an editor, include the goal, length, reference videos, fonts and colours, caption requirements and the deadline. How to split tasks in a team is covered in [What an SMM manager does](/en/blog/what-an-smm-manager-does/).

## Questions and answers

### How long does it take to learn editing?

A few days of practice is enough for simple Reels: cutting, captions, music and export. Complex effects can come later — they're not what drives sales.

### Do free apps add a watermark?

Some apps add their logo or an end screen to videos in the free version. Check the settings before exporting and remove it if needed.

### Is it better to edit on a phone or a computer?

A phone is quick and convenient for short Reels. A computer is more comfortable for long videos, YouTube, and serious colour or sound work.

### What phone do you need for editing?

Most apps run well on modern mid-range phones too. What matters more is free storage and speed: delete unneeded files before editing, and move finished videos to the cloud or a computer. If the app slows down, edit the project in shorter parts.
