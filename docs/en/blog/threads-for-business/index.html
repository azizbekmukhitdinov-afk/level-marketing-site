Threads for business: do you need it and how to start

Threads is Meta's text-based social network, launched in 2023 on top of Instagram accounts. Its core format is short text: questions, opinions and discussion. Many owners ask: "Isn't Instagram enough? Do I really need another network?" The answer depends on the type of business. Here's who benefits from Threads and how to start.

How Threads differs from Instagram

InstagramThreads
Main formatVideo and imagesText (images and video possible)
Content productionShooting, editingWriting — fast
InteractionComments and DMsDiscussions, reply chains
Best suited toVisual productsExperts, opinions and knowledge

You open Threads with your Instagram account — no separate sign-up needed.

Who it suits

  • Experts and personal brands: lawyers, doctors, marketers, coaches sharing knowledge and opinions — personal brand: where to start.
  • B2B companies: industry views, case studies, discussions — B2B marketing.
  • Founders: the process of building a business, decisions, mistakes.
  • Brands that want conversation: Q&As, polls, news.

Who doesn't need it yet: small businesses selling mainly visual products with limited social media capacity.

Content that works

  1. Opinion and stance: "Many businesses switch off ads after 3 days. Here's why that's a mistake:"
  2. Questions: "What do you say when a customer says it's too expensive?" — it starts a discussion.
  3. Short lists: tips in 3–5 points.
  4. Threads: a sequence of posts on one topic — a mini-article.
  5. Behind-the-scenes stories: what happened today, what we learned.

An Instagram Reel idea can be reused on Threads as text — what is content marketing.

Advertising on Threads

In 2025, Meta announced it was opening Threads as an ad placement to all eligible advertisers, with a phased rollout (Search Engine Land). With Advantage+ placements, the Threads feed is usually switched on automatically. So check your Meta ad reports by placement to see how Threads is performing.

A 30-day test plan

  • Week 1: complete your profile and post 5 times a week: an opinion, a question, a tip.
  • Week 2: post thoughtful replies to other experts' and customers' posts.
  • Week 3: publish 1–2 threads on the topics people ask about most.
  • Week 4: review results: reach, replies, profile visits and DM enquiries.

If there's no conversation or enquiries after 30 days, put your effort back into Instagram and Telegram.

Common mistakes

  • Posting Instagram captions unchanged.
  • Nothing but ads and promotions — Threads expects discussion.
  • Not replying to replies.
  • Sharp statements in the brand's name on controversial topics.

Questions and answers

Does Threads work in Uzbekistan?

Yes, the app is available. But check for yourself how active your audience is there.

Does Threads help Instagram reach?

Not demonstrably, directly. But the profiles are linked, and activity on Threads can bring people to your Instagram profile.

How much time does it take?

One to two hours a week — writing text is much faster than producing video.

Conclusion

Threads is a platform for text-based opinions and discussion, especially suited to experts, B2B companies and founders.
