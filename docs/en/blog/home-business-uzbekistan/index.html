A business from home: first customers via Instagram and Telegram

"Home business ideas" and "home business ideas for women" are searched every year. Baking, sewing, handmade goods, tutoring, beauty services, online services — many businesses start at home. Their biggest challenge isn't the product but the first customers: there's no shop, no sign, and nobody knows you. Here's how to build marketing for a home-based business.

First: the marketing advantages of a home business

A home business isn't a weakness. Presented well, it's an advantage:

  • a personal approach — you work with every customer yourself;
  • handmade — a difference from mass-produced goods;
  • the owner is visible — a strong trust signal. More in founder on camera.

Step 1: offer and price

In one sentence: what, for whom, what's different. For example: "Cakes made from natural ingredients for children's birthdays, order 2 days ahead" (illustrative example).

Calculate the price: ingredients + your time + delivery + future advertising costs. Many home businesses forget their time and end up "working a lot with no money to show for it". How to calculate — unit economics. A clear package and price — entry offer.

Step 2: your page is your shop window

For a home business, an Instagram or Telegram page is the shop. The minimum:

  • bio: what, for whom, how to order, which area;
  • highlights: prices, work, reviews, delivery, how to order;
  • the first 9 posts: your best work, the process, you;
  • real photos: a phone is enough, but keep the light and background clean — AI product photos.

More on the profile — an Instagram profile that sells. Which channel — Telegram or Instagram.

Step 3: your first 10 customers

First customers come not from ads but from your close circle:

  1. Friends and relatives — but not for free: at the real price or with a small "first customer" condition.
  2. Neighbourhood and building chats — a short, useful message, not spam.
  3. Ask for reviews — after every order, with a photo.
  4. Referrals — "bring a friend and get a gift with your next order".
  5. Partnerships — with related businesses: wedding venues, flower shops, photographers.

Your first 10 reviews are the foundation for all later marketing. More in online reputation and reviews.

Step 4: trust

People are more doubtful about home businesses: "what's the quality?", "hygiene?", "will it be on time?". Trust signals:

  • process videos — how it's made;
  • a clean workspace;
  • customer reviews and photos — social proof;
  • clear terms: timing, payment, returns.

Step 5: small-budget ads

Once you have 10–20 reviews and a good page, advertise:

  • Instagram ads: your district or city, your best Reel;
  • objective — DMs: conversation suits a home business better — lead form or messages;
  • a 2–3 week test, then adjust based on results.

Launching ads before the page is ready wastes money: people visit, see an empty profile and leave.

On registering the business

Earning income from home should be done legally — for example, as a self-employed person or a sole proprietor. Check which regime fits and the current requirements with official tax authority sources, or consult an accountant. Rules for handling customer data — customer database and personal data.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving your own time out of the price.
  • Working for friends for free — later they won't pay.
  • Launching ads with an empty or messy profile.
  • Not asking for reviews.
  • Taking every order — quality and deadlines suffer.

Questions and answers

Which channel is best for a home business?

Often Instagram for showing your work and Telegram for regular customers and orders. They work well together.

When should I spend money on ads?

When the page is ready and you have at least 10–20 genuine reviews.

How can I grow a home business?

First get the process in order: taking orders, delivery, a helper. Then you can think about a workshop or store. Before opening — marketing before opening.

What should I look for when choosing an idea?

Demand, competition and profit — business ideas for 2026.

Conclusion

Marketing a home business takes five steps: a clear offer and the right price, a page that works as a shop window, your first 10 customers and reviews from your close circle, trust signals — and only then small-budget ads.
