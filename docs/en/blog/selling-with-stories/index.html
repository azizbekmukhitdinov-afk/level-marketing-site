Selling with Stories: formats that work every day

Stories are watched mostly by people who already know you — your followers. That makes Stories the easiest place to touch a warm audience every day and sell. Here is why Stories sell, which sequences work and a simple daily plan.

Why Stories sell

  • A warm audience. Viewers follow you and are interested.
  • Daily contact. Showing up every day keeps you top of mind.
  • Interactivity. Poll, question and link stickers start conversations.
  • Authenticity. Unpolished footage builds trust.

A simple daily plan

TimeWhat to showExample
MorningProcess, the start of the day"Today we're putting together 3 orders"
AfternoonValue or productA tip, a product review
EveningProof or offerA review, a result, a promotion

For most businesses, 5–10 Stories a day is enough.

A sequence: the 4-day "warm-up"

Before a new product or promotion:

  1. Day 1 — the problem. A poll about a problem your customers face.
  2. Day 2 — the story. How you solve it, the process.
  3. Day 3 — proof. Reviews, results, before/after.
  4. Day 4 — the offer. Specific terms, a deadline and a link or a "message us" call.

Interactive stickers

  • Poll — "Which colour do you like?" — engagement and opinions.
  • Question — "Ask me anything" — topics for future content.
  • Quiz — teaching through play.
  • Link — to your website or Telegram.
  • Countdown — counting down to a promotion.

You can message people who answered a sticker in Direct — that's a ready-made conversation.

Rules

  • The first frame is the hook: a question or an intriguing line.
  • Large, readable text that doesn't hide under the phone's interface.
  • Face and voice — talk to camera in at least a few Stories.
  • Save your best Stories to highlights — more in an Instagram profile that sells.

How to measure results

Track reach, exits after the first frame, the number of replies and link clicks. Note which sequences bring more messages.

A weekly Stories plan (example)

Adapt the plan to your business but keep the rhythm: one main topic and one interactive sticker every day.

From Stories to DMs: turning a conversation into a sale

Message anyone who replied to a sticker straight away — they're already interested. First answer their question, then ask one clarifying question, and only then make an offer. For ready-made message templates, see Handling comments and DMs; for the stages of a sale, see Sales script.

Common mistakes

  • Only advertising. If followers see nothing useful or interesting, they skip your Stories.
  • Too many Stories a day. Few people watch 30 of them to the end.
  • Small text. Nobody reads captions hidden under the phone's interface.
  • Not messaging people who replied. That's where the warmest conversations get lost.

Questions and answers

How many Stories should you post a day?

For most businesses 5–10 is enough. What matters is posting every day: consistency counts for more than quantity.

Do you have to show your face?

No, but a face and a voice noticeably increase trust. If talking to camera is hard, start with short videos a few times a week and build up.

Should you promote Stories with ads?

Yes, your best-performing Stories can run as ads — they're usually cheap and look natural. But ads don't replace daily Stories for your followers. For setting up ads, see How to set up Instagram ads.

10 Stories ideas

  1. The process of preparing today's order.
  2. A customer question and a short answer.
  3. A before-and-after result.
  4. A first look at a new product.
  5. Meet one person from the team.
  6. Common mistakes when using the product.
  7. A screenshot of a customer review.
  8. A "pick one of two" poll.
  9. What the price is made of.
  10. The best-selling product of the week.

Conclusion

Stories are selling without ads: they warm up your audience every day and bring warm people into conversation. Plan them together with your content plan and repeat your best sequences.
