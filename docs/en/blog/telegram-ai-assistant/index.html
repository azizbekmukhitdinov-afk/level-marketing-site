A Telegram AI assistant: replying to leads 24/7

Telegram is one of the main places where customers in Uzbekistan message businesses. The problem is familiar: messages arrive in the evening, at night and at weekends, the salesperson replies in the morning — and by then the customer has written to someone else. An AI reply bot can close that gap: it gives the first answer instantly and leaves complex questions for a salesperson. Here's how to build an AI assistant on Telegram and which mistakes to avoid.

Two options: a separate bot or a bot on your business account

OptionHow it worksBest for
A separate botCustomers write to @your_bot; the bot collects leads and repliesLeads from ads, orders, bookings
A bot connected to a business accountCustomers write to your regular account; the bot replies in your nameWhen customers are used to messaging a personal account

The second option arrived with Telegram Business: in March 2024, Telegram announced that business users can connect bots that process and answer messages on their behalf (Telegram). Terms and required subscriptions may change over time — check Telegram's current rules before setting it up.

On collecting leads through a separate bot — Telegram bot for leads.

What an AI reply bot can do

  • Answer frequent questions: price ranges, address, hours, delivery, warranty.
  • Collect leads: name, phone, what's needed, when — and write it to the CRM.
  • Triage: a hot lead (needed today) triggers an instant alert to a salesperson.
  • Reply out of hours: "A manager will message you at 9 am; meanwhile, here are our prices."

What it shouldn't do: haggle over price, promise discounts, handle complaints or change contract terms. Those are human jobs.

The knowledge base: where answers come from

An AI bot should work only from the information you provide. Otherwise it makes answers up. The knowledge base:

  1. 20–40 questions and answers — what your salespeople hear most.
  2. Prices — exact or "from", with the date last updated.
  3. Terms — delivery, payment, returns, warranty.
  4. Boundaries — a list of "don't answer this, hand it to a salesperson".

To collect the questions, use the method from content from customer objections. Reply templates — replying to "How much?".

Hand-over rules

A good bot knows its limits. Hand over to a salesperson when:

  • the customer is complaining or upset;
  • the question isn't in the knowledge base;
  • the customer asks for a discount or special terms;
  • the customer writes "I want to talk to a person";
  • the order is large (above a set amount).

When handing over, the bot sends the salesperson a short summary so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

Be open: say it's a bot

It's best to say so in the first message: "Hello! I'm [company]'s automated assistant. I'll answer questions about prices, address and delivery right away and pass complex questions to a manager." It preserves trust and sets the right expectations.

Data and the law

The bot collects customers' names and phone numbers — personal data. What you need:

  • request and store consent — customer database and personal data;
  • give the AI service only what it needs to answer;
  • restrict access to the bot.

Since January 2026, there's been a specific fine for unlawfully processing personal data using AI (ZRU-1115, lex.uz). More in AI and the law in Uzbekistan.

Launch: 4 steps

  1. Test: the bot is for the team only — check it on 50 real questions.
  2. Night mode: the bot replies only outside working hours.
  3. Monitor: read 10–20 conversations daily and fix errors in the knowledge base.
  4. Expand: if results are good, add daytime first replies too.

Measuring results

  • first response time (especially at night);
  • share of conversations handed to salespeople;
  • conversion of night-time leads into sales;
  • the number of complaints and "I want a person" requests.

How response speed affects sales — lead response time.

Common mistakes

  • Telling the bot to "answer everything" with no knowledge base.
  • Letting the bot decide on prices and discounts.
  • No hand-over — customers go in circles with the bot.
  • Hiding that it's a bot.
  • Nobody reading the conversations.

Questions and answers

How is an AI bot different from a simple auto-reply?

An auto-reply sends one fixed text. An AI bot understands the question and gives a matching answer from the knowledge base.

Does it work well in Uzbek?

It works, but makes mistakes. Put ready-written answers in the knowledge base and review conversations regularly.

Will the bot replace salespeople?

No. It handles first replies and triage; selling stays with people.

How long does it take to launch?

A simple version takes a few days, but allow 1–2 weeks for the knowledge base and testing.

Conclusion

A Telegram AI assistant helps you stop losing leads at night and at weekends: first replies, lead collection and triage. Success needs three things: a clear knowledge base, hand-over rules and regular reviews.
