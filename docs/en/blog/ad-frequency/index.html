Ad frequency: how many times should one person see your ad?

An ad seen once is often forgotten. An ad seen ten times becomes irritating. Frequency shows how many times, on average, one person has seen your ad. It tells you whether an ad is fatigued, whether your audience is exhausted or, conversely, whether the ad is barely being seen. Here's how to read and manage frequency.

The formula

Frequency = impressions ÷ reach

An illustrative example: 40,000 impressions and a reach of 10,000 give a frequency of 4. The difference between reach and impressions — reach vs impressions.

The risk of low frequency

A frequency near 1 means many people saw the ad only once. For a cheap product with a quick decision, that may be enough. But expensive, complex services (renovation, education, real estate) need several touches — which is what retargeting does well — how retargeting works.

The risk of high frequency

When an ad is shown too often to the same people:

  • CTR falls;
  • CPM and cost per lead rise;
  • negative comments and "hide ad" clicks increase.

These are signs of creative fatigue — creative fatigue.

A guide by goal

GoalFrequency direction
Leads from a new audienceLower — more new people
Retargeting (site visitors)Higher — but for a limited time
Brand awarenessModerate and steady
A short promotionHigher over a short period

These are directions, not numbers: decide in each account based on your own data.

How to manage frequency

  1. Refresh creatives: with several different creatives, people see a different one each time — Meta Andromeda.
  2. Broaden the audience: narrow audiences get used up quickly.
  3. Adjust the budget: a big budget on a small audience means high frequency.
  4. Exclusions: remove people who've already submitted a lead or bought.
  5. Frequency caps in reach campaigns: some campaign types let you cap how often ads are shown.

What to watch

Don't look at frequency alone — watch it with CTR and cost per lead. If frequency rises while CTR falls and leads get pricier, it's time to act.

Questions and answers

What's a "normal" frequency?

There's no universal number. The frequency at which CTR and cost per lead start to worsen in your account is your limit.

Where can I see frequency?

In Meta Ads Manager, add the "Frequency" column at campaign, ad set or ad level.

Do organic posts have frequency?

Yes — the ratio of views to reach in Instagram insights shows exactly that.

Conclusion

Frequency tells you how many times your ad has been shown to one person. Too low and it isn't remembered; too high and it wears people out.
