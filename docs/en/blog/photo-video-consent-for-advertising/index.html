Your sales assistant showed a product in a Reel, a happy customer posed in the showroom, a technician walked into the frame mid-job. From the day those videos go into ads, one question matters: do you have photo and video consent for advertising, and can you prove it? Below is what the law says today, who gives consent and how, the 8 points the text needs, and what happens to the video when an employee leaves.

This article is not legal advice. For a large campaign or a dispute, show your documents to a lawyer.

Photo and video consent for advertising: what the law says today

Two laws in force matter to a business owner in Uzbekistan.

Law "On Advertising", Article 16. Using a person's name or image in advertising without their consent is prohibited (lex.uz). "Advertising" is broad here: Instagram ads, a Telegram channel post, a banner, a photo on your website. We covered the law's other bans in Advertising law in Uzbekistan.

Law "On Personal Data", Article 21. Consent is given in a form that lets you confirm it was obtained; for a minor, parents or a guardian give it. Consent is tied to a purpose and can be withdrawn. More in Customer database and personal data.

Three practical conclusions follow:

  1. Consent is collected before the ad runs, not after a complaint.
  2. Proving consent is your job. A spoken "sure" is worth nothing in a dispute.
  3. Consent is given for a specific purpose. "A photo for the staff group chat" is not consent for advertising.

Employee consent: the employment contract is not enough

Many managers think "they work for us, so we can film them". But an employment contract sets out job duties, not that the employee's face will be on a banner. The right way is a separate written consent.

Three rules:

  • Voluntary. Forcing people on camera gives you bad video and bad morale. Film those who speak naturally — see Employees in your content.
  • The right to say no. "I don't want to be filmed" is not a disciplinary issue.
  • Leaving is covered in advance. This is where most disputes start.

What happens to the video when an employee leaves

Pick one of three options in the consent text:

OptionWhat it meansBest for
Immediate stopFrom the last working day the material is not used in paid adsStaff close to a personal brand: salesperson, doctor, craftsman
Transition periodAfter leaving, ads are replaced within an agreed time (for example, 3 months)A comfortable middle path for most businesses
Fixed-term consentConsent runs until a set date whether or not the person still works thereBig shoots, outdoor advertising

Illustrative example: a furniture showroom runs 6 ads featuring a salesperson. He resigns and asks for the videos to be taken down. If the consent says "3-month transition period", the showroom shoots new videos in that time and swaps the old ones calmly. If nothing is written, the ads may have to stop the same day.

Agree on old posts too: usually paid ads stop while old posts stay on the page. Write that down as well.

8 points the consent text needs

One page is enough. What matters is precise answers, not general wording.

  1. Who gives consent — full name; for a child, the parent or guardian.
  2. To whom — your company name. Even if an agency does the shoot, consent is made out to your company.
  3. What is recorded — photo, video, voice, whether name and job title are shown.
  4. Where it is used — Instagram and Facebook page, paid ads, Telegram channel, website, outdoor advertising. List each one.
  5. For how long — a specific date or "period of employment + 3 months".
  6. Editing — whether you may cut, add subtitles, or include it in other videos.
  7. Payment — free or paid. For employees it is usually free, but it must be stated.
  8. How to withdraw — who to write to and within how many days the ad stops.

Sample (adapt it to your business):

I, [full name], consent to [company name] using my image, voice and name in photos and videos taken on [date] 2026 on the company's Instagram, Facebook and Telegram pages, in paid advertising and on its website. This consent is valid until [date]. The materials may be cut and subtitled. Consent is given free of charge. I may withdraw it by written notice; in that case the company will stop paid advertising within [N] working days.

Customers, children and people in the background

Customers. Ask for consent on the day of the shoot, while the mood is good. If paper is awkward, send the text in Telegram and save the "I agree" reply — the date and sender are visible. Have them say it on camera too: "I agree to this video being used in advertising." The interview questions are in Customer testimonial video.

Clinics and salons. Here a picture says something about a person's health or appearance. For "before and after" photos always take separate written consent and state exactly which pictures will be used.

Children. A learning centre, a kindergarten, a kids' clothing shop — a parent or guardian gives consent. A convenient way: a separate sheet signed with the contract at the start of the school year. The camera operator and the SMM manager should have the list of parents who said no.

People in the background. When you film inside a shop, customers end up in the frame. Three ways out: film when the shop is empty, frame the shot so faces are not visible, or blur them in editing. A "Filming in progress today" sign at the door helps, but it does not replace the consent of a clearly recognisable person.

The new bill: what may change

Parliament has considered a separate bill making consent mandatory for filming a person and using their image. According to Spot.uz, the Legislative Chamber passed it on 7 October 2025 and sent it to the Senate. The bill says:

  • consent is required to film a person and to use their image;
  • for a child under 16, one parent or a guardian gives consent;
  • there is a list of exceptions: public places and mass events, images the person made public themselves, an employer filming the work process, and others;
  • places with surveillance cameras need a warning sign; cameras in changing rooms and toilets are banned.

Important: as of the day this article was written, we could not find the signed text of the law in open sources. Treat the above as a direction, not a rule in force, and check the status on lex.uz.

One more note of caution: the "filming the work process" exception in the bill is about filming itself. Use in advertising still falls under Article 16 of the Law "On Advertising". The safe path is one: consent for every face that appears in an ad.

How to store consents, and 5 mistakes

A consent you cannot find is the same as no consent. Keep a simple table:

WhoConsent dateWhere it is usedValid untilWhere the document is
Salesperson (employee)17.11.2026Instagram, paid ads, websiteEmployment + 3 months"Consents" folder, scan
Customer (video review)17.11.2026Instagram, paid ads17.11.2027Telegram chat, screenshot

The rows are an illustrative example. Share the folder with your agency or SMM manager: they need to know which video can be used until when.

Common mistakes:

  1. Spoken consent. A year later nobody remembers who said what.
  2. "Everything, forever." A text with no term and no limits scares people and looks weak in a dispute.
  3. Consent made out to the agency. Change agencies and the document does not stay with you.
  4. A former employee still in the ads. Old creatives run in the ad account for months — check the list on the day someone leaves.
  5. No consent for a child. The most common case at learning centres.

Shoot-day checklist

  • A list of everyone who will be in the frame
  • A consent sheet printed for each person, or the electronic text ready
  • Parents' consents for children collected in advance
  • The operator knows who must not be filmed
  • Signed sheets scanned and entered in the table
  • The expiry date added to the calendar

Questions and answers

Is spoken consent enough?

You will have to prove consent, and spoken words cannot be proven. The minimum is a clear "I agree" in a chat or a confirmation on video; the best is a signed sheet.

What do we do if an employee withdraws consent?

Stop paid ads and replace the creative within the time stated in the consent. That is why agreeing a transition period in advance matters: it protects both sides.

Can we run an ad with a video the customer posted to their own Stories?

Reposting and using it in paid ads are two different things. Ask the customer for separate consent for advertising. We also covered this in UGC content.

Is consent needed if the face is not visible?

Hands, a shot from behind or an unrecognisable background usually cause no trouble. But if the person can be recognised by voice, a tattoo or their workplace, it is better to get consent.

Do we have to pay an employee for consent?

In the provisions we checked there is no such requirement. But whether there is payment or not must be stated in the text, or later you get an "I was promised" dispute.

Conclusion

Photo and video consent is a one-page document, but it saves you from the disputes that take ads down. Get written consent in advance from everyone who appears in advertising, state the channels, term and withdrawal procedure, write a separate rule for when an employee leaves, and keep everything in one table. If the new law is adopted, requirements will only get stricter — a business that sets this up now will be ready.
