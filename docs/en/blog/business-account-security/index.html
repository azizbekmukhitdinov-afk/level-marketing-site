Business account security: protecting Instagram and Telegram

A business's Instagram page or Telegram account holds an audience built over years, customer conversations and a sales channel. Losing it is like handing a stranger the keys to your shop. Hacks are common: fake "copyright violation" messages, links from "Meta Support" and requests for SMS codes. Here's how to protect your business accounts.

7 core rules

  1. Two-step verification — mandatory on Instagram, Facebook and Telegram. An authenticator app is more secure than SMS.
  2. A Telegram cloud password — set one under "Two-Step Verification", so that even a stolen SMS code isn't enough to log in.
  3. Never share codes — staff at Meta, Telegram or your bank will never ask for an SMS code.
  4. Company-owned accounts — use the company's email and phone number, not employees' personal ones.
  5. Access rights, not passwords — don't give passwords to agencies or staff; assign roles through Meta Business — give an agency access to your Meta ad account.
  6. At least two admins — if one loses access or gets blocked, the other can recover it.
  7. Recovery details — store backup codes and keep your email and phone up to date.

How to spot a scam message

SignExample
Urgency"Your page will be deleted within 24 hours"
An official lookAn account named "Meta Support" or "Instagram Copyright"
A linkA suspicious domain: meta-support-…, instagram-verify-…
A request for dataPassword, SMS code, two-step code
A "gift""A free blue tick for your page"

Official messages appear in the Security section of the Instagram app — not in your DMs.

Meta Business: keeping order

  • The business portfolio (Business Manager) belongs to the company owner.
  • Each employee and agency gets only the roles and assets they need.
  • Review the access list every quarter: who can log in?
  • Restrict who can add payment methods to the ads account — ad account blocked.

When an employee leaves

Add digital steps to your offboarding process:

  • remove all their roles from Meta Business;
  • change shared passwords;
  • end active sessions on work Telegram accounts;
  • check that conversations and customers remain in the CRM — CRM for small business.

If you're hacked: the first hour

  1. Instagram: request recovery at instagram.com/hacked, or use the "this wasn't me" link in Instagram's email.
  2. Facebook and Meta Business: facebook.com/hacked and the business help centre.
  3. Telegram: end unknown sessions under "Devices"; if you've lost access, contact Telegram support.
  4. Warn customers through other channels (your Telegram channel, Stories): "If anyone asks for payment in our name, don't pay."
  5. The ads account: check payment methods and pause suspicious campaigns.

Security is part of social media management

Your contract with an agency or social media manager should state who owns the account, who has which rights and how access is returned when the work ends. That's part of marketing too — the first 30 days with a marketing agency.

Questions and answers

Does a blue tick (Meta Verified) improve security?

It offers extra support and protection features, but without two-step verification and proper access rights, it guarantees nothing.

Can the whole team share one phone number?

A company number, yes — but each person needs their own access rights.

Where should passwords be kept?

In a password manager — not in a notebook or a chat.

Conclusion

Business account security means two-step verification, a Telegram cloud password, roles instead of passwords, two admins, recognising scams and a clear process when staff leave.
