Click-to-Direct ads are among the most common ad formats in Uzbekistan: a person taps the ad and lands in a chat instead of on a website. In late September 2026 Meta changed an important rule for this format: the window in which a business can message someone who came from an ad grew from 24 hours to up to 7 days. Here is how the window works, who benefits and how to plan that week.

What Click-to-Direct ads and the "window" are

A Click-to-Direct ad is an ad whose button opens an Instagram conversation. On Facebook the same format opens Messenger. It is easy for the customer: no form to fill in, no website to wait for, they simply ask their question.

Messaging on Meta's platform has its own rule. A business cannot message a user whenever it likes, only while the standard messaging window is open. The customer opens it by sending a message or tapping a button. From that moment the business has a set time to reply. It used to be the same for every conversation: 24 hours.

In its 28 September 2026 update Meta wrote that the standard messaging window may extend up to 7 days for conversations that start from these ads, that the period starts when the person sends their first message after clicking the ad, and that conversations started through other entry points stay within 24 hours (Meta for Developers changelog).

How the 7-day window works: 5 rules

1. The clock starts with the first message, not the click. If someone taps the ad and leaves without writing, no window opens at all.

2. It covers two ad types only. Ads that open Instagram Direct and ads that open Messenger. The Message button on your profile, story replies, chats that follow a comment and conversations started from a link still get 24 hours.

3. "Up to 7 days" is not a guarantee. Meta's documentation says the window "may" last up to 7 days and does not explain when it would be shorter. Do not leave your important message for day seven.

4. Offers are allowed inside the window. Meta's messaging policy says messages sent within the standard window may contain promotional content. The same section reminds you that people can block or mute a business at any time.

5. A new customer message opens a new window. Even after the seven days, every new message from the customer opens the usual 24-hour window.

Do not confuse the third column with the second. The human agent tag has existed for a long time: it lets a staff member resolve a customer's question manually after 24 hours, for example when the request arrived on a day off. It is a support tool, not a sales one.

Who the rule applies to

This is where most people get confused. The policy covers the Messenger Platform and the Instagram Messaging API, in other words the tools connected to your Direct inbox: CRMs, chatbots, shared inbox services and automated message sequences.

  • You run Direct only from the phone app. When a staff member replies manually, they usually do not run into this limit. Technically little changes for you, but the follow-up plan below still applies.
  • Direct is connected to a CRM or a bot. Before, if a customer stayed silent for a day, the service could no longer message them. Now, in a chat that started from an ad, a reminder, a quote or an offer can go out within the week.

Check your service's documentation or support to see whether it supports the new window. Some tools still close every conversation after 24 hours.

The first reply: greeting and speed

A longer window does not replace a fast reply. Interest peaks the minute a person sees the ad; an hour later they may already be chatting with another shop. We cover this in detail in the article on lead response time.

Three things to set up in the ad:

  • The greeting. The first line the customer sees when the chat opens. Keep it short and name the next step: "Hello! Which model caught your eye? We'll send the price and delivery time."
  • Suggested questions. Two or three questions the customer can send in one tap: "How much is it?", "Do you deliver?", "Where are you located?". They make the first message easier to send, and the window opens with that first message.
  • The auto-reply. A short text that says when you will answer a message that arrived outside working hours. Setup is covered in automated responses in Meta Business Suite.

Menu names in Ads Manager change often, so we do not quote button labels: in a campaign with the messages objective, look for the message template section at the ad level.

A 7-day follow-up plan: illustrative example

The table below is an illustrative example: a furniture shop in Tashkent advertising made-to-order kitchens. The days and texts are a template; adapt them to your own sales cycle.

Day 0, first minutesA greeting and one specific question: kitchen dimensions or a photo of the roomBot or manager

Three rules for the plan:

  1. No more than 3–4 messages in the week. Writing every day is the shortest road to a block.
  2. Every message brings something new. Instead of "Have you decided?", send a photo, a quote or a specific time slot.
  3. Make the main offer on day 3–4, not day seven: if the customer replies, a new window opens and the conversation continues.

If the customer stalls at "I'll think about it", the 14-day follow-up plan covers the next steps.

What to check when you connect a CRM or a bot

  • Does the service show where the chat came from: an ad or a regular message? Meta passes a source marker and the ad ID with a message that came from an ad. If the service can read them, you will see which ad started the conversation.
  • Does every chat have an owner and a date for the next step?
  • Do the reminders fit inside 7 days rather than slipping to day eight?
  • Is the chat handed to a person when the bot cannot answer?
  • Does the sequence stop when the customer says "not interested"?
  • Are unanswered chats reviewed once a week? The routine is in our unanswered Instagram DM audit.

5 common mistakes

  1. Replying slowly because "we have a week now". The window is a second chance, not permission to delay the first reply.
  2. Treating every chat as a 7-day one. Chats from the profile, stories and comments still get 24 hours.
  3. Sending the same offer every day. The customer will block you, and that does the account no good.
  4. Sending promotions under the human agent tag. It is for support only. Misuse can lead to messaging restrictions.
  5. Counting everyone who tapped the ad as a lead. No message, no window. Judge the ad by conversations started and sales, not by clicks.

Questions and answers

Do I need to switch anything on in Ads Manager to get the 7-day window? No. It is a platform rule, not a setting. If the message came from an ad that opens Direct or Messenger, it applies automatically.

Is it the same for ads that open WhatsApp? WhatsApp has its own rules and they are structured differently. This article covers Instagram Direct and Messenger only.

If the customer writes again mid-week, do the 7 days restart? Meta's documentation does not say. To be safe, count from the first message. Any new customer message does open at least a 24-hour window.

Which is better: Click-to-Direct ads or a lead form? It depends on your sales process. We compare them in Lead form or Direct messages.

Seven days have passed and the customer has not replied. What now? You can no longer message them through a connected service. You can bring them back with retargeting or wait for them to write.

Conclusion

Click-to-Direct ads now give you up to a week of conversation instead of a day, but only if the customer sent a first message and only in a chat that started from an ad. To make that time work you need three things: a greeting that makes it easy to write, a fast first reply and 3–4 useful messages across the week.
