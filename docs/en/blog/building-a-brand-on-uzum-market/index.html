When dozens of sellers offer the same product on a marketplace, competition comes down to price and profit disappears. The way out is building your own brand. Here is how to move from reselling to a brand on marketplaces like Uzum Market.

Why a brand protects you from price wars

  • Your own listing. Other sellers can't sell your branded product under the same name.
  • Reviews accumulate with you. Every rating works for your product.
  • Repeat purchases. Customers remember the brand and search for it again.
  • You set the price. People pay more for a brand they know.

Steps

  1. Your own product. Your own production, or ordering from a manufacturer under your name (private label).
  2. Name and packaging. A memorable name and recognisable packaging — rules in how to name a business.
  3. Register a trademark. In Uzbekistan this goes through the Intellectual Property Agency — it protects your name from being copied.
  4. A strong listing. A keyword-rich title, quality photos and infographics, video, a clear description and specs.
  5. Work with reviews. Answer every question and review — more in our online reputation article.
  6. External traffic. Bring people straight to your listing from Instagram, Telegram and ads.
  7. Analytics. Track views, add-to-carts, sales and returns.

Listing checklist

ElementRequirement
Main photoWhite or clean background, product clearly visible
Extra photosSize, material, use, packaging
Infographics3–5 key benefits
VideoThe product in action
TitleType + brand + key feature
DescriptionWho it's for, why, how to care for it

Why external traffic matters

Relying only on internal search is hard when competition inside the marketplace is fierce. Buyers coming from Instagram and Telegram often improve your sales history, which may also help the listing's visibility on the platform. For setting up Instagram ads, see our separate guide.

Common mistakes

  • Using competitors' photos.
  • Ignoring negative reviews.
  • Not tracking stock — when a product runs out, the listing loses its position.
  • Selling only through discounts.

A sample product title and description

  • Title: "Electric kettle [Brand] 1.7 l, stainless steel, auto shut-off"
  • Description structure: who it's for → 3–5 key advantages → specifications (capacity, power, material) → care → warranty and what's in the box.

Put the words buyers search for in the title, and answers to their questions in the description.

Profit per sale: a simple calculation

Turnover on a marketplace can look big, but profit is another matter. Calculate it for every product.

An example (illustrative numbers): price 200,000 sum, cost of goods 90,000, marketplace commission and logistics 50,000, advertising 20,000. The profit from one sale is 40,000 sum. Add returns and discounts, and the figure shrinks further.

Commission and logistics rates vary by category — take them from the current terms in your seller account. How to measure ad efficiency is covered in How to calculate ROMI.

Improving the product from reviews

Once a month, group negative reviews and questions:

ComplaintWhat to do
"Smaller than I expected"Add a size chart and a comparison photo to the listing
"The colour differs from the photo"Reshoot the photos in daylight
"The packaging arrived crushed"Make the packaging sturdier
"I didn't understand how to use it"Add short instructions and a video

Fixing a recurring complaint helps your rating more than asking for new reviews.

Launch: the first 30 days

PeriodWhat to do
Week 1A complete listing: all photos, a video, a description; a small stock
Week 2Ask the first buyers for reviews; bring outside traffic from Instagram and Telegram
Weeks 3–4Analyse view → cart → order conversion; fix photos, title or price
OngoingTrack stock and restock before the product runs out

The first month is a test: the goal isn't big sales but understanding how the listing and the price perform.

Questions and answers

Can you sell without registering a trademark?

You can, but it's risky: another seller may use your name or register it before you. Some marketplaces may also ask for documents before showing a brand name — check the terms in advance.

Do you need your own website as well as the marketplace?

Not at the start: Instagram and Telegram are enough to build brand awareness. Once you have more repeat customers, your own website or Telegram shop can become a commission-free sales channel.

How many products should you start with?

One to three. Test demand with a small batch first, then expand the range — starting with a large batch can freeze your money in the warehouse.

Conclusion

A brand on a marketplace is the way out of price wars and toward stable profit. Your own product, a registered name, a strong listing and external traffic are the four main steps. To find what sets you apart, read our USP article.
