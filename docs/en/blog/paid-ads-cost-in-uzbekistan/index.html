"How much do paid ads cost?" is the question business owners ask most. The honest answer: there is no single price. But once you know what the budget consists of and how to calculate it for your business, you can say exactly how much to invest.

What the costs consist of

Many people only ask about the specialist's fee, but the total cost has three parts:

CostWhat you pay forPaid to
Ad budgetShowing ads to peopleMeta, Google or TikTok directly
Specialist or agency feeStrategy, setup, optimisation, reportingThe specialist or agency
ContentVideo, photo, design, editingVideographer, designer or agency

Keep the ad budget and the fee separate: a good specialist decides how to spend the budget but does not replace it.

How to calculate the ad budget

Start not with "how much money do I have" but with "how many customers do I need". You need four numbers:

  1. New customers needed per month.
  2. Lead-to-sale conversion — how many out of 10 leads buy.
  3. Expected cost per lead (CPL) — from your experience or a test.
  4. Average order value — how much one customer brings.

The formula is simple:

Ad budget = customers needed ÷ conversion × cost per lead

Example (illustrative numbers): a furniture store needs 20 new buyers a month, 2 out of 10 leads buy (20%), and the test CPL was $3. That means 20 ÷ 0.2 = 100 leads, and 100 × $3 = $300 of ad budget. With a $400 average order, those $300 bring $8,000 in sales — a calculation that immediately shows whether to scale.

What affects cost per lead

The same budget gives very different results in different businesses. The main factors:

  • Industry and competition. Real estate, education and healthcare have more competition for impressions, so leads can cost more.
  • Product price. People think longer about expensive products — leads cost more, but each sale earns more.
  • Geography. Tashkent only versus the whole country changes audience size and price.
  • Creative quality. A strong video and a clear offer can change CPL several times over.
  • Season. Impressions get more expensive before holidays and in seasonal peaks.

Can you start with a minimal budget?

Yes, but the test needs enough data. On a tiny budget the algorithm does not have time to learn and you cannot draw conclusions. A practical rule: in the test phase, let each creative or audience collect at least a few dozen results (clicks, messages or leads) before adding budget to the winners.

How specialist fees are structured

There are three common models:

  1. Fixed monthly fee — a set amount per project.
  2. Percentage of ad spend — the fee grows as the budget grows.
  3. Hybrid — a base fee plus a bonus for results.

Whatever the model, clarify what you pay for: how many campaigns, who makes the creatives and how often you get a report. Our article on choosing a specialist lists 9 questions to ask.

How to keep spending under control

  • Have your own access to the ad account — it should belong to your business.
  • Get a monthly report with spend, number of leads and CPL.
  • Mark leads in a CRM or spreadsheet: which ones became sales.
  • Compare results against sales, not just numbers in the ad account.

The cost of content: where the money goes

  • A shoot day — several videos and photos in one day.
  • Editing — separately for each video.
  • Design templates — made once for posts and Stories.
  • Customer videos (UGC) — often cheaper and more convincing than a studio production.

Plan at least 3–6 new creatives a month: ads wear out quickly on old videos.

Splitting the budget between platforms

Put most of the budget where your customers are. A simple scheme to start: about 70% on the main platform and 30% on a second one as a test. After a month, adjust the split based on cost per sale. Telegram ads are covered in Telegram advertising.

Payments and currency

In Meta you choose the ad account currency, and payment is usually made by card. Bank fees, currency conversion and taxes can add to the total cost — check the payment documents in your account. Some businesses find it easier to pay through an agency.

When to raise the budget

When cost per lead has stayed below target for several weeks, the sales team can handle more leads and ROMI is positive. Raise the budget in stages — a sharp increase can make results unstable.

Common mistakes

  • Counting only the ad budget and forgetting content and service fees.
  • Choosing the cheapest contractor.
  • No budget for creatives.
  • Judging results after 2–3 days.
  • Splitting a small budget across too many campaigns.

What must be ready before you run ads

A profile or website, a clear offer, a CRM or spreadsheet for leads, someone to answer them and at least 3 creatives. If any of these is missing, the ad budget may bring leads, but they won't turn into sales.

Questions and answers

What's the minimum amount for Instagram ads?

Meta's minimum daily budget is very small, but a meaningful test needs enough for each variant to collect a few dozen results. Otherwise your conclusions will be random.

Is Telegram advertising cheaper?

It depends on the niche and the channels. Compare platforms by cost per lead and per sale, not by cost per click or impression.

How long should ads run?

Tests run for 1–2 weeks; main campaigns run continuously and get refreshed with new creatives. Stopping and starting ads often makes results worse.

Can you compare your cost per lead with competitors'?

With caution. Cost per lead depends on the offer, the creative, the location and the sales process. The most reliable benchmark is your own results from previous months.

What happens when the budget runs out?

The ads stop, and the campaigns may have to go through learning again. Keep an eye on your balance and daily limit in advance.

How do you calculate the cost of acquiring one customer?

Divide all marketing costs — ads, services, content — by the number of new customers. This figure should be lower than the profit you make from one customer.

When will the money spent on ads come back?

It depends on the sales cycle. For quick-decision products, within a few weeks; for services people think over for a long time, after months. So calculate ROMI month by month, based on when customers arrived.

What happens if you cut the budget?

Leads will drop too. Don't cut every campaign equally — switch off the weakest and keep the best.

Conclusion

The cost of paid ads is the sum of the ad budget, the specialist's fee and content. When you calculate the budget from the customers you need, conversion and CPL, advertising stops being an expense and becomes a predictable investment.
