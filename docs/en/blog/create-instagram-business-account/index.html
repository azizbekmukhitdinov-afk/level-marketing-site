How to create an Instagram business account: step-by-step setup

A business's Instagram page starts like an ordinary personal account, but without the right setup it has no analytics, no contact buttons and no ad options. Setting it up properly once saves a lot of trouble later: blocked ad accounts, lost access, or a "company page" that's really an employee's personal account. Here's how to open an Instagram business account step by step.

Step 1: create the account in the company's name

  • Email and phone should belong to the company, not an employee.
  • Username: short, easy to read, close to the brand name — @brand_uz, @brand.tashkent.
  • Name field: brand plus a keyword, for search — Instagram bio examples.

Step 2: switch to a professional account

In settings, under "Account type and tools", switch to a professional account and choose the type:

For a company, choose Business.

Step 3: category and contacts

  • Category: "Furniture store", "Dentist" — shown on your profile.
  • Contacts: phone, address, Telegram link.
  • Links: up to 5 in your bio, with UTM tags — UTM tags.

Step 4: connect to Meta Business

For ads and security, link Instagram to a Facebook Page and a Meta business portfolio — Meta business portfolio setup. You need this:

  • to advertise from the ads account;
  • to give staff and agencies access without sharing the password;
  • to get support if something goes wrong with the account.

Step 5: security

  • Two-step verification — straight away.
  • At least two admins (via Meta Business).
  • Saved backup codes.

More in business account security.

Step 6: complete the profile

  • A logo that's legible even when small.
  • A bio covering what, where, why you and the next step.
  • Highlights: prices, work, reviews, delivery, contacts — an Instagram profile that sells.

Step 7: the first 9 posts

New visitors look at the grid first. Your first 9 posts should show who you are, the product or service, results, a review, the team, and a price or offer. Start with Reels, since they're shown to new people — Reels hooks.

Questions and answers

Can I turn a personal account into a business one?

Yes — you can switch to a professional account in settings. But check that old personal content suits a business reputation.

Does a business account reduce reach?

There's no official rule saying so. Reach depends on content quality — how the Instagram algorithm works.

One account for several branches?

Usually one main account is enough; show branches in highlights and addresses.

Conclusion

An Instagram business account needs company email and phone, the professional "Business" type, the right category and contacts, a link to Meta Business, two-step verification and well-planned first posts. In a free audit, Level Marketing will check your page's setup.
