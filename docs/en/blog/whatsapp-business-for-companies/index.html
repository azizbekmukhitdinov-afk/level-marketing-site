In Uzbekistan customers mostly write on Telegram, so many business owners never think about WhatsApp Business. But if your customer is in Kazakhstan, Turkey, Europe or the Gulf, they will message you on WhatsApp, not Telegram. Here is who needs WhatsApp Business, how to set it up and how to stop losing leads in it.

When you need WhatsApp Business and when you don't

You don't have to give up Telegram. The real question is whether some of your customers are on WhatsApp. If they are, they need a separate, well-organised channel.

BusinessWhy WhatsApp matters
Exporting manufacturerBuyers from Turkey, Kazakhstan and the Middle East use WhatsApp
Hotel, guesthouseForeign tourists book and ask questions via WhatsApp
Travel agency, guideChatting with clients from abroad
Services for the diasporaUzbeks in the US, Europe and Korea often use WhatsApp
Medical tourism, clinicStaying in touch with patients from abroad
Local shop, café, salonTelegram is usually enough

Be careful with customers in Russia: in February 2026 the Russian authorities moved to block WhatsApp, and the company said so publicly (The Moscow Times). Ask in the first conversation which messenger works for them.

The specifics of selling to dealers and export buyers are covered in marketing for manufacturers. Here we focus on the messenger itself.

How WhatsApp Business differs from regular WhatsApp

WhatsApp Business is a separate free app for companies. Compared with regular WhatsApp it adds:

  • a business profile — address, opening hours, description, website, email;
  • a catalogue — products or services with photos, prices and descriptions;
  • a greeting message — an automatic reply to new customers;
  • an away message — an automatic reply outside working hours;
  • quick replies — ready-made answers to frequent questions, called up with "/";
  • labels — colour-coded groups for chats.

These features are described on WhatsApp's official page (WhatsApp Business). Some may vary by country and app version — check in your own app.

One important limit: a phone number works on either regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business at a time, not both. Don't mix your personal number with the business — get a separate company number.

Set up the profile in 20 minutes

  1. A separate number. A SIM card registered to the company or a corporate number. If an employee leaves, the number stays with the business.
  2. Name and logo. The name customers see on their phone should be the brand name; the photo, a clear logo.
  3. Description. One or two sentences: what you do and for whom. For foreign customers, write it in English or Russian.
  4. Opening hours and time zone. State Tashkent time — a foreign customer will notice the difference.
  5. Address and website. A website link and a map location.
  6. Catalogue. At least your 10–20 main products or services with photos and short descriptions.

Illustrative example: a guesthouse in Samarkand adds its room types, breakfast and an airport transfer to the catalogue. When a tourist writes "how much?", the administrator sends the catalogue with one tap and replies with a question: "Which dates, and for how many people?"

Quick replies and automatic messages

Automatic messages keep customers from waiting, but they don't replace a salesperson.

The greeting should be short and specific:

  • who you are;
  • when you'll reply;
  • a first question to start the conversation.

For example: "Hello! This is a furniture factory in Tashkent. A manager will reply within 15 minutes. What are you interested in: office or hotel furniture?"

The away message should state your hours and when you'll reply. For foreign customers, include the time zone.

Prepare 8–12 quick replies:

  • for price questions (a range plus a clarifying question);
  • delivery and payment terms;
  • minimum order;
  • address and opening hours;
  • a link to the catalogue or price list;
  • an invitation to a meeting or call.

How to answer "How much?" is covered in a separate article — turn those 5 steps into quick replies. Automatic replies for Instagram and Facebook are set up in Meta Business Suite; keep the WhatsApp Business texts in the same tone.

Labels: so chats don't get lost

With 20–30 conversations a day it's hard to remember who asked what. Labels work as a simple funnel:

LabelMeaningNext step
NewFirst message receivedReply within 15 minutes
Price sentOffer madeFollow up in 1–2 days
Sample / meetingHigh interestAgree on a date
Awaiting paymentDeal agreedInvoice, reminder
CustomerHas boughtContact about the next order

Review the "Price sent" label every evening — that's where most money is lost. When conversations pile up, move contacts into a CRM and follow the rules for storing customer data — see customer databases and personal data.

Ads that open a WhatsApp chat

In Meta Ads Manager, campaigns built around messages can use WhatsApp as the destination: a person taps the ad and a chat with you opens in WhatsApp. This is especially handy when advertising to foreign audiences — for a buyer in Turkey or Kazakhstan WhatsApp is the familiar channel.

Before launching, check:

  • whether your WhatsApp Business number is connected to your Facebook Page;
  • whether you have a ready greeting and first question;
  • who replies and when — ads keep running outside working hours;
  • how results are counted: not the number of chats, but qualified chats and sales.

"Conversations started" in Ads Manager are not customers yet. If the sales team labels every chat, you can see which audiences bring real orders. Availability and terms for messaging ads can differ by country — check in Ads Manager.

Telegram or WhatsApp: running both

Two messengers don't mean double the work if the rules are shared:

  • one response standard — a reply within 15 minutes during working hours on both channels;
  • one set of quick replies — the same texts in Telegram Business and WhatsApp Business (Telegram setup is in our Telegram Business guide);
  • one owner — it's written down who watches which channel;
  • one spreadsheet or CRM — wherever a customer writes from, they end up on one list.

Put both buttons on your website and Instagram profile: "Message us on Telegram" and "Message us on WhatsApp". For foreign audiences, put the WhatsApp button first.

Business AI: the next step after auto-replies

In June 2026 Meta announced Business Agent: an AI that answers customers on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram, recommends catalogue products and books appointments, while the business decides when a human takes over. Meta said more than 1 million businesses had already used business AIs on WhatsApp and Messenger (US News / Reuters).

There is no reliable information on availability or pricing in Uzbekistan — check in your WhatsApp Business app and Meta Business Suite. But you can prepare now: a complete catalogue, written-down FAQs and answers, and clear rules for when a manager steps in. A similar solution for Telegram is described in A Telegram AI assistant.

Common mistakes

  • Running the business on a personal number — the employee leaves and the customers leave with them.
  • An empty profile — no hours, address or catalogue.
  • A long greeting — nobody reads five lines of text.
  • No labels — "price sent" customers get forgotten.
  • Auto-replies only — the customer waits hours for a real person.
  • Bulk messages without consent — complaints can get the number restricted.

Questions and answers

Is WhatsApp Business paid?

The WhatsApp Business app itself is free. For large volumes and integrations there is the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), with different terms and pricing — check them on Meta's official page or with your provider.

Can I use one number on both Telegram and WhatsApp?

Yes, one number can be used on both messengers. Within WhatsApp, though, it will be on either regular WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, not both at once.

Can several employees use one WhatsApp Business account?

WhatsApp Business can be linked to several devices, so two or three managers can reply from one number. With many managers and heavy traffic, look at the API and a CRM integration.

Does a local business need WhatsApp?

If your customers are only in Uzbekistan, Telegram is usually enough. But add a WhatsApp button to your website and watch for a month how many people use it. The numbers will answer for you.

Conclusion

WhatsApp Business is for companies working with exports, tourism, hotels and the diaspora: that's where foreign customers write. Get a separate number, complete the profile and catalogue, prepare a short greeting and 8–12 quick replies, sort chats with labels and keep one response standard across WhatsApp and Telegram.
