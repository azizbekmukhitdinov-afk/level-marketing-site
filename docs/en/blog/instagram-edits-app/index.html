Instagram's Edits app: editing Reels on a phone for business

Edits is Instagram's standalone video editing app, released free on iOS and Android in April 2025 (Engadget). Its main difference: exported videos carry no watermark, and the tools built for Reels are all in one place. For businesses, it's a way to produce Reels quickly and well on a phone. Here's how to use Edits.

What Edits offers

  • Free — all features were free at launch.
  • Watermark-free export — so videos can go to other platforms too.
  • High-quality recording inside the app.
  • Automatic captions for people watching on mute.
  • Green screen, cutting, effects and AI tools — background replacement, object cut-outs.
  • Music and sound from Instagram's library.
  • Ideas and drafts for planning.

Features keep changing — check the latest list in the app.

A business workflow

  1. Script: 3–5 sentences, with a hook in the first second — Reels script templates.
  2. Shooting: on a phone, in good light — shooting Reels on a phone.
  3. Editing in Edits: trim, set the pace, add captions.
  4. Sound and music: music cleared for business accounts — music for video ads.
  5. Export and post: straight to Instagram, then the watermark-free file to YouTube Shorts and TikTok — YouTube Shorts for business.
  6. Test: post important videos as trial Reels — Instagram Trial Reels.

Tips for businesses

  • A brand template: the same font, colours and caption style keep your page tidy — visual identity for small business.
  • A bank of drafts: 5–10 videos from one shoot, saved as drafts.
  • Several people: the social media manager edits, the founder just approves.
  • Careful with AI effects: don't alter how the product looks — customers must see the truth.

Questions and answers

Does Edits work without Instagram?

The app works with an Instagram account, but you can post the exported file anywhere.

Does it replace desktop editing?

For most Reels, yes. For complex ads and long videos, desktop software is more convenient.

What improves quality more: the app or the shoot?

The shoot: light, sound and a steady camera. Editing can't rescue poor footage.

Conclusion

Instagram Edits lets you produce Reels on a phone quickly, for free and without watermarks. With a script, a good shoot, a brand template and a bank of drafts, it gives a small business a complete video workflow.
