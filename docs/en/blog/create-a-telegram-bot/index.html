A Telegram bot is a 24-hour assistant for your business: it takes enquiries, sends price lists, reports order status and alerts a salesperson. Creating a bot takes minutes; making it useful is a matter of the right flow and integrations. Here's the step-by-step. How bots work for lead generation — a Telegram bot for leads.

Step 1: create the bot with BotFather

  1. Find the official @BotFather in Telegram (it has a blue tick).
  2. Send the /newbot command.
  3. Enter the bot's name — e.g. "Zamon Mebel Assistant" (an illustrative example).
  4. Enter a username, which must end in "bot": @zamonmebel_bot.
  5. BotFather gives you a token — the key to the bot.

Step 2: protect the token

The token gives full control of the bot. So:

  • share it only with a trusted developer or bot builder;
  • never keep it in chats, screenshots or public places;
  • if it leaks, regenerate it in BotFather immediately.

Step 4: the lead flow

A good bot is short:

  1. Greeting: "Hello! We'll price your kitchen in 1 minute."
  2. 2–3 questions: what's needed, size or budget, district.
  3. Phone number via a "Share number" button.
  4. Finish: "A salesperson will call within 15 minutes" — and keep that promise.

More questions mean fewer leads. On questions — lead form questions.

Step 5: menus and buttons

  • A main menu: "Prices", "Catalogue", "Order", "Contact a salesperson".
  • Buttons are easier than typing, especially on a phone.

Step 6: connect to a CRM and your team

Leads mustn't get stuck in the bot:

  • send every lead to the CRM or a sales team group;
  • tag the source: a parameter in the bot link shows which ad the person came from — UTM tags;
  • monitor response speed — lead response time.

The law and privacy

If the bot collects names and phone numbers, that's personal data. You need consent wording and a privacy policy — customer database and personal data. Rules for promotional broadcasts via bots — Telegram bot broadcast rules.

Questions and answers

Is a bot free?

Creating a bot is free. A builder, hosting or developer work may cost money.

Can I connect AI to the bot?

Yes, to answer questions — but prices and complex questions should go to a salesperson — a Telegram AI assistant.

How is a bot different from Telegram Business?

A bot is a separate, automated account. Telegram Business adds business features to a personal account — Telegram Business features.

Conclusion

Creating a Telegram bot takes 5 minutes in BotFather, but results depend on the flow: short questions, a phone button, a CRM connection and fast replies.
