In pharma, advertising is restricted and buyers trust doctors and pharmacists. So in both projects we worked on consumers and professionals at the same time.

Loxen — pain-relief patch

Goal: create a visual identity that reflects the product's uniqueness, communicate its benefits across channels and build interest.

Solution:

  • Showed all of the product's benefits in videos for consumers.
  • Produced expert videos.
  • Ran promotions for medical representatives.
  • Organised an official mailing with commercial offers to major pharmacy chains.

Grippomiks — antiviral powder against colds and flu

Goal: create an identity that reflects the product's uniqueness, communicate its benefits and attract the target audience.

Solution:

  • Shot and distributed videos demonstrating Grippomiks' effectiveness.
  • Produced expert videos featuring doctors.
  • Ran promotions and presentations for medical representatives.
  • Sent pharmacy chains commercial offers focused on the product's effectiveness.

How to apply this approach

  1. Plan the two audiences separately: the consumer, and the professional and pharmacy.
  2. Have doctors take part in expert videos — they're the source of trust.
  3. Don't forget the B2B channel: commercial offers and presentations for pharmacy chains.
  4. Check advertising rules and legal requirements before the campaign.

Who this suits

Pharmaceuticals, medical products and any business whose product reaches consumers through an intermediary. More in Marketing a clinic and Expert interview videos.

Questions and answers

Why isn't consumer advertising enough in pharma?

Buyers often rely on a doctor's or pharmacist's recommendation, and the product has to be on the pharmacy shelf. So working with professionals and pharmacies matters as much as consumer advertising.

What should you be careful about in medical advertising?

Don't guarantee results, don't point directly at the reader's health, and check product claims against official information. Review the legal requirements with a lawyer in advance.

Takeaway

A pharma brand has to win two audiences: the consumer and the pharmacy. Expert videos build trust with both.
