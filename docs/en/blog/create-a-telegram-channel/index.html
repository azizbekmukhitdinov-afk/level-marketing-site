Telegram is Uzbekistan's most popular messenger, and a business channel is a direct line to customers: new collections, promotions and useful tips arrive on their phones as notifications. Creating a channel takes a minute, but turning it into a business tool takes the right setup. Here it is, step by step.

Step 1: create the channel

In Telegram, choose "New Channel":

  • Name: brand + topic, e.g. "Zamon Mebel | Kitchens" (an illustrative example).
  • Description: 2–3 lines — what, for whom, how to order, contacts.
  • Picture: a logo that's legible at small sizes.

Step 2: public or private

For most businesses, a public channel with a short, memorable link.

Step 3: admins and permissions

  • At least two admins, one of them the business owner.
  • Give each admin only the permissions they need: posting, editing, managing members.
  • Two-step verification on their accounts — business account security.

Step 4: a discussion group

Linking a group to the channel adds comments under posts. It's a place for questions and opinions, but it needs moderation (removing spam and ads).

Step 5: the first 10 posts

Newcomers look at the latest posts. To start:

  • 1. Who we are and what the channel will cover.
  • 2–4. Products or services, with photos and prices.
  • 5. A customer review.
  • 6. A useful tip.
  • 7. A behind-the-scenes video.
  • 8. A Q&A.
  • 9. An offer or promotion.
  • 10. How to order — a link to a bot or salesperson.

Step 6: connect it to sales

  • A bot or salesperson link in every post — a Telegram bot for leads.
  • Posts with buttons, published via a bot.
  • A salesperson's account with Telegram Business — Telegram Business features.

A content plan

3–5 posts a week: useful content (40%), products (30%), process and team (20%), offers (10%) — an illustrative split. More in what is content marketing.

Growing subscribers

  • A channel link and QR code on Instagram, your website and signage.
  • An invitation to every buyer.
  • Ads in other channels — advertising in Telegram channels.
  • Telegram Ads — Telegram advertising.

Growth strategy — grow a Telegram channel.

Questions and answers

Channel or group?

In a channel, you talk and subscribers read. In a group, everyone talks. For business, the channel comes first, with a discussion group as an extra.

Can I rename the channel later?

Yes, you can change the name and link, but the old link stops working — update QR codes and ads.

Where are the statistics?

Channel statistics appear in the channel's settings (once it reaches a certain number of subscribers).

Conclusion

Creating a Telegram channel means a name, description, public link, two admins and security, the first 10 posts and a bridge to sales in every post. After that comes regular content and subscriber growth. In a free audit, Level Marketing will review your Telegram and Instagram channels.
