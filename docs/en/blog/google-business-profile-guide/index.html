Google Business Profile: how to add your company to Google Maps

People searching for "… near me" often look not at websites but at the list of businesses on the map. On Google, that list is filled through Google Business Profile. It's free, yet many businesses either haven't created one or filled it in halfway and abandoned it. Here's a step-by-step guide to adding your company to Google Maps and making the profile work in search. For Yandex and 2GIS — add your business to maps.

Step 1: create the profile

  1. Go to business.google.com with a Google account (ideally a company account).
  2. Enter the company name — the real name, with no extra keywords.
  3. Choose the primary category, e.g. "Dentist" or "Furniture store" — one of the most important settings.
  4. Add the address (if customers visit you) or a service area (tradespeople, delivery).
  5. Add a phone number and website.

Step 2: verification

Google checks that the business is real. Google itself offers the verification method — for example, video, phone or another option. Video verification usually shows the signage, entrance and workplace. An unverified profile isn't fully visible.

Step 3: complete the profile

SectionWhat to add
HoursAccurate, including holidays
Additional categories2–4 for your main services
Description2–3 sentences: what, for whom, what sets you apart
Services / productsA list with "from" prices (in soum)
PhotosExterior, interior, team, products — updated regularly
AttributesPayment methods, delivery, accessibility

Step 4: reviews

Reviews are the basis of ranking and trust:

  • ask every happy customer, sharing the review link by QR code or message;
  • reply to every review, especially negative ones — calmly and with a solution — online reputation and reviews;
  • never buy fake reviews — Google's policies prohibit it, and your profile may be restricted.

Ranking higher: 3 factors

Google ranks local results mainly on three things: relevance (category and information matching the query), distance and prominence (reviews, activity, mentions online). So a complete profile, the right category and reviews are your main levers.

Measuring results

Profile statistics show search views, calls, direction requests and website clicks. Add a UTM tag to your website link — UTM tags.

Common mistakes

  • Stuffing keywords into the name: "Furniture Tashkent cheap kitchens" breaks the rules.
  • Wrong or outdated opening hours.
  • A single photo from 2019.
  • No replies to reviews.
  • Several profiles for one business.

Questions and answers

Is Google Business Profile paid?

No, it's free.

What if I don't have an office?

Set a service area — the address is hidden, but the profile appears in that area.

What if I have several branches?

Create a separate profile for each branch, with its own address and phone number — marketing a second branch.

Conclusion

Google Business Profile is free and one of the most powerful local marketing tools: the right category, complete information, fresh photos, reviews and replies. Fill it in once and update it monthly. In a free audit, Level Marketing will check your maps profiles.
