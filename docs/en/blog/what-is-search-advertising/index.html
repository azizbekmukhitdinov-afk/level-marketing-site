What is search advertising: search and display ads explained

Contextual advertising is advertising shown to match a person's search query or the content (context) of the page they're reading. The most familiar example: you type "custom kitchen" into Google and see listings marked "Ad" above the results. These ads appear at the moment of need — which is why they often bring the warmest leads. Here's what it is and how it works. A practical guide for Tashkent — search ads in Tashkent.

Two types

Search adsDisplay ads
WhereGoogle and Yandex search resultsWebsites, apps, YouTube and more
When shownWhen someone types a queryWhen someone views a related page, or based on interests
IntentHigh — actively searchingLow to medium
FormatText adsBanners, images, video

For most businesses, the starting point is search ads.

How it works: the auction

Every search triggers an auction for the ad slots. Who appears higher depends not only on the bid but on ad quality: relevance to the query, expected click-through rate and landing page quality. So a good ad can rank higher even with a lower bid.

What you pay for

With search ads you usually pay per click — if the ad is shown but not clicked, you aren't charged. Cost per click — what is CPC.

Google and Yandex

  • Google Ads — the most-used search engine in Uzbekistan — how to set up Google Ads.
  • Yandex Direct — extra reach, especially for Russian-language queries — Yandex Direct in Uzbekistan.

Who it suits

  • Services and products people actively search for: renovation, tradespeople, clinics, legal services, education, auto parts.
  • Urgent needs: "call a plumber", "tow truck".
  • B2B: "wholesale", "supplier".

Less suitable: new products people don't know about yet — paid social works better for those — Google Ads vs Meta Ads.

Pros and cons

Pros: high intent, precise measurement, a fast start and a small minimum budget.

Cons: demand is limited by search volume; clicks on competitive keywords are expensive; you need a website or landing page — a landing page that converts.

Questions and answers

How does search advertising differ from SEO?

Search ads are paid and fast; SEO earns free rankings but takes long-term work — what is SEO.

Are there searches in Uzbek?

Yes, and they're growing; account for Latin and Cyrillic spelling — Uzbek search: Latin and Cyrillic.

Does it work without a website?

Partly, with call ads. But a website or landing page makes it far more effective.

Conclusion

Contextual advertising reaches people at the moment of need: search and display formats, an auction of quality and bids, and payment per click. For services people actively search for, it's often the most effective channel. In a free audit, Level Marketing will show you whether search advertising suits your business.
