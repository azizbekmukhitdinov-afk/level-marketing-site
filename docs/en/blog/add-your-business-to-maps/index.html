How to add your business to Google and Yandex Maps

"Dentist near me", "car service in Chilanzar" — people increasingly look for services on maps. A listing on Google and Yandex Maps is free and one of the "hottest" sources of customers. Here is how to create a business listing, verify it and rank higher.

What maps give you

  • Customers searching nearby find you.
  • Calls, directions and website visits in one tap.
  • Reviews and ratings — the main source of trust.
  • Opening hours, prices and photos — decisions without extra questions.

Google Business Profile: steps

  1. Go to business.google.com and choose "Add business".
  2. Enter the name, category, address or service area, phone and website.
  3. Verify — Google offers SMS, a call, email, video or another method; the options depend on the business.
  4. Complete the profile: hours, description, services or products, photos.
  5. Post updates regularly — promotions, news, new photos.

Yandex Business: steps

  1. Add your organisation at yandex.uz/sprav.
  2. Enter the details: name, address, phone, category, hours.
  3. Verify ownership — by call, SMS or documents.
  4. Add photos, prices and services.

Other map services (2GIS, for example) are also used in Tashkent — a listing there doesn't hurt.

How to rank higher

Maps weigh three factors:

FactorWhat to do
RelevanceThe right primary category, a full description, a list of services
DistanceA precise address; check the pin on the map
ProminenceReviews, rating, activity, photos

Also make sure your name, address and phone number match everywhere — website, Instagram, directories.

Common mistakes

  • Stuffing keywords into the name ("Dentist cheap Tashkent best") — it's against the rules and can get the listing suspended.
  • The wrong category.
  • Several listings for one business (duplicates).
  • Not replying to reviews — see our online reputation article.
  • Outdated hours and phone numbers.

What photos your listing needs

  • The facade and entrance — so customers can easily find the building and the door.
  • The interior — the hall, office or workspace.
  • The team — who will greet the customer.
  • Products and examples of work — what the customer will get.
  • Logo and cover — so the listing matches your brand.

Use only real photos: stock images lower trust. Refresh them from time to time — for the season, new products or after a renovation.

How to get more reviews

  1. Ask after a successful service — while the customer is happy.
  2. Send a direct link — in a messenger or on the receipt, so nobody has to search.
  3. Put a QR code at the till or reception.
  4. Reply to every review — positive and negative.

Don't promise a gift or discount for a positive review, and don't order fake reviews: it breaks the platforms' rules and can harm your listing. How to set up feedback collection is covered in Collecting customer feedback.

A monthly upkeep checklist

  • Are the opening hours correct, with special hours set for holidays?
  • Have new reviews been answered?
  • Have questions on the listing been answered?
  • Have new photos and updates been added?
  • Have edits suggested by users been checked? Sometimes they change details incorrectly.
  • Do the phone number and website link work?

Questions and answers

Can you create a listing without an office?

Yes. If you go to your customers (delivery, tradespeople, mobile services), Google lets you hide your address and set a service area. Other map services may have different rules — check them when you register.

How long until the listing appears?

After verification and moderation. This can take from a few days to a few weeks, so start early — before an opening or a move.

Can you advertise on maps?

Yes, both Google and Yandex offer paid placement on their maps. But fill in the free listing completely first — advertising an incomplete listing wastes money.

Can you delete a negative review?

A business owner can't delete a review themselves. If the review breaks the platform's rules — insults, spam or a story that has nothing to do with you — you can report it for moderation. Otherwise, reply calmly and to the point: other customers read your answer too.

Conclusion

A maps listing is free advertising you set up once and maintain every month. Complete information, real photos and working with reviews push it up the results. For where it fits among your channels, see our internet marketing article.
