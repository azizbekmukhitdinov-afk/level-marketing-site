YouTube for business: channel, Shorts and long videos

YouTube isn't just an entertainment platform — it's a huge search engine where people look up "how to choose", "what's the difference" and "review". Here is which businesses YouTube suits, the roles of Shorts and long videos and how to start a channel.

Who it suits best

  • Complex or expensive products: real estate, cars, furniture, electronics — buyers research a lot.
  • Expert services: lawyers, doctors, marketers, teachers.
  • Education: courses, language centres, workshops.
  • B2B: equipment, manufacturing, corporate services.

Shorts vs long videos

ShortsLong videos
JobReach and introductionsTrust and search traffic
LengthUp to 60 seconds5–20 minutes or more
LifespanShortCan be watched for years
ContentA hook, one ideaA guide, review, comparison

The best strategy uses both: Shorts bring new people, long videos turn them into customers. The same Reel can be posted as a Short.

Topics that bring views from search

  • "How to choose…", "… what's the difference", "… how much does it cost".
  • Product reviews and comparisons.
  • Answers to customers' frequent questions.
  • Process: "how … is made".

Title, thumbnail and description

  • Title — with the words people search for: "How to choose a sofa: 7 tips".
  • Thumbnail — a large face, 3–4 big words, contrasting colours.
  • Description — a short summary in the first lines, a website or Telegram link, timestamps.

A starting plan

  1. Set up the channel: name, avatar, banner, links.
  2. List 10 topics from customers' questions.
  3. One long video every 2 weeks and 3 Shorts a week.
  4. Film one expert interview and cut several Shorts from it.
  5. Review the stats every month.

What to measure

Not views, but: watch time, thumbnail click-through rate (CTR), subscribers, clicks on description links and leads from YouTube.

The structure of a long video

  1. The first 30 seconds — say what the viewer will get: "By the end of this video you'll know…".
  2. A short introduction — who you are and why they should listen, in one or two sentences.
  3. The main part in chapters — each chapter answers one question; add timestamps to the description.
  4. An example or demonstration — the product, the process or a calculation on screen.
  5. A summary — the key points once more.
  6. A call to action — a link in the description, a question in the comments or a consultation.

A month of content from one shoot

A long video is raw material for other channels too. One 15-minute video can become:

  • 4–6 Shorts and Reels from the strongest points;
  • an article for your website based on the video's script;
  • a Telegram channel post and quotes for Stories.

That way one day of shooting gives you content for a whole month. How to develop a Telegram channel is covered in Grow a Telegram channel.

Common mistakes

  • Opening with a logo animation and a long intro.
  • A title nobody searches for: "Our new video".
  • A video with no description or links.
  • Giving up after three or four videos.
  • Bad sound — it drives viewers away faster than a bad picture.

Questions and answers

When will YouTube show results?

It's a slow channel. Judge it after 3–6 months of regular publishing: videos people find through search keep gathering views over time, while first-month numbers mean little.

Do you need expensive equipment?

No. A phone, a microphone and good light are enough to start; sound matters most. Tips in Shooting Reels on a phone.

Which language should you speak?

The one your audience searches in. If customers search in both Uzbek and Russian, make separate videos for each language, or at least add subtitles in the second one.

Do Shorts bring viewers to long videos?

Sometimes, but not automatically. You can attach a related long video to a Short, and it appears as a link under it. It works best when the Short is a clip from the long video and says openly that the full answer is in the video on the channel.

Conclusion

YouTube is a slow but long-term channel: a video posted today can bring customers for years. Start with short videos, give detailed answers to customers' questions and measure results in leads. For editing tools, see our video editing apps article.
