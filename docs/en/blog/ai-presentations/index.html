"AI for presentations" is one of the most popular AI search topics. Business owners need presentations often: a proposal for a big client, a partner meeting, a bank or investor, a staff meeting. AI cuts presentation prep from hours to minutes — but it just as easily produces beautiful slides with empty content. Here's how to make a useful presentation with AI.

Content first, design second

Many people start a presentation with design: template, colours, images. The right order is the reverse:

  1. Goal: what should the audience do after the presentation? Sign a contract, agree to a meeting, commit money?
  2. Audience: what do they know, what don't they know, what worries them?
  3. Key points: 3–5 points — each slide proves one.
  4. Evidence: numbers, case studies, customer reviews.
  5. Next step: a concrete proposal.

AI is very good at helping with this structure. Design comes last.

A prompt for the AI

I'm [who]. I need a presentation for [whom]: the goal is [what should happen]. The audience [knows what] and is worried about [what]. My facts: [list]. Build an 8–10 slide structure: for each slide, a headline that states a conclusion (an idea, not a topic) and 2–3 key points. Use only the facts I've given.

The key detail is the conclusion headline. Not "Our experience" but "300 kitchens installed in 5 years" (illustrative example). Even if the audience reads only the headlines, they should get the main idea.

3 templates

A commercial proposal for a client (B2B)

#Slide
1The client's task — in their words
2The current situation and what it costs them
3Our solution — in 3 stages
4An example result: a case or an illustrative calculation
5Process and timeline
6Price and terms
7Why us: 3 proof points
8Next step

On B2B sales — B2B marketing.

For a partner

What the partner gains, what's needed from you and from them, how you'll measure it, and a plan for the first 30 days.

For a bank or investor

The problem and the market, the solution, customers and sales, unit economics, numbers and plans, the team, the amount requested and what it will fund. The maths — unit economics. Business plans — the marketing section of a business plan.

Numbers are the riskiest part

AI tends to add "impressive" numbers to presentations: market size, growth rates, statistics — often unsourced or made up. If an investor or a major client spots one wrong number, trust in the whole presentation disappears.

Rules:

  • every number has a source, in small type on the slide;
  • your own numbers come from the CRM and the accounts;
  • label illustrative calculations as "illustrative example";
  • check or delete every statistic the AI added.

Design: AI tools and simple rules

AI design tools create slides from text. When using them:

  • one slide, one idea;
  • little text: 3–5 bullet points, one line each;
  • brand colours and fonts — visual identity for small business;
  • real photos: product, team, work — instead of stock or AI images;
  • check legibility: on a phone and on a projector.

Presentation language

For an Uzbek presentation, have a native speaker read the AI copy: it shouldn't sound bureaucratic or translated. For a bilingual audience, prepare two versions rather than mixing languages on one slide.

Common mistakes

  • Starting with design without thinking about the goal and audience.
  • Topic headlines: "About us", "Services" — no idea.
  • Unsourced statistics from AI.
  • Ten lines of text per slide.
  • No concrete next step at the end.

Questions and answers

How many slides should there be?

Usually 8–12. If there are more, cut the main section and move the rest to an appendix.

Will an AI design tool replace PowerPoint?

For a quick draft, yes. The final version is often easier to edit in your usual software.

Can I give AI client data for a presentation?

Personal data — no. A company name and the general task — with care; if there's an NDA, don't. More in AI and the law in Uzbekistan.

Can a presentation be turned into a video?

Yes, a short narrated video is a good format. On AI voices — AI voice-over in ads.

Conclusion

AI prepares presentations fast: structure, conclusion headlines, slide copy and a draft design. But the goal, the audience and the numbers are a human responsibility: check every figure and finish with a concrete next step.
