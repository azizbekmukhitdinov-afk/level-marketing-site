"What is SMM", "what does SMM do", "SMM vs targeted ads" — some of the most common marketing questions in Uzbekistan. Many people think SMM means "posting on Instagram". In reality it's much broader: understanding the audience, content, conversations, linking up with ads and measuring results. Here's what SMM is, in plain words.

A simple definition

SMM (social media marketing) is marketing through social networks: presenting your business on Instagram, Telegram, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube, talking with customers and leading them to buy.

The goal of SMM isn't a pretty page but trust and sales: someone looks at your page, thinks "I can trust them" and gets in touch.

ContentPosts, Reels, Stories, carousels

SMM vs targeted ads

The two are often confused:

SMMTargeted ads
What it isPage, content, conversationsPaid advertising
Who it reachesMostly followers and recommendationsA chosen new audience
Speed of resultsSlower, cumulativeFaster
JobTrust, brand, repeat contactA flow of new leads

They complement each other: ads bring in new people, SMM convinces them. On targeted ads — what is targeted advertising.

Who is an SMM manager?

An SMM manager runs the page: plans, produces or commissions content, replies to comments and analyses results. Their tasks and how to choose one — what an SMM manager does.

What SMM gives a business

  • trust: real photos, the team, reviews — social proof;
  • discoverability: new people through Reels and recommendations;
  • repeat contact: followers see your news;
  • better ad performance: a good page turns people arriving from ads into leads.

If the page looks great but doesn't sell, the cause is usually structure, not content: why social media doesn't sell.

How to measure SMM

MetricWhat it shows
ReachHow many people saw the content
Saves and sharesWhether the content is useful
DM enquiriesWhether interest turns into action
"How did you hear about us" answersImpact on sales
Follower growthA secondary metric

Follower count isn't the main metric. A thousand real customers are worth more than ten thousand "empty" followers. Growing followers the right way — how to grow Instagram followers.

Where to start

  1. Write down the goal: sales, leads, brand or trust.
  2. Choose one main network — Telegram or Instagram.
  3. Tidy up the profile: bio, highlights, first 9 posts.
  4. Plan 3–5 posts a week.
  5. Set up fast DM replies.
  6. Review results once a month.

Common mistakes

  • Treating SMM as design only.
  • Buying followers — real reach drops.
  • Trying to be on every network at once.
  • Replying to DMs late.
  • Judging results by likes alone.

Questions and answers

Can you make money with SMM?

That question usually means becoming an SMM specialist. For a business, SMM is a tool for sales and trust.

How much does SMM cost?

It depends on volume, content type and who does it. How pricing works — SMM service pricing.

Can I do SMM myself?

At the start, yes. As the business grows, time runs out and you need a specialist.

When will I see results?

Organic SMM is slow — usually several months. With ads, faster.

Conclusion

SMM builds trust and sales through social networks: strategy, content, profile, conversations and analytics. It works best together with ads and is measured by enquiries and sales, not likes.
