What is engagement rate (ER) and how to calculate it on Instagram

Engagement rate (ER) shows what share of your audience reacted to your content with a like, comment, save or share. In social media it measures whether a page is "alive", and it's used to vet influencers: 100,000 followers with a 0.2% ER often signals a fake audience. Here's how to calculate ER and interpret it properly.

Two main formulas

ER (by followers) = (likes + comments + saves + shares) ÷ followers × 100%

ERR (by reach) = (likes + comments + saves + shares) ÷ reach × 100%

An illustrative example: a post gets 400 likes, 30 comments, 50 saves and 20 shares — 500 actions in total. The account has 10,000 followers, and the post's reach is 8,000.

  • ER = 500 ÷ 10,000 × 100% = 5%
  • ERR = 500 ÷ 8,000 × 100% = 6.25%

Reels are now shown widely to non-followers, so ERR by reach is often more accurate — reach vs impressions.

Which actions matter most

Not all actions are equal:

  • Saves and shares signal valuable content and influence recommendations — how the Instagram algorithm works.
  • Comments mean conversation, especially questions.
  • Likes are the easiest and least informative action.

What ER does and doesn't show

ER shows whether content resonates with your audience and which formats and topics work.

ER doesn't show sales. Entertaining content with a high ER may bring no leads. For a business, the next steps are profile visits, DM enquiries and leads — why social media doesn't sell.

How to raise ER

  1. A strong hook in the first seconds — Reels hooks.
  2. Content worth saving: lists, tips, "how to choose".
  3. An invitation to comment, posed as a question.
  4. Fast, human replies to comments — handling comments and DMs.
  5. Series and recurring segments that bring people back.

Using ER to vet influencers

  • A very low ER with many followers suggests a fake or inactive audience.
  • Look at comment content: identical emojis and generic words are suspicious.
  • Use the average of the last 10–12 posts, not a single post's ER.

Questions and answers

What's a good ER?

There's no universal benchmark: it depends on follower count, industry and format. Smaller accounts usually have higher ER. Track your own trend.

How does ER differ from CTR?

ER is the reaction to content (organic and paid); CTR is clicks on ads — what is CTR.

Do ads hurt ER?

Ads increase reach, but if the audience isn't a good fit, the ER percentage can fall.
