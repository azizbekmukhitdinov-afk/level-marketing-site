Sales techniques: 8 methods that work for sales reps

Ads bring leads, but a salesperson brings in the money. With the same leads, one rep closes 5 sales and another closes 15. The difference isn't luck — it's sales techniques: how you run the conversation, uncover needs and set the next step. Here are 8 methods that work for small business sales reps.

1. Consultative selling

The rep isn't a pusher but an advisor. They first understand the customer's situation, then propose a solution. This matters most for expensive services and B2B.

2. The right questions

Ask more than you talk. Use open questions: "How do you handle this today?", "What result matters most to you?" A question framework — SPIN selling.

3. Features → advantages → benefits (FAB)

  • Feature: "The sofa fabric is water-repellent."
  • Advantage: "Stains don't stick."
  • Benefit: "Your sofa stays like new, even with kids."

Customers buy benefits, not features.

4. Stories and examples

"A client with a similar situation came to us with the same problem…" — people remember stories better than numbers. Use only real examples you have permission to share.

5. The alternative close

Instead of "Would you like it?", ask "Is tomorrow morning or evening better for you?" The customer chooses between two options rather than yes or no. It's not pressure — it's a clear next step.

6. Upselling and cross-selling

  • Upselling — a higher tier: "You can extend the warranty to 2 years."
  • Cross-selling — an add-on: "Cushions to match the sofa."

Offer them only when they genuinely help the customer — they raise your average order value — units per transaction (UPT).

7. Handling objections

"Too expensive" and "I'll think about it" are a normal part of selling. More — handling objections.

8. Follow-up

Many deals close not in the first conversation but at the next contact. End every conversation with a next step and a date — the "I'll think about it" customer.

Turning techniques into a system

  • Build your script on these techniques — sales scripts.
  • Record and analyse calls — AI call analysis.
  • Track each rep's conversion rate in the CRM — sales rep conversion.
  • Respond quickly — Level Marketing recommends replying to leads within 30 minutes — lead response time.

Questions and answers

Which technique matters most?

Asking questions and listening: without understanding the need, the other techniques don't work.

Aren't these techniques manipulation?

No, if the goal is to find the right solution for the customer. Lies and pressure are something else.

How should I train a new rep?

With a script, by listening to recorded calls and through weekly role-play.

Conclusion

Sales techniques aren't luck but a learnable skill: questions, the language of benefits, stories, the alternative close, upselling and follow-up. A script, call analysis and a CRM turn them into a system. In a free audit, Level Marketing will look at how your leads turn into sales.
