B2B marketing: how to attract corporate clients

B2B means selling to companies: equipment, raw materials, wholesale, IT services, logistics, building materials, consulting. Here clients don't buy straight after seeing a nice Instagram video. Several people make the decision, a deal takes weeks or months, and one contract can equal dozens of retail purchases. So B2B marketing plays by different rules. This article sets out a client acquisition system for a B2B company in Uzbekistan.

How B2B differs from B2C

B2CB2B
Who decidesOne personSeveral: the head, a specialist, an accountant
Decision timeMinutes or daysWeeks or months
Main motiveEmotion, convenienceProfit, lower risk, reliability
PriceOften publicOften depends on volume and terms
ChannelAds, InstagramReferrals, direct outreach, search, trade shows
RelationshipOne-off or repeatLong-term partnership

Step 1. Ideal client profile and decision-makers

In B2B, "all companies" is not an audience. Describe the ideal client precisely:

  • Industry: construction firms, food producers, pharmacy chains.
  • Size: headcount, branches, monthly purchase volume.
  • Problem: "deliveries are late", "quality is inconsistent", "reports are done by hand".

Then, who decides in each company:

RoleWhat matters to them
Head or ownerProfit, risk, long-term results
Technical specialistQuality, specifications, compatibility
ProcurementPrice, lead times, terms
AccountingPaperwork, payment terms, tax

Prepare a separate argument for each role. Defining the audience — target audience and customer persona.

Step 2. Trust materials

B2B clients fear risk: the wrong supplier means a halted production line or a delayed project. So marketing's main job is reducing risk:

  • Case studies — which company, what problem, what result (with the client's permission).
  • Client list and logos — those who agreed.
  • Certificates, licences, test results.
  • Production site or warehouse — video and the option to visit.
  • A commercial proposal — 2–4 pages: problem, solution, result, price structure, next step.

For how to write cases, see the examples on our site, such as the Express School case: point A, what we did, point B.

Step 3. Expert content

In B2B, content should be useful rather than entertaining. It does two jobs: being found in search and building trust during the deal.

FormatExample
Guide"Choosing warehouse shelving: 7 parameters"
Comparison"Two types of insulation: where each works best"
Calculation"How many months until new equipment pays for itself"
Expert interviewThe technical director on common mistakes
Case studyA real project with numbers

The CEO's personal brand is especially strong in B2B: corporate clients trust a person before a company. Where to start — personal brand; video formats — expert interview videos. We've done this work with lawyers, doctors and experts — personal brand case.

Step 4. Search and the website

"Wholesale …", "… supplier Tashkent", "… manufacturer" — B2B buyers often start with search. You need:

  • a separate page for each product or service line;
  • precise contacts, company details, address;
  • a form to request or download the commercial proposal;
  • Google and Yandex search ads — with exact keywords and negative keywords.

Search ads — Google Ads vs Meta Ads, Yandex Direct. A maps listing helps B2B too — adding your business to maps.

Step 5. Direct outreach and referrals

The biggest B2B contracts often come from personal contact, not ads:

  1. A target list — 50–100 companies matching the ideal profile.
  2. Find the decision-maker — who's responsible and how to reach them.
  3. First contact — not a generic "we offer cooperation" but a specific problem and useful information.
  4. A meeting — to understand the problem in depth.
  5. Proposal and trial — a small order or pilot project.

Make asking current clients for referrals systematic too: after a successful project, "Who else in your industry could we help?".

No spam: follow the law on personal data and promotional messages — customer database and personal data, SMS advertising rules.

Step 6. Trade shows and events

Industry exhibitions, conferences and your own seminars are a strong way to build trust in B2B. Book meetings in advance, have a concrete offer and a way to collect contacts at the stand, and follow up with every contact within 48 hours.

Step 7. Paid ads in B2B

Meta and Telegram ads work in B2B too, but differently:

  • the goal is not an instant sale but a contact in exchange for useful material (a guide, calculator or trial);
  • the audience — job title and industry interests, plus retargeting of website visitors;
  • the creative — a problem and a number: "How to cut warehouse costs — 5 ways".

Track qualified leads, not total leads. Cost per lead — CPL guide.

Step 8. CRM and the long sales cycle

To keep leads from getting lost in a long cycle, a CRM is essential: each company, contact people, stage, next step and date. Marketing and sales should share the same data: which channel brings quality leads and which only brings "just asking". How to choose — CRM for small business; working with sales — marketing and sales alignment.

What to measure

  • qualified leads and their source;
  • lead-to-meeting and meeting-to-contract conversion;
  • average contract size and sales cycle length;
  • cost to acquire one new client — CAC;
  • client retention and repeat orders.

Common mistakes

  • Working like B2C: pretty posts but no case studies or commercial proposal.
  • The same message to every company.
  • Calling a lead once and forgetting it — long cycles need follow-up.
  • Competing on price alone.
  • Marketing and sales working in isolation.

Questions and answers

Does a B2B company need Instagram?

Yes, but not as the main channel. Instagram and Telegram build trust — they show production, the team and projects. Most leads usually come from search, referrals and direct outreach.

Should the website show prices?

If you can — a range or "from…". It saves time and filters out unsuitable leads. For complex projects, explain the price structure.

When will B2B marketing show results?

Because of the long sales cycle — in 3–6 months. Track leads and meetings in the first months, then contracts.

Where should a small B2B company start?

With an ideal client profile, 2–3 good case studies, a commercial proposal and a list of 50 target companies. Then search ads and expert content.

Conclusion

B2B marketing is about trust and lowering risk: a precise ideal client, case studies and a commercial proposal, expert content, visibility in search, direct outreach and a CRM that holds the long cycle together.
