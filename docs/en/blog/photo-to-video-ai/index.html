"AI video from a photo" has become one of the most searched AI queries of recent months. A single product photo becomes a 5–10-second video with motion: the camera circles, steam rises, fabric ripples. For an online store, café, furniture maker or real-estate agency, that means dozens of video creatives without a shoot. But let's look at where this tool helps — and where it turns into misleading customers.

How it works

You upload a photo and describe the motion: "The camera slowly moves forward, steam rises from the coffee." The AI uses the photo as a base and creates a short video. Many services produce 5–10-second clips, and some let you extend them. The result depends on the photo's quality and the precision of the prompt.

Where it works well

Works wellUse with care
Camera movement around a productChanging the product's shape or colour
Animating the background: steam, light, leavesPeople's and hands' movements
A "walk-through" of an interiorText and logos (they distort)
A short opener for Stories and ReelsComplex motion and physics

5 uses

  1. Product listings — a 5-second spin instead of a static photo — AI product listings on Uzum.
  2. Reel openers — unexpected motion as a hook — Reels hooks.
  3. Cafés and restaurants — steam over a dish, a drink being poured.
  4. Real estate and furniture — a slow camera move through an interior.
  5. Creative tests — five motion variants from one photo — A/B testing ad creatives.

Example prompts (illustrative examples)

  • "The camera slowly circles the product by 90 degrees; the lighting stays the same; the product's shape doesn't change."
  • "Light steam rises from the teapot; the camera is still; the background is slightly blurred."
  • "The camera moves slowly from the room's door towards the window, in morning sunlight."

Tip: always include the constraint "the product doesn't change".

Honesty: the key boundary

Customers must see what they're buying. If the AI makes a product's colour, size or quality look "better", that may be misleading advertising — advertising law in Uzbekistan. Compare every video with the real product. Using AI properly for product photos — AI product photos.

Creating videos from photos of people requires their consent — photo and video consent for advertising.

Quality tips

  • Start with a high-quality, well-lit photo.
  • A simple background means fewer artefacts.
  • Generate several variants and pick the best.
  • Add text and logos afterwards in editing — video editing apps.
  • Add sound and music separately — music for video ads.

Does it replace a real shoot?

No. Photo-to-video is an extra source of creatives. For trust, real video — the product in someone's hands, the process, people — is still stronger — product video mistakes.

Questions and answers

Which service is best?

The services change fast. Test 2–3 on the same photo and compare quality, price and commercial-use terms.

Can I use it in ads?

Yes, as long as the product is shown accurately and the service's terms allow commercial use.

Do AI videos need labelling?

There's no specific requirement in Uzbekistan for now, but platforms may label some AI content.
