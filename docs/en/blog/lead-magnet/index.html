The lead magnet: useful material people trade their contact details for

Most people who see your ad aren't ready to buy right now: they're still comparing, thinking it over, or the problem isn't pressing yet. An ad that says "leave a request" loses them. A lead magnet is a useful piece of material or a tool that people get in exchange for their contact details. That way you build a base of people who won't buy today but might tomorrow. This article covers how to choose a lead magnet, deliver it and turn it into sales.

Why you need a lead magnet

In any market, people fall into three groups:

  • ready to buy now — a small share; a direct offer works for them;
  • interested, but not yet — the largest group;
  • not aware of the problem yet.

Most ads only work on the first group. A lead magnet holds on to the second: you stay in touch, and you're the first name that comes to mind when it's time to decide.

What makes a good lead magnet

  • Narrow and specific. Not "a marketing guide" but "A restaurant's first 30 days on Instagram".
  • Quick value. Read or used in 5–10 minutes, with an immediate payoff.
  • Tied to your product. The natural next step after the lead magnet should be your service.
  • Looks valuable. Good design, a clear title, a "finished" feel.
  • Honest. What you promise is actually delivered.

How to deliver it: a Telegram bot

The handiest method is a Telegram bot:

  1. An ad or post: the lead magnet's name and benefit.
  2. A "Get it" button that opens the bot.
  3. The bot asks for a phone number and sends the material.
  4. Over the next few days, the bot sends useful messages and an offer.

On Instagram, a DM auto-reply or a lead form also works — Telegram bot for leads, lead form or messages.

After the lead magnet: a message sequence

Sending the material isn't the end. A simple sequence:

WhenMessage
ImmediatelyThe material + "message us with any questions"
Day 1A tip on how to use the material
Day 3A customer story or case
Day 5A common mistake and how to avoid it
Day 7A concrete offer: a consultation or an entry package — entry offer

After that, send 2–4 useful messages a month, which can also go through a Telegram channel — grow a Telegram channel.

Illustrative examples

  • Renovation company: "Renovation cost calculator" → estimate → booking a measurement.
  • Education centre: "Find your English level in 5 minutes" → result → trial lesson.
  • Real estate: "15 questions before buying your first flat" → consultation.
  • Beauty salon: "Skin type quiz" → aftercare advice → first visit.

What to measure

  • cost per contact;
  • what share of contacts move on to a consultation or purchase;
  • how long that takes;
  • cost per sale for lead-magnet customers — compare with ordinary leads.

A lead-magnet contact is cheaper than an ordinary lead but converts to a sale more slowly. So judge results over at least 1–2 months — what is a sales funnel.

Personal data

When collecting contacts, follow the law: explain why you're collecting them, get consent and store the base securely — customer database and personal data.

Common mistakes

  • Material so broad or generic that nobody needs it.
  • Material unrelated to the product — lots of contacts, no sales.
  • Sending the material and then going silent.
  • Jumping straight into a hard sell.
  • Judging results after one week.

Questions and answers

Is a lead magnet better than an ordinary lead form?

They serve different people. An ordinary lead form is for those ready now; a lead magnet is for those still interested. Using both together gives the best result.

What format should the lead magnet take?

Whatever your audience consumes: for many, a PDF or short video on Telegram is easiest. Calculators and quizzes are the most engaging formats.

How long does it take to prepare?

A simple checklist or collection takes a few days. A calculator or mini-course takes longer.

Does it work in every industry?

It works especially well where decisions take a long time and purchases are expensive: renovation, real estate, education, B2B.

Conclusion

A lead magnet keeps you from losing people who aren't buying yet but are interested: narrow, useful material, delivery through a Telegram bot, then a message sequence and a concrete offer. It doesn't replace ordinary leads — it complements them. During the strategy stage, Level Marketing builds the whole funnel: ads, lead magnet, message sequence and hand-off to the sales team.
