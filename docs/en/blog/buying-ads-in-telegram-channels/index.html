Advertising in Telegram channels: buying posts and measuring

Telegram channels are one of Uzbekistan's biggest media spaces: news, city, women's, car, jobs and humour channels often have hundreds of thousands of subscribers. Buying sponsored posts in them is a fast route for businesses and channels alike. But it's also easy to lose money: fake subscribers, the wrong audience, results you can't measure. Here's how to buy advertising in Telegram channels properly. On Telegram's official ad platform — Telegram advertising.

Sponsored posts vs Telegram Ads

Sponsored post in a channelTelegram Ads
FormatA full post published by the channel ownerA short text ad inside a channel
DealDirectly with the channel adminThrough the official platform
FlexibilityHigh: images, video, textLimited format
RiskFake audiences, no guaranteesPlatform rules

Step 1: choosing channels

  • Topic and audience: is the channel for the people your product is for? Children's clothing, for example, fits parenting and family channels.
  • Region: Tashkent channels for Tashkent businesses.
  • Language: an Uzbek- or Russian-speaking audience — ad language: Uzbek or Russian.

Step 2: checking channel quality

  • Views-to-subscribers ratio: lots of subscribers but very few views is suspicious.
  • View growth: in a genuine channel, views build gradually over hours; a sudden jump can signal inflated numbers.
  • Reactions and comments: real audiences comment.
  • Statistics: ask the admin for a screenshot of channel stats and check Telegram analytics services.
  • Past placements: ask other advertisers about their results.

Step 3: preparing the post

An ad in a channel competes with its content. A good post:

  • matches the channel's style, with a headline people want to read;
  • has one offer and a concrete benefit;
  • has one action: a link to a bot, website or channel;
  • includes a visual: an image or short video.

It must be clear that it's an ad — avoid hidden advertising — advertising law in Uzbekistan.

Step 4: measurement

Separately for each channel:

  • a link: a distinct bot parameter, UTM tag or invite link — UTM tags;
  • a promo code for offline and phone sales — promo codes in marketing;
  • a spreadsheet: channel, price, views, clicks, leads, sales.

Key metrics: cost per 1,000 views (CPM), cost per click and cost per lead.

About pricing

Prices depend on channel size, topic and season; posts are often sold in formats like "1/24" (24 hours at the top) or "1/48". Compare channels by CPM — the price divided by views, not subscribers.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing by subscriber count alone.
  • Spending the whole budget on one big channel, without testing.
  • One link for all channels, so you can't tell which worked.
  • A generic ad banner that doesn't fit the channel's audience.

Questions and answers

Do sponsored posts work for growing my own channel?

Yes — it's one of the main ways to grow a Telegram channel — grow a Telegram channel.

How many channels should I test?

Start with 3–5 small and mid-sized channels, then repeat in the best ones.

Isn't paying upfront risky?

Work with trusted admins and put the deal in writing: timing, format and when the post comes down.

Conclusion

Advertising in Telegram channels means the right channels, quality checks, a post in the channel's style and separate measurement for each channel. Decide on CPM and cost per lead, not subscriber counts. In a free audit, Level Marketing will analyse the results of your ad channels.
