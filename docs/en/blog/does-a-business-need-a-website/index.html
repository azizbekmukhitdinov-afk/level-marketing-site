Does your business need a website, or is Instagram enough?

In Uzbekistan, many businesses live on Instagram and Telegram alone — and sell well. So the question "do we need a website?" is often answered with "our customers don't visit websites". That's partly true: in many niches, the purchase happens in DMs. But a website isn't just an "online business card". It brings customers from search, builds trust and makes advertising easier to manage. Here's when Instagram is enough and when a site is genuinely needed.

Instagram and a website: different jobs

JobInstagramWebsite
How new people find youReels, recommendations, adsGoogle and Yandex search
TrustLive content, faces, reviewsStructured information, company details, address
Amount of informationLimited, lost in the feedUnlimited, structured
Who's in controlThe platform's rulesYou
LeadsDMs, lead formsForms, calls, a bot
AnalyticsInside the platformFull: source, page, conversion

Instagram is the place for attention; a website is the place for decisions. People see you on Instagram, then find information and decide on the site. In many niches, both stages happen inside Instagram — and then a website isn't essential.

When Instagram is enough

  • Impulse and low-cost purchases: clothing, accessories, flowers, food — people see it and message straight away.
  • A small range: 10–20 products fit in highlights and posts.
  • A small local service: a salon, a craftsman — customers come through referrals and maps.
  • Nobody searches for you: demand appears in the feed, not in search.

In that case, strengthening your profile matters more than a website — an Instagram profile that sells. A maps listing partly does a website's job too — add your business to maps.

When you need a website

1. People search for your service

If customers type "custom kitchen Tashkent", "driving school Chilanzar" or "freight Tashkent–Samarkand" into Google or Yandex, that demand is invisible on Instagram. A website can bring customers from search without ads. On search language — Latin and Cyrillic in Uzbek search.

2. B2B and big purchases

Corporate clients, property buyers and wholesale customers often vet a company: legal details, history, projects, certificates. Instagram alone gives the impression of "not a serious company". More in B2B marketing.

3. A large catalogue

More than 100 products get lost on Instagram. A catalogue with filters, search and prices is a website's job.

4. Google Ads and search advertising

Search ads usually need a website or landing page. Google Ads vs Meta — Google Ads vs Meta Ads.

5. Platform dependency risk

If your Instagram account is blocked, all your marketing stops. A website is your own property. On blocking risks — ad account blocked.

Which website: 3 options

OptionWhat it isFor whomComplexity
Landing pageOne page: offer, trust, formOne service or promotion, for adsLow
Brochure site5–10 pages: services, prices, work, contactsService businesses, B2BMedium
Catalogue / storeProducts, filters, cartA large rangeHigh

For many businesses, a brochure site or landing page is enough. A large online store is a separate project.

What a good website needs

  • Above the fold: what you do, for whom, and the next step (a button).
  • Prices, or at least "from" prices — more in should you show the price in your ads.
  • Proof: work, reviews, customer videos — social proof.
  • Contacts: phone, Telegram, address, map, opening hours.
  • Speed: fast on a phone — many visitors use mobile internet.
  • Two languages: Uzbek and Russian, plus English if needed.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, Meta Pixel — Meta Pixel and Conversions API.
  • A privacy policy, if a form collects data.

The site is live — now what?

Many websites launch and nobody visits. A website doesn't bring traffic on its own. Ways to get people there:

  • search (SEO) — useful articles, service pages, a maps listing; results usually take months;
  • ads — Google Ads, Yandex Direct, Meta ads to the site;
  • Instagram and Telegram — bio link, links in posts;
  • offline — business cards, signage, a QR code on packaging.

Common mistakes

  • Building a site and sending no traffic to it.
  • No prices, contacts or next step on the site.
  • Slow loading on phones.
  • A site built once and never updated for years.
  • Abandoning Instagram to pour everything into the site — they don't replace each other.

Questions and answers

How much does a small business website cost?

It depends on the option: a simple landing page on a site builder is cheap, a large catalogue costs more. More important than price is whether you have a plan to bring traffic to it.

Does a "Linktree" on Instagram replace a website?

Partly — as a list of links. But it brings no search traffic and offers little information for trust.

When will the site appear on Google?

A new site starts appearing in search within a few months, depending on competition. If you need fast results, use search ads.

Can a Telegram bot replace a website?

For leads and orders, often yes. For search and trust, no. More in Telegram bot for leads.

Conclusion

Not every business needs a website, but if people search for your service, if you're B2B, have a large catalogue or run search ads, it makes money. Instagram attracts attention; a website helps people decide. If you build one, plan the traffic too.
