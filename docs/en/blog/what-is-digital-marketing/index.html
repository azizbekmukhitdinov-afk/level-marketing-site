What is digital marketing: channels, measurement, first steps

Digital marketing means finding customers and selling through the internet and digital channels: social networks, search engines, messengers, websites and marketplaces. In Uzbekistan, many purchases start on a phone: people see something on Instagram, message on Telegram and find the address on a map. Here's what digital marketing is and where a small business should start. Marketing in general — what is marketing.

Digital vs offline marketing

Offline (traditional)Digital
ExamplesBillboards, TV, flyersInstagram, Google, Telegram
TargetingBy area, broadBy interest, location, behaviour
MeasurementDifficultEvery click and lead
BudgetOften a high minimumFrom small amounts
SpeedSlow to changeChanges in a day

They can work together — and offline results can be measured too — measuring offline advertising.

The main channels

  • Social media — pages and content — what is SMM.
  • Paid social — paid ads on Instagram/Facebook, TikTok and Telegram — what is targeted advertising.
  • Search ads — ads in Google and Yandex search — search ads in Tashkent.
  • SEO — ranking your website in search for free — what is SEO.
  • Telegram — channels, bots, groups — how to create a Telegram channel.
  • Maps — Google, Yandex, 2GIS — Google Business Profile.
  • Marketplaces — Uzum and others — how to start selling on Uzum Market.
  • Influencers and content marketing — trust and reach.

Digital marketing's main advantage: measurement

You can see the result of every soum:

  • ad impressions and clicks;
  • leads and cost per lead — cost per lead;
  • sales and return on marketing — how to calculate ROMI.

But that needs a system: UTM tags, a pixel, a CRM — UTM tags.

A starter plan for small businesses

  1. Foundations: a maps profile, an Instagram business account and Telegram contact — free or nearly free.
  2. An offer that's clear and easy to understand — unique selling proposition.
  3. One paid channel where your customers are — usually Instagram ads or search ads.
  4. Lead handling: fast replies and a CRM — lead response time.
  5. Measure and expand: scale the channel that works, then add a second.

Common mistakes

  • Spreading a little across every channel at once.
  • Measuring results by likes and followers.
  • Replying to leads late, wasting ad money.
  • A website or page that doesn't match the ad's promise.

Questions and answers

Are digital marketing and SMM the same?

No. Social media marketing is one part of digital marketing.

What budget do I need?

It depends on your industry and goals — planning a marketing budget.

Can I do it myself, or do I need an agency?

You can start yourself; to grow, you'll need specialists — agency, freelancer or in-house.

Conclusion

Digital marketing means finding customers through online channels and measuring every soum. Start with the foundations, launch one paid channel, reply to leads fast and expand based on the numbers.
