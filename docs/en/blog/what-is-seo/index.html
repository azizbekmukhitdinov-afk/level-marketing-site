"What is SEO", "how do I get my site to number one on Google" — every business owner with a website asks this eventually. SEO is surrounded by myths: "use more keywords", "number one in a month", "a special secret". Here's a plain-language look at what SEO is, how search engines judge a site and where a small business should start.

A simple definition

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the work of making a website rank higher in search engine results (Google, Yandex). The goal is for people to find you without ads when they search for your service.

The difference from search ads: with ads you pay for every click, and when you stop, visibility disappears. SEO is slow, but results accumulate. On search ads — search ads in Tashkent.

How a search engine works: 3 steps

  1. Crawling — a bot finds your page.
  2. Indexing — it reads the content and adds it to the index.
  3. Ranking — it puts the most helpful answers to a query near the top.

If a page isn't indexed, it doesn't exist in search at all. So step one is adding your site to Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster.

What Google recommends

The core message of Google's SEO starter guide: create content that's helpful, clear and trustworthy for people, and build your site so search engines can understand it (Google SEO starter guide). In a separate guide, Google says it rewards helpful content created for people, not for search engines (Google: helpful content).

The practical takeaway: don't repeat keywords — give the best answer to the customer's question.

Local SEO: the fastest route for small businesses

For a service business in Tashkent, the fastest results often come from maps:

  • complete listings on Google and Yandex Maps;
  • ongoing reviews and replies;
  • your address and services on the website.

An Uzbekistan-specific factor: language and script

The same service is searched in Latin, Cyrillic and Russian. There's little quality content in Uzbek — an opportunity: a useful Uzbek page often climbs faster. More in Uzbek search.

Visibility in AI answers is also an extension of SEO — GEO: getting into AI answers.

Where to start: 7 steps

  1. Add your site to Google Search Console and Yandex Webmaster.
  2. List 20–30 queries your customers search for.
  3. A separate page for each main service.
  4. A clear title and description on every page.
  5. Check the site on a phone: does it load fast, does the form work?
  6. Articles answering customers' questions — 2–4 a month.
  7. Maps listings and reviews.

How long results take

SEO is a marathon. For a new site, the first noticeable results usually take several months, depending on competition. Be wary of anyone promising "number one in a week". If you need fast results, combine SEO with search ads.

Common mistakes

  • Overusing keywords.
  • Putting every service on one page.
  • Copying text from other sites.
  • Buying low-quality links.
  • Expecting results in 2 weeks and giving up.

Questions and answers

Is SEO free?

You don't pay for clicks from search, but content and technical work take time or money.

Can you do SEO without a website?

Maps listings are part of local SEO and work without a site. Full SEO needs a website — how to build a business website.

Do Google and Yandex need separate SEO?

The foundation is the same. Add your site to both and track results.

Are articles needed for SEO?

Yes, if they genuinely answer customers' questions. Articles written only for keywords add little.

Conclusion

SEO is ongoing work on helpful content, technical order and trust signals so your site rises in search. Google itself says it rewards content that's helpful to people. The fastest route for a small business is maps listings and a dedicated page for each service.
