Marketing an education centre: how to attract students

Education is about trust and results. Students — or their parents — choose a learning centre carefully: they invest time as well as money. Here is how people decide on courses and how to attract students.

How the decision is made

  • Who decides: parents for children, the students themselves for adults.
  • What matters: the teacher, results (exam scores, certificates), location and schedule, price, atmosphere.
  • The path: search → reviews and videos → trial lesson → payment.

The trial lesson: the lowest barrier

A free or low-cost first lesson, a level test or an open class lets people try with no risk. What matters is the next step: a call within 24 hours of the trial, feedback on how it went and an offer to join a group.

Graduates are the best advertising

In education, a real result beats any promise. In our Express School driving school case, Reels featuring students with their new licences, videos answering the most common questions and paid ads took monthly sales from 40 to 300+, with reach of over 5 million.

Ideas for graduate videos:

  • "Before/after": the level before and after the course.
  • A short interview with an exam result or certificate.
  • "Why I chose this centre".

Teachers as personal brands

People don't go to a centre — they go to a teacher. Teachers' mini-lessons, tips and authentic videos build trust. Our personal brand cases include an IELTS teacher. For where to start, see personal brand: where to start.

Seasonality

At many learning centres, demand rises before the academic year starts, at the beginning of the new year and before exams. Check your own data and step up advertising 2–4 weeks before the peak.

Paid ads and Telegram

  • Campaign objective: a lead form or Direct messages — see our separate article on which to choose.
  • A Telegram channel: free materials, useful tips and student results. In our online courses case, ads built Telegram audiences of 35,000 and 25,000 subscribers from zero.

Conclusion

Marketing an education centre rests on three pillars: graduates' results, teachers' personal brands and the trial lesson. Combine them with regular content and paid ads. Level Marketing produces Reels and ad campaigns for education projects.
