Ad account blocked: what to do and how to prevent it

A blocked ad account is one of the most unpleasant things that can happen to a business owner or ads specialist: campaigns stop and the flow of leads is cut off. Here is why Meta blocks accounts, what to do after a ban and how to reduce the risk.

Why accounts get blocked

ReasonExample
Breaking advertising policiesPromises like "guaranteed weight loss" or "earn $10,000 a month"
Referring to personal attributes"Are you overweight?", "Do you have debts?"
Prohibited or restricted productsSome medicines, weapons, dubious financial services
Payment problemsDeclined payments, frequently changing cards
Suspicious activityA sudden large budget on a new account, logins from many devices and VPNs
Linked assetsLinks to a previously banned profile or Page

What to do after a ban

  1. Open Account Quality (business.facebook.com/accountquality) and find out exactly what is restricted: the ad account, a Page, a personal profile or the Business Manager.
  2. Read the reason. If a specific ad is named, fix it to comply with the policies.
  3. Request a review — once, with a clear and short explanation.
  4. Wait for the answer. Sending the same request repeatedly or opening dozens of new accounts only makes things worse.
  5. While you wait, if you have another verified account, run only fully compliant ads there.

How to reduce the risk

  • Verify your business and turn on two-factor authentication for every admin.
  • A stable payment method — don't change it often and keep enough balance.
  • Increase budgets gradually, especially on a new account.
  • Don't refer to personal attributes: write "a solution to this problem", not "your illness".
  • Don't guarantee results, and avoid before/after images in sensitive topics.
  • Keep a backup admin — access shouldn't depend on one person.
  • Never give an agency your password — grant partner access through Business Manager.

If you work with an agency

The ad account and Page should belong to your business, with the agency working through granted access. That way, even when the partnership ends, the history, audiences and Pixel data stay with you. More in our article on choosing an ads specialist.

A sample review request

Keep it short, polite and factual:

"Hello. Our account runs ads for [product or service]. We saw the restriction notice and reviewed the advertising policies again. We changed the text in [ad name]: [what changed]. We kindly ask you to review the account again."

Don't write an emotional, long or accusatory message, and don't send the same request over and over.

Checking an ad before launch

  • The text doesn't refer directly to a person's personal attributes.
  • No results are guaranteed, and "before/after" isn't used on sensitive topics.
  • The ad and the landing page match: what's promised is on the page.
  • There are no fake buttons or misleading elements like "new message".
  • You have the rights to the music and images.
  • Ads about credit, employment, housing or social issues have the special category declared.

Common mistakes

  • Sending an appeal without reading the reason for the block.
  • Keeping all campaigns and the whole budget in one ad account.
  • An unverified business with a single admin on the account.
  • Changing the payment card frequently.
  • Giving your paid ads specialist the password to your personal profile.

A backup plan

Don't depend on one ad account. To keep leads flowing, keep other channels running too: Telegram ads, Google and Yandex, organic content and messages to your customer base. More on Telegram ads in Telegram advertising.

Questions and answers

How long does a review take?

Usually a few days, sometimes longer — you can't know the timing in advance. Keep working through other channels in the meantime.

Can you open a new ad account and carry on?

Creating new accounts to get around restrictions breaks Meta's rules and can lead to even stricter restrictions. Resolve the issue through the official route first.

What if your personal profile gets blocked?

You lose access to the ad account through that profile — which is why a backup admin matters. To recover it, follow the verification steps Meta shows: you may be asked for an ID document or a video selfie.

Where can you read the rules?

On Meta's Advertising Standards page. Read the section on restricted categories for your industry separately — the requirements are especially strict in healthcare and finance.

Is an agency ad account safer?

An agency account can be more convenient for payments and limits, but the rules are the same: ads that break them get blocked there too. Also make sure your page and Pixel data stay in your own business account.

Conclusion

Most bans come from ignoring the rules and rushing. A verified business, stable payments, careful copy and gradual growth are the best protection. To set up an account properly from scratch, see our Instagram ads guide.
