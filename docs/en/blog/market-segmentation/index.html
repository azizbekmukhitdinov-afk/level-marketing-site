Market segmentation: how to divide customers into groups

A business whose customer is "everyone" usually can't speak clearly to anyone. Segmentation means dividing the market into groups with similar needs, behaviour or characteristics. Choosing a message, offer and channel for each group makes advertising cheaper and selling easier. Here's how to segment. Building a single customer persona — target audience and customer persona.

The 4 main criteria

CriterionExamples
GeographicCity, district, Tashkent or the regions
DemographicAge, gender, family status, income, occupation
PsychographicValues, lifestyle, interests
BehaviouralPurchase occasion, frequency, loyalty, price sensitivity

For B2B: industry, company size, decision-maker, purchase volume — B2B marketing.

Example: an education centre (illustrative)

School pupils and parentsUniversity entry"Results and discipline"Telegram, Instagram
University studentsStudying abroad"IELTS 7+ fast"Instagram, TikTok
Working adultsCareer"Evening groups that fit around work"Search ads, Telegram

One product, three different ads. That often brings cheaper leads than one general ad.

How to choose a segment

A good segment is:

  • big enough to be profitable;
  • measurable, so you can find it in advertising;
  • distinct in its needs from other segments;
  • reachable, so you can serve it.

Small businesses should start with one or two core segments.

Putting segmentation into practice

  1. Data: customers in your CRM — who, where from, what they bought — marketing research.
  2. Grouping: 3–5 segments.
  3. For each: need, message, offer, channel.
  4. Advertising: separate creatives or ad sets.
  5. Measurement: which segment brings cheaper leads and higher order values.

Common mistakes

  • Only age and gender, ignoring needs.
  • Too many tiny segments, spreading the budget thin.
  • The same message for every segment.
  • Segmenting on guesswork rather than data.

Questions and answers

How does segmentation differ from a target audience?

Segmentation divides the market into groups; the target audience is the group (or groups) you choose.

Is segmentation still needed now that Meta ads favour broad audiences?

Yes — segmentation now shows up in the creative and message, with a creative for each segment — Meta Andromeda.

When should I revisit segments?

Once a year, or when your product or market changes.
