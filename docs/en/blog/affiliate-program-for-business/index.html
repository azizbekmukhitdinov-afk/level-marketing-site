Affiliate programmes: selling through referrals and partners

The most trusted advertising is a recommendation from someone you know. An affiliate programme turns that into a system: customers, tradespeople, influencers or other businesses bring you customers and earn a reward on each sale. You pay only for results — which makes it a safe channel for small businesses. Here's how to build one.

Who can be a partner

PartnerExample
Happy customersBring a friend and you both get a bonus
Industry specialistsA builder for a building materials store, a designer for a furniture store
Related businessesA wedding venue for a photographer and florist; an accountant for a lawyer
Influencers and channelsA commission on every sale via a personal promo code

Types of reward

  • A commission on sales — the fairest: payment matches results.
  • A fixed amount for each new customer.
  • A bonus or service instead of cash: a discount, a free service.
  • Two-sided: for both the referrer and the new customer — works best for customer referrals.

The economics

The reward must cost less than your other acquisition channels. An illustrative example: if a customer from ads costs 300,000 soum, a 150,000 soum partner reward is a good deal. The calculation — customer acquisition cost.

Tracking who brought whom

  • A personal promo code for offline and phone sales — promo codes in marketing.
  • A personal link with a parameter for your website or bot — UTM tags.
  • A source field in the CRM: "Referral: Ali (builder)".

Write the terms down

One page of rules:

  • who can become a partner;
  • when rewards are paid (for example, once the customer has paid in full);
  • what happens with returns;
  • which promotion methods are banned (spam, fake reviews, advertising in the brand's name);
  • payment timing and method.

Agree reward and tax questions with your accountant.

Fraud protection

  • Rewards only for real sales, not leads.
  • Check for self-referrals and duplicate phone numbers.
  • Partners' advertising must comply with the law — advertising law in Uzbekistan.

Launching the programme

  1. Find your happiest customers — those who gave an NPS of 9–10 — what is NPS.
  2. Give them a personal offer and promo code.
  3. Meet 5–10 potential partners in your industry.
  4. Each month, review: who brought how many customers, and the rewards paid.
  5. Reward your best partners.

Questions and answers

Does a small business need an affiliate programme?

Yes — it's a channel where you pay only for results. You can start with a simple referral bonus.

How big should the reward be?

It depends on your margin and customer value. It should be noticeable for the partner and profitable for you — repeat sales and LTV.

With influencers: commission or upfront payment?

A commission model lowers your risk, but big influencers often ask for a fixed fee — influencer advertising.

Conclusion

An affiliate programme turns recommendations into a system: the right partners, fair rewards, personal promo codes and links, written terms and monthly accounting — and you pay only for results. In a free audit, Level Marketing will analyse your customer acquisition channels.
