Yandex Direct in Uzbekistan: when it helps and how to start

When people think of search advertising, most only think of Google Ads. But in Uzbekistan, especially among Russian-speaking users, Yandex search and Yandex Maps are also widely used. Yandex Direct is Yandex's advertising system. Here is when it helps, how it differs from Google Ads and how to launch your first campaign.

What Yandex Direct is

Yandex Direct shows ads in several places:

  • Search — in Yandex results when someone types a query.
  • Advertising network (YAN) — banners on Yandex partner websites and apps.
  • Yandex Maps — a highlighted pin among businesses.

Who it suits best

  • Businesses whose customers search in Russian.
  • B2B: equipment, wholesale, corporate services.
  • Local services: repairs, car services, clinics — especially where visibility on Yandex Maps matters.

How it differs from Google Ads

The best results usually come from testing both systems and comparing cost per lead. For choosing between Google and Meta, see Google Ads vs Meta Ads.

Your first campaign: 6 steps

  1. Create a Yandex Direct account and check which payment methods are available for Uzbekistan — sometimes paying through an agency is easier.
  2. Install Yandex Metrica on your site and set up goals: form submissions, call button clicks, Telegram clicks.
  3. Collect keywords: product + city, service + price, problem + solution.
  4. Add negative keywords: "free", "vacancy", "download" and other irrelevant queries.
  5. Write ads: the query in the headline, your offer and advantage in the text, plus sitelinks.
  6. Send each ad to a relevant page, not the home page.

Common mistakes

  • Broad keywords without negatives — budget goes on irrelevant searches.
  • Running without Metrica and goals — you can't tell which keywords bring sales.
  • Mixing search and network placements in one campaign.
  • Judging results by cost per click alone.

Give each group its own ad and its own landing page, so the ad answers the query precisely.

A sample ad

  • Headline: "Corner sofas in Tashkent — direct from the manufacturer"
  • Text: "Made to your measurements. Delivery across the city. Instalments available."
  • Sitelinks: "Catalogue", "Prices", "Instalments", "Contacts".

This is an illustrative example: your text should only make promises you actually keep.

The first two weeks: what to watch

  1. Check the search query report every 2–3 days and add irrelevant queries as negative keywords.
  2. Pause keywords that spend without reaching goals, or lower their bids.
  3. In Metrica, check bounce rates and differences between devices.
  4. Change one thing at a time — otherwise you won't know what made the difference.
  5. Reconcile website leads with your CRM — a goal in Metrica isn't always a real customer.

Questions and answers

How much budget do you need for a test?

The exact amount depends on your niche. A simple calculation: multiply your expected cost per lead by 10–20 — after that many leads you can draw conclusions. With less, the result will be random. Budget planning is covered in Planning a marketing budget.

Can you advertise on Uzbek-language queries?

If your customers also search in Uzbek, test those queries in a separate group and write the ad in the language of the query. Check in advance which languages and regions the account supports.

What does working through an agency give you?

Convenient payment, setup experience and regular reports. But the account and Metrica should be registered to you — then the data stays with you even after the partnership ends. How to choose a specialist is covered in How to choose a paid ads specialist.

What does it take to be visible on Yandex Maps?

First, complete your organisation's listing in Yandex Business: category, opening hours, photos and prices — and reply to reviews regularly. There are paid placement options too, but they work better with a complete listing. More in Add your business to maps.

Conclusion

Yandex Direct doesn't fully replace Google Ads, but it can be an extra source of "hot" leads from Russian-speaking and B2B audiences. Always compare results by cost per lead and per sale, using the method from our CPL guide.
