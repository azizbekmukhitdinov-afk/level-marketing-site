Not every follower sees your Instagram posts and Reels: the algorithm decides who gets them. A broadcast channel works differently. Your message lands in a channel inside Direct, and everyone who has joined the channel can see it. It is similar to a Telegram channel, only inside Instagram. This guide explains what the tool is, how to create a channel, which formats it supports and how a business can get value from it.

What an Instagram broadcast channel is

A broadcast channel is a one-to-many messaging tool: the owner posts, members read and react. Instagram announced it on 16 February 2023 and later expanded it worldwide.

According to the official announcement, the basic rules are:

  • Only the owner sends messages. Members can react and vote in polls.
  • Formats: text, photos, videos, voice notes and polls.
  • Joining: followers join through a sticker in Stories, a link pinned to the profile, or a one-time notification.
  • Notifications: all followers are notified once about the first message. After that, only members receive updates. Members can leave or mute the channel at any time.
  • The channel lives in Direct. Channels a person has joined appear in their inbox.

In the announcement Instagram also mentioned plans for question prompts, collaborators, moderators and channel end dates. The app changes quickly, so before launching check which features your own app actually offers.

Who can create a channel and how

Instagram originally launched channels for creator accounts. Who can create one may depend on account type and region, so the most reliable check is the app itself. If the "Create broadcast channel" option below does not appear, the feature has not been opened for your account yet.

The steps, according to the Instagram Help Center:

  1. Open Direct and tap the new message icon.
  2. Choose "Create broadcast channel".
  3. Enter a name. It should say who you are and what the channel offers, for example "Furniture X: new collections".
  4. Choose the audience. Accounts with subscriptions switched on can open the channel to all followers or to paying subscribers only.
  5. Set an end date and choose whether to show it on your profile. For a permanent channel, leave the end date empty and show it on the profile.
  6. Tap create and send the first message.

If your account is not a professional account yet, switch it first; the steps are in how to create an Instagram business account.

Formats: what you can send

The most underrated format is the voice note. Thirty or forty seconds of a real voice feels closer than text and turns the channel from a flyer into a conversation. The poll is the channel's strongest tool: people share opinions and you get free market research. For ideas, see Telegram channel polls; the logic is the same.

6 ways a business can use a channel

  1. Show new products first. Members see a collection a day before it appears in the feed. That gives people a clear reason to join.
  2. A channel-only offer. A small bonus such as free delivery, a gift or early booking. Do not discount in every message, or the channel becomes a promotions flyer.
  3. Decisions by poll. A question like "which colour should we produce?" shows demand before you sell.
  4. Behind the scenes. Production, the team, delivery: shots that are not worth a post but build trust.
  5. Expert advice. For a clinic, a training centre or a law firm, one useful tip a week.
  6. Event and live stream reminders. A separate message to members before and after a live session.

The channel works best alongside Stories: Stories bring in new people, the channel keeps them close. Story formats are covered in selling with Stories.

The takeaway: a broadcast channel does not bring new customers. It keeps your most interested Instagram followers closer. If most of your audience is on Telegram, an Instagram channel will not replace it. Where to invest your effort is discussed in Telegram channel or Instagram.

Illustrative example: what a channel adds to sales

Illustrative example. A clothing shop in Tashkent has 12,000 Instagram followers.

  • After the first message, 900 people join the channel (7.5% of followers).
  • The shop sends 3 messages a week: a new collection, a poll and a behind-the-scenes post.
  • A new collection message is seen by 540 people on average (60% of members).
  • Each message ends with a call to tap the profile link to a lead form. The link carries UTM tags, so leads from the channel show up separately in the CRM; setup is explained in UTM tags.
  • In a month the channel brings 36 leads and 12 of them buy. With an average order of 450,000 soums, that is 5.4 million soums in monthly sales.

The direct cost of the channel is 1–2 hours of a social media manager's time per week. So judge it not as a separate ad channel but as a way to work with an audience you already have. Your numbers will differ: track them for 2–3 months before deciding.

Weekly rhythm and content rules

  • 2–4 messages a week. Several messages a day push members to mute you.
  • One idea per message. A channel is not a feed; long texts are unnecessary.
  • Same day and time. Members learn the habit: "new collection every Thursday".
  • Exclusivity. Every message should offer something the feed does not: earlier, deeper or more personal.
  • Track views, not member count. If the share of members viewing drops, rethink frequency or content.

Plan channel messages together with your monthly content plan; the structure is in Instagram content plan. The etiquette is the same as for Telegram bot broadcasts: less often, more useful. More in Telegram bot broadcast rules.

Common mistakes

  • Duplicating feed posts in the channel. If it is the same content, there is no reason to join.
  • Sending only discounts. Members start waiting for a price cut and stop buying at full price.
  • Not preparing the first message. The notification to all followers goes out only once. The first message must explain clearly what the channel is for and give a reason to join.
  • Abandoning the channel. After a month of silence, the next message feels like it comes from a stranger.
  • Not measuring. Without UTM tags and a lead form you can only guess whether the channel helps.

Questions and answers

Does a broadcast channel affect post reach?

There is no official information on a direct link. But a channel brings your most active followers back to your profile, and active followers interact with content more. What does affect reach is covered in how the Instagram algorithm works.

Can customers ask questions in the channel?

Members cannot post in the channel: they react and vote in polls. Questions go through regular DMs or a lead form, so every message should say where to get in touch.

How many followers do I need to create a channel?

Instagram does not publish an official minimum, and different sources quote different numbers. The reliable way is to check whether the create-channel option appears in the new message menu in Direct.

Can an employee run the channel?

The channel is managed from the account's Direct inbox, so whoever has access to the account runs it. If you give someone access, switch on two-factor authentication and change the password when they leave; details in business account security.

Conclusion

An Instagram broadcast channel is not advertising that brings new customers; it is a way to stay close to the followers you already have. It runs on exclusive messages, polls and a clear call to action. Two to four useful messages a week, a measurable link and a steady rhythm are the three rules that connect a channel to sales.
