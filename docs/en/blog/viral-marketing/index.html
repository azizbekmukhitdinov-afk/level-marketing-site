Viral marketing: why content spreads and how to plan for it

"We need a viral video" is many business owners' dream. Viral marketing means spreading without paid ads, through content people share themselves. A single video can reach millions. But the honest answer is that virality can't be guaranteed — only made more likely. Here's why people share content and how businesses can use it.

Why people share

People share content to say something about themselves:

  • Useful — "You'll need this" (a tip, a life hack).
  • Funny — to lift the mood.
  • Surprising — "Can you believe this?"
  • Relatable — "That's so us!" — a familiar situation.
  • Identity — "I stand for these values."
  • Emotional — warmth, pride, sadness.

Elements of viral content

  1. A strong hook in the first seconds — Reels hooks.
  2. One simple idea that needs no explanation.
  3. Emotion — nobody shares content that leaves them indifferent.
  4. Local context — Uzbekistan, the city, the language — a sense of "this is ours".
  5. A reason to share — tagging a friend, saving it.
  6. Brevity — often 15–45 seconds.

Formats for businesses

  • Behind the scenes — production, "a day in the life" — interesting and authentic.
  • Before/after — a striking result.
  • Relatable situations — humour from the lives of customers and sellers.
  • Trends — suitable ones, in your brand's style — using trends for business.
  • Micro-dramas — people wait for the next episode — micro-drama Reels.

An honest warning: going viral isn't a strategy

  • Nobody can guarantee virality — be wary of promises to "sell viral videos".
  • The right approach is consistent content plus testing: out of many attempts, one takes off — Instagram Trial Reels.
  • A viral video doesn't turn into sales automatically: your profile, offer and link must be ready.

The risks

  • Virality that doesn't fit the brand — lots of views from the wrong audience.
  • Hype for hype's sake — provocation can damage your reputation.
  • The law — offensive, misleading or rights-infringing content is prohibited — advertising law in Uzbekistan.
  • Copyright — music and other people's content — music for video ads.

Turning virality into sales

  • A clear offer and link on your profile — an Instagram profile that sells.
  • Boost the viral video as an ad — it has already proven it works.
  • Reply quickly to comments and messages.

Questions and answers

How many followers do I need to go viral?

Not many — the recommendation feed shows videos from new accounts too.

How often should I try?

Regularly — several videos a week. Virality comes from many attempts.

What happens after the spike?

Have follow-up content ready to keep your new followers.
