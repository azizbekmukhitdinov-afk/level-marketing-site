Client: iComfort — Apple and electronics store selling on instalments.

Point A

  • Monthly sales: 100
  • No marketing strategy
  • The social media page did not inspire trust

What we did

  • Built a marketing strategy from scratch.
  • Rebuilt the Instagram page: expert videos, product reviews and clear content about prices and instalments.
  • Grew reach through Reels and paid ads.

Point B

MetricPoint APoint B
Monthly sales100700+
Monthly reach—2 million+
Marketing budget—$3,000 / month

How to apply this approach

  1. Check your profile through a buyer's eyes: is there a face, are there reviews and an open price?
  2. Show the price and instalment terms clearly — customers shouldn't have to wait for an answer.
  3. Show the seller's knowledge through product reviews and expert videos.
  4. Raise the ad budget once the profile is ready.

Who this suits

Electronics stores, businesses that sell on instalments and highly competitive retail. More in Instagram profile that sells and Expert interview videos.

Questions and answers

Why the profile first and ads second?

Ads bring people to the profile, but the decision is made there. Advertising a page that doesn't inspire trust wastes much of the money.

Takeaway

When selling phones, customers first need to trust the seller. A page with real faces, answers to questions and open prices turns every ad dollar into more sales.
