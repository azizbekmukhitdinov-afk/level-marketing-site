Google AI Mode ads: where they show and how to prepare

Google's conversational search mode, AI Mode, is running in more and more countries, and ads now appear inside its answers. Google AI Mode ads are not shown in Uzbekistan yet, but for any business that sells abroad they are already a present-day issue. This article covers where the ads appear, which campaigns are eligible and what to prepare now.

What ads in AI Mode are

AI Mode is a separate mode in Google Search: instead of a short query, a person asks a detailed question, Google's AI builds an answer and keeps the conversation going with follow-ups. For example: "Which hotels and tours are best for a 3-day trip to Samarkand?" How this mode affects website traffic, SEO and visibility is covered in our article on Google AI Mode. Here we look only at advertising.

Ads appear alongside the AI answer and are labelled as ads. On 28 October 2026 Google announced it was expanding ads in AI Mode to 10 more countries and launching "multiturn ads" — ads on follow-up questions — in the US (Google blog). So if, after the first question, someone asks "is there a cheaper one?" or "which suits a family?", an ad can be matched to that exact follow-up.

The main difference from regular search ads is that the context of the whole conversation matters, not a single keyword. A person asks a ten-word question, and Google reads the intent and picks a relevant ad.

Which countries it works in

As of November 2026, ads in AI Mode run in 11 countries:

StatusCountries
FirstUnited States
Added from 28.10.2026Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, Philippines, Singapore
PlannedBrazil, Mexico, the UK and others
Multiturn adsUS only for now

For now this mostly covers English-language queries (Search Engine Journal). Uzbekistan is not on the list, and Russian- and Uzbek-language queries are not part of this update yet.

So for a furniture store or a dental clinic in Tashkent nothing has changed. But for two kinds of business it already matters today.

Who this already affects

Uzbek companies advertising abroad. If you show ads to people in any of these 11 countries, your ad may appear in AI Mode. Examples:

  • a Samarkand or Bukhara travel agency looking for tourists from India, Malaysia or Singapore;
  • an IT outsourcing company winning clients in the US and Canada;
  • a medical university or training centre recruiting students from Pakistan;
  • a manufacturer selling abroad, if those countries are selected in Google Ads.

Such companies should check two things in their campaign settings. First, location: are ads shown to people who are in the country, or to everyone "interested in" it? Second, language: is there a separate campaign for English-language queries, with ads written in English? If your ads are only in Russian, they will hardly work in a conversation with someone in India.

Businesses planning ahead. Google keeps widening the list. When it will reach Uzbekistan, and in which languages, has not been announced — but if you set up your account properly now, you won't have to rush later.

Which campaigns can show in AI Mode

A key fact: there is no separate AI Mode campaign type and no separate budget. Ads come from your existing campaigns.

Campaign typeCan show in AI Mode
Performance MaxYes
ShoppingYes
Search, broad matchYes
AI Max for SearchYes
Search with exact and phrase match onlyNot on the eligible list

One more nuance: you cannot opt out of AI Mode separately from Search. If you run a broad-match search campaign and the country is on the list, your ad can appear there too. The only way to control this is to set the campaign up well and watch the results.

How campaign types differ in general is explained in plain language in our article on search advertising.

What to do now: 6 steps

1. Track conversions properly

Broad match and automated bidding only work well when Google knows which click turned into a lead or a sale. Website form, phone call, Telegram button — each should be set up as a conversion. Without this, broad match spends your budget on random queries.

2. Start broad match with a small test

Don't switch everything at once. Try it in one campaign with its own budget and watch the search terms report for 2–3 weeks. Don't change the bid strategy too often during the test: automated bidding needs time to learn. Judge the result after at least two weeks, by cost and quality of leads.

3. Update your negative keywords

Broad match brings plenty of irrelevant queries: "free", "job", "vacancy", "download". Review search terms weekly and add the unwanted ones as negatives. In the Tashkent market, remember Latin, Cyrillic and Russian variants — we covered this in our article on Uzbek search.

4. Answer the question in the ad itself

In AI Mode people ask detailed questions. So your headlines and descriptions should carry concrete facts: price range, timing, warranty, who it suits. In responsive search ads (RSA), write as many distinct headlines as you can. For example, "3-day Samarkand tour with hotel and guide" answers the question far better than a generic "best tours".

5. Make your landing page ready for a conversation

Someone arriving from an AI answer has already had many questions answered. If your page greets them with generic claims, they leave. You need a concrete offer, a price or pricing logic, a Q&A block and a quick lead form. For the structure, see our article on landing pages that convert.

6. Clean up your product feed

If you sell products through Shopping or Performance Max, your Merchant Center feed is the main source for AI Mode. Clear titles, good images, correct price and availability. An outdated price or an out-of-stock item in an AI answer costs you money and trust.

How to keep broad match under control

Many people read "preparing for AI Mode" as "hand everything to automation". That's a mistake. Automation works under supervision.

Illustrative example. A Samarkand travel agency advertises to India. After moving to broad match, clicks rose within a week, but irrelevant queries such as "Samarkand job visa" and "free tour guide course" appeared too. Once negatives were added, cost per lead settled. An illustrative example without figures.

A weekly control checklist:

  • search terms report reviewed, negatives added;
  • daily budget cap set and not raised abruptly;
  • cost and quality of leads checked against CRM data;
  • no outdated prices or promotions in the ads;
  • landing page loads fast on a phone.

If you want to see AI Mode traffic separately, there is no clear information yet about a dedicated Google Ads report that splits it out from Search — watch your overall Search results and follow Google's official updates.

Common mistakes

  • Panic. "AI Mode is here, ads are dead" — no. Nothing has changed in Uzbekistan yet.
  • Moving everything to broad match at once. Without conversion tracking, it eats the budget.
  • Forgetting negatives. Uncontrolled broad match brings in irrelevant queries.
  • A generic landing page. Someone with a detailed question needs more than "we're the best".
  • A stale feed. If prices and availability aren't updated, the AI shows wrong information.

Questions and answers

Are AI Mode ads shown in Uzbekistan?

As of November 2026, no: Uzbekistan is not among the 11 countries Google announced. But if you advertise to audiences in those countries, your ads can show there.

Do I need a separate campaign for AI Mode?

No. There is neither a separate campaign nor a separate budget. Ads come from Performance Max, Shopping, broad-match Search and AI Max for Search campaigns. For setting up a search campaign, see our guide to setting up Google Ads.

Can I switch off ads in AI Mode?

According to Google, you can't opt out of AI Mode separately from Search. Only exact- and phrase-match campaigns are outside the eligible list, but they also get less traffic — decide based on results.

Does this affect SEO?

Ads and organic results work separately. But AI Mode changes website traffic overall: fewer clicks, better-prepared visitors. Facts on your site, a Q&A section and clear prices help both SEO and ads.

Conclusion

Google AI Mode ads currently run in 11 countries, mostly in English, and are not shown in Uzbekistan. No separate campaign is needed: ads come from Performance Max, Shopping, broad-match Search and AI Max for Search. For businesses advertising abroad this is a present-day issue; for everyone else it's time to prepare — set up conversions, test broad match under control, update negatives, and get your landing page and feed in order.
