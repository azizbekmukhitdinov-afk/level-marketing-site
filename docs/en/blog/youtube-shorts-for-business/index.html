YouTube Shorts for business: how it differs from Reels

Many businesses post vertical videos only to Instagram Reels. But the same video can work on YouTube Shorts too — at no extra cost. Since October 2024, Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long (YouTube blog). YouTube has another advantage: it's part of Google's ecosystem, and videos stay discoverable for a long time. Here's how to use YouTube Shorts in a business.

Shorts vs Reels vs TikTok

Instagram ReelsTikTokYouTube Shorts
AudienceBroad, very active in UzbekistanYoungerBroad, YouTube users
A video's "lifespan"Mostly the first few daysMostly the first few daysLonger: search and recommendations

SearchInside InstagramInside TikTokYouTube and Google

The insight: useful Shorts such as "how to choose" or "what's the difference" can keep getting views from search for months.

Who it suits best

  • Experts and education: Q&As, tips, explanations.
  • Complex products: tech, cars, construction — "how it works".
  • Businesses with long-form video: Shorts are a door to the channel — YouTube for business.
  • Multilingual audiences: separate videos in Uzbek and Russian.

Adapting Reels for Shorts

  1. Upload the original file — without another platform's watermark.
  2. Title and description: include a search keyword for YouTube — "Choosing kitchen furniture: 3 mistakes" (an illustrative example).
  3. The first seconds: the hook decides here too — Reels hooks.
  4. Related video: link the Short to a long video, if your channel has one.
  5. Channel profile: description, links, contacts.

A content plan: 3 segments

  • Q&A: customers' real questions — twice a week.
  • Process: how it's made, how it's installed — once a week.
  • Results: before/after, a case — once a week.

Several videos from one shoot day — one shoot, 10 ad creatives.

Longer Shorts: when they make sense

The option to go up to 3 minutes doesn't mean everything should be long. A longer format suits explanations, case studies and before/after stories. Short videos remain the core — video ad length.

Measuring results

  • YouTube Studio: views, average view duration, subscribers, traffic sources (search or the Shorts feed).
  • The business metric: clicks from the channel to your site, and leads — UTM tags.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading with a TikTok watermark.
  • No title, or just "#shorts".
  • No contacts or link on the channel.
  • Expecting results in 2–3 weeks — YouTube is slower but lasts longer.

Questions and answers

Can I post the same video everywhere?

Yes, but adapt the title and description for each platform.

Can I advertise on Shorts?

Yes — video campaigns in Google Ads can also run on Shorts — Google Ads vs Meta Ads.

What do I need to open a channel?

A Google account and a few minutes to create a YouTube channel.

Conclusion

YouTube Shorts is a cheap way to get extra views and long-term search traffic from videos you've already made for Reels. The original file, a YouTube-ready title, three content segments and measurement are enough to start.
