TikTok for business: a business account and organic growth

On TikTok, a single video can reach hundreds of thousands of people without ads — because the recommendation feed looks at the video itself, not at follower count. That makes TikTok one of the cheapest ways for a new business to get noticed. Yet many owners simply repost their Instagram content there and see nothing. Here's how to set up a TikTok business account and what kind of videos to make.

First, check that your audience is there

TikTok's availability in Uzbekistan has been restricted at various times. Before investing effort, ask your customers: do they use TikTok? What do they watch? If your audience lives on Instagram and Telegram, the same vertical videos can go to Reels and YouTube Shorts. A video made for TikTok works on other platforms too.

Business account vs personal account

You can switch to a business account in TikTok's settings. The differences:

Personal accountBusiness account
AnalyticsLimitedDetailed: reach, audience, timing
Contact buttonsNoneWebsite, phone, email
MusicAll popular tracksMainly the commercial library
AdvertisingSeparateConnects to the ads account

An important nuance: many popular tracks aren't available to business accounts — you need to use the library of music cleared for commercial use. That's not a real drawback: on TikTok, voice and speech often matter more than music.

What works on TikTok

TikTok is an entertainment platform. People don't open it to watch ads. So:

  • The first 1–2 seconds are the hook: a question, an unexpected action or a result. More in Reels hooks.
  • A face and a voice. A founder or employee speaking to the camera builds trust — founder on camera.
  • A raw, unpolished look. Studio-made clips often get swiped away as ads.
  • One video, one idea. Don't cram three topics into one clip.
  • Subtitles, so people watching on mute still understand.

7 formats for businesses

  1. Behind the scenes — how an order is prepared.
  2. Before / after — renovation, beauty, detailing.
  3. Answering a question — a real customer question, a short answer.
  4. Mistakes and myths — "Most people get this wrong…"
  5. A day in the life — of an employee or the founder.
  6. Product test — strength, quality, how it's used.
  7. Video reply to a comment — a separate video answering a comment.

Script templates — Reels script templates.

A posting plan

  • First 4–6 weeks: 4–7 short videos a week. More videos means you'll learn faster which format the algorithm favours.
  • Check analytics weekly: average watch time, completion rate, profile visits.
  • Repeat the formats that work and drop the weak ones.

How to get several videos out of one shoot day — one shoot, 10 ad creatives.

Turning TikTok views into leads

Views aren't sales yet. You need a bridge:

  • a clear offer and link in your profile (a Telegram bot, website or form);
  • one concrete action at the end of each video: "For prices, message us via the link in our profile";
  • fast replies to comments and messages;
  • tracking the source of every lead — UTM tags.

An organic video that performs well can later be boosted with ads — TikTok Ads in Uzbekistan.

Common mistakes

  • Reposting Instagram posts and ad clips unchanged.
  • Videos that open with a logo.
  • Posting 3–4 videos and quitting: "TikTok doesn't work".
  • Nothing but trending dances — content unrelated to the business.
  • No link or offer in the profile.

Questions and answers

Does follower count matter on TikTok?

Less than elsewhere. The recommendation feed judges video quality, so a new account's video can still get many views.

How soon will I see results?

Usually after 4–8 weeks of regular posting it becomes clear which formats work.

Can I post the same videos on Instagram?

Yes, but upload the original file without the TikTok watermark.

Who should film?

To start, a phone and one responsible person are enough — shooting Reels on a phone.

Conclusion

TikTok can introduce a new business to people without ads, but it's an entertainment platform: you need a hook, a face, raw video, one idea per clip and consistency. Check first that your audience is there, then turn views into leads with an offer and link in your profile.
