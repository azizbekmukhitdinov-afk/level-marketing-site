Instagram content plan: 30 post ideas for a month

October 22, 2026 · 2 min read

  2. Balancing content types
  3. 30 ideas
  4. How to lay out the plan
  5. Conclusion
  6. More on this topic

If "what are we posting today?" comes up every day, the page becomes chaotic and doesn't sell. A content plan solves this: you know a month ahead what you'll post, when and why. Here is how to build a content plan, plus 30 ideas for any business.

How much to post

A practical minimum for a small business: 3–4 posts or Reels a week and a few Stories every day. Consistency beats volume: three posts every week is better than 30 posts in a month followed by a month of silence.

Balancing content types

TypeShareGoal
Useful~40%Trust and saves
Proof~25%Reviews, results, cases
Process and team~20%Closeness to the brand
Offer~15%Sales

It isn't a strict rule, but a page made only of offers turns into a notice board.

30 ideas

Useful

  1. How to choose the product — 5 tips.
  2. Answers to 3 frequent customer questions.
  3. Mistakes people make using the product.
  4. "Cheap vs expensive option: what's the difference".
  5. A seasonal tip.
  6. A mini glossary: your industry's terms in plain words.
  7. A "what to check before buying" checklist.
  8. Myth vs fact.

Proof

  1. A customer review (video or screenshot).
  2. Before / after.
  3. A project case with numbers.
  4. A customer's question and your solution.
  5. Awards, certificates, partners.
  6. A year in numbers: customers, orders, projects.

Process and team

  1. How the working day starts.
  2. How an order is prepared.
  3. Meet a team member.
  4. Inside the office or production.
  5. How we fixed a mistake — an honest story.
  6. The founder's story: why they started the business.
  7. How a new product is born.
  8. The delivery process.

Offer

  1. Launching a new product.
  2. A time-limited promotion.
  3. Instalments or special terms.
  4. A bundle offer.
  5. A "followers only" bonus.
  6. A free consultation or measurement.
  7. Gift ideas.
  8. The month's results and next month's plans.

How to lay out the plan

A simple table is enough (Google Sheets or Notion): date, content type, format (post, Reel, carousel), topic, call to action, status. Batch your shooting into one day — filming 8–10 videos in a day is far easier than one a day. For writing Reels scripts, see Reels script templates; for the first seconds, our hooks article.

Conclusion

A content plan turns a page of random posts into a systematic sales channel. Rotate content types, post consistently and review each month which posts brought leads. And don't forget your profile — see an Instagram profile that sells.
