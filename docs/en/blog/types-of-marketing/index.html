"Performance marketing", "brand marketing", "content marketing", "digital" — there are so many marketing terms that owners lose track of which one they need. In reality, they don't contradict each other: they're parts of one system. Here are the main types of marketing in plain language, and where a small business should start.

The main types of marketing

TypeKey questionResultSpeed
Performance marketingHow many leads and sales today?Measurable leadsFast
Brand marketingDo people remember and trust us?Awareness, trust, higher pricesSlow
Content marketingWhat value do we give customers?Trust, search trafficSlow

Performance marketing

Marketing where every soum is measured against results: targeted ads, search ads, leads, cost per sale. Its strength is speed and precision. Its weakness: stop the ads and the flow stops. The basics — what is targeted advertising and what is a lead.

Brand marketing

Work that makes people remember you, trust you and tell you apart: name, visual style, tone of voice, values. Results are slow, but brand makes performance cheaper — more people respond to ads from a familiar brand. More in what is a brand and what is a brand book.

Content marketing and SMM

Trust through useful content: videos, articles, advice. SMM is the part of that content on social networks, plus conversations. More in what is content marketing and what is SMM.

Local marketing

For stores, restaurants, clinics and salons: maps listings, reviews, signage, ads within a radius of the location. Often the fastest-acting type — ranking higher on Yandex Maps.

Partnership and influencer marketing

Reaching people through other businesses' or influencers' audiences: joint promotions, influencer ads, brand ambassadors. More in influencer advertising.

Referral and retention marketing

The cheapest customer is an existing one, plus the friend they bring. Reviews, a referral programme, attention to regulars — types of customers and repeat sales and LTV.

B2B and B2C

This isn't a "type" of marketing but who you sell to: companies (B2B) or individuals (B2C). The approach differs a lot — B2B vs B2C.

Where a small business should start

You don't need to do everything at once. A logical order:

  1. The offer and brand basics: for whom, what, what's different — unique selling proposition.
  2. Local marketing: a maps listing and reviews — cheap and fast.
  3. Performance: ads in one channel, judged by sales.
  4. Content and SMM: for trust and better ad performance.
  5. Retention and referrals: once a customer base builds up.

How it fits into a system — marketing system for growth.

Common mistakes

  • Performance only — without brand and trust, leads keep getting pricier.
  • Brand only — pretty, but unmeasured and not selling.
  • Every type at once on a small budget.
  • Forgetting existing customers.
  • Deciding "we need digital" based on buzzwords rather than results.

Questions and answers

Performance or brand — which matters more?

Both. Performance delivers today's sales; brand delivers tomorrow's sales and cheaper advertising.

What is digital marketing?

All marketing through online channels: ads, SMM, SEO, content, messengers.

How many types does a small business need?

Two or three to start: local, one performance channel and basic content.

Who manages the different types?

In a small business, the owner or a single marketer. As you grow, a team or an agency — agency, freelancer or in-house.

Conclusion

The types of marketing are parts of one system: performance brings today's leads, brand and content build trust, local marketing brings nearby customers and retention marketing earns more from existing ones.
