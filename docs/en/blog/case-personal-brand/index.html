A personal brand is an expert's cheapest client acquisition channel: people trust a person before they trust a company. We build experts a profile, a content strategy and regular video production.

How we work

  1. Define the expert's positioning and audience.
  2. Rebuild the profile: bio, highlights, visual style.
  3. Content plan: expert videos, personal stories, cases.
  4. Our own team handles shooting and editing.
  5. Where needed, paid ads speed up audience growth.

If you're starting on your own: 4 steps

  1. Positioning — in one sentence: whom you help and to what result.
  2. A schedule — 2–3 videos a week, prepared in a single shoot day.
  3. Topics — taken from your customers' real questions.
  4. Measurement — not followers, but the number of enquiries that came through your content.

Who this suits

Lawyers, doctors, teachers, consultants and entrepreneurs — any field where clients trust a person first and choose the service second. More in Personal brand: where to start and Expert interview videos.

Questions and answers

How much time should an expert spend on content?

With the right system, mostly just filming time: a few hours a week. The team handles scripts, editing and posting, while the expert shares their knowledge.

When does a personal brand show results?

The first enquiries may come after a few months of regular work, but a personal brand is a long-term asset: the longer it's built, the stronger it works.

Takeaway

Consistency and authenticity matter most in a personal brand. Once the system is in place, the expert just talks to the camera — the team does the rest.
