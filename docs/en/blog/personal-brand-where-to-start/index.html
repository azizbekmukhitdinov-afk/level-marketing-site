Personal brand: where an expert should start

People trust a person faster than a company. That's why, for a doctor, lawyer, teacher, marketer or business owner, a personal brand is one of the cheapest ways to attract clients. Here is where to start, how to choose your positioning and how to plan the first 90 days.

What a personal brand gives you

  • Trust. Clients already "know" you from your videos.
  • Cheaper leads. Referrals and organic reach cut ad spend.
  • Pricing power. People pay more for a well-known expert.
  • Opportunities. Partnerships, speaking, interviews.

Positioning in one sentence

Formula: "I help [who] achieve [result] through [method]."

  • "I help Tashkent entrepreneurs get a steady flow of leads through paid ads."
  • "I help parents get their children's teeth treated without pain."

The more specific, the more memorable. "Everything for everyone" isn't positioning.

Choosing a platform

PlatformFor whom
InstagramThe main platform for almost every expert
Telegram channelDeeper content and a loyal audience
YouTubeComplex topics and detailed answers
LinkedInB2B and corporate clients

One main and one secondary platform are enough to start.

Content pillars

  1. Expertise — tips, mistakes, how-tos.
  2. Cases — results and client stories.
  3. Personal — values, your journey, moments from your workday.
  4. Opinion — your stance on industry issues.
  5. Offer — a consultation, service or product.

A plan for the first 90 days

PeriodWhat to do
Weeks 1–2Positioning, profile, bio, pinned posts
Weeks 3–83–4 posts or Reels a week, Stories every day
Weeks 9–12A first offer: a consultation or service; collaborations, interviews

For setting up your profile, see an Instagram profile that sells; for the video format, expert interview videos.

Common mistakes

  • Copying others — you'll be compared with the original.
  • Only selling — people leave a page that gives nothing.
  • Inconsistency — a month of activity, then silence.
  • Hiding your face — a personal brand needs a person.

Conclusion

A personal brand is one-sentence positioning, consistent useful content and honesty. Start small, stay consistent for 90 days and turn results into cases. The personal brands we've worked with are on our personal brand cases page.
