Video ad production cost: what drives it and how to save

"How much does a video ad cost?" The answer ranges from a Reel shot on a phone to several shoot days with a large crew. Prices can differ tenfold or more — and an expensive video doesn't always sell better. Here's what makes up the cost of a video and which videos are worth investing in for advertising.

What makes up the cost

ComponentWhat's includedWhat pushes the price up
Idea and scriptConcept, copy, shot listA complex plot, many variants
CrewCamera operator, director, lighting, soundCrew size, number of days
EquipmentCamera, lenses, lights, droneCinema cameras, specialist gear
LocationStudio, office, street, rented venueRental, permits, several locations
Actors and modelsProfessionals or customers/staffFamous faces, many roles
EditingCutting, colour, pacing, subtitlesMany versions and formats
Graphics and animationText, logo, 2D/3D3D and complex animation
SoundVoice-over, music licenceA professional voice, original music

The biggest cost drivers are the number of shoot days, crew size and graphics.

Types of video, from simple to complex

  1. UGC-style phone video — an employee or customer speaks. The cheapest, and often effective in ads — UGC content.
  2. A Reels series with a small crew — several videos from one shoot day.
  3. Product video — in a studio with good lighting — product video mistakes.
  4. A brand film — a storyline, actors, several locations.
  5. Animated explainer — to explain a complex service.

Each type has its own job — types of video ads.

The insight: for paid social, expensive isn't always better

In Instagram and TikTok feeds, an overly polished clip is often recognised as an ad and swiped away. A natural, raw video of a person talking to camera often brings cheaper leads. So for paid social, 5–10 simple videos with different hooks can be more useful than one expensive clip: you test them and find what works — A/B testing ad creatives.

A brand film makes sense for TV, big screens, presentations and introducing the brand.

How to lower the cost

  • Many videos in one day: 10 scripts prepared in advance, one location, one crew — one shoot, 10 ad creatives.
  • Approve the script in advance: changes on the shoot day are the most expensive.
  • Your own location and staff: office, store, production floor — authentic and cheaper.
  • Many formats from one shoot: 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 — done in editing.
  • Reuse: good footage can be used again in later months.

What to check when you ask for a quote

  1. How many finished videos, and in which formats?
  2. How many shoot days and hours?
  3. Who writes the script, and how many revisions are allowed?
  4. How many post-edit revisions are included?
  5. Is the music licensed for use in advertising?
  6. Will you receive the raw footage?
  7. Have the actors consented to use of their image? By law, using a person's image in advertising requires consent — advertising law in Uzbekistan.

Questions and answers

How long should a video ad be?

On social media, usually short — video ad length.

Is AI video cheaper?

For some tasks, yes: voice-over, subtitles, simple animation. But real people and real products still matter for trust — AI video ads.

Can I film it myself on a phone?

Yes — in many cases that's a good start — shooting Reels on a phone.
