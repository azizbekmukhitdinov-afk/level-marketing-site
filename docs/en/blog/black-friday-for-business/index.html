Black Friday for business: how to sell without losing margin

Black Friday started in the US, but every year more shops and online platforms in Uzbekistan run a promotion at the end of November. In 2026, Black Friday falls on 27 November. For many businesses, the day ends one of two ways: record sales — or record sales with no profit. The difference lies in preparation and how the offer is built. Here's how to run a Black Friday promotion without losing your margin.

Do the maths first: can you afford the discount?

An illustrative example: a product sells for 200,000 soum, costs 120,000, leaving an 80,000 margin. A 30% discount is 60,000. That leaves a 20,000 margin — before subtracting ad spend. Announcing "−50%" without doing this maths can mean selling at a loss. More in the discount trap and unit economics.

6 kinds of offer instead of a discount

  1. Bundles: "Buy 2, get the third free". The average order value rises.
  2. A gift with purchase: a product or service free above a certain spend.
  3. A discount on one category only: an old collection or surplus stock.
  4. Limited quantity: "Only 50 units" — it must be true.
  5. Early access: one day early for followers or customers in your database.
  6. A coupon for the next purchase: full price today, a bonus next time — it brings customers back.

A preparation plan

3 weeks beforeWork out the offer and margin, check stock

1 week beforeA waiting list: collect followers and contacts, warm-up ads
2–3 days beforeSales team: script, shifts, reply templates
Promotion daysRaise the ad budget, monitor response speed
The week afterReview results, thank buyers, make the next offer

Advertising: warm up in advance

Ads often get more expensive during the promotion, because everyone advertises at once. So:

  • 7–10 days ahead, publish "promotion coming" content and invite sign-ups to the waiting list;
  • on the day, retarget the audience you warmed up — how retargeting works;
  • run several creatives: the offer, the product, the deadline — A/B testing ad creatives.

How to scale the budget — before increasing your ad budget.

The sales team is the weakest link

Enquiries spike during the promotion. If salespeople can't keep up, ad money is wasted. Prepare in advance:

  • an extra shift or helper;
  • ready answers to frequent questions;
  • an automatic first reply on Telegram or Instagram DMs — Meta Business Suite automated responses;
  • hourly monitoring of response speed — lead response time.

The law: an honest promotion

  • The discount must be real: raising the price before the promotion and then "cutting" it is misleading advertising.
  • Prices are shown in soum; comparative claims such as "the lowest prices" must be provable — advertising law in Uzbekistan.
  • State the terms (dates, limits) openly.
  • Offering instalments has its own rules — installment advertising rules.

Measuring results

Look beyond total sales:

  • net profit after discounts and ads;
  • the number of new customers and their repeat purchases;
  • average order value;
  • returns.

Questions and answers

Does Black Friday work in Uzbekistan?

Yes, especially for online stores, electronics, clothing and cosmetics. But check last year's results to see whether your audience waits for this day.

One day or a week?

Many run a "Black Week". A promotion that runs too long loses impact; 3–5 days is often the right balance.

Does a small shop need one?

Not necessarily. If your margin can't carry a discount, choose a bundle or gift format.

Conclusion

Black Friday isn't a discount contest but a preparation contest: margin maths, offers without discounts, an audience warmed up in advance, a ready sales team and honest terms.
