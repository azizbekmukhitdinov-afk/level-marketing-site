GEO: getting your business into ChatGPT and Google AI answers

People now ask questions not only in Google Search but also in ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI assistants: "Where's a good dentist in Tashkent?", "Where can I order a custom kitchen?". Google itself now shows AI answers above search results. That's why a new search is growing fast: "promotion in AI", "GEO". Here's how your business can appear in AI answers and what actually works.

What GEO is

GEO (generative engine optimisation) is the work of making sure information about your business appears accurately and often in generative AI answers — Google's AI answers, ChatGPT, Gemini and others. It's a continuation of SEO, not separate magic.

The key difference: in regular search, people see ten links and choose. In an AI answer, they see a ready-made conclusion — with two or three sources or brand names. Being among them or not makes a big difference.

What Google says

Google's official guidance for site owners says there are no additional requirements or special optimisations to appear in AI Overviews and AI Mode: a page must be indexed and eligible to appear in Search with a snippet, and you don't need new "AI files" or special markup (Google Search Central).

The important takeaway: be careful with anyone selling "secret GEO techniques". The foundation is good SEO:

  • pages are open to search crawlers;
  • important information is in text, not inside images;
  • pages are linked internally;
  • structured data matches the visible page text.

Google Search Console now also has a report on visibility in generative AI results — track how your site appears there.

What influences AI answers: 5 factors

1. Content that answers the question clearly

AI answers are built from pages that answer the question. Not an "we're the best" page, but a precise answer to a precise question: "what affects the price of a kitchen", "how long does an implant last". You can build this content from customers' questions — content from customer objections.

2. Consistent information everywhere

Your name, address, phone, hours and services should match across your site, Google and Yandex Maps listings, Instagram and directories. Conflicting information confuses AI. More in add your business to maps.

3. Reviews and mentions

AI assistants often rely on opinions on other sites: reviews, articles, forums. Genuine reviews and mentions elsewhere are an important signal. More in online reputation and reviews and crowd marketing.

4. Language

There's little quality content in Uzbek — that's an opportunity. Questions are asked in Uzbek and Russian, in Latin and Cyrillic. More in Uzbek search.

5. Expertise and transparency

Who writes, where the company is, what experience it has — make this visible on your site. Real work and case studies are trust signals.

A practical plan: 6 steps

  1. A question list: write down 20–30 questions your customers ask AI — price, choosing, comparing, "where".
  2. A check: ask those questions in ChatGPT, Gemini and Google — which brands and sources appear?
  3. Answer pages: a clear answer on your site for each important question — an article or FAQ.
  4. Listings: make your maps and directory listings consistent and complete.
  5. A review system: new genuine reviews every month.
  6. Recheck monthly: has the result changed for the same questions?

What not to do

  • Fake reviews and articles — if exposed, they damage your reputation and carry legal risk (misleading advertising).
  • Keyword-stuffed copy — bad for people and for search.
  • Trusting sellers of "special AI files" — Google officially says such files aren't needed.
  • GEO alone — AI answers are only part of traffic; you still need classic search, maps and ads.

Measuring results

GEO is hard to measure precisely for now, but you can track:

  • a monthly manual check on chosen questions: is your brand in the AI answer;
  • visibility reports in Search Console;
  • "from ChatGPT" or "from AI" answers to "How did you hear about us?" — add this option to your CRM;
  • growth in searches for your brand name.

Questions and answers

Are GEO and SEO the same?

GEO is an extension of SEO. The core work is the same: quality content, technical order, trust signals. The difference is more attention to question-style queries and mentions on other sites.

Can appearing in AI answers be guaranteed?

No. Nobody can guarantee it — the AI composes the answer itself. You can only raise the odds.

Does a small business need GEO?

Yes, especially local services: "... near me" questions are increasingly asked of AI. The key steps — listings, reviews and answer pages — are inexpensive.

How long until results?

Like SEO — months. Content indexing and mentions on other sites take time.

Conclusion

Visibility in AI answers is a new channel, but its foundation is old: content that answers questions clearly, consistent information everywhere, genuine reviews and transparency. Google itself says no special tricks are needed. Level Marketing's own site runs in three languages with articles that answer customers' questions — and we apply the same approach for our clients.
