Client: Chorsu Gold Center — jewellery store, Tashkent.

Goal

Create a visual identity that reflects what makes the store unique, communicate its advantages across channels and drive sales.

Solution

  • Together with the client we built a unique offer for the market: a 5-year interest-free instalment plan.
  • We focused the audience's attention on the benefits of that offer.
  • All creatives and content followed the brand's visual identity.

Results

MetricResult
Campaign length3 months
Sales$600,000

How to apply this approach

  1. Identify the buyer's main barrier — for expensive products it's often price or trust.
  2. Develop an offer that removes that barrier — only if the business can genuinely deliver it.
  3. Put the offer at the centre of every creative and present it in a consistent brand style.
  4. Measure results by sales, not clicks.

Who this suits

Expensive products people think hard about: jewellery, real estate, furniture, electronics. How to find your offer is covered in Unique selling proposition, and style in Visual identity for small business.

Questions and answers

Are instalments a good offer for any business?

No. They work for expensive products where price is the main barrier, and the business must be financially ready for them. In other cases, a warranty, speed or service may be a stronger offer.

Takeaway

For expensive products like jewellery, the offer matters more than the ad itself. Price is the buyer's main barrier, and an offer that removes it strengthens both the creative and the cost per lead.
