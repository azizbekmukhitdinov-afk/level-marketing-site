What is an ad creative: types and signs of a good creative

What does a media buyer mean when they say "the creative isn't working"? A creative is the part of an ad people actually see: the image, video, carousel, copy and headline together. Today Meta and other platforms increasingly choose the audience themselves, so the creative does more than anything else to decide an ad's results. Here's what a creative is and what makes a good one. How creatives are made — how ad creatives are produced.

Types of creative

TypeWorks best for
Static imageA simple offer, a promotion, a price
Video / ReelsProcess, results, people and stories — types of video ads
CarouselSeveral products or a step-by-step explanation
UGC styleA customer or employee speaking — trust — UGC content
Text (search)Google and Yandex ads — how to write ad copy

The structure of a good creative

  1. Hook — the first 1–3 seconds or the first line, which stops the scroll — Reels hooks.
  2. Problem or desire — the customer recognises themselves.
  3. Offer — exactly what, and for whom.
  4. Proof — a result, a review, a number — social proof.
  5. Call to action — one specific step — what is a call to action.

How to judge a creative

  • Hook rate — the share of viewers who watch the first seconds of a video.
  • CTR — clicks — what is CTR.
  • Cost per lead — the main metric.

A creative doesn't have to be beautiful — it has to work. A simple phone video often beats a studio production.

Testing and refreshing

  • Run 3–6 creatives built on different ideas at once — A/B testing ad creatives.
  • "Testing" one creative with 10 captions isn't diversity — Meta Andromeda.
  • When CTR falls and frequency rises, it's time to refresh — creative fatigue.

Common mistakes

  • Videos that open with a logo.
  • Three offers in one creative.
  • Text too small to read on a phone.
  • Making the product look better than it is — a trust and legal problem — advertising law in Uzbekistan.
  • The same creative for months.

Questions and answers

How does a creative differ from content?

Content is for your page (organic); a creative is for ads. But a good organic Reel often makes a good creative.

How many creatives do I need?

On a small budget, 3–6 genuinely different creatives are a good start, with new ones every month.

Can I make creatives with AI?

For ideas, backgrounds and drafts, yes — but the product must be shown as it really is — AI images for business.
