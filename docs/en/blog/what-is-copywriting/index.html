What is copywriting: the basics of writing copy that sells

Copywriting is writing text that prompts the reader to take a specific action: order, sign up, call. Ad headlines, website pages, Instagram captions, commercial proposals and even sales scripts are all copywriting. Good copy brings more leads from the same ad budget. Here's what copywriting is and how to improve it.

Copywriting vs content writing

CopywritingContent writing
GoalAction: an order, a leadInformation, trust, interest
ExamplesAds, landing pages, proposalsBlogs, tips posts, articles
Measured byConversion, leadsRead-through, saves, return visits

They work together: content builds trust, copywriting prompts action — what is content marketing.

7 rules of good copy

  1. One reader — know exactly who you're writing for.
  2. Customers' words — phrases from reviews and conversations.
  3. Benefits, not features — not "8 kg" but "a big family's laundry in one wash".
  4. Specific numbers and proof — real ones.
  5. Short sentences — people read on phones.
  6. One action — a clear step at the end.
  7. Editing — the first draft is never final.

Copywriting with AI

AI quickly produces drafts and variants, but:

  • customers' words, facts and brand voice must come from you;
  • a native speaker should check Uzbek copy;
  • watch out for generic text that sounds like everyone else's.

Prompts — marketing prompts.

How to choose a copywriter

  • Portfolio: work in a niche close to yours.
  • A test task: one ad or a landing page's hero section.
  • Do they ask questions about the customer, offer and competitors? A good copywriter asks first.
  • Results: what conversion rates their copy achieved.

The law

Claims like "the best" or "No. 1" in ad copy require proof, and prices must be in soum — advertising law in Uzbekistan.
