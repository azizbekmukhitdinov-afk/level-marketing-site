AI images for business: rules and prompts that work

Today, an AI image generator can create a post image, a banner background or a visual for an ad idea in seconds. That cuts design costs and lets you test ideas fast. Used badly, though, AI images make a brand look cheap, mislead customers or cause legal trouble. Here are the rules for creating AI images for a business.

Where it works and where it doesn't

Good fitUse with careNot suitable
Post and story backgroundsImages of peopleAs a "real" product photo
Concepts and moodboardsLogos — AI logoBefore/after results
Illustrations and infographicsImages with text (errors)A customer review or a "real" person
Quick tests of ad ideasLocal context (clothing, streets)Certificates, documents, price tags

The main rule: AI is for illustrations and ideas. Images of what the customer is actually buying must be real. Using AI properly for product photos — AI product photos.

6 business scenarios

  1. Post backgrounds and collages — a clean background in brand colours for text.
  2. Article and blog illustrations — an image that shows the topic.
  3. Ad concepts — showing the idea to a designer and camera operator before the shoot.
  4. Seasonal design — backgrounds for holiday and promotion banners.
  5. Creative tests — quickly create 5 visual directions, then shoot the best one for real — A/B testing ad creatives.
  6. Presentations and proposals — illustrations for slides — AI presentations.

Prompt structure

Good results come from precise descriptions. The structure:

  • Subject: what is shown.
  • Setting and background: where, what kind of environment.
  • Style: photorealistic, minimalist, 3D, illustration.
  • Light and colour: warm daylight, brand colours.
  • Composition: close-up, empty space on the left for text.
  • Format: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16.

An illustrative example: "Modern kitchen interior, warm morning light, minimalist style, white and wood tones, empty space on the left for text, 4:5 format, photorealistic."

Many services render text inside images with errors — add text afterwards in a design tool.

Keeping a consistent brand style

AI images in mixed styles make a page look chaotic. The rules:

  • A style prompt: reuse the same style description every time.
  • Brand colours: name the exact colours in the prompt.
  • A reference image: many services can reproduce a style from a sample.
  • One template: the AI image plus your brand font and logo, applied in a design tool.

On visual style — visual identity for small business.

Legal questions

  • Copyright: works created solely by AI may not be protected by copyright in Uzbekistan (anhor.uz) — so a competitor could use a similar image too. Create your brand's core elements with a designer.
  • Images of people: using a person's image in advertising requires their consent; be careful with images resembling real people (Advertising Law).
  • Misleading claims: showing a product as better than it is may count as misleading advertising — advertising law in Uzbekistan.
  • Service terms: read each AI service's commercial-use terms.

Common mistakes

  • Passing off an AI image of a product as a real photo.
  • A different style in every post.
  • Misspelled text inside the image.
  • "Plastic" people and wrong fingers, published without checking.
  • Copying a competitor's or another brand's style.

Questions and answers

Which AI image tool is best?

The tools change fast. Try 2–3 on your own task: quality, style control, commercial terms and price.

Will I no longer need a designer?

You will. AI is a fast tool, but a designer is needed for the brand system, curation and final quality.

Do AI images in ads need labelling?

Uzbek law has no specific labelling requirement for now, but platforms may label some AI content. What matters most is not misleading customers.

Conclusion

AI image generation is a powerful tool for illustrations, backgrounds, concepts and quick tests. But what the customer buys must be shown as it really is, your brand style must stay consistent, and the legal rules must be followed. In a free audit, Level Marketing will review your ad creatives.
