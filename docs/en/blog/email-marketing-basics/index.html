Email marketing: who needs it in Uzbekistan and how to start

In Uzbekistan, customers mostly live on Telegram and Instagram. So is email still worth it? Not for every business — but for some it remains the cheapest sales channel. Email marketing means sending useful and sales emails to customers who have given their consent. Here's who it suits and how to start.

Which businesses it suits

  • B2B — decisions go through corporate email — B2B marketing.
  • Online stores — order confirmations, abandoned carts, new collections.
  • Education and courses — lessons, materials, reminders.
  • International customers — exports, tourism.

For local B2C, a Telegram bot or SMS is often more convenient — Telegram bot broadcast rules.

Building a list with consent

  • Only email people who have consented — a bought list is a spam and reputation risk.
  • Rules for collecting and storing personal data — customer databases and personal data.
  • Give people a reason to subscribe: a lead magnet, a discount, a useful guide — lead magnets.

Types of emails

  1. The welcome email — sent right after sign-up; the most-opened email you'll send.
  2. Useful emails — tips, guides, case studies.
  3. Offer emails — promotions, new products.
  4. Transactional emails — orders, payments, delivery.
  5. Re-engagement — for people who haven't bought in a while.

Automated sequences

Set them up once and they run on their own:

  • Welcome sequence: day 0 — a gift, day 2 — useful material, day 5 — an offer.
  • Abandoned cart: a reminder after 1–3 hours.
  • Post-purchase: thank you → usage tips → a review request → a repeat offer — repeat sales and LTV.

Staying out of spam

  • Use email on your own domain with technical settings (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Since 2024, Gmail and Yahoo require these from bulk senders.
  • Include a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Clean inactive addresses from your list.
  • Avoid spammy subject lines like "FREE!!!".

Metrics

  • Open rate — depends on the subject line and sender (though Apple's privacy features can distort this number).
  • Clicks (CTR) — the email's content and offer — what is CTR.
  • Conversions and revenue — the metric that matters most.
  • Unsubscribes and spam complaints.

Common mistakes

  • Only promotions, no useful content.
  • The same email for everyone — no segmentation — market segmentation.
  • Designs that can't be read on a phone.
  • Not measuring results.

Questions and answers

Does email work in Uzbekistan?

In B2B, e-commerce and education, yes. In local B2C, Telegram is often more effective.

How often should I send?

Once a week is a good start. Consistency and value are what matter.

Which service should I use?

Any professional service that supports consent, unsubscribes and statistics.

Conclusion

Email marketing is a cheap, automatable sales channel for B2B, e-commerce and education. The foundations are a consent-based list, useful emails, automated sequences and proper technical setup. In a free audit, Level Marketing will show you which channels suit your business.
