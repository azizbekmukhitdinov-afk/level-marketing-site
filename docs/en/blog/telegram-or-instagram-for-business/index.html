Telegram channel or Instagram: where should a business invest?

Almost every business owner in Uzbekistan asks this at some point: should we open a Telegram channel, or is it better to focus on Instagram? Many start both at once, post the same content in both places and conclude a few months later that "neither works". The problem is that the two channels have different jobs. Let's compare them and look at which channel suits which business.

The key difference: being found vs keeping in touch

Put simply:

  • Instagram is where new people find you. Through Reels, recommendations, search and ads, someone who didn't know you arrives. The visual format helps build trust: faces, process, results.
  • Telegram is where you stay in touch with people who already know you. Subscribers see channel posts in their chat list. But strangers rarely find a channel on their own — Telegram has no strong mechanism playing the role of a recommendation feed.

So the question isn't "which is better" but what do you need more right now: a flow of new customers or repeat contact with existing ones?

Which channel for which business

Instagram first

  • Visual products: furniture, clothing, beauty salons, restaurants, renovation — you need to show them.
  • One-off or infrequent purchases: people find you once and buy.
  • A new business: nobody knows you yet.

Telegram first

  • Experts and education: long texts, useful materials, lessons — Telegram's format fits. See the example in the online courses case.
  • Frequent repeat purchases: food delivery, flowers, cosmetics — news and offers for regular customers.
  • Businesses with lots of updates: price changes, new arrivals, wholesale price lists — handy for B2B and wholesale.

You need both

For many businesses the best model is using both, with different jobs: Instagram brings new people, Telegram keeps them and drives repeat sales.

How to connect the two

If you need both, link them into one chain:

  1. Instagram → Telegram: at the end of useful Instagram content, "the full list is on our Telegram channel". Channel link in bio.
  2. Telegram → sale: channel posts link to a salesperson or bot. Collecting leads with a bot — Telegram bot for leads.
  3. Customer → Telegram: invite every buyer to subscribe; that's your repeat-sales base. More in repeat sales and LTV.

Posting the same thing in both places saves time but hurts results. Write a short, visual version for Instagram and a more detailed, text-led version for Telegram.

Limited resources: choosing one channel

In small businesses, one person often runs both channels and both end up half-done. If you can only resource one, answer these:

  1. How do customers find me — by chance or through recommendations?
  2. How soon does a customer buy again?
  3. Does my product need showing or explaining?
  4. Which channel brings more real enquiries, according to the CRM?

For most new businesses the answer is Instagram plus ads, with Telegram once a customer base builds up. For experts with a good audience and repeat-purchase businesses, it may be the other way round.

Common mistakes

  • Posting the same content in both channels unchanged.
  • Opening a Telegram channel without ads and waiting for organic growth.
  • Only promotions in the channel — subscribers leave quickly.
  • Bringing new people to Instagram and not keeping them anywhere.
  • Not measuring which channel brings enquiries. More in UTM tags.

Questions and answers

How do you grow a Telegram channel?

Mostly through ads, placements in other channels, partnerships and inviting existing customers. More in grow a Telegram channel.

Telegram group or channel — which is better?

A channel is for one-way announcements, a group for discussion. Businesses usually need a channel, sometimes with a linked discussion group.

Should I move Instagram followers to Telegram?

Not all of them. Give people a reason: a discount, useful material, exclusive offers. Pushing them loses followers.

Which channel brings cheaper customers?

It depends on the niche and content. Tag enquiries and sales from each channel in your CRM and you'll see for yourself within 2–3 months.

Conclusion

Instagram and Telegram aren't rivals but two links in one chain: one finds new people, the other keeps them. Work out which link is weaker for your business right now and invest there.
