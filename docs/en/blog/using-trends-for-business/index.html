How to use Reels and TikTok trends for business

A trend is a format, sound or idea that lots of people use at the same time. Used well, it helps you reach a new audience fast; used badly, it makes a brand look silly or awkward. Here is where to find trends, when to join them and how to adapt them for a business.

Where to find trends

  • The Reels tab — popular sounds show a growth indicator.
  • TikTok Creative Center — stats on popular sounds, hashtags and formats.
  • Competitors' and international brands' pages.
  • Topic-specific Telegram channels — local memes and topics.

When to join

Trends are short-lived — often days to a few weeks. Join early and you get more reach; once everyone has done it, the trend looks stale.

How to adapt a trend to your brand

  1. Keep the format — the familiar sound, rhythm or shot structure.
  2. Change the meaning — make it about your product, your customers' lives or your process.
  3. Match your brand voice — a serious clinic and a youth café use the same joke differently.
  4. Add value — trend + a tip, or trend + a product demo.

Examples

BusinessTrend idea
Café"How an order is made" to a popular sound
Education centreThe "student's expectation vs reality" format
Furniture storeA room makeover with a before/after transition
Car detailingA rhythmic, fast-cut "cleaning process"

When to skip a trend

  • Trends tied to politics, tragedy or mocking someone.
  • When your audience won't get it.
  • When it doesn't fit your brand at all — forced attempts show.

Important: trending music and ads

A popular track may be fine in organic Reels, but you may not have the rights to use it in ads. Choose royalty-free music for ads — more in our video editing apps article.

Balancing trends and evergreen content

Trends bring reach, but sales usually come from consistent useful content. A practical split: about 20–30% trends, the rest from the content types in your content plan.

Publishing a trend video within 48 hours: a workflow

Trends pass quickly, so the release process needs to be ready in advance:

  1. 15 minutes of monitoring every day — the Reels feed, saved audio and competitors' pages.
  2. Filter — does the trend fit your brand, your audience and your current offer?
  3. A three-line script — hook, main shot, ending or call to action.
  4. Shoot — a phone, daylight, several takes; tips in Shooting Reels on a phone.
  5. Edit and add captions — with a ready template this doesn't take long.
  6. Publish — within a day or two of spotting the trend.

If every video goes through long approvals, the trend will be stale before it's out. Decide in advance who makes the call.

How to measure the result

Compare a trend video with the average results of your regular posts:

MetricWhat it shows
Reach among non-followersWhether the video reached a new audience
Saves and sharesWhether the content was useful or interesting
Profile visits and new followersWhether the trend sparked interest in the brand
DMs and leadsWhether the reach moved people closer to buying

If reach is high but nobody visits the profile, the trend isn't connected to your brand. How the algorithm distributes reach is covered in How the Instagram algorithm works.

Common mistakes

  • Joining a trend after everyone has already used it.
  • Copying a competitor's video without changing anything.
  • No sign of the brand, product or benefit in the video.
  • No hook and no call to action: reach, but no leads.
  • Chasing every trend and dropping your regular content series.

Questions and answers

Do you need to join every trend?

No. Pick only the trends that fit your brand and make sense to your audience. One well-adapted trend a week is worth more than ten random videos.

Can a B2B company use trends?

Yes, but carefully: borrow the format and rhythm rather than the joke, and fill it with expertise. For example, an "expectation vs reality" format can show how a project unfolds.

Can you use a trend video in ads?

If it performed well, yes — but you may need to swap the music for a royalty-free track. Check the audio rights before running it as an ad.

Conclusion

A trend is an accelerator, not a strategy. Join early, adapt the meaning to your brand and steer clear of questionable topics. To turn reach into sales, you need a strong hook and a clear offer — read our hooks article.
