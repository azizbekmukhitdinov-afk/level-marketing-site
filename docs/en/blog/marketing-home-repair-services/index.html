Marketing home services: plumbers, electricians and cleaners

When a pipe leaks, the power goes off or the air conditioner breaks, nobody watches ads — they search straight away and call whichever tradesperson answers first. For plumbers, electricians, air-conditioner technicians and cleaning services, marketing means being found, replying fast and earning trust. Here's how to build all three.

How customers search

  • "Plumber call-out Chilanzar"
  • "Air conditioner regas price"
  • "Apartment cleaning Tashkent"
  • Asking for recommendations in Telegram groups and from neighbours

These are urgent, specific-need searches. The job goes to whoever answers first and to the point — lead response time.

1. Being found

  • Maps: service area, hours and phone number — add your business to maps.
  • Search ads on "service + district" queries — search ads in Tashkent.
  • Telegram groups for neighbourhoods and housing complexes (following their rules).

2. Transparent prices

The customer's biggest fear: "they'll turn up and quote double". The fix:

  • a price list for common jobs ("from" prices, in soum);
  • a call-out fee known in advance;
  • agreeing the final price before work starts.

3. Trust

  • Who the tradesperson is: name, photo, experience — letting a stranger into your home is scary.
  • Work videos: before/after and the process — faceless content.
  • A guarantee on completed work, with a time limit.
  • Reviews: ask after every job — online reputation and reviews.

4. Taking orders

  • One number or Telegram, so enquiries don't get lost — Telegram Business features.
  • Standard questions: address, problem, a photo or video — so the tradesperson arrives prepared.
  • A precise arrival time, with a message if you're running late.

5. Repeat orders

Air conditioners need servicing every year, and cleaning is regular. Keep a customer record (with consent) and send seasonal reminders: "Time to get your air conditioner ready for summer" — repeat sales and LTV.

Seasons

  • Late spring: air-conditioner servicing.
  • Autumn: heating systems.
  • Before holidays: deep cleaning.

Match the ad budget to the season — advertising in low season.

Questions and answers

Does a solo tradesperson need advertising?

Yes — especially a maps profile and Telegram, the cheapest and most effective start.

Do I need Instagram?

It helps build trust with work videos and reviews. But urgent jobs come mostly from maps and search.

If I publish prices, won't competitors see them?

They will, but so will customers. Transparency builds trust — often more important than price.
