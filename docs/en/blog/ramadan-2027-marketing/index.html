During Ramadan, people's daily routines, shopping habits and social media activity all change. For restaurants, groceries, clothing, gifts, delivery and charity-related businesses, it's one of the most important seasons of the year. In 2027, Ramadan is expected to begin around 8 February (timeanddate) — the official date will be announced closer to the time by the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan. Eid al-Fitr (Ramazon hayiti) will fall around 9–10 March. Preparation should start in January.

What changes during Ramadan

  • Routine: suhoor early in the morning, iftar in the evening. Activity peaks shift to the hours after iftar and late evening.
  • Shopping: groceries, the iftar table, hosting guests, gifts, clothing for Eid.
  • Content: family, spiritual and charity themes draw more interest.
  • Restaurants: iftar bookings and corporate iftars.

A preparation plan (illustrative example)

WhenWhat to do
Mid-JanuaryThe offer: iftar menu, bundles, Eid collection
End of JanuaryContent plan, shoots, creatives
A week before RamadanTake corporate iftar and large orders
During RamadanAlign ad timing with active hours
10 days before EidEid gifts and clothing campaign
After EidThank customers, review results

Advertising: timing and audience

  • Scheduling: in the ads manager you can schedule delivery for active hours — pay attention to the post-iftar hours and the evening. Check against your own statistics from last year.
  • Delivery: "delivered before iftar" must be a specific promise you can keep.
  • Corporate iftars: a B2B offer to companies from early February — B2B marketing.
  • Retargeting people who viewed your menu or catalogue — how retargeting works.

Content ideas

  1. Recipes or bundles for the iftar table.
  2. How your team breaks the fast together — a sincere video — team content.
  3. A charity project: part of every order goes to charity — only if it really happens, with a report.
  4. Suhoor and iftar times — useful, but name your source.
  5. Eid gift ideas.

Etiquette rules

Ramadan is a spiritual month, and audiences are sensitive. The rules:

  • Don't turn religion into a sales slogan: a "Ramadan offer" is different from "the cheapest prices in the holy month".
  • Don't set those who fast against those who don't.
  • Keep charity promises and show it openly.
  • Too many ads in the evening are irritating. Watch your frequency.
  • General advertising rules — advertising law in Uzbekistan.

The sales team

Working hours may change during Ramadan. Announce your hours to customers in advance and keep someone on duty to answer enquiries after iftar too — lead response time.

Questions and answers

Should I pause advertising during Ramadan?

For many businesses, no — it's peak season. But adapt your message, timing and tone.

Which businesses see a low season in Ramadan?

Daytime eateries and some entertainment services may see lower daytime demand. They shift to iftar and evening formats — advertising in low season.

Where can I find the official date?

From announcements by the Muslim Board of Uzbekistan and official media.

Conclusion

Ramadan 2027 is expected to start around 8 February, so begin preparing in January: the offer, content, corporate iftars, ad timing and the Eid campaign. Respect and honesty are the foundation of a brand's reputation this month. In a free audit, Level Marketing will review your seasonal plan.
