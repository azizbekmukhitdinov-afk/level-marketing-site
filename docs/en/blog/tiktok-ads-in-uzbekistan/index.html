TikTok Ads in Uzbekistan: who it suits and how to start

TikTok is a short-video platform, and advertising there works differently from Instagram: people come for entertainment, and your ads have to look the same way. Here is which businesses TikTok Ads suit, what to check before you start and which creatives work.

Check first: is your audience on TikTok

TikTok's availability in Uzbekistan has been restricted at different times. So before committing a budget, check two things:

  1. Are your customers actively using the app right now? Ask them and look at competitors' TikTok activity.
  2. Does TikTok Ads Manager let your account target Uzbekistan and accept your payment?

If the answer is no, the same vertical videos can run on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Who it suits best

  • Products for a young audience: clothing, cosmetics, gadgets.
  • Food, cafés and delivery.
  • Education: language courses, IT, online lessons.
  • Services with visible results: beauty, renovation, car detailing.

Getting started: 5 steps

  1. In TikTok Ads Manager (ads.tiktok.com), open a business account and fill in your company details.
  2. Add a payment method.
  3. If you have a website, install the TikTok Pixel and set up key events.
  4. Choose a campaign objective: traffic, leads or conversions.
  5. Launch a small-budget test campaign with 3–5 different videos.

Which creatives work on TikTok

  • Don't look like an ad. A lively phone-shot video beats a polished commercial.
  • The first 2 seconds are the hook. A problem, a result or an unexpected action. More in our Reels hooks article.
  • Sound and captions. Many TikTok users watch with sound, but captions are still a must.
  • A person talking to camera. A founder, seller or customer — a face builds trust.
  • Spark Ads. Boosting a strong organic video as an ad keeps its likes and comments.

How to judge results

Clicks on TikTok can be cheap, but what matters is cost per lead and per sale. Compare them the same way as your Meta ads: spend, leads, CPL and conversion to sale. For comparing creatives, see our A/B testing guide.

Creative ideas: 6 formats

  1. POV — "When you come to [service] for the first time…" — viewers see themselves in the hero's place.
  2. "3 reasons" — why this product, with fast, rhythmic editing.
  3. A video reply to a comment — answering a customer's question with a separate video.
  4. Behind the scenes — how an order is prepared or a service is done.
  5. Before / after — the result in a few seconds.
  6. An employee's or founder's story — straight to camera, in plain language.

A test plan (example)

Week 13–5 different videos, one audience, a small budget
Week 2Keep the best 1–2 and make 3 variations of them with new hooks
Week 3Gradually raise the winner's budget; run strong organic videos as Spark Ads

Common mistakes

  • Running a polished Instagram ad unchanged.
  • A video without a hook that opens with a logo.
  • A reposted video carrying another platform's watermark.
  • Judging results by cost per click alone.
  • Changing the audience, creative and budget all at once.

Questions and answers

Do you need separate videos for TikTok?

Often one vertical video can run on Reels, Shorts and TikTok — without watermarks and with text adapted to each platform. But a faster pace and a more casual style may work better on TikTok, so test several versions.

What's the minimum budget?

TikTok Ads has a minimum daily budget at the campaign and ad group level — check the current amount in your account. For a test, plan a budget that gives each video enough impressions to draw conclusions.

Should you combine organic TikTok and ads?

Yes. Organic videos let you test ideas cheaply, and the best ones can be promoted as Spark Ads. How to use trends is covered in Using trends for business.

What if leads from TikTok are low quality?

Add one or two qualifying questions to the form (such as city or timing), show the price or a "from" price in the ad, and judge results by sales rather than lead count. Choosing between a form and messages is covered in Lead form or messages.

Which audience settings should you start with?

Avoid very narrow settings: region, age and language are enough to begin with. TikTok finds the people who watch the video to the end on its own — on this platform the creative matters more than targeting.

Conclusion

TikTok Ads is a strong channel for reaching a young, active audience, but first make sure your audience is there and the platform works in Uzbekistan. Either way, the videos will serve you on Reels and Shorts. Level Marketing's videographers turn one shoot into creatives for several platforms at once.
