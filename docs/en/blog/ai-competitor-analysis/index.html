Competitor analysis is something many owners know they should do but never find time for. Scrolling competitors' pages, noting offers, comparing prices — it takes days. AI speeds up the technical part: it organises the data you collect, compares it and suggests conclusions. Here's how to run a competitor and market analysis with AI in a single day.

The goal: difference, not a copy

You study competitors not to copy or criticise them, but to find your own place: what customers aren't getting, where there's a gap, what you can do differently and better. Basic methods — competitor analysis.

Ethical limits

Use public information only:

  • public Instagram and Telegram pages, websites, maps listings;
  • public reviews;
  • ad libraries (such as Meta's public Ad Library);
  • the reply you get as a customer — without false promises.

Not allowed: fake accounts, obtaining private data, asking a competitor's staff for internal information. And don't use the results in ads against a competitor: the law prohibits comparative advertising that damages a competitor's business reputation (Article 16, lex.uz).

A one-day plan

Morning: gathering data (2–3 hours)

For your 3–5 main competitors, fill in a table:

DataSource
Main offer and price guideWebsite, profile, highlights
Topics and formats of the last 20–30 postsInstagram, Telegram
Active adsAd library
Reviews: 20–50Google/Yandex Maps, marketplaces
Response speed and qualitySend a question as a customer

After lunch: analysis with AI (2 hours)

Give the AI the collected text (anonymised, public data) and ask:

  1. Offers: "Compare these 5 companies' offers: what, for whom, with what advantage. Make a table."
  2. Content: "Group the topics of these posts. Which topics does everyone cover, and which does nobody?"
  3. Ads: "Which promises and hooks repeat across these ads?"
  4. Reviews: "What do customers praise and complain about in these reviews? The 5 most frequent problems."
  5. Gaps: "Based on all of the above, which customer need is nobody serving well?"

How to analyse reviews — AI review analysis.

Evening: conclusions and decisions (1–2 hours)

AI's conclusions are hypotheses, not facts. Human work:

  • check each conclusion against your own knowledge of the market;
  • pick the 3 main gaps;
  • plan changes to your offer, content or service based on them.

Strengthening your offer — unique selling proposition.

What the result should look like

A one-page summary (illustrative example: a furniture store):

FindingMeaningOur action
Everyone says "quality furniture", nobody shows proofTrust is an open spaceProduction videos, warranty details
The most frequent complaint in reviews is installation timeSpeed is a valueA specific installation timeframe in the offer
Nobody shows prices openlyPrice transparency is a differentiator"From" prices in highlights

Where AI goes wrong

  • Big conclusions from little data: a "market trend" from 10 reviews.
  • Invented market figures: "the market is worth X million dollars" — don't trust numbers without a source.
  • Outdated knowledge: if AI tells you about competitors from its own knowledge, it may be old or wrong — have it analyse only your material.

How often to repeat it

Quarterly — a short version (half a day). When launching a product, changing prices or when a new competitor appears — the full version.

Common mistakes

  • Analysing in order to copy.
  • Asking AI what it "knows" about competitors without giving it data.
  • Looking only at content, not reviews and service quality.
  • Not turning conclusions into actions.
  • Using findings in ads to run down a competitor.

Questions and answers

How many competitors should I analyse?

Three to five main ones: the businesses your customers compare you with.

Can AI read a competitor's page by itself?

Some tools can read links, but results may be incomplete. It's more reliable to collect the data yourself and give it to the AI as text.

Can AI calculate market size?

Only from reliable sources you provide. Don't use market figures the AI produces without checking them.

Is it better if an agency does the analysis?

An agency brings experience across niches and an outside view. But you can do a basic analysis yourself with this guide.

Conclusion

AI turns competitor analysis into a one-day job: collect public data, compare and group it with AI, check the conclusions as a human and turn them into three actions.
