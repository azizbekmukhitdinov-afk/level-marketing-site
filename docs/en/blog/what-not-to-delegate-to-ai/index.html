What not to delegate to AI: 7 costly business mistakes

Most articles about AI cover "what you can hand over". This one covers the opposite: which tasks, if fully handed to AI, cost a business money, customers or reputation. The list isn't anti-AI — it shows where a human decision is essential. Where AI does help — AI in marketing for small business.

A decision matrix

Rate each task with two questions: how costly is a mistake, and how quickly would you notice it?

Cheap mistakeCostly mistake
Visible immediatelyHand it to AI freely (post ideas)AI + human check (ad copy)
Visible lateAI + spot checks (review analysis)A human decides; AI only assists

The seven tasks below sit in the bottom-right cell.

1. Pricing and discount decisions

AI can suggest pricing ideas, but it doesn't fully know your costs, competition, customers or cash flow. If an AI bot promises a customer a discount on its own initiative, that's a direct loss. On pricing decisions — how to raise prices and the discount trap.

2. Complaints and conflicts

An automated reply to an upset customer pours oil on the fire. AI can draft a reply, but sending it and fixing the problem are human jobs. Handling negative reviews — online reputation and reviews.

3. Decisions about staff

Hiring, firing, appraisals, bonuses — they affect people's rights and interests. Law No. ZRU-1115 of 21 January 2026 prohibits basing legally significant decisions that affect people's rights and freedoms solely on AI outputs (lex.uz). AI can help sort CVs; a human makes the decision.

4. Legal texts and contracts

AI drafts a contract quickly — but compliance with Uzbek law, the current version of the rules and the terms that matter in your situation need a lawyer. A flawed contract shows its problems months later, in a dispute.

5. Claims and promises in ads

"The best", "guaranteed results", "100% safe", statistics without a source — AI writes these easily. The law prohibits unproven comparative claims and misleading advertising (lex.uz, Articles 5 and 16). Every claim needs proof. More in advertising law in Uzbekistan.

6. Customer data

Uploading the customer base to an AI service "for segmentation" is the most common mistake. Unlawfully processing personal data with AI carries a fine of 50–100 BRV (ZRU-1115). Anonymised data only. More in AI and the law in Uzbekistan.

7. Strategy and key decisions

A new branch, a new product, a big budget, choosing an agency or partner — AI offers options and questions, but responsibility and context are yours. An AI plan will be generic because it doesn't know your market, team and finances the way you do. The foundation of strategy — one-page marketing strategy.

Using AI properly for these tasks

AI is useful here too — as an assistant:

StaffSort CVs against criteriaInterview and decide
ContractsA list of questions, a draftLawyer review
AdsCopy variationsVerify the claims
DataAnalyse anonymised dataAnonymise, draw conclusions
StrategyOptions, a list of risksChoose and take responsibility

An internal rule: "AI drafts, a human signs"

Give your team one simple principle: AI may draft any document, reply or plan, but anything that goes outside and affects money, rights or a customer is read and "signed off" by a human. Training your team — train your team on AI.

Questions and answers

Can AI never do these tasks?

It helps with drafts and analysis. The point is the final decision and responsibility — they should stay with a human.

Can an AI bot tell a customer the price?

Yes, if the price is approved in the knowledge base. Discounts and special terms — salespeople only. More in Telegram AI assistant.

Aren't these rules overkill for a small business?

The opposite: in a small business, one mistake — one big client lost or one fine — hurts more.

Which mistake is most common?

Uploading customer data and putting unchecked AI copy into ads.

Conclusion

AI speeds up a lot of work, but final decisions on pricing, complaints, staff, contracts, advertising claims, customer data and strategy must stay with people. The principle is simple: AI drafts, a human signs. The Level Marketing team uses AI tools exactly this way — a specialist checks everything that goes to a client or into an ad.
