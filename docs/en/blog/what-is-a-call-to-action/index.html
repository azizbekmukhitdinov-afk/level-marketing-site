What is a call to action (CTA) and how to write one that works

People liked the ad and the post was interesting — but they don't know what to do next, so they leave. The cause is often the same: the CTA (call to action) is missing or vague. A CTA is the line or button that tells the reader the next step clearly: "Get a price", "Book a free measurement". It looks minor, but it has a big effect on conversion. Here's how to write and place CTAs.

Signs of a good CTA

  • A clear action: "message", "book", "get" — a verb.
  • A benefit: what you'll get — "get the price", "get the catalogue".
  • An easy step: "in 2 minutes", "free".
  • Just one: one main action in one place.

"Learn more""Get a price in 1 minute"
"Contact us""Send your measurements on Telegram — we'll price it"
"Submit""Book a free consultation"

(Illustrative examples.)

For different places

  • Paid social: at the end of the creative, by voice or text, plus a button ("Learn more", "Sign up" and so on) — pick the button that matches your objective.
  • Landing pages: in the hero section and at the bottom — the same action — a landing page that converts.
  • Instagram: in the bio and posts, with clear instructions like "Get a price in 1 minute — link in bio" — Instagram bio examples.
  • Telegram: a button or link in the post, to a bot or salesperson — a Telegram bot for leads.
  • Search ads: in the headline or description — how to write ad copy.

Where to place it

  • Video: in the last 2–3 seconds — but state the offer at the start too, since most people don't watch to the end.
  • Long pages: several times, after each key section.
  • Posts: at the end, on a separate line.

The one-main-action rule

"Call us, message us on Telegram, visit our site, follow us" — four CTAs confuse people. Pick one main action and make the rest secondary.

After the action

The CTA worked and the person got in touch. Now a fast reply matters, or the lead will be lost — lead response time.

Common mistakes

  • No CTA at all.
  • Generic words: "Learn more", "Click".
  • Several equally weighted actions.
  • A button that doesn't match what the page delivers.
  • Too big a step, like "Sign a contract" at first contact.

Questions and answers

How does a CTA differ from an offer?

The offer is what you get; the CTA is how you get it — the next step.

Can I test CTAs?

Yes: same creative, different CTAs — an A/B test — A/B testing ad creatives.

Do pressure CTAs ("today only!") work?

If the deadline is real, yes. Fake urgency destroys trust and can count as misleading advertising.

Conclusion

A CTA is the line that names the next step clearly: a verb, a benefit, an easy step and one main action. Place it in the right spot in your ads, website, Instagram and Telegram, and reply fast at the next stage. In a free audit, Level Marketing will check the conversion of your ads and pages.
